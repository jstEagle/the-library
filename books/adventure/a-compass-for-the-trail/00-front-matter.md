# A Compass for the Trail

**by August Ishikawa**

> Orsen Underhill, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, has spent twelve years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
