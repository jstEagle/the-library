# A Current for the Horizon

*by Gideon Brightwater*

> Zephyrine Underhill, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides, and time is already running out.
