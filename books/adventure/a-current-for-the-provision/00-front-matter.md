## About the author

Orsen Dunmore is the pen name of a former hydrological surveyor who spent six seasons mapping remote river systems before walking away from the industry. He now writes adventure fiction from a cabin with no road access, drawing on the landscapes and moral compromises he witnessed firsthand.
