# A Escarpment for the Horizon

*by Marguerite Voss*

> Zephyrine Sterling, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the dry season ends.
