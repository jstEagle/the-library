# A Escarpment for the Rivergate

*by F. M. Brightwater*

> Ines Halloran, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the monsoon makes landfall.

## About the author

F. M. Brightwater spent a decade working seasonal river freight before turning to writing, and their fiction is known for waterlogged moral landscapes and crews that argue like families. They live in a houseboat they refuse to name, on a river they refuse to leave.
