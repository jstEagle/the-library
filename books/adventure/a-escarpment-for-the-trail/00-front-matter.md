## About the author

Rafael Winterbourne is a former floatplane mechanic who spent six years in remote northern camps before turning to writing full-time. His fiction draws on the pilots, prospectors, and quiet liars he met along the rivers, and on his conviction that wilderness is less cruel than the men who claim to own it.
