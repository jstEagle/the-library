# A Escarpment for the Wreck

*by Wren Pemberton*

> Emrys Nystrom, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must cross the range ahead of the snows, and time is already running out.
