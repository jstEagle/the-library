# A Horizon for the Current

*by Marguerite Marchetti*

> Zephyrine Pemberton, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Marguerite Marchetti is a former river pilot who spent a decade charting tidal waterways across three continents before turning to fiction. She writes adventure novels about people the maps forgot, from a houseboat moored wherever the current allows.
