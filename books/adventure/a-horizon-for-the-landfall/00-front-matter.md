# A Horizon for the Landfall

**by Wren Villalobos**

> Elodie Zhou, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Wren Villalobos is a former river guide who spent six seasons on glacial waterways before turning to fiction full-time. Their work explores how people build families in transit, and their stories are written mostly in ferry terminals and cheap motels.
