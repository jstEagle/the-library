## About the author

Yara Sterling spent a decade leading river expeditions before turning to fiction, and her prose carries the mud, cold, and silence of real wilderness travel. She writes from a cabin she refuses to locate precisely, on the theory that good adventure stories need somewhere unaccounted for.
