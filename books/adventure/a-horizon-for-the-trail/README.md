# A Horizon for the Trail

*by Ines Zhou*

> Marguerite Ashgrove, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, keeps moving because stopping costs more. Then the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
