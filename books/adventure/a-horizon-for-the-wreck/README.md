# A Horizon for the Wreck

*by Beatrix Voss*

> Sable Villalobos, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, has spent fifteen years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must cross the range ahead of the snows before the concession permits expire.

## About the author

Beatrix Voss is a former tugboat deckhand who spent six seasons on inland waterways before turning to writing full-time from a houseboat moored outside Astoria. Her fiction draws on river lore, salvage law, and the quiet radicalism of people who live at the water's edge.
