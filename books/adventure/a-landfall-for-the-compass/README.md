# A Landfall for the Compass

*by Zephyrine Vasquez*

> Wren Nakamura, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.
