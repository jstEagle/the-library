# A Landfall for the Current

*by Beatrix Nystrom*

> Paloma Almeida, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.
