# A Landfall for the Horizon

*by Dominic Hartwell*

> Rosalind Marchetti, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.
