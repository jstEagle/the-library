## About the author

Zephyrine Vasquez is a former deckhand turned novelist who spent six seasons working river freight routes in Southeast Asia before writing full-time. Her fiction draws on the rhythms of monsoon country, where every journey is a negotiation with water, weather, and the people you can't leave behind.
