# A Monsoon for the Horizon

*by Zephyrine Vasquez*

> Oscar Fairweather, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the concession permits expire.

## About the author

Zephyrine Vasquez is a former deckhand turned novelist who spent six seasons working river freight routes in Southeast Asia before writing full-time. Her fiction draws on the rhythms of monsoon country, where every journey is a negotiation with water, weather, and the people you can't leave behind.
