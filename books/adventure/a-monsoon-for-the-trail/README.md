# A Monsoon for the Trail

*by Paloma Underhill*

> Silas Villalobos, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, has spent three years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides, and time is already running out.
