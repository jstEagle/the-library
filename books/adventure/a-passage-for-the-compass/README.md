# A Passage for the Compass

*by Hazel Sandoval*

> Edmund Joubert, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then an expedition upriver stops answering the radio — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must cross the range ahead of the snows before the monsoon makes landfall.

Hazel Sandoval is the pen name of a former river guide who spent six seasons working commercial routes in remote delta country before turning to fiction. She writes adventure stories about people who belong more to water and weather than to any one place.
