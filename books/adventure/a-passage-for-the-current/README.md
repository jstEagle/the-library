# A Passage for the Current

*by Paloma Oyelaran*

> Ada Quintana, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must cross the range ahead of the snows before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
