# A Provision for the Horizon

*by Lena Brightwater*

> Bram Vane, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, has spent five years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the dry season ends.

## About the author

Lena Brightwater is the pen name of a former river surveyor who spent a decade mapping waterways from the Orinoco to the Mekong before turning to fiction. She writes adventure novels about people who measure life in miles and find out, too late, that miles were never the unit that mattered.
