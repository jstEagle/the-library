## About the author

Coral Ashgrove is the pen name of a former deckhand turned novelist who spent six seasons working freight runs on inland waterways before trading the wheelhouse for a writing desk. Her fiction is known for its riverine settings, weather-worn characters, and stubborn belief that people can choose their own families.
