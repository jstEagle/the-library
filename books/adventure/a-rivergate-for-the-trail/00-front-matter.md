## About the author

Ivo Kavanagh is the pen name of a former river guide turned writer who spent six seasons leading expeditions through coastal cave systems before a near-drowning ended that career. Kavanagh now writes adventure fiction from a houseboat, drawing on a private archive of tide charts, expedition logs, and stories guides tell after dark.
