# A Summit for the Monsoon

*by Emrys Vasquez*

> Felix Joubert, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
