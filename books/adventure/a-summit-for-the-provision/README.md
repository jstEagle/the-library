*by Emrys Montrose*

> Rafael Ishikawa, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
