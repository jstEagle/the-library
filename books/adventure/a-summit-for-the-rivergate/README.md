# A Summit for the Rivergate

*by Odile Sandoval*

> Ivo Eastwick, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then an expedition upriver stops answering the radio — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the dry season ends.
