# A Summit for the Wreck

*by Rosalind Winterbourne*

> Ines Halloran, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source, and time is already running out.
