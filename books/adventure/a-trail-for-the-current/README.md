# A Trail for the Current

*by Sebastian Underhill*

> Sable Nystrom, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. Then the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the monsoon makes landfall.
