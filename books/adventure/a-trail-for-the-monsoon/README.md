# A Trail for the Monsoon

*by August Voss*

> Coral Underhill, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the monsoon makes landfall.
