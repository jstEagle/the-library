# A Trail for the Rivergate

*by Hazel Joubert*

> Ulyana Okonkwo, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, keeps moving because stopping costs more. Then the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides, and time is already running out.
