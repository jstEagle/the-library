# A Wreck for the Escarpment

*by Paloma Brightwater*

> Marguerite Zhou, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, has spent twenty years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then an expedition upriver stops answering the radio — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the concession permits expire.

## About the author

Paloma Brightwater is the pen name of a former hydrographic surveyor who spent six seasons on tropical rivers before turning to fiction. She writes adventure novels about people whose professions demand they read the weather better than they read each other.
