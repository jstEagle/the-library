# A Wreck for the Rivergate

*by Hazel Dunmore*

> Hazel Thornbury, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a dying prospector trades a map for a promise, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the concession permits expire.

## About the author

Hazel Dunmore spent six years as a float-plane dispatcher in northern bush country, where she collected the river stories that fuel her fiction. She writes adventure novels about people who survive by their wits and are haunted by what survival cost them.
