# A Wreck for the Summit

*by Paloma Oyelaran*

> Petra Zhou, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the dry season ends.
