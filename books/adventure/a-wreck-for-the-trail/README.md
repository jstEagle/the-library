# A Wreck for the Trail

*by Juniper Fairweather*

> Sable Fairweather, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
