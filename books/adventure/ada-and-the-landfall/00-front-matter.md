## About the author

Paloma Sterling is the pen name of a former deckhand turned novelist who spent six seasons working freight runs on tropical rivers before trading the wheelhouse for a writing desk. Her fiction draws on the boatfolk's oral traditions, blending salt-crusted adventure with quiet meditations on memory and obligation.
