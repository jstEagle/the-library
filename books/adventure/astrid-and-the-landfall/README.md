# Astrid and the Landfall

*by Yara Kavanagh*

> Edmund Amberdale, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, has spent five years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.
