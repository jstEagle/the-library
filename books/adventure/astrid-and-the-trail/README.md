# Astrid and the Trail

*by Vesper Reyes*

> Marguerite Coldharbour, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent five years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.
