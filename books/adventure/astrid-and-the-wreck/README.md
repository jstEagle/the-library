# Astrid and the Wreck

*by Sable Ishikawa*

> Orsen Dunmore, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.
