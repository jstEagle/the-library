# Bram and the Horizon

*by Odile Blackwood*

> Jonah Blackwood, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a dying prospector trades a map for a promise, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the monsoon makes landfall.

## About the author

Odile Blackwood is the pen name of a former expedition cook who spent a decade in Himalayan base camps writing in the margins of trail ledgers. Bram and the Horizon is their second novel, drawn from stories overheard at high-altitude tea houses.
