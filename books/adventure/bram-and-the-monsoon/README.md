*by Felix Iversen*

> Isandro Sterling, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a dying prospector trades a map for a promise, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the dry season ends.
