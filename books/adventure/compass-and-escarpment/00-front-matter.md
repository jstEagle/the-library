## About the author

Gideon Coldharbour is the pen name of a former maritime surveyor who spent a decade mapping coastlines from Norway to Patagonia before turning to fiction. He writes adventure novels about people who live at the edge of maps, drawing on his own seasons aboard research vessels and in high-altitude base camps.
