# Compass and Escarpment

*by Gideon Coldharbour*

> Casper Whitlock, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must cross the range ahead of the snows before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

## About the author

Gideon Coldharbour is the pen name of a former maritime surveyor who spent a decade mapping coastlines from Norway to Patagonia before turning to fiction. He writes adventure novels about people who live at the edge of maps, drawing on his own seasons aboard research vessels and in high-altitude base camps.
