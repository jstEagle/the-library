# Compass and Horizon

**by Marguerite Lockridge**

> Coral Underhill, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, has spent twenty years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the dry season ends.

## About the author

Marguerite Lockridge is the pen name of a former bush-strip radio operator who spent a decade in remote river settlements collecting the stories pilots tell only at altitude. She writes adventure novels where the landscape keeps the receipts and the maps are never quite finished.
