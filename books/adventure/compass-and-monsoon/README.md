# Compass and Monsoon

*by Wren Sterling*

> Gideon Zhou, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, has spent three years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.
