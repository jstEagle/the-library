# Compass and Trail

*by Odile Underhill*

> Tamsin Nakamura, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, has spent fifteen years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source, and time is already running out.
