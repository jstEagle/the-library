# Coral and the Passage

*by Felix Zhou*

> Astrid Ashgrove, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must cross the range ahead of the snows before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
