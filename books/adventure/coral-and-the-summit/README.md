# Coral and the Summit

*by Xanthe Iversen*

> Hazel Nystrom, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent twenty years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a dying prospector trades a map for a promise — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
