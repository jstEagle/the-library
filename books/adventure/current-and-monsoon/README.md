# Current and Monsoon

*by Casper Reyes*

> Felix Sterling, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then an expedition upriver stops answering the radio — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the dry season ends.

## About the author

Casper Reyes is the pen name of a former river guide who spent six seasons working the monsoon trade routes of Southeast Asia before turning to writing. Their fiction blends engineering precision with the restless ache of people searching for a place to belong.
