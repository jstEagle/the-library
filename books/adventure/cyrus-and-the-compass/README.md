*by Sebastian Ravensworth*

> Noor Ashgrove, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.
