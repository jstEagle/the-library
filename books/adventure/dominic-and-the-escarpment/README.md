# Dominic and the Escarpment

*by Mirabel Brightwater*

> Dominic Dunmore, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the monsoon makes landfall.
