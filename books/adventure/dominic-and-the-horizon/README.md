# Dominic and the Horizon

*by Isandro Blackwood*

> Rosalind Sterling, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

## About the author

Isandro Blackwood is the pen name of a former river guide turned novelist who spent six seasons in the Himalayan foothills before trading rapids for manuscripts. Known for adventure fiction where landscapes are characters and survival is never the hardest part, Blackwood writes from a houseboat on an unnamed tributary.
