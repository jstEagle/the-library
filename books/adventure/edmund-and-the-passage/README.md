# Edmund and the Passage

*by Casper Fairweather*

> Felix Nakamura, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, keeps moving because stopping costs more. Then the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
