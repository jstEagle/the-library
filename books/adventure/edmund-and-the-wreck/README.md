# Edmund and the Wreck

*by Tamsin Galloway*

> Beatrix Underhill, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, has spent five years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then an expedition upriver stops answering the radio — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides, and time is already running out.
