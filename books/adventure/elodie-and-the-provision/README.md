# Elodie and the Provision

*by Ulyana Whitlock*

> Jude Blackwood, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
