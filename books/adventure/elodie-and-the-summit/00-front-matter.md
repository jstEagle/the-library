# Elodie and the Summit

**by Rosalind Eriksen**

> Xanthe Crane, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, has spent fifteen years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the dry season ends.
