# Emrys and the Compass

*by Tamsin Oyelaran*

> Kestrel Amberdale, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.
