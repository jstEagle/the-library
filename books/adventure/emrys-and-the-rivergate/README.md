# Emrys and the Rivergate

*by Sable Blackwood*

> Ada Eriksen, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source, and time is already running out.
