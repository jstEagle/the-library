# Emrys and the Wreck

*by Sebastian Delacroix*

> Odile Blackwood, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.
