## About the author

H. M. Villalobos is the pen name of a former marine surveyor who spent a decade working coastal salvage contracts across the Gulf of Mexico and Central America. They now write adventure fiction from a houseboat, drawing on firsthand knowledge of rivers, wrecks, and the people who make their living from both.
