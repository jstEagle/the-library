# Escarpment and Horizon

*by Rosalind Nakamura*

> Petra Eastwick, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, has spent fifteen years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides, and time is already running out.
