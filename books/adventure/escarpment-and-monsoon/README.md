# Escarpment and Monsoon

*by Marguerite Zhou*

> Felix Vane, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.
