# Escarpment and Summit

**by Elodie Iversen**

> Lena Marchetti, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, has spent twenty years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
