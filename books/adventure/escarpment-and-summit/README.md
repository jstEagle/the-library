# Escarpment and Summit

*by Elodie Iversen*

> Lena Marchetti, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, has spent twenty years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the concession permits expire.
