# Freya and the Compass

*by Yara Whitlock*

> Lior Dunmore, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. Then the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the monsoon makes landfall.

## About the author

Yara Whitlock is a former river surveyor who spent a decade mapping waterways across three continents before turning to fiction. She writes adventure stories about people the maps forgot, from a cabin she shares with a one-eared dog named Sextant.
