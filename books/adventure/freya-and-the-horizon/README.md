# Freya and the Horizon

*by Oscar Brightwater*

> Rosalind Whitlock, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. Then the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the concession permits expire.

## About the author

Oscar Brightwater is the pen name of a former river-logging surveyor who spent eleven years guiding in fjord country before trading the water for the page. He writes adventure novels about people the wilderness refuses to either forgive or forget.
