# Freya and the Monsoon

*by Astrid Coldharbour*

> Juniper Ravensworth, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, has spent fifteen years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.
