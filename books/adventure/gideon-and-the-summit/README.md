# Gideon and the Summit

*by Hazel Pemberton*

> August Joubert, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source, and time is already running out.
