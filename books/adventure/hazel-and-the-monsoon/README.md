# Hazel and the Monsoon

*by Zephyrine Halloran*

> Oscar Nakamura, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, has spent twelve years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the dry season ends.
