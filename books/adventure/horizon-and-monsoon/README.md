# Horizon and Monsoon

*by Kestrel Vasquez*

> Quentin Ashgrove, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, keeps moving because stopping costs more. Then a dying prospector trades a map for a promise — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the monsoon makes landfall.

## About the author

Kestrel Vasquez spent a decade working seasonal river freight before turning to writing full-time, and her fiction is known for its granular knowledge of water, weather, and the people who live by both. She lives half the year aboard a restored sternwheeler with her dog, Barge.
