# Ines and the Trail

**by Vesper Hartwell**

> Mirabel Montrose, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, has spent twelve years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a dying prospector trades a map for a promise, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the monsoon makes landfall.

## About the author

Vesper Hartwell is the pen name of a former deckhand turned novelist who spent six seasons working freight rivers in Southeast Asia before writing full-time. Their fiction blends nautical realism with quiet character studies of people who live at the edge of maps.
