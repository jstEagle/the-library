## About the author

Mirabel Ishikawa spent six years as a hydrology field technician before turning to fiction, and her novels are known for landscapes that behave like characters with grudges. She writes from a converted water station on the edge of a dry lakebed, where she keeps meticulous journals of weather she refuses to romanticize.
