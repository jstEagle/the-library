# Isandro and the Escarpment

*by Mirabel Ishikawa*

> Elodie Yarrow, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the concession permits expire.

## About the author

Mirabel Ishikawa spent six years as a hydrology field technician before turning to fiction, and her novels are known for landscapes that behave like characters with grudges. She writes from a converted water station on the edge of a dry lakebed, where she keeps meticulous journals of weather she refuses to romanticize.
