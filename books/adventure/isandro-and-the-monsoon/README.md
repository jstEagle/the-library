# Isandro and the Monsoon

*by Juniper Reyes*

> Hazel Brightwater, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent fifteen years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
