# Ivo and the Summit

**by Ines Montrose**

> Rosalind Fairweather, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Ines Montrose is the pen name of a former alpine rescue dispatcher who spent a decade listening to radio traffic from mountains she never climbed. She writes adventure fiction from a small farmhouse, where she keeps a wall of topographic maps annotated with stories that never made the official reports.
