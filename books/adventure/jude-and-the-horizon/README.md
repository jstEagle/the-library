# Jude and the Horizon

*by Lior Reyes*

> Petra Lockridge, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the concession permits expire.
