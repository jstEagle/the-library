# Jude and the Wreck

*by Juniper Voss*

> Coral Galloway, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the dry season ends.
