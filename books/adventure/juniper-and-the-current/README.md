# Juniper and the Current

*by Noor Sandoval*

> Rafael Thackeray, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must cross the range ahead of the snows before the concession permits expire.
