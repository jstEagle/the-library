## About the author

Tamsin Nystrom is a former deckhand on Arctic survey vessels who turned to writing after a storm off the Lofoten Islands left her with a broken wrist and too many stories. She lives in a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage in Norway, where she still climbs, dives, and keeps a brass compass on her desk that has never once pointed north.
