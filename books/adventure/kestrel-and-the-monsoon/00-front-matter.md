## About the author

Isandro Kavanagh is a former barge surveyor who spent eleven years charting seasonal waterways across three continents before turning to fiction. He writes adventure novels about people whose homes are vehicles, drawing on his notebooks of river pilots, mountain porters, and the maps they refuse to sell.
