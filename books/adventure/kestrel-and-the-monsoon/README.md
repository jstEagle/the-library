# Kestrel and the Monsoon

*by Isandro Kavanagh*

> Ivo Crane, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must cross the range ahead of the snows before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

## About the author

Isandro Kavanagh is a former barge surveyor who spent eleven years charting seasonal waterways across three continents before turning to fiction. He writes adventure novels about people whose homes are vehicles, drawing on his notebooks of river pilots, mountain porters, and the maps they refuse to sell.
