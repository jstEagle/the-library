## About the author

Noor Ishikawa is a former marine surveyor who traded sonar readouts for sentences and now writes adventure fiction from a houseboat on a slow northern river. Kestrel and the Wreck is her third novel and her first to draw directly on years spent mapping waterways no one was supposed to question.
