# Landfall and Compass

*by Ansel Ravensworth*

> Juniper Winterbourne, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, keeps moving because stopping costs more. Then the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the monsoon makes landfall.

Ansel Ravensworth is the pen name of a former barge-hand turned novelist who spent six years working the inland waterways of three continents before trading the wheelhouse for a writing desk. His fiction is known for its river-haunted prose and characters who mistake motion for freedom.
