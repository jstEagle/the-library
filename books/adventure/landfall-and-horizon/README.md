# Landfall and Horizon

*by Coral Voss*

> Freya Reyes, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the dry season ends.
