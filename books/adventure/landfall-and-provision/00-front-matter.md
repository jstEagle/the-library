## About the author

Paloma Ishikawa is a former wilderness first-responder who spent six seasons leading expeditions in Patagonia and the Karakoram before turning to fiction full-time. She writes adventure novels from a cabin outside Haines, Alaska, where she still guides part-time and keeps a wall of maps annotated with stories that never made it home.
