**by Edmund Dravenmoor**

## About the author

Edmund Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former merchant marine who spent a decade running salvage contracts along cold northern rivers before turning to fiction. He writes adventure novels in which landscapes are adversaries and every moral choice has a current pulling against it.
