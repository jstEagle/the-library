# Lena and the Landfall

*by Yara Joubert*

> Mara Lockridge, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when a dying prospector trades a map for a promise, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the monsoon makes landfall.
