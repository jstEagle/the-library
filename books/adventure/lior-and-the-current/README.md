# Lior and the Current

*by August Blackwood*

> August Sandoval, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, has spent twelve years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then an expedition upriver stops answering the radio — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must cross the range ahead of the snows before the concession permits expire.
