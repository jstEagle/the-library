# Lior and the Provision

*by Casper Eastwick*

> Isandro Vane, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a dying prospector trades a map for a promise, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
