## About the author

Dominic Voss is a former bush pilot turned novelist who spent six years flying supply runs across remote river country before trading his logbook for a manuscript. He writes adventure fiction about people who measure their lives in miles, drawing on real maps, real weather, and the rumors pilots trade over bad coffee.
