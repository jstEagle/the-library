# Lior and the Rivergate

*by Dominic Voss*

> Isandro Fairweather, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, has spent twelve years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a dying prospector trades a map for a promise — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the dry season ends.

## About the author

Dominic Voss is a former bush pilot turned novelist who spent six years flying supply runs across remote river country before trading his logbook for a manuscript. He writes adventure fiction about people who measure their lives in miles, drawing on real maps, real weather, and the rumors pilots trade over bad coffee.
