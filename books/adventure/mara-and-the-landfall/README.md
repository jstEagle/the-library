*by Sebastian Brightwater*

But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Sebastian Brightwater is the pen name of a former harbor pilot who spent eleven years working coastal freight routes before turning to fiction full-time. He writes adventure novels about people whose competence outstrips their options, drawing on a lifetime of charts, tide tables, and the stories crews tell after dark.
