# Mara and the Passage

*by Z. M. Voss*

> Freya Dravenmoor, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must cross the range ahead of the snows before the concession permits expire.

## About the author

Z. M. Voss is the pen name of a former northern freight pilot turned novelist who spent six years flying supplies into communities the maps forgot. Their fiction draws on that time, blending aviation realism with stories about the people and places official records overlook.
