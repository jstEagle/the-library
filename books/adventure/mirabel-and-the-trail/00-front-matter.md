## About the author

Edmund Vasquez is the pen name of a former floatplane mechanic who spent nine years ferrying supplies along remote coastlines before turning to fiction. He writes adventure novels from a converted boathouse, drawing on the tides, wrecks, and stubborn pilots he came to know.
