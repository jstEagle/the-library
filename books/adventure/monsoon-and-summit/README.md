# Monsoon and Summit

*by G. M. Thornbury*

> Mirabel Ishikawa, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the monsoon makes landfall.
