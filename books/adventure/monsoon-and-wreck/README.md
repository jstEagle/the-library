# Monsoon and Wreck

*by Mirabel Sterling*

> Wren Yarrow, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source, and time is already running out.
