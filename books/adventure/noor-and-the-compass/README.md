# Noor and the Compass

*by Freya Montrose*

> Freya Fairweather, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then a dying prospector trades a map for a promise — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
