# Noor and the Provision

*by Marguerite Pemberton*

> Jonah Zhou, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.
