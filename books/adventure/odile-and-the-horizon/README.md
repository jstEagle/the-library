*by Astrid Dunmore*

> Ansel Vasquez, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the concession permits expire.

## About the author

Astrid Dunmore is the pen name of a former maritime surveyor who spent seven years charting coastlines from Baja to Patagonia before turning to fiction. She writes adventure novels about people who trust tides more than institutions.
