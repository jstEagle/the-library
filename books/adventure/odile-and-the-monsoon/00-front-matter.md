# Odile and the Monsoon

**by Beatrix Whitlock**

> Ulyana Underhill, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. ## About the author

Beatrix Whitlock is the pen name of a former expedition cook who spent six seasons in the Himalayas writing field notes nobody asked for. She now lives in a coastal town where the rain arrives on schedule, and writes adventure novels about people who run out of places to hide.
