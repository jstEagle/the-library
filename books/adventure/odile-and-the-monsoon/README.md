# Odile and the Monsoon

*by Beatrix Whitlock*

> Ulyana Underhill, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a dying prospector trades a map for a promise, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the concession permits expire.

## About the author

Beatrix Whitlock is the pen name of a former expedition cook who spent six seasons in the Himalayas writing field notes nobody asked for. She now lives in a coastal town where the rain arrives on schedule, and writes adventure novels about people who run out of places to hide.
