# Passage and Horizon

*by Ansel Sterling*

> Sebastian Villalobos, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ansel Sterling is the pen name of a former merchant marine who spent eleven years working coastal salvage routes across Southeast Asia before turning to fiction. He writes adventure novels about people the sea keeps, and lives in a port town he refuses to name.
