# Passage and Rivergate

**by Quentin Marchetti**

> Gideon Yarrow, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Quentin Marchetti is the pen name of a former floatplane mechanic who spent six seasons in remote northern camps before turning to fiction. Passage and Rivergate is his third novel and the first written entirely from memory rather than research.
