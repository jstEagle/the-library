# Passage and Summit

*by Kestrel Marchetti*

> Petra Reyes, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent five years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then an expedition upriver stops answering the radio — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the dry season ends.
