# Provision and Monsoon

*by Felix Eriksen*

> Juniper Vane, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a dying prospector trades a map for a promise, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the dry season ends.
