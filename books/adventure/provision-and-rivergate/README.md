# Provision and Rivergate

*by Odile Whitlock*

> Oscar Yarrow, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.
