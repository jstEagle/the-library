# Rivergate and Compass

*by Lior Villalobos*

> Coral Voss, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the dry season ends.
