# Rivergate and Escarpment

*by Dominic Marchetti*

> Orsen Vasquez, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, has spent five years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the concession permits expire.

## About the author

Dominic Marchetti is a former river barge pilot who spent a decade working the freight lines between forgotten river towns before turning to fiction. He writes adventure novels about people who measure their lives in water levels, border crossings, and the things they refuse to say out loud.
