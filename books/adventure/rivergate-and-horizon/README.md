# Rivergate and Horizon

*by Yara Fairweather*

> Tamsin Dunmore, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, has spent seven years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides, and time is already running out.
