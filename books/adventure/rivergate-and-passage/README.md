# Rivergate and Passage

*by Vesper Underhill*

> Odile Lockridge, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, has spent three years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must cross the range ahead of the snows before the dry season ends.
