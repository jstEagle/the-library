# Rivergate and Trail

*by Ada Eastwick*

> Mirabel Vane, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, keeps moving because stopping costs more. Then an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.
