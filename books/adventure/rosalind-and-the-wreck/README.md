# Rosalind and the Wreck

*by C. M. Joubert*

> Quentin Eriksen, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. Then the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
