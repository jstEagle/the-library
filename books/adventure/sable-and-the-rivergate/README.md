# Sable and the Rivergate

*by Jude Okonkwo*

> Gideon Dunmore, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, has spent five years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the monsoon makes landfall.
