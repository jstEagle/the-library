# Sable and the Summit

*by August Lockridge*

> Edmund Dunmore, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, has spent seven years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must cross the range ahead of the snows before the dry season ends.
