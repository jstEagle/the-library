# Silas and the Wreck

*by Isandro Okonkwo*

> Casper Ravensworth, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent five years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.
