# Summit and Horizon

*by Vesper Okafor*

> Ada Eriksen, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides, and time is already running out.
