# The Broken Compass

*by B. M. Nakamura*

> Lena Halloran, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the monsoon makes landfall.

## About the author

B. M. Nakamura is the pen name of a former expedition logistics coordinator who spent eight seasons in the Karakoram before trading base camps for a writing desk in Vancouver. Their fiction draws on real route notes, rescued journals, and the quiet debts climbers owe each other.
