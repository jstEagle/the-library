# The Broken Current

*by R. M. Sterling*

> August Yarrow, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the concession permits expire.
