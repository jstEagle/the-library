# The Broken Monsoon

*by Juniper Amberdale*

> Rosalind Hartwell, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, has spent twelve years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Juniper Amberdale is a former maritime archaeologist who spent six seasons on salvage crews in the Coral Sea before turning to fiction full-time. She writes adventure novels from a houseboat office, drawing on real dives and the quiet moral compromises of people who work beyond the reach of law.
