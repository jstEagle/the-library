# The Broken Summit

*by Tamsin Okafor*

> Casper Okafor, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the concession permits expire.
