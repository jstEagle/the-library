# The Compass of Patagonia's dry side

*by A. M. Thackeray*

> Wendell Whitlock, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent fifteen years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must cross the range ahead of the snows before the dry season ends.

## About the author

A. M. Thackeray is the pen name of a former field cartographer who spent six seasons mapping remote trails across southern South America before turning to fiction. Drawing on years of expedition journals and a lifelong fascination with forgotten explorers, Thackeray writes adventure stories where landscapes act as both antagonist and confessor.
