# The Compass of the Sepik Basin

*by Sable Vasquez*

> Rafael Brightwater, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.
