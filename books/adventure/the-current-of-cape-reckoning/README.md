# The Current of Cape Reckoning

*by Wren Winterbourne*

> Ulyana Ashgrove, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, has spent twelve years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides, and time is already running out.
