## About the author

Kestrel Lockridge is the pen name of a former marine surveyor who spent six years charting waterways across Papua New Guinea and Southeast Asia before turning to fiction. Their novels blend hard-edged adventure with an anthropologist's patience for the places where rivers remember more than people do.
