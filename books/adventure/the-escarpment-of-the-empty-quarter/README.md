# The Escarpment of the Empty Quarter

*by Ivo Fairweather*

> August Ashgrove, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must cross the range ahead of the snows, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ivo Fairweather is the pen name of a former surveyor who spent a decade mapping river basins before turning to fiction, and whose novels are known for weather treated as a character with its own grudges. Fairweather lives at altitude, keeps no fixed address, and claims this is a lifestyle choice rather than an evasion.
