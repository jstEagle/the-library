## About the author

Freya Kavanagh spent a decade working supply runs on Papua New Guinea's river systems before turning to fiction full-time. Her novels draw on oral histories gathered in river towns, and she now writes from a stilt house in Queensland with her partner and an ill-tempered kelpie.
