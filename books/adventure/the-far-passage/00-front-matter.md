## About the author

Oscar Voss is the pen name of a former logistics coordinator for Himalayan survey expeditions who spent a decade moving supplies over passes most people only photograph from below. He writes adventure fiction in a converted shipping container in Kathmandu, where the monsoon gives him four months a year to type.
