# The Far Passage

*by Oscar Voss*

> Mara Okafor, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, has spent twenty years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a dying prospector trades a map for a promise — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must cross the range ahead of the snows before the monsoon makes landfall.

## About the author

Oscar Voss is the pen name of a former logistics coordinator for Himalayan survey expeditions who spent a decade moving supplies over passes most people only photograph from below. He writes adventure fiction in a converted shipping container in Kathmandu, where the monsoon gives him four months a year to type.
