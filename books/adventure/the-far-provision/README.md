# The Far Provision

*by Xanthe Nakamura*

> Theo Blackwood, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, has spent twenty years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
