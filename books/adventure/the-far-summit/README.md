# The Far Summit

*by O. M. Yarrow*

> Ansel Thornbury, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the monsoon makes landfall.
