# The Far Trail

*by Paloma Voss*

> Wendell Montrose, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the dry season ends.
