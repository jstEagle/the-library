# The High Compass

*by Wendell Ravensworth*

> Ulyana Thornbury, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the dry season ends.

## About the author

Wendell Ravensworth is the pen name of a former railway surveyor who spent eleven years mapping decommissioned lines across three continents before turning to fiction. They write in station waiting rooms and claim never to have taken the same train twice.
