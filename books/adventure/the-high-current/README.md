# The High Current

*by I. M. Okafor*

> Quentin Coldharbour, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, has spent seven years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides, and time is already running out.

## About the author

I. M. Okafor is the pen name of a former floatplane mechanic turned storyteller who grew up between coastal airstrips and her grandmother's archive of unsent letters. They write adventure fiction about people who measure their lives in distances until something forces them to count in years.
