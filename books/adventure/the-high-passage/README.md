# The High Passage

*by Marguerite Montrose*

> Oscar Fairweather, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, has spent three years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a dying prospector trades a map for a promise — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the dry season ends.
