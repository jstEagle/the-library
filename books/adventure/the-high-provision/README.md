# The High Provision

*by Hazel Thackeray*

> Wendell Lockridge, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, has spent three years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
