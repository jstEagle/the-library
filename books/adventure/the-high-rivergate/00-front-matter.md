## About the author

Mara Nakamura is the pen name of a former maritime archaeologist who spent five years cataloguing wrecks off monsoon-battered coastlines before turning to fiction full-time. She writes adventure novels about stubborn people, salt water, and the families we build when our first ones fail us.
