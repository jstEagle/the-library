# The High Trail

*by Beatrix Nakamura*

> Hazel Thornbury, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the monsoon makes landfall.
