# The Horizon of Patagonia's dry side

*by Wendell Okonkwo*

> Orsen Nystrom, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, has spent twelve years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a dying prospector trades a map for a promise — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.
