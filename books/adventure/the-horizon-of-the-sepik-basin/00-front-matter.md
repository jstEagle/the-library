## About the author

Petra Amberdale is the pen name of a former aid-flight logistics coordinator who spent six years organizing supply runs across remote Melanesia before turning to fiction. She writes adventure novels about people whose loyalties are tested by geography, and she still refuses to fly in anything with fewer than two engines.
