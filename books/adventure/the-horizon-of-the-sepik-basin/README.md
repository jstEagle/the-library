# The Horizon of the Sepik Basin

*by Petra Amberdale*

> Jude Almeida, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when a dying prospector trades a map for a promise, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must cross the range ahead of the snows before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

## About the author

Petra Amberdale is the pen name of a former aid-flight logistics coordinator who spent six years organizing supply runs across remote Melanesia before turning to fiction. She writes adventure novels about people whose loyalties are tested by geography, and she still refuses to fly in anything with fewer than two engines.
