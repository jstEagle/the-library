# The Landfall of Patagonia's dry side

*by Rosalind Almeida*

> Paloma Dunmore, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.

## About the author

She writes from a farmhouse outside Puerto Madryn, where she keeps unreliable weather instruments and even less reliable dogs.
