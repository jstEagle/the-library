# The Landfall of the Empty Quarter

**by B. M. Nystrom**

> August Nakamura, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

## About the author

B. M. Nystrom is the pen name of a former field cartographer who spent a decade mapping seasonal ice roads across the northern territories before turning to fiction. They write from a cabin that moves every few years, and refuse to confirm whether that is preference or habit.
