# The Last Escarpment

*by Mirabel Okonkwo*

> Lena Delacroix, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, has spent twenty years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must cross the range ahead of the snows, and time is already running out.
