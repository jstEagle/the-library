# The Last Landfall

*by Rafael Kavanagh*

> Wendell Underhill, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the dry season ends.
