# The Last Passage

*by Sebastian Crane*

> Kestrel Villalobos, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent five years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must cross the range ahead of the snows before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
