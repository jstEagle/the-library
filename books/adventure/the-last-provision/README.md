# The Last Provision

*by Paloma Marchetti*

> Marguerite Okafor, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, has spent three years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.
