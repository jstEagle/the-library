# The Leeward Compass

*by Z. M. Kavanagh*

> Mirabel Nystrom, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

## About the author

Z. M. Kavanagh spent seven years as a deckhand on inland freight routes before turning to writing, and still keeps a waterlogged chart of a river that no longer exists. Kavanagh writes adventure fiction about people whose livelihoods depend on reading landscapes that governments would prefer to erase.
