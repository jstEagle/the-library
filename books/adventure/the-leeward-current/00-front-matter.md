## About the author

Emrys Sandoval is a former expedition cook who spent six seasons in high-altitude base camps before turning to fiction, claiming the best stories were always told over rehydrated dinners. The Leeward Current is a fourth novel, written largely in notebooks that survived two river crossings and one avalanche.
