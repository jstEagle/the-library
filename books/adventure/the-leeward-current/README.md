# The Leeward Current

*by Emrys Sandoval*

Then an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

## About the author

Emrys Sandoval is a former expedition cook who spent six seasons in high-altitude base camps before turning to fiction, claiming the best stories were always told over rehydrated dinners. The Leeward Current is a fourth novel, written largely in notebooks that survived two river crossings and one avalanche.
