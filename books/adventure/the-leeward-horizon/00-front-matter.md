## About the author

Wendell Halloran spent a decade piloting cargo barges on inland waterways before turning to fiction, and his prose carries the salt-and-mud cadence of the river towns he called home. He writes adventure stories about people the maps forget, insisting that the wildest country is always just past the last dock light.
