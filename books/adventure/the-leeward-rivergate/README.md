# The Leeward Rivergate

*by Coral Vasquez*

> Sebastian Montrose, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
