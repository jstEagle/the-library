# The Leeward Trail

*by Rosalind Brightwater*

> Zephyrine Reyes, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, has spent three years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a dying prospector trades a map for a promise, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the dry season ends.

## About the author

Rosalind Brightwater spent a decade crewing research vessels out of Bridgetown before turning to fiction, and her prose carries the salt-rot smell of real deck work. She writes from a stilt house on Carriacou, where she keeps her late father's diving logs and an unhealthy collection of tide tables.
