# The Lost Monsoon

*by Ansel Ishikawa*

> August Nystrom, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ansel Ishikawa is a former maritime archaeologist turned novelist who spent a decade cataloguing wrecks across Southeast Asian rivers before trading the dive slate for the writing desk. Their fiction blends technical diving detail with stories of people rebuilding themselves from salvage.
