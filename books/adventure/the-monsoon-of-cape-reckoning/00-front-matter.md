## About the author

Coral Sandoval spent a decade flying supply runs along remote coastlines before turning to fiction, drawing on the pilots, salvagers, and stubborn townsfolk she met there. She writes adventure stories about people whose loyalties are tested by landscapes that keep no secrets and forgive no mistakes.
