# The Monsoon of Patagonia's dry side

*by Kestrel Oyelaran*

> Dominic Ishikawa, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the dry season ends.
