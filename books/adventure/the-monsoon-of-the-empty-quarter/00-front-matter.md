## About the author

Emrys Lockridge is the pen name of a former field surveyor who spent six years mapping coastlines from Oman to the Hebrides before turning to fiction. Known for adventure novels steeped in real terrain and quieter emotional weather, Lockridge writes from a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage.
