# The Monsoon of the Sepik Basin

*by Silas Thornbury*

> Casper Iversen, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
