# The Open Monsoon

*by Wren Coldharbour*

> Wendell Eriksen, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent three years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must cross the range ahead of the snows before the dry season ends.
