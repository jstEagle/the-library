# The Open Rivergate

*by Tamsin Sterling*

> Lior Almeida, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source, and time is already running out.
