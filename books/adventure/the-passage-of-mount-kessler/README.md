# The Passage of Mount Kessler

*by Wren Vasquez*

> Felix Joubert, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
