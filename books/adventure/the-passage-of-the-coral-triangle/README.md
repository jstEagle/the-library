# The Passage of the Coral Triangle

*by Bram Halloran*

> Lena Lockridge, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, has spent twelve years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must cross the range ahead of the snows before the dry season ends.
