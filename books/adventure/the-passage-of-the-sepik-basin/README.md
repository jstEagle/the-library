# The Passage of the Sepik Basin

*by Astrid Vane*

> Wren Blackwood, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must cross the range ahead of the snows before the monsoon makes landfall.
