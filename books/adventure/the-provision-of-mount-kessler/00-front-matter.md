## About the author

Mirabel Coldharbour is the pen name of a former floatplane mechanic who spent a decade in remote northern camps before turning to writing full-time. She is known for adventure novels that pair white-knuckle wilderness sequences with quietly fierce meditations on the families people build when the world has written them off.
