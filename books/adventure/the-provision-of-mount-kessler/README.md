# The Provision of Mount Kessler

*by Mirabel Coldharbour*

> Zephyrine Marchetti, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, has spent seven years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a dying prospector trades a map for a promise, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

Mirabel Coldharbour is the pen name of a former floatplane mechanic who spent a decade in remote northern camps before turning to writing full-time. She is known for adventure novels that pair white-knuckle wilderness sequences with quietly fierce meditations on the families people build when the world has written them off.
