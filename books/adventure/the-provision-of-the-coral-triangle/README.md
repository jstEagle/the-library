# The Provision of the Coral Triangle

*by Mara Thornbury*

> Lena Brightwater, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the monsoon makes landfall.
