## About the author

Paloma Blackwood is the pen name of a former bush pilot turned novelist who spent six years flying freight across northern ranges before trading the cockpit for a writing desk. Her fiction draws on real flight logs, vanished expeditions, and the quiet ethics of people who live where roads don't go.
