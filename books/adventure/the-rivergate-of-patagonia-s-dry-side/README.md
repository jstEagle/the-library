# The Rivergate of Patagonia's dry side

*by August Galloway*

> Noor Thornbury, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, keeps moving because stopping costs more. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the concession permits expire.
