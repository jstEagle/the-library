# The Rivergate of the Empty Quarter

*by J. M. Pemberton*

> Wren Quintana, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, keeps moving because stopping costs more. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
