# The Rough Current

*by J. M. Yarrow*

> Ada Okonkwo, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the dry season ends.

## About the author

J. M. Yarrow is the pen name of a former field surveyor who spent six years mapping river routes across West Africa before turning to fiction. Their novels blend engineering precision with a deep reverence for wild places and the people who call them home.
