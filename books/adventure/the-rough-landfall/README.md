# The Rough Landfall

*by Odile Ravensworth*

> Cyrus Nakamura, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, has spent five years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the monsoon makes landfall.
