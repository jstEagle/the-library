# The Rough Wreck

*by Zephyrine Thackeray*

> Tamsin Montrose, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, keeps moving because stopping costs more. Then a dying prospector trades a map for a promise — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the monsoon makes landfall.

## About the author

Zephyrine Thackeray is the pen name of a former merchant marine radio operator who spent eleven years on salvage crews across the Coral and Andaman seas before trading the deck for a desk. They write adventure novels from a houseboat that moves, on principle, twice a year.
