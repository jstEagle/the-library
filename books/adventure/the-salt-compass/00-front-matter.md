# The Salt Compass

**by C. M. Oyelaran**

> Tamsin Okafor, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

C. M. Oyelaran is a former marine cartographer who spent five years charting wrecks along the Gulf of Guinea before turning to fiction. Their work draws on oral histories gathered in coastal fishing towns, blending archival research with salt-stained adventure.
