# The Salt Current

*by Isandro Villalobos*

> Ada Ashgrove, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the concession permits expire.

## About the author

Isandro Villalobos is the pen name of a former hydrographic surveyor who spent six seasons mapping ephemeral rivers before turning to fiction. He writes adventure novels about landscapes that punish anyone who mistakes emptiness for safety.
