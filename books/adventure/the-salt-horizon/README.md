# The Salt Horizon

*by D. M. Vane*

> Casper Quintana, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, has spent five years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source, and time is already running out.
