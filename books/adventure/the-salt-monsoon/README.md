# The Salt Monsoon

*by Felix Brightwater*

> Rosalind Yarrow, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Felix Brightwater is the pen name of a former hydrographic surveyor who spent a decade mapping coastlines before turning to fiction. He writes adventure novels about landscapes that keep secrets, drawing on his obsession with tides, deserts, and the people who vanish into both.
