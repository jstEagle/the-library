# The Salt Passage

*by Noor Okafor*

> Theo Oyelaran, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, has spent twenty years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then an expedition upriver stops answering the radio — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the monsoon makes landfall.
