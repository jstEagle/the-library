# The Salt Provision

**by Xanthe Nakamura**

> Ulyana Okonkwo, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, has spent twelve years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Xanthe Nakamura is a former hydrologist turned novelist who has canoed four of the world's great river systems and writes about people who run from home until home runs after them. She lives in a converted boathouse with two dogs and an unhealthy respect for weather.
