# The Salt Rivergate

*by Rafael Lockridge*

> Wendell Ishikawa, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must cross the range ahead of the snows before the monsoon makes landfall.

He writes adventure fiction from a cabin near the confluence of two unnamed rivers, where he insists the mail plane still comes every Thursday.
