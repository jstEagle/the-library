# The Summit of Devil's Throat

*by Freya Whitlock*

> Elodie Ashgrove, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the dry season ends.
