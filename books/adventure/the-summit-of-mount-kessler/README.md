# The Summit of Mount Kessler

*by Oscar Thornbury*

> Noor Oyelaran, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then an expedition upriver stops answering the radio — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

## About the author

Oscar Thornbury is the pen name of a former river guide who spent six seasons on commercial expeditions before turning to writing full-time. His fiction is known for its claustrophobic landscapes and its obsession with debts, both financial and moral.
