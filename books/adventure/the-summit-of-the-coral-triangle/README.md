# The Summit of the Coral Triangle

*by Tamsin Halloran*

> Coral Crane, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, keeps moving because stopping costs more. Then the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the monsoon makes landfall.

## About the author

Tamsin Halloran is a former charter pilot turned novelist who spent five years living aboard a ketch in Southeast Asian waters. Her fiction draws on real tides, real storms, and the people who live one weather system from ruin.
