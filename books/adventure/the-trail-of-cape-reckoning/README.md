# The Trail of Cape Reckoning

*by H. M. Voss*

> Xanthe Okafor, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source, and time is already running out.
