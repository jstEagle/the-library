Now she must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Juniper Blackwood is the pen name of a former maritime archaeologist who spent a decade documenting river wrecks across Southeast Asia and Melanesia before turning to fiction. She writes adventure novels about people who find family in the unlikeliest places, drawing on real expeditions she can never fully talk about.
