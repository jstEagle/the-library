# The Uncharted Horizon

*by Rosalind Winterbourne*

> Petra Sterling, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a dying prospector trades a map for a promise, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the concession permits expire.
