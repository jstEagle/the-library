# The Uncharted Landfall

*by Elodie Brightwater*

> Sable Winterbourne, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Elodie Brightwater is the pen name of a former northern bush pilot who logged over four thousand hours above the tree line before turning to fiction. She writes from a cabin outside Whitehorse, where the nearest neighbor is a floatplane base and the stories are all true, more or less.
