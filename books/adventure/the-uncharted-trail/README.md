# The Uncharted Trail

*by Sable Ishikawa*

> Silas Vasquez, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, has spent twelve years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then an expedition upriver stops answering the radio — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.
