# The Wreck of the Coral Triangle

*by Edmund Vane*

> Ines Dravenmoor, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Edmund Vane is the pen name of a former river surveyor who spent a decade charting tributaries in contested borderlands before turning to fiction. He writes adventure novels about people the maps forgot, from a houseboat he refuses to anchor in any one country for longer than a season.
