# The Wreck of the Empty Quarter

*by Mirabel Pemberton*

> Vesper Ravensworth, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
