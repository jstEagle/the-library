# The Wreck of the Upper Fly River

**by Marguerite Nystrom**

> Wren Okonkwo, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, has spent three years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it.
