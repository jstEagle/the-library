## About the author

Ines Okonkwo grew up between her grandmother's ferry landing on the Niger and a boarding school in Lagos, where she learned that rivers keep better records than courts. She now writes adventure novels from a houseboat, insisting that every plot must survive being read aloud over engine noise.
