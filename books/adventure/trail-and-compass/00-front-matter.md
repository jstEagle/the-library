## About the author

Yara Iversen is a former expedition logistics coordinator who spent six seasons in the Himalaya before turning to fiction full-time. She writes from a farmhouse in western Norway, where she keeps her grandfather's brass compass on the desk as a reminder that every direction is a choice.
