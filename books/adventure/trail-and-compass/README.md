# Trail and Compass

*by Yara Iversen*

> Gideon Zhou, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent three years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
