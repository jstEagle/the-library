# Trail and Landfall

*by Emrys Kavanagh*

> Beatrix Zhou, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.
