# Trail and Summit

*by Coral Halloran*

> Edmund Vane, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent twelve years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must cross the range ahead of the snows before the dry season ends.

## About the author

Coral Halloran is a former alpine rescue volunteer who writes adventure fiction from a farmhouse at the foot of the northern ranges, where she keeps meticulous trail journals that feed her novels. Trail and Summit is her fourth book and the first in a loosely connected series about people the wilderness refuses to let go.
