## About the author

Gideon Eastwick is the pen name of a former surveyor who spent a decade mapping abandoned rail corridors across three continents before turning to fiction. Their novels are known for granular technical detail, river-soaked atmospherics, and crews of misfits who argue their way into becoming families.
