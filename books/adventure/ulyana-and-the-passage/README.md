# Ulyana and the Passage

*by Kestrel Quintana*

> Xanthe Okafor, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must cross the range ahead of the snows, and time is already running out.
