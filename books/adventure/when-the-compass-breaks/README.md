# When the Compass Breaks

*by Paloma Ravensworth*

> Felix Eriksen, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Paloma Ravensworth is a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipwreck salvage claims before turning to fiction. She writes adventure novels from a converted trawler moored off the Cornish coast, where she insists the weather improves her prose.
