# When the Current Burns

*by Sable Dunmore*

> Orsen Nystrom, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent twelve years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source, and time is already running out.
