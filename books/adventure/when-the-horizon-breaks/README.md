# When the Horizon Breaks

*by Vesper Halloran*

> Ines Nakamura, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. Then the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the concession permits expire.
