# When the Horizon Sings

*by Hazel Ishikawa*

> Paloma Fairweather, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the monsoon makes landfall.

## About the author

Hazel Ishikawa spent six years working seasonal river transport in Southeast Asia before turning to fiction, a period she credits for her obsession with water, weather, and the lies people tell to survive both. She writes from a houseboat she refuses to name publicly.
