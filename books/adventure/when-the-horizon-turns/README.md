# When the Horizon Turns

*by Mirabel Galloway*

> Freya Amberdale, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. Then the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the monsoon makes landfall.
