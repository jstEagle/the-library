# When the Horizon Wakes

*by A. M. Iversen*

> Edmund Dravenmoor, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.

## About the author

A. M. Iversen is the pen name of a former river-survey cartographer who spent six seasons mapping floodplains across three continents before turning to fiction. Their novels blend field-accurate wilderness survival with slow-burning moral thrillers about what families leave buried.
