# When the Landfall Burns

**by Ines Fairweather**

> Sable Amberdale, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, has spent fifteen years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the dry season ends.
