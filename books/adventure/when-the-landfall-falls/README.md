# When the Landfall Falls

*by Jude Eriksen*

> Quentin Kavanagh, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the monsoon makes landfall.
