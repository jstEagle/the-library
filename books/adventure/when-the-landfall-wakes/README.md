*by Rosalind Reyes*

> Lena Marchetti, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found before the concession permits expire.
