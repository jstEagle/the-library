# When the Monsoon Sings

*by Jonah Brightwater*

> Isandro Coldharbour, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source, and time is already running out.
