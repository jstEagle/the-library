*by B. M. Galloway*

> Hazel Amberdale, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent seven years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.
