# When the Passage Breaks

**by Edmund Okafor**

> Casper Sterling, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Edmund Okafor is a former merchant marine who spent a decade working coastal salvage before turning to fiction full-time. He writes adventure novels from a houseboat, drawing on real wrecks, real tides, and the people who make their living beneath them.
