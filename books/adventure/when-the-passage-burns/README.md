# When the Passage Burns

*by Petra Sandoval*

> Odile Ishikawa, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the concession permits expire.
