# When the Provision Burns

*by Petra Delacroix*

> Coral Underhill, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, has spent twenty years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source, and time is already running out.
