# When the Provision Falls

*by Casper Thornbury*

> Rosalind Eriksen, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, keeps moving because stopping costs more. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the dry season ends.
