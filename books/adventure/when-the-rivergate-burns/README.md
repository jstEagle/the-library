# When the Rivergate Burns

*by Paloma Sandoval*

> Orsen Montrose, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent five years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides, and time is already running out.
