# When the Rivergate Sings

*by Noor Eastwick*

> Mara Lockridge, a desert ranger tracking a thief across nothing, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the monsoon makes landfall.
