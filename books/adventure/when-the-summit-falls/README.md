# When the Summit Falls

*by Rosalind Joubert*

> Rafael Sterling, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, has spent three years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must cross the range ahead of the snows before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

## About the author

Rosalind Joubert is a former maritime archaeologist who spent a decade cataloguing wrecks along contested coastlines before turning to fiction full-time. She writes adventure novels about people whom history tried to erase, drawing on fieldwork in three continents and a lifelong obsession with mountains she is legally advised not to discuss.
