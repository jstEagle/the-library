# When the Summit Turns

*by Lena Dunmore*

> Oscar Delacroix, a storm chaser who owes money to dangerous optimists, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the concession permits expire.
