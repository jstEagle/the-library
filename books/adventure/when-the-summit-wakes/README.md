# When the Summit Wakes

*by Dominic Sterling*

> Hazel Oyelaran, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides, and time is already running out.
