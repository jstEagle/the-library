# When the Trail Falls

*by Zephyrine Ashgrove*

> Elodie Delacroix, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. Then an expedition upriver stops answering the radio — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.
