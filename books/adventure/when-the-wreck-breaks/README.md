To get through this, she must cross the range ahead of the snows before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

## About the author

Marguerite Nakamura grew up in a family of tugboat pilots on the Pacific coast and spent a decade working seasonal river freight before turning to fiction. Her novels are known for their weather-wise prose and crews of misfits who argue their way into becoming families.
