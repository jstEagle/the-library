## About the author

Casper Brightwater spent a decade working seasonal jobs on inland waterways before turning to writing, and their fiction is steeped in the rhythms of river towns and the people who never quite settle there. They live on a restored sternwheeler with a dog named Barge and write only when the fog lifts.
