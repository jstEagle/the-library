# When the Wreck Sings

*by Casper Brightwater*

> Casper Joubert, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, has spent twenty years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must bring the expedition home without telling anyone what it found, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Casper Brightwater spent a decade working seasonal jobs on inland waterways before turning to writing, and their fiction is steeped in the rhythms of river towns and the people who never quite settle there. They live on a restored sternwheeler with a dog named Barge and write only when the fog lifts.
