# Wreck and Landfall

**by Rafael Yarrow**

> Casper Pemberton, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
