# Wreck and Landfall

*by Rafael Yarrow*

> Casper Pemberton, a salvage diver working a wreck the navy won't discuss, has spent ten years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when the river gives up a chest stamped with a dead nation's seal, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must follow a river upstream to its forbidden source before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
