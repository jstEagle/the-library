## About the author

M. M. Blackwood is the pen name of a former floatplane mechanic who spent a decade flying supplies into coastal Alaskan villages before turning to writing full-time. Their fiction draws on real salvage lore and the unforgiving arithmetic of tide tables, weather windows, and trust.
