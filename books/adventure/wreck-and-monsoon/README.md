# Wreck and Monsoon

*by M. M. Blackwood*

> Isandro Yarrow, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. Then the tide reveals a cave that legends charge admission to — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
