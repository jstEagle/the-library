# Wreck and Passage

*by Elodie Ashgrove*

> Hazel Joubert, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, knows the wilderness forgives nothing and forgets less. But when a dying prospector trades a map for a promise, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the concession permits expire.

## About the author

Elodie Ashgrove is the pen name of a former floatplane dispatcher who spent seven years in coastal aviation towns listening to pilots trade stories over bad coffee. She writes adventure fiction about people whose survival depends on machines, weather, and the lies they tell themselves.
