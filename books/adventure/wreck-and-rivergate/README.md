## About the author

Ansel Blackwood spent a decade as a deckhand and commercial diver in Southeast Asian ports before turning to fiction, writing at anchor in whatever harbor would have him. His novels draw on the salt-bitten rhythms of salvage crews and the quiet moral arithmetic of people who live outside paperwork.
