# Wreck and Trail

*by Paloma Amberdale*

> Cyrus Brightwater, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, keeps moving because stopping costs more. But when a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must cross the range ahead of the snows before the concession permits expire.
