To get through this, they must cross the range ahead of the snows before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

## About the author

Ines Winterbourne is the pen name of a former land surveyor who spent six seasons mapping high alpine passes before turning to fiction. She writes adventure novels about people small against enormous landscapes, from a cabin she refuses to disclose the location of.
