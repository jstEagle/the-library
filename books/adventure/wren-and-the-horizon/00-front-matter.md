## About the author

Coral Thornbury is the pen name of a former surveyor's assistant who spent four seasons mapping high-altitude rail corridors before turning to fiction. They write adventure novels about landscapes that keep secrets, drawing on a family archive of railroad ledgers and expedition letters.
