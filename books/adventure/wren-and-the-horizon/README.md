# Wren and the Horizon

*by Coral Thornbury*

> August Whitlock, a railroad engineer with a map of closed lines, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. Then a government survey team vanishes, leaving gear and no tracks — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

## About the author

Coral Thornbury is the pen name of a former surveyor's assistant who spent four seasons mapping high-altitude rail corridors before turning to fiction. They write adventure novels about landscapes that keep secrets, drawing on a family archive of railroad ledgers and expedition letters.
