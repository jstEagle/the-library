*by H. M. Oyelaran*

> Yara Hartwell, a riverboat captain who knows every sandbar's mood, has spent three years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help, and time is already running out.

## About the author

H. M. Oyelaran is a former deckhand on cargo barges who spent six seasons navigating West African river systems before turning to writing full-time. Their fiction blends hard-won nautical detail with quiet meditations on home, debt, and the families we choose.
