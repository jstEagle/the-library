# Xanthe and the Horizon

*by Ines Eastwick*

> Jude Quintana, an archaeologist's estranged child and better digger, carries a compass, a debt, and very little else. But when a dying prospector trades a map for a promise, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.
