# Xanthe and the Trail

**by Mirabel Eastwick**

> Kestrel Hartwell, a bush pilot flying medicine and rumors into the interior, has spent fifteen years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when an expedition upriver stops answering the radio, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must outlast the monsoon with a leaking boat and a stranger's help before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

## About the author

Mirabel Eastwick is the pen name of a former bush-country radio operator who spent six seasons in fly-in communities before turning to fiction. She writes adventure novels about people who measure their lives in weather rather than calendars.
