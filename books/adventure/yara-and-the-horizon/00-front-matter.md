# Yara and the Horizon

**by Odile Winterbourne**

## About the author

Odile Winterbourne is the pen name of a former expedition cook who spent a decade in high-altitude base camps listening to guides tell stories they swore never to repeat. She writes adventure fiction about people whose pasts are harder terrain than any mountain.
