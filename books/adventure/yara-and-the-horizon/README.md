# Yara and the Horizon

*by Odile Winterbourne*

> Coral Delacroix, a mountain guide blacklisted from two countries, has spent twenty years measuring life in miles rather than years. But when an old rival hires the crew for the one job that was sworn off long ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must beat the salvage fleet to the wreck by six tides before the glacier calving season opens the route — briefly.

Odile Winterbourne is the pen name of a former expedition cook who spent a decade in high-altitude base camps listening to guides tell stories they swore never to repeat. She writes adventure fiction about people whose pasts are harder terrain than any mountain.
