# A Choir for the Crown Anew

*by Kestrel Ravensworth*

> Astrid Pemberton, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
