## About the author

Ansel Eastwick is a former trail surveyor who spent six years mapping passes through the Carpathians before turning to fiction, a career he credits for his obsession with borders and the people who cross them. He writes in a cabin above the tree line, where he claims the silence is 'good for plotting treason.'
