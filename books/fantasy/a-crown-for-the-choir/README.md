# A Crown for the Choir

*by I. M. Brightwater*

> Lena Sandoval, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
