# A Crown for the Salt Anew

*by Ulyana Marchetti*

> Rafael Vasquez, a thief who steals memories, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
