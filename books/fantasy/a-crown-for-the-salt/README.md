*by Paloma Amberdale*

Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Paloma Amberdale is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent years cataloguing shipwreck salvage before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about people who survive at the edges of empires, drawing on coastal folklore and her collection of half-legible sea charts.
