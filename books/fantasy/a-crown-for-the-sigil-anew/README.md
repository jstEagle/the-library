# A Crown for the Sigil Anew

*by S. M. Villalobos*

> Kestrel Okonkwo, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

S. M. Villalobos is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing trade manifests from ports that no longer appear on modern maps. They write fantasy about paperwork, borders, and the people history forgets on purpose.
