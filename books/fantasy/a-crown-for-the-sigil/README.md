# A Crown for the Sigil

*by Mara Halloran*

> Marguerite Voss, a bone-carver who hears the dead, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Mara Halloran is the pen name of a former archivist who spent seven years cataloguing the ephemera of defunct nations before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about paperwork, grief, and the stubborn machinery of survival, and lives near a coast that appears on older maps than her house does.
