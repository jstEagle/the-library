# A Crown for the Thorn

*by Theo Fairweather*

> Jonah Blackwood, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
