# A Crown for the Wolf

*by Wren Dunmore*

> Beatrix Eriksen, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
