# A Ember for the Choir Revisited

*by O. M. Okafor*

> Elodie Whitlock, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
