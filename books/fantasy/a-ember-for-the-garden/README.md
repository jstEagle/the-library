# A Ember for the Garden

*by Ivo Hartwell*

> Tamsin Joubert, a thief who steals memories, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
