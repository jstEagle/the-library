# A Ember for the Hollow

*by Ivo Coldharbour*

> Jude Vane, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
