# A Ember for the Veil

**by Ulyana Oyelaran**

> Odile Coldharbour, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Ulyana Oyelaran is a former archivist who spent six years cataloguing a private collection of West African and Slavic folk grimoires before turning to fiction. She writes from a farmhouse outside Ibadan, where she keeps bees and insists the prophecies in her novels are 'only mostly invented.'
