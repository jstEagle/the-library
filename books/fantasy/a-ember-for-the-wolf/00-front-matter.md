# A Ember for the Wolf

**by W. M. Oyelaran**

> Odile Winterbourne, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

W. M. Oyelaran is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing folk charms in West African and Celtic oral traditions before turning to fiction. They write quiet, memory-haunted fantasy about people who carry more history than they know.
