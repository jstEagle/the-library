# A Ember for the Wolf

*by W. M. Oyelaran*

> Odile Winterbourne, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
