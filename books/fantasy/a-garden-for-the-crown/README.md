# A Garden for the Crown

*by Theo Voss*

> Silas Ravensworth, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Theo Voss is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing funeral customs across three continents before turning to fiction. They write about grief, inheritance, and the small magics people perform to survive being remembered.
