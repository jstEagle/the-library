## About the author

Freya Yarrow is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the belongings of people with no one left to claim them, an experience that haunts every page of her fiction. She writes fantasy about the paperwork of grief, the geography of belonging, and the small rebellions of quiet people.
