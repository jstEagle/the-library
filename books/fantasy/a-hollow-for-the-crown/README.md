# A Hollow for the Crown

*by Theo Hartwell*

> Orsen Ashgrove, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
