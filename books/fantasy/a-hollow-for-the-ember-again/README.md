# A Hollow for the Ember Again

*by Wren Marchetti*

> Kestrel Vasquez, an exiled storm-caller, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
