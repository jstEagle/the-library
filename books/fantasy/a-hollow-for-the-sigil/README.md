*by Cyrus Zhou*

But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Cyrus Zhou is the pen name of a former cartographer who spent six years mapping river valleys before turning to fiction, a trade that left an enduring obsession with borders and the people who live just inside them. They write quiet, wintry fantasy about inherited obligations and the small rebellions that undo empires.
