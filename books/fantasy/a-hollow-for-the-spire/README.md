# A Hollow for the Spire

*by O. M. Whitlock*

> Marguerite Montrose, a thief who steals memories, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.
