# A Hollow for the Veil

*by Beatrix Vane*

> Sebastian Villalobos, an exiled storm-caller, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
