But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Zephyrine Pemberton is the pen name of a former choir librarian who spent years cataloguing hymnals in coastal free cities before turning to fiction. They write about outsiders who find their voices in unexpected company, and claim all their best ideas arrive during rehearsals they were eavesdropping on.
