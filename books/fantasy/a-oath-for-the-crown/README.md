# A Oath for the Crown

*by Isandro Underhill*

> Emrys Fairweather, a thief who steals memories, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
