# A Oath for the Sigil

*by Quentin Amberdale*

> Sebastian Reyes, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
