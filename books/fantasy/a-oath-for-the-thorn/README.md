# A Oath for the Thorn

*by Juniper Marchetti*

> Zephyrine Sandoval, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Juniper Marchetti is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters between estranged siblings before turning to fiction. She writes about doors, debts, and the families we build when the old ones fail us.
