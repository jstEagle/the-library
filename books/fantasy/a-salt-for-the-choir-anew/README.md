# A Salt for the Choir Anew

*by Vesper Fairweather*

> Emrys Nystrom, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
