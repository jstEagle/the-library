# A Salt for the Ember

*by Odile Sandoval*

> Isandro Brightwater, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.
