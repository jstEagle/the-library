# A Salt for the Hollow Again

**by Bram Montrose**

> August Vane, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
