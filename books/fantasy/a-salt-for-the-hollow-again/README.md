# A Salt for the Hollow Again

*by Bram Montrose*

> August Vane, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
