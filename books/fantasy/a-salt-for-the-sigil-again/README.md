# A Salt for the Sigil Again

*by Jonah Zhou*

> Gideon Villalobos, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
