Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Gideon Marchetti is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters from families separated by borders that no longer exist. They write fantasy about inheritance, salt, and the things people refuse to forget.
