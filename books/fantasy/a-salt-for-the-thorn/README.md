# A Salt for the Thorn

*by D. M. Whitlock*

> Zephyrine Eriksen, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

D. M. Whitlock is the pen name of a former surveyor who spent a decade mapping coastlines that kept shifting under their instruments. They write fantasy about places that refuse to be pinned down, and live somewhere they decline to specify on any map.
