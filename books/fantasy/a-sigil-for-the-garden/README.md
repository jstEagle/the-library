*by Zephyrine Thornbury*

> Lena Villalobos, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Zephyrine Thornbury is the pen name of a former trail guide who spent six seasons leading treks through mountain passes before turning to fiction full-time. She writes fantasy rooted in the geography of borders, smuggling routes, and the small bargains people make to cross them.
