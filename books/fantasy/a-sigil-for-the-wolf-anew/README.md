*by Sable Fairweather*

> Wendell Joubert, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Sable Fairweather is the pen name of a former cartographer who spent a decade drawing borders before deciding to write about the people who cross them. They live at the edge of a forest that may or may not be enchanted, and insist the wolves started it.
