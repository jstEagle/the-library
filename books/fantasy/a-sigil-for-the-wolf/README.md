# A Sigil for the Wolf

*by Silas Sandoval*

> Mara Almeida, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Silas Sandoval is a former archivist who spent six years cataloguing confiscated grimoires before turning to fiction, a job he describes as 'reading other people's endings so I could write my own.' He lives in a coastal city much like Vharen, where he repairs antique clocks and insists none of them are enchanted.
