# A Spire for the Crown

*by Sable Whitlock*

> Lena Brightwater, a cartographer of unmapped realms, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
