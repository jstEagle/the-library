## About the author

Felix Oyelaran is a former archivist who spent six years cataloguing trade ledgers from defunct merchant guilds, an obsession that bleeds into every page of his fiction. He writes fantasy about people caught between institutions and their consciences, and lives quietly where the winters are long enough to be characters.
