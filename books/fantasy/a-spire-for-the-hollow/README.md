# A Spire for the Hollow

*by Jonah Galloway*

> Freya Nakamura, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
