# A Spire for the Thorn

*by Beatrix Halloran*

> Cyrus Voss, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Beatrix Halloran is a former cartographer who spent a decade mapping mountain passes before turning to fiction, and her love of half-remembered roads bleeds into every page she writes. She lives in a stone cottage at the edge of nowhere with two ill-tempered goats and an ever-growing collection of impossible maps.
