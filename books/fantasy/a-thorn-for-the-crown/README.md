# A Thorn for the Crown

*by Marguerite Underhill*

> Ulyana Okonkwo, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Marguerite Underhill is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing merchant ledgers from abandoned mountain towns before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about people who do the math on miracles and refuse to accept the sum.
