# A Thorn for the Garden Again

*by T. M. Ravensworth*

> Edmund Sterling, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
