# A Thorn for the Garden Again

*by Theo Eriksen*

> Vesper Blackwood, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
