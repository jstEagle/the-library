# A Thorn for the Garden

*by Cyrus Lockridge*

> Hazel Reyes, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
