# A Thorn for the Hollow

*by Emrys Eriksen*

> Jude Okafor, a bone-carver who hears the dead, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
