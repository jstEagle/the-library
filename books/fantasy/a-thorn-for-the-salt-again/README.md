# A Thorn for the Salt Again

*by Wendell Winterbourne*

> Edmund Underhill, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.

Wendell Winterbourne is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging debt ledgers from defunct trade guilds before turning to fiction. They write about small magics, large bureaucracies, and the people caught between the two.
