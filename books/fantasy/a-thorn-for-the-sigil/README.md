# A Thorn for the Sigil

*by Lior Fairweather*

> Freya Reyes, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
