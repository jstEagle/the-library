# A Thorn for the Wolf Again

*by Coral Iversen*

> Bram Almeida, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Coral Iversen is a former archivist who claims she left the profession after cataloguing a box of letters addressed to people who had never been born. She writes fantasy about paperwork, poison, and the quiet heroism of people who keep records.
