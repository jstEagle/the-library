# A Veil for the Choir Anew

*by I. M. Fairweather*

> Noor Kavanagh, the youngest member of a dying guild, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
