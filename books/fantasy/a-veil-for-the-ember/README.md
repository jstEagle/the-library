# A Veil for the Ember

*by Wendell Dunmore*

> Ines Oyelaran, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.

Wendell Dunmore is the pen name of a former cartographer who spent a decade drawing borders for governments before turning to stories about the people borders fail. They live in a drafty house full of obsolete maps and write about doors, debts, and the geography of forgetting.
