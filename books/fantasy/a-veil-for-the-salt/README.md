# A Veil for the Salt

*by Lena Halloran*

> Noor Oyelaran, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Lena Halloran is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing trade ledgers from coastal port cities before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about bureaucracy, bargains, and the quiet heroism of people who keep records.
