# A Veil for the Thorn Again

*by Hazel Pemberton*

> Hazel Dunmore, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
