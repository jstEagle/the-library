# A Veil for the Thorn

*by Cyrus Almeida*

> Vesper Winterbourne, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Cyrus Almeida is the pen name of a former trail guide who spent six winters leading expeditions through remote mountain passes before turning to fiction. Their work blends folklore, animal behavior, and a deep distrust of institutions into fantasy that asks who the law actually serves.
