# A Wolf for the Choir

*by Zephyrine Ishikawa*

> Casper Villalobos, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
