# A Wolf for the Ember

*by Ulyana Fairweather*

> Wendell Okafor, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ulyana Fairweather is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing funerary carvings in Eastern European village churches before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about grief, memory, and the small magics people practice to survive.
