# A Wolf for the Sigil

*by Lena Whitlock*

> Sable Villalobos, a debt-bound temple scribe, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Lena Whitlock is a former archivist who spent six years cataloguing medieval grimoires before turning to fiction full-time. She writes fantasy about ordinary people caught in machinery far older than themselves, from a farmhouse office guarded by an opinionated dog.
