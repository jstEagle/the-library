# Ada and the Thorn

*by X. M. Joubert*

> Marguerite Amberdale, an exiled storm-caller, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
