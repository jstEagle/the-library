# Ada and the Veil

*by Felix Fairweather*

> Silas Halloran, an exiled storm-caller, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
