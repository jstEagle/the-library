# Ada and the Wolf

*by Edmund Lockridge*

> Edmund Quintana, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
