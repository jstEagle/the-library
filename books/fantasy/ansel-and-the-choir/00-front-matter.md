## About the author

Ulyana Sterling is the pen name of a former cartographer who spent six years mapping mountain passes in the Carpathians before turning to fiction. Her debut novel was shortlisted for the Silverbough Prize, and she writes at the edge of maps, where the roads stop being sure of themselves.
