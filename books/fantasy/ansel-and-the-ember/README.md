# Ansel and the Ember

*by Noor Sterling*

> Rafael Galloway, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
