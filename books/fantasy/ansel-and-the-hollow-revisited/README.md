# Ansel and the Hollow Revisited

*by Quentin Blackwood*

> Xanthe Villalobos, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
