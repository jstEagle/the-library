# Ansel and the Salt

*by Beatrix Hartwell*

> Rosalind Eastwick, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Beatrix Hartwell is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing forgotten trade ledgers from coastal cities, an obsession that seeps into every page of her fiction. She writes fantasy about people who survive empires by being overlooked, and lives near a cold gray sea she refuses to swim in.
