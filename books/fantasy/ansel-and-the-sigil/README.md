# Ansel and the Sigil

*by Wren Halloran*

> Rafael Ishikawa, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
