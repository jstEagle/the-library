# Ansel and the Spire

*by Kestrel Winterbourne*

> Rosalind Lockridge, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
