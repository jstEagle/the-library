# Ansel and the Thorn

*by Mara Dunmore*

> Quentin Blackwood, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Mara Dunmore is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing medieval herbals and poison ledgers before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about ordinary people caught in machinery far older than themselves.
