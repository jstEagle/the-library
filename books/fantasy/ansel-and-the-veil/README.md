# Ansel and the Veil

*by J. M. Vane*

> Lena Zhou, a thief who steals memories, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
