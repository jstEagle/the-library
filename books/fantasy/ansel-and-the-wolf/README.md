# Ansel and the Wolf

*by Gideon Yarrow*

But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Gideon Yarrow is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters never meant to be read, a habit that seeps into every story of memory and obligation. They live at the edge of a forest they refuse to name, writing fantasy that treats loyalty as both a wound and a gift.
