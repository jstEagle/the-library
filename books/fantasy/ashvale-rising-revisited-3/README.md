# Ashvale Rising Revisited

*by Juniper Whitlock*

> Isandro Iversen, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Juniper Whitlock is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing funeral customs across northern coastal towns before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about grief, inheritance, and the stubborn persistence of forgotten things.
