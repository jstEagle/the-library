# Ashvale Rising

*by Freya Hartwell*

> Jonah Eastwick, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Freya Hartwell is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing maps of places that no longer appear on any chart. She writes fantasy about doors, debts, and the people paid to keep both.
