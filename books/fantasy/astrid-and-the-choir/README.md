## About the author

Kestrel Thornbury is the pen name of a former cartographer who spent six years mapping mountain passes before turning to fiction, a trade that left her with a lifelong obsession with borders and the people who cross them illegally. She writes fantasy about outsiders, oaths, and the geography of belonging from a drafty farmhouse she refuses to heat properly on principle.
