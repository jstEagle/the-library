# Astrid and the Hollow

*by Petra Ravensworth*

> Kestrel Eastwick, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Petra Ravensworth is the pen name of a former archivist who spent seven years cataloguing confiscated grimoires before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about people whose small, practical magics go unnoticed until they become dangerous.
