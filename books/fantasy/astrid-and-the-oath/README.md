*by Astrid Yarrow*

> Ulyana Delacroix, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
