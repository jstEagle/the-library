# Astrid and the Salt

*by Silas Vane*

> Felix Eastwick, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
