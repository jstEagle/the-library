# Astrid and the Sigil

*by Ada Brightwater*

> Sable Marchetti, an exiled storm-caller, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
