# Astrid and the Spire

*by Elodie Whitlock*

> Astrid Galloway, a debt-bound temple scribe, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
