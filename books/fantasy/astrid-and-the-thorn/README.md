# Astrid and the Thorn

*by Lena Winterbourne*

> Astrid Joubert, a thief who steals memories, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Lena Winterbourne is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing forgotten folk songs in mountain villages before turning to fiction. She writes fantasies about memory, debt, and the families we build from strangers.
