# August and the Crown

*by Vesper Yarrow*

> Xanthe Yarrow, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Vesper Yarrow is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing merchant guild records in coastal free cities, an experience that seeps into every ledger, toll, and trade in her fiction. She writes fantasy about people who keep systems running while empires burn above them.
