# August and the Ember

*by Ulyana Dravenmoor*

> Lena Pemberton, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
