# August and the Hollow

*by Casper Joubert*

> Elodie Coldharbour, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
