# August and the Oath

*by Rafael Dunmore*

> Freya Thackeray, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
