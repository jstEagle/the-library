# August and the Salt

*by Isandro Zhou*

> Juniper Sandoval, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Isandro Zhou is the pen name of a former surveyor who spent six years charting coastlines that no longer appear on modern maps. They write fantasy about borders, exile, and the people who live in the margins of official geography.
