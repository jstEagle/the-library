# August and the Thorn

*by Jonah Zhou*

> Dominic Kavanagh, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
