# August and the Veil

*by Coral Sterling*

> Oscar Reyes, a debt-bound temple scribe, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
