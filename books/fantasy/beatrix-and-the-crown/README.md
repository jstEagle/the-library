# Beatrix and the Crown

*by Hazel Iversen*

> Odile Brightwater, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Hazel Iversen is the pen name of a former folklorist who spent a decade recording oral tales in remote mountain communities before turning to fiction. She writes fantasies about quiet people and loud magic from a farmhouse full of rescued animals who, she insists, answer back.
