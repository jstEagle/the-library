# Beatrix and the Ember

*by Jude Winterbourne*

> Edmund Yarrow, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
