# Beatrix and the Veil

*by Wendell Crane*

> Edmund Underhill, a debt-bound temple scribe, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
