*by Wren Joubert*

> Orsen Almeida, a debt-bound temple scribe, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
