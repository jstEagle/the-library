# Bram and the Choir

*by Ines Kavanagh*

> Cyrus Vane, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
