# Bram and the Garden Anew

*by Isandro Marchetti*

> Zephyrine Marchetti, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Isandro Marchetti is the pen name of a former botanist who left research to write fantasies about plants that remember and people who forget. They live near a neglected public garden they are slowly, illegally restoring.
