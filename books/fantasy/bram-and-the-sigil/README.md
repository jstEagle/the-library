# Bram and the Sigil

*by Vesper Zhou*

> Freya Kavanagh, a thief who steals memories, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
