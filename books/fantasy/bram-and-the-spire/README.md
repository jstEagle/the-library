# Bram and the Spire

*by Hazel Thackeray*

> August Iversen, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
