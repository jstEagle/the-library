# Bram and the Thorn

**by Z. M. Eriksen**

> Wren Vane, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Z. M. Eriksen is the pen name of a former line-cook turned archivist who grew up in a mining town where half the families claimed descent from a kingdom no map remembered. They write fantasy about people the histories skip, and this is their second novel.
