# Bram and the Thorn

*by Z. M. Eriksen*

> Wren Vane, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
