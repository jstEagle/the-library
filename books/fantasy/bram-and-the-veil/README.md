# Bram and the Veil

*by Orsen Fairweather*

> Mara Thornbury, a debt-bound temple scribe, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Orsen Fairweather is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing legal documents from defunct monasteries, an experience that left him obsessed with what names and debts do to people. He writes fantasy about ordinary clerks caught in extraordinary bargains from a farmhouse he refuses to geotag.
