# Caerlum Rising Anew

*by Astrid Montrose*

> Mirabel Yarrow, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Astrid Montrose is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing confiscated grimoires in a northern university library before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about people the record forgot, and lives off-grid with two ill-tempered goats and an annotated atlas of imaginary places.
