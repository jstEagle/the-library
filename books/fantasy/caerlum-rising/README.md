# Caerlum Rising

*by Odile Oyelaran*

> Dominic Okafor, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
