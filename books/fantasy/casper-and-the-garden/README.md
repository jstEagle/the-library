# Casper and the Garden

*by Cyrus Joubert*

> Xanthe Dunmore, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
