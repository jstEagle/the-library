# Casper and the Spire

*by L. M. Eriksen*

> Beatrix Ashgrove, an exiled storm-caller, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the long winter finishes falling.
