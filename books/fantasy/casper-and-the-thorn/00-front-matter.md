## About the author

Coral Blackwood spent seven years cataloguing rare manuscripts in a private collection before turning to fiction, a period that seeded her obsession with documents that outlive their keepers. She writes from a coastal town where the fog arrives on schedule, and claims her best ideas come from marginalia left by strangers.
