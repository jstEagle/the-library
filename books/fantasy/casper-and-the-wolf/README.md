# Casper and the Wolf

*by Coral Crane*

> Oscar Eriksen, a cartographer of unmapped realms, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
