# Choir and Ember

*by Mara Yarrow*

> Zephyrine Dunmore, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.
