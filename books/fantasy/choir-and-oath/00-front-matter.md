# Choir and Oath

**by Rafael Zhou**

> Casper Brightwater, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option.
