# Choir and Sigil

*by Hazel Joubert*

> Marguerite Underhill, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Hazel Joubert is a former choir singer and amateur cartographer who grew up in mountain towns where weather was treated as a neighbor with moods. She writes fantasy about people whose pasts arrive uninvited, usually in bad conditions.
