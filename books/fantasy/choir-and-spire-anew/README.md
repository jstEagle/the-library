*by Juniper Sandoval*

Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Juniper Sandoval is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing oral histories in remote mountain villages before turning to fantasy. They write about memory, borders, and the people who live outside both.
