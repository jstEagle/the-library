# Choir and Thorn

*by Juniper Ashgrove*

> Beatrix Vasquez, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
