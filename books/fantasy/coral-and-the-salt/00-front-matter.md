## About the author

Paloma Winterbourne is the pen name of a former tide-pool surveyor who grew up in coastal fishing towns and now writes fantasy from a house perpetually full of sea glass and half-finished maps. Her work explores what justice looks like when the law belongs to the people who broke it first.
