# Coral and the Wolf

*by R. M. Sandoval*

> Cyrus Amberdale, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.
