## About the author

Jude Nakamura spent a decade restoring liturgical instruments in coastal monasteries before turning to fiction, a trade that taught him how objects hold the prayers of their handlers. Crown and Choir is his second novel, following the quietly acclaimed The Salt Reliquary.
