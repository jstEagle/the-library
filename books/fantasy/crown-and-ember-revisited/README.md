# Crown and Ember Revisited

*by Jonah Reyes*

> Xanthe Ravensworth, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
