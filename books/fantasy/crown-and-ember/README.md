# Crown and Ember

*by Tamsin Underhill*

> August Okafor, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
