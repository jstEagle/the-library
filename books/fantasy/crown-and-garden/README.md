# Crown and Garden

*by Jonah Thornbury*

> Theo Amberdale, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
