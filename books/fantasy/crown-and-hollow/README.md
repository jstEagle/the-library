# Crown and Hollow

*by W. M. Brightwater*

> Silas Joubert, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
