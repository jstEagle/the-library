## About the author

Elodie Vane is a former maritime archaeologist who spent a decade cataloguing drowned temples before turning to fiction full-time. She writes fantasy about ordinary craftspeople caught in the machinery of history, and lives in a coastal town where the fog rolls in like a debt collector.
