# Crown and Salt

*by Elodie Vane*

> Paloma Zhou, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
