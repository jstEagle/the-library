# Crown and Spire

*by Ulyana Nakamura*

> Quentin Eriksen, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Ulyana Nakamura is a former pastry chef who spent a decade working palace-adjacent kitchens before turning to fiction, a history that flavors her obsession with servants, secrets, and what royalty swallows without tasting. She writes from a drafty apartment above a tea shop, where she keeps ledgers of her own—mostly of debts owed to cats.
