# Crown and Thorn

*by Ada Dunmore*

> Zephyrine Dunmore, a debt-bound temple scribe, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ada Dunmore is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing medieval legal documents before turning to fantasy. They write about people trapped by systems — debts, oaths, crowns — and the small rebellions that free them.
