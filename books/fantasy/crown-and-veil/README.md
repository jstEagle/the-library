# Crown and Veil

*by Marguerite Voss*

> Quentin Sterling, a cartographer of unmapped realms, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
