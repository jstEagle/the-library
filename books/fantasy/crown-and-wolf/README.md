# Crown and Wolf

*by Ivo Fairweather*

> Sable Kavanagh, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
