# Cyrus and the Choir

*by Beatrix Vane*

> Wendell Dunmore, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Beatrix Vane is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging funeral rites across three continents before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about the obligations the living owe the dead, and lives near a cemetery she insists is excellent company.
