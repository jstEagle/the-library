# Cyrus and the Ember

*by Hazel Thackeray*

Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Hazel Thackeray is the pen name of a former cartographer who once mapped mountain passes for a government that later redrew them without explanation. She writes fantasy about borders, oaths, and the people who refuse to let either define them.
