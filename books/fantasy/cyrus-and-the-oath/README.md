# Cyrus and the Oath

*by Ansel Villalobos*

> Sable Reyes, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the long winter finishes falling.
