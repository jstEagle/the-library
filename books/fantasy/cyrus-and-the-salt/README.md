# Cyrus and the Salt

*by Beatrix Coldharbour*

> Mara Lockridge, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
