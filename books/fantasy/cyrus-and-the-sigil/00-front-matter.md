Yara Reyes is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing trade ledgers in port cities, an experience that taught her that every empire is built on paperwork and secrets. She writes fantasy about ordinary keepers of extraordinary things, and Cyrus and the Sigil is her second novel.
