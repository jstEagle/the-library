# Cyrus and the Spire

*by Ines Sandoval*

> Ulyana Crane, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
