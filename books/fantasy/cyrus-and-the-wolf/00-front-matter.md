## About the author

Mirabel Voss is the pen name of a former trail guide who spent six seasons mapping passes through ranges she refuses to name, filling notebooks with the stories porters told at night. She writes fantasy about people bound by promises they never meant to keep, and lives somewhere high enough that the mail arrives late.
