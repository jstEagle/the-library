# Cyrus and the Wolf

*by Mirabel Voss*

> Oscar Joubert, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Mirabel Voss is the pen name of a former trail guide who spent six seasons mapping passes through ranges she refuses to name, filling notebooks with the stories porters told at night. She writes fantasy about people bound by promises they never meant to keep, and lives somewhere high enough that the mail arrives late.
