# Dominic and the Choir

*by Mara Winterbourne*

> Theo Blackwood, a thief who steals memories, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Mara Winterbourne is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters nobody ever claimed, which is where she learned that memory is the truest form of treasure. She writes fantasy about outsiders finding their people from a drafty house at the edge of the mountains.
