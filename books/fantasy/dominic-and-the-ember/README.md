# Dominic and the Ember

*by Rosalind Okafor*

> Freya Reyes, a thief who steals memories, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Rosalind Okafor is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing confiscated letters in a coastal city's records office before turning to fiction full-time. She writes about thieves, ledgers, and the quiet violence of institutions from a house full of rescued cats and unreliable clocks.
