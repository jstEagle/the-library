# Dominic and the Hollow

*by Orsen Blackwood*

> Lior Eastwick, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
