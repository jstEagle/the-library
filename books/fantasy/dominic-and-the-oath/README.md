# Dominic and the Oath

*by Noor Hartwell*

> Wren Galloway, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
