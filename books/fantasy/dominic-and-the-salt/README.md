# Dominic and the Salt

*by Lior Villalobos*

> Hazel Zhou, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Lior Villalobos is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging debt records from defunct merchant houses before turning to fiction. They write about people who keep accounts with things that cannot be repaid.
