# Dominic and the Spire

*by Lior Sandoval*

> Lena Hartwell, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
