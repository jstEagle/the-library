# Dominic and the Thorn

*by Petra Sandoval*

> Wren Zhou, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
