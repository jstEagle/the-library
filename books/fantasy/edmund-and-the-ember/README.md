# Edmund and the Ember

*by Vesper Halloran*

> Petra Vasquez, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
