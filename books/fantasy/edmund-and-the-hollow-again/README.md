# Edmund and the Hollow Again

*by Beatrix Eastwick*

> Jonah Eastwick, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.

Beatrix Eastwick is the pen name of a former cartographer who spent a decade mapping mountain passes across three continents before turning to fiction. She writes from a stone farmhouse full of old maps, and claims every story begins with a road that shouldn't exist.
