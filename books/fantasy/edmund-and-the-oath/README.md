# Edmund and the Oath

*by Orsen Yarrow*

> Lena Blackwood, an exiled storm-caller, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.
