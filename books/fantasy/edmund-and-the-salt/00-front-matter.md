## About the author

Gideon Nystrom is the pen name of a former lighthouse keeper turned archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the papers of defunct trade guilds along the Baltic coast. Their fiction blends bureaucratic fantasy with quiet, aching meditations on what it costs to be remembered.
