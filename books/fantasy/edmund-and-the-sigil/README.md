# Edmund and the Sigil

*by Ines Winterbourne*

> Sebastian Delacroix, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
