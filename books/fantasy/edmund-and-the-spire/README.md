# Edmund and the Spire

*by E. M. Whitlock*

> Petra Underhill, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

E. M. Whitlock is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing confiscated grimoires before turning to fiction. Edmund and the Spire is the first volume of a planned trilogy about countries that outlive their own names.
