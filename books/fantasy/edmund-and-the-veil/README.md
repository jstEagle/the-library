# Edmund and the Veil

*by Edmund Whitlock*

> Sebastian Fairweather, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
