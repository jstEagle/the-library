# Elodie and the Choir

*by E. M. Voss*

> Mirabel Iversen, the youngest member of a dying guild, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
