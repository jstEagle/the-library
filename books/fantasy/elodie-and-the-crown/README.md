*by Coral Kavanagh*

> Juniper Crane, a bone-carver who hears the dead, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Coral Kavanagh is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing estate ledgers in a northern mill town before turning to fantasy. She writes stories about people who keep accounts—of money, of grief, of promises—and what it costs to forgive a debt.
