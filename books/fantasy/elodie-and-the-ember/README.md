# Elodie and the Ember

*by Mara Coldharbour*

> Elodie Coldharbour, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
