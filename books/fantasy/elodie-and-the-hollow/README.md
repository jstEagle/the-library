# Elodie and the Hollow

*by Vesper Halloran*

> August Okonkwo, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
