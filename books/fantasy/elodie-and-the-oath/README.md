# Elodie and the Oath

*by Gideon Montrose*

> Ansel Thackeray, a thief who steals memories, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
