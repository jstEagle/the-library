# Elodie and the Sigil

*by Elodie Underhill*

> Ansel Fairweather, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
