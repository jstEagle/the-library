# Elodie and the Thorn

*by Dominic Eastwick*

> Zephyrine Nystrom, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
