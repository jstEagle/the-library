# Ember and Choir Again

*by L. M. Villalobos*

> Jude Thackeray, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

They write from a converted chapel in the Pacific Northwest, where they claim the acoustics are excellent for listening to things that are not there.
