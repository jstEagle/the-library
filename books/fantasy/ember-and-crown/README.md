# Ember and Crown

*by Astrid Galloway*

> Noor Vasquez, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Astrid Galloway is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the belongings of people with no one left to claim them. She writes fantasy about the paperwork of magic and the families we build from strangers.
