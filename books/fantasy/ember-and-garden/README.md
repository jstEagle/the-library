# Ember and Garden

*by Tamsin Okafor*

> Yara Whitlock, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
