## About the author

L. M. Okonkwo is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing oral histories from communities displaced by border wars, an experience that shapes their fiction's obsession with what survives when memory fails. They write fantasy about chosen families from a small house at the edge of a large forest, accompanied by two opinionated cats.
