# Ember and Oath Anew

*by L. M. Okonkwo*

> Lior Hartwell, the youngest member of a dying guild, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

L. M. Okonkwo is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing oral histories from communities displaced by border wars, an experience that shapes their fiction's obsession with what survives when memory fails. They write fantasy about chosen families from a small house at the edge of a large forest, accompanied by two opinionated cats.
