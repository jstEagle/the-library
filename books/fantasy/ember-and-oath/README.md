# Ember and Oath

*by Odile Sterling*

> Tamsin Dravenmoor, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Odile Sterling is the pen name of a former surveyor who spent a decade mapping coastlines before turning to coastlines that do not exist. She writes from a house full of obsolete atlases, and claims her characters arrive the way weather does.
