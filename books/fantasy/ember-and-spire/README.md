# Ember and Spire

*by Bram Thornbury*

> Ines Galloway, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
