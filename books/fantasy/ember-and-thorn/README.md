# Ember and Thorn

*by Isandro Winterbourne*

> Jude Amberdale, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
