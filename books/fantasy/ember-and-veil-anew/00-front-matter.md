# Ember and Veil Anew

**by Ivo Oyelaran**

> Gideon Amberdale, a debt-bound temple scribe, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Ivo Oyelaran is a former archivist from Lagos who writes fantasy about bureaucracies, faith, and the people history forgets to record. 'Ember and Veil Anew' is the second novel in the Ember and Veil sequence.
