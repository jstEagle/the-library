# Ember and Veil Anew

*by Ivo Oyelaran*

> Gideon Amberdale, a debt-bound temple scribe, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
