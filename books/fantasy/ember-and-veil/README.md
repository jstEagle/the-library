*by W. M. Ravensworth*

Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

W. M. Ravensworth is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing maps of places that no longer appear on any modern atlas. They write fantasy about borders, both real and imagined, from a house at the edge of a valley much like Jonah's.
