# Emrys and the Crown

*by Petra Pemberton*

> Felix Fairweather, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
