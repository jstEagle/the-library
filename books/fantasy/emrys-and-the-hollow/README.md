# Emrys and the Hollow

*by Silas Voss*

> Rosalind Dunmore, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
