To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Mirabel Reyes is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing oral histories from mountain villages before turning to fiction full-time. She writes fantasy about memory, inheritance, and the small betrayals families keep, and lives somewhere with unreliable winters.
