# Felix and the Choir

*by Ivo Fairweather*

> Tamsin Thackeray, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Ivo Fairweather is the pen name of a former choir singer turned archivist who spent a decade cataloguing border folklore in remote mountain villages. Their fiction blends the textures of old ballads with the hard practicalities of people who live by their wits.
