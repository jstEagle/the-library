# Felix and the Thorn

**by Felix Yarrow**

But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Felix Yarrow spent seven years as a line cook in a mountain resort town before turning to fantasy, and he still measures every fictional feast by whether he could actually make it. Felix and the Thorn is their first novel, written mostly during night shifts and finished in a rented cabin where, reassuringly, no stars went out.
