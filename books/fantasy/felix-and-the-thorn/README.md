# Felix and the Thorn

*by Felix Yarrow*

> Xanthe Reyes, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Felix Yarrow spent seven years as a line cook in a mountain resort town before turning to fantasy, and he still measures every fictional feast by whether he could actually make it. Felix and the Thorn is their first novel, written mostly during night shifts and finished in a rented cabin where, reassuringly, no stars went out.
