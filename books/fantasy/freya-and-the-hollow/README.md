# Freya and the Hollow

*by P. M. Crane*

> Juniper Vane, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
