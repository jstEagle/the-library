# Freya and the Salt Again

*by Elodie Whitlock*

> Lena Nakamura, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
