# Freya and the Sigil

*by Elodie Kavanagh*

> Oscar Hartwell, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
