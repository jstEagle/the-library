## About the author

Rafael Whitlock is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing merchant ledgers before turning to fiction, a history that seeps into his obsession with records, debt, and forgotten tongues. He writes from a cluttered study overlooking a harbor city, where he insists every fantasy world deserves its own bureaucracy.
