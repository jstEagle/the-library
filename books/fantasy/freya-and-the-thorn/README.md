# Freya and the Thorn

*by Coral Lockridge*

> Xanthe Fairweather, an exiled storm-caller, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
