# Freya and the Wolf

*by Coral Fairweather*

> Paloma Thackeray, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
