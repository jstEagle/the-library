# Garden and Choir

*by Mirabel Joubert*

> Vesper Winterbourne, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Mirabel Joubert is the pen name of a former choir singer who left music after losing her voice to illness and turned instead to writing about things that sing without throats. She lives near wetlands she refuses to name, keeping notebooks full of overheard animal sounds and half-remembered hymns.
