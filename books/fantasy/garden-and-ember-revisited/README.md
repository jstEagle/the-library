# Garden and Ember Revisited

*by Noor Voss*

> Sebastian Villalobos, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.
