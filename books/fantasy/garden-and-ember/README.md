# Garden and Ember

*by Ivo Crane*

> August Dunmore, a thief who steals memories, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
