# Garden and Hollow

*by Wren Vane*

> Juniper Pemberton, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Wren Vane is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing guild ledgers and now writes fantasies about the people who keep the books. They live in a drafty house with a talkative cat and a stubborn belief that every prophecy is a filing error waiting to be corrected.
