# Garden and Veil Anew

*by Juniper Joubert*

> Emrys Ashgrove, an exiled storm-caller, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Juniper Joubert is the pen name of a former botanist who traded field notebooks for fantasy manuscripts after a decade cataloguing alpine wildflowers. They write stories about stubborn people, small magics, and the families we build when the old ones fail us.
