# Garden and Veil

*by Elodie Okonkwo*

> Mara Oyelaran, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
