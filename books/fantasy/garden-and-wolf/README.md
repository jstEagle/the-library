# Garden and Wolf

*by Ansel Amberdale*

> Sebastian Montrose, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
