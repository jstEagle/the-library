# Gideon and the Choir

*by Isandro Brightwater*

> Jude Yarrow, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
