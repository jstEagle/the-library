# Gideon and the Ember

*by Rafael Nakamura*

> Orsen Dunmore, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
