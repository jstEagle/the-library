# Gideon and the Garden

*by Noor Iversen*

> Casper Dunmore, an exiled storm-caller, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the long winter finishes falling.
