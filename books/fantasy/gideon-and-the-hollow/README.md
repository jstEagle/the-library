# Gideon and the Hollow

*by W. M. Ishikawa*

> Coral Nystrom, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
