# Gideon and the Salt

*by Petra Villalobos*

> Quentin Whitlock, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Petra Villalobos is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the paperwork of defunct nations. She writes about borders, hunger, and the people who keep records no one asked them to keep.
