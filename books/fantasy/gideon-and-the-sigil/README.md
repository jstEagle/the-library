# Gideon and the Sigil

*by Elodie Fairweather*

> Emrys Delacroix, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
