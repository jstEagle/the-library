# Gideon and the Thorn

*by Theo Oyelaran*

> Kestrel Nakamura, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
