# Gideon and the Veil

*by G. M. Pemberton*

> Marguerite Ashgrove, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.
