# Gideon and the Wolf

*by Xanthe Villalobos*

> Gideon Winterbourne, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author
