# Hazel and the Crown

*by Sable Sandoval*

> Silas Crane, an exiled storm-caller, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Sable Sandoval is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing weather records from abandoned mountain towns before turning to fantasy. They write about stubborn people in cold places and insist every story should end with someone choosing to stay.
