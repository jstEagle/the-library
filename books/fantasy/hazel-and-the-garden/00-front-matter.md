## About the author

Theo Eriksen is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing estate sales and unclaimed inheritances, an experience that left him convinced every object keeps a grudge. He writes fantasy about paperwork, memory, and the terrible arithmetic of inheritance from a drafty house full of other people's letters.
