# Hazel and the Garden

*by Theo Eriksen*

> Hazel Delacroix, the youngest member of a dying guild, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Theo Eriksen is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing estate sales and unclaimed inheritances, an experience that left him convinced every object keeps a grudge. He writes fantasy about paperwork, memory, and the terrible arithmetic of inheritance from a drafty house full of other people's letters.
