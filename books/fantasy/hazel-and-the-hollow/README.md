# Hazel and the Hollow

*by Hazel Dravenmoor*

> Wendell Brightwater, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Hazel Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the paperwork of defunct institutions and now writes fantasy about what those papers were hiding. They live near a border town that has changed countries four times without moving an inch.
