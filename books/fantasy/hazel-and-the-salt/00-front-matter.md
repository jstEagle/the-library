# Hazel and the Salt

**by Gideon Amberdale**

> Bram Hartwell, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
