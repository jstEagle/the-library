# Hazel and the Spire

*by Tamsin Dravenmoor*

> Xanthe Vane, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
