# Highmoor Rising Again

*by Felix Dravenmoor*

> Ivo Brightwater, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Felix Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former wildlife rehabilitator who spent a decade listening to what animals couldn't say, and now writes fantasy about the cost of staying silent. They live at the edge of a pine forest with two opinionated dogs and a growing collection of antique books on prophecy.
