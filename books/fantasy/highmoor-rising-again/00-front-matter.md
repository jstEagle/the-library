## About the author

Lena Halloran is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing obscure trade ledgers before turning to fantasy, a background that infuses her fiction with meticulous, bureaucratic magic. She lives in a coastal town where she writes stories about ordinary people caught in the gears of enormous debts.
