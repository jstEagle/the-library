# Hollow and Choir Anew

*by Quentin Lockridge*

> Kestrel Thackeray, a cartographer of unmapped realms, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Quentin Lockridge is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing maps of places that no longer exist. Hollow and Choir Anew is their second novel, following the critically praised The Tithe of Small Hours.
