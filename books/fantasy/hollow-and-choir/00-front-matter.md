# Hollow and Choir

**by Ines Almeida**

> Xanthe Thornbury, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Ines Almeida is a former archivist who spent years cataloguing grimoires in a Portuguese monastery before turning to fiction. She writes about magic as inheritance—the things our teachers leave us, whether or not we asked for them.
