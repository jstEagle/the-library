# Hollow and Crown

*by Oscar Hartwell*

> Emrys Oyelaran, a debt-bound temple scribe, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.
