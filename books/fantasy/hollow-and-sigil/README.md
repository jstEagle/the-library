# Hollow and Sigil

*by L. M. Amberdale*

> Lena Vane, the youngest member of a dying guild, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
