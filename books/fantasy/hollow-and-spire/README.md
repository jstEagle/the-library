*by Odile Eriksen*

> Rafael Okafor, a cartographer of unmapped realms, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Odile Eriksen is the pen name of a former surveyor who spent six years mapping remote mountain passes before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about people who make their living from edges — borders, coastlines, and the spaces between names.
