## About the author

Noor Eastwick is the pen name of a former cartographer's apprentice who grew up in mining towns at the edge of real mountain ranges, where old roads outlasted the names of the villages they served. They write fantasy about borders, oaths, and the people who carry places in their heads after the places are gone.
