# Hollow and Veil Again

*by Beatrix Vane*

> Sable Quintana, an exiled storm-caller, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
