# Ines and the Choir

*by Mara Eastwick*

> Ulyana Pemberton, a cartographer of unmapped realms, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Mara Eastwick is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing guild ledgers in coastal trading cities before turning to fiction. Her novels blend bureaucratic precision with folkloric wonder, exploring what institutions bury to survive.
