# Ines and the Ember

*by L. M. Eriksen*

> Ada Blackwood, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.

## About the author

L. M. Eriksen is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing legal documents about debts, inheritances, and disputes, which explains the obsession with ledgers that runs through all their fiction. They live near a mountain they refuse to go under.
