# Ines and the Hollow

*by P. M. Thackeray*

> August Villalobos, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
