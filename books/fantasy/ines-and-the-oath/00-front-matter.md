## About the author

J. M. Ashgrove is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing funeral effigies in northern cathedral crypts, an experience that seeped into every page of this debut. They write in a drafty stone cottage and claim, only half-jokingly, that the house remembers its previous owners.
