# Ines and the Oath

*by J. M. Ashgrove*

> Marguerite Coldharbour, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

J. M. Ashgrove is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing funeral effigies in northern cathedral crypts, an experience that seeped into every page of this debut. They write in a drafty stone cottage and claim, only half-jokingly, that the house remembers its previous owners.
