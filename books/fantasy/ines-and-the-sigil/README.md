# Ines and the Sigil

*by Juniper Okafor*

> Orsen Vane, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.
