## About the author

Elodie Oyelaran is a former trail guide who spent six seasons leading expeditions through mountain passes that appear, thinly disguised, throughout her fiction. She writes fantasy about oaths, debts, and the people who survive both, and lives in a stone farmhouse with too many maps and one opinionated goat.
