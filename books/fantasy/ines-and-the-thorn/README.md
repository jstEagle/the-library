# Ines and the Thorn

*by Elodie Oyelaran*

> Marguerite Thornbury, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Elodie Oyelaran is a former trail guide who spent six seasons leading expeditions through mountain passes that appear, thinly disguised, throughout her fiction. She writes fantasy about oaths, debts, and the people who survive both, and lives in a stone farmhouse with too many maps and one opinionated goat.
