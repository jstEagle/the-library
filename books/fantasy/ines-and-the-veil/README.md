# Ines and the Veil

*by Emrys Blackwood*

> Ulyana Delacroix, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
