## About the author

Jude Kavanagh is the pen name of a former line cook who spent a decade working kitchens before turning to fantasy, and whose fiction is obsessed with what people swallow—literally and otherwise. They live somewhere rainy with an opinionated cat and a growing collection of antique poison bottles.
