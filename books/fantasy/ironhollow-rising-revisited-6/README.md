# Ironhollow Rising Revisited

*by Rafael Ashgrove*

> Quentin Lockridge, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
