## About the author

Bram Sterling is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing merchant guild records in northern port cities, an experience that seeps into every ledger-bound corner of their fiction. They write fantasy about quiet people forced into loud histories, and claim their best ideas arrive while listening to birds they cannot understand.
