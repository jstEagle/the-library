# Isandro and the Garden

*by S. M. Joubert*

> Wendell Thornbury, the youngest member of a dying guild, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
