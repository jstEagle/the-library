# Isandro and the Hollow Anew

*by Astrid Oyelaran*

> Isandro Dunmore, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
