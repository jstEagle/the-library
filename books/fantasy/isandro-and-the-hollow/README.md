# Isandro and the Hollow

*by Zephyrine Nakamura*

> Hazel Sandoval, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
