# Isandro and the Oath Again

*by August Hartwell*

> Gideon Okafor, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
