# Isandro and the Sigil Anew

*by Ines Underhill*

> Jonah Sandoval, an exiled storm-caller, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
