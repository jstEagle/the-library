# Isandro and the Spire

*by O. M. Blackwood*

> Dominic Marchetti, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
