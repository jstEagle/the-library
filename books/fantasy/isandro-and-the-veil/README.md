## About the author

Orsen Nakamura is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing estate sales and probate ledgers before turning to fiction. They write fantasy about paperwork, bargains, and the quiet violence of contracts, and live near a coastline whose lighthouses feature in everything they write.
