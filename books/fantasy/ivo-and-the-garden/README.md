# Ivo and the Garden

*by Theo Oyelaran*

> Xanthe Blackwood, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
