# Ivo and the Salt

*by M. M. Ravensworth*

> Ines Oyelaran, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
