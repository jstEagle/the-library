## About the author

Mara Sterling is a former cartographer's assistant who grew up in three countries and belongs to none of them, a rootlessness that fuels her fiction about doors, borders, and belonging. She writes at dawn in a converted lighthouse on the northern coast, where she lives with her partner, an ill-tempered cat named Marginalia.
