# Ivo and the Sigil

*by Mara Sterling*

> Astrid Joubert, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Mara Sterling is a former cartographer's assistant who grew up in three countries and belongs to none of them, a rootlessness that fuels her fiction about doors, borders, and belonging. She writes at dawn in a converted lighthouse on the northern coast, where she lives with her partner, an ill-tempered cat named Marginalia.
