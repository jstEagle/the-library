# Ivo and the Thorn

*by F. M. Amberdale*

> Beatrix Brightwater, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
