# Ivo and the Veil

*by Edmund Marchetti*

> Mirabel Quintana, a cartographer of unmapped realms, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
