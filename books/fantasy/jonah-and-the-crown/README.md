# Jonah and the Crown

*by Felix Zhou*

> Noor Ravensworth, an exiled storm-caller, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
