# Jonah and the Oath Revisited

*by J. M. Quintana*

> Petra Hartwell, an exiled storm-caller, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
