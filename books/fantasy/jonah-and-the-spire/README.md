# Jonah and the Spire

*by Ulyana Eastwick*

> Isandro Almeida, an exiled storm-caller, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
