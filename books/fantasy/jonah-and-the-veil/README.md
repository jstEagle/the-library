# Jonah and the Veil

*by I. M. Kavanagh*

> Coral Halloran, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
