Now they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Silas Joubert is the pen name of a former choir director from the Karoo who traded hymnals for folklore after finding his grandmother's hand-copied prophecy book in a locked piano bench. He writes fantasy about small towns, large silences, and the price of being remembered.
