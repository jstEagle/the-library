# Jude and the Hollow

*by Beatrix Joubert*

> Quentin Kavanagh, a debt-bound temple scribe, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
