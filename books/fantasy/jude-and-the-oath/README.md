*by Ansel Vasquez*

> Bram Vane, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
