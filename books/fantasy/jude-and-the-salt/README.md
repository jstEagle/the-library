# Jude and the Salt

*by Kestrel Villalobos*

> Felix Fairweather, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Kestrel Villalobos grew up in a coastal town where the tide charts were kept by three generations of her family, and she still swears the sea keeps accounts of its own. She writes fantasy about bargains, bookkeeping, and the people who fall between the lines.
