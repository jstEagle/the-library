# Jude and the Thorn

*by Kestrel Halloran*

> Mara Dravenmoor, the youngest member of a dying guild, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
