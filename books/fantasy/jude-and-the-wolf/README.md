# Jude and the Wolf

*by Yara Montrose*

> Juniper Thackeray, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
