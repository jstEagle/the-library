# Juniper and the Garden

*by Ada Sandoval*

> August Montrose, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
