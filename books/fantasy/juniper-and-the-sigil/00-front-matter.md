## About the author

Rosalind Reyes is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing estate papers and unclaimed inheritances, an obsession that bleeds into her fiction. She writes fantasy about bargains, ledgers, and the people who keep them, from a rain-dark coastal town.
