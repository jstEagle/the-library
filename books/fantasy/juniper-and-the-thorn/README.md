# Juniper and the Thorn

*by Wren Voss*

> Casper Thackeray, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
