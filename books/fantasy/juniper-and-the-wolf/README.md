# Juniper and the Wolf

*by Jonah Vasquez*

> Paloma Villalobos, a thief who steals memories, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
