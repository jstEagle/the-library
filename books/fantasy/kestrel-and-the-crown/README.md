# Kestrel and the Crown

*by J. M. Eastwick*

> Lena Ashgrove, a debt-bound temple scribe, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
