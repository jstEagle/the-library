# Kestrel and the Ember

*by Ines Halloran*

> Bram Ashgrove, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
