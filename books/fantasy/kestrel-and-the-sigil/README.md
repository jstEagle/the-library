# Kestrel and the Sigil

*by Yara Lockridge*

> Mirabel Montrose, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
