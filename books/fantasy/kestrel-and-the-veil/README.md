# Kestrel and the Veil

*by Kestrel Montrose*

> Paloma Ashgrove, a debt-bound temple scribe, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
