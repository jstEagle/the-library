# Lena and the Choir

*by Beatrix Lockridge*

> August Ishikawa, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Beatrix Lockridge is the pen name of a former choir singer turned archivist who writes fantasy about the quiet violence of institutions. She lives near a river valley she refuses to name, and swears the acoustics there are magic.
