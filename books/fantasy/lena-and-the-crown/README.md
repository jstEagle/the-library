# Lena and the Crown

*by Astrid Amberdale*

> Vesper Brightwater, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Astrid Amberdale is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing maps of places that no longer exist. She writes fantasy about people who keep things—gates, ledgers, secrets—and what happens when the keeping ends.
