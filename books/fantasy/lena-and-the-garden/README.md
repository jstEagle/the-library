# Lena and the Garden

*by Rafael Underhill*

> Casper Villalobos, a cartographer of unmapped realms, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Rafael Underhill is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the papers of defunct cartographic societies before turning to fiction. They write fantasy about paperwork, inheritance, and the violence hidden inside beautiful old institutions.
