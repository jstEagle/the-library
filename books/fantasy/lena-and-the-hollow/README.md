# Lena and the Hollow

*by Lena Thornbury*

> Edmund Yarrow, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Lena Thornbury is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing other people's forgotten letters before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about people who keep things — ledgers, promises, each other.
