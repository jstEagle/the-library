# Lena and the Spire

*by Emrys Coldharbour*

> Sebastian Okafor, an exiled storm-caller, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
