# Lena and the Veil Anew

*by Wendell Villalobos*

> Paloma Blackwood, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
