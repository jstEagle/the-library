# Lena and the Veil

*by I. M. Brightwater*

> Oscar Sandoval, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
