# Lena and the Wolf

*by Emrys Okonkwo*

> Odile Kavanagh, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
