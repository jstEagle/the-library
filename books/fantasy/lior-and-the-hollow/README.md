# Lior and the Hollow

*by Quentin Underhill*

> Casper Joubert, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Quentin Underhill is the pen name of a former wildlife rehabilitator who spent a decade listening to animals and claims it changed how he hears people. He writes fantasy about quiet gifts and loud worlds from a farmhouse surrounded by opinionated geese.
