# Lior and the Spire

*by Tamsin Montrose*

> Theo Dravenmoor, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Tamsin Montrose is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging the private letters of minor nobility, an experience she describes as 'reading a hundred novels that never got finished.' She writes fantasy about people whose jobs history forgets, and lives in a drafty coastal house with too many clocks.
