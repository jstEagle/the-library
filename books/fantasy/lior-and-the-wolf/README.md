# Lior and the Wolf

*by Juniper Hartwell*

> Jonah Thackeray, a bone-carver who hears the dead, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Juniper Hartwell is a former cartographer's apprentice from the Welsh borders who spent a decade collecting half-remembered place names from dying villages. She writes fantasy about countries that exist only in the mouths of the old.
