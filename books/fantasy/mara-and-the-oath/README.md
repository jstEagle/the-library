# Mara and the Oath

*by Oscar Quintana*

> Vesper Crane, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
