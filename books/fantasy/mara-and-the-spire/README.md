# Mara and the Spire

*by Wren Blackwood*

> Petra Villalobos, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.
