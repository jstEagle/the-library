# Mara and the Thorn

*by Beatrix Kavanagh*

> Freya Hartwell, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Beatrix Kavanagh is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing medieval legal documents before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about ordinary craftspeople caught in the machinery of power, drawing on folklore of the English borderlands.
