# Mara and the Wolf

*by Coral Pemberton*

> Bram Hartwell, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
