# Marguerite and the Choir

*by Zephyrine Vasquez*

> Ines Almeida, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Zephyrine Vasquez is the pen name of a former church choir alto who grew up in three countries, none of which appear on her birth certificate. She writes about borders, appetite, and the gods people invent to survive their families.
