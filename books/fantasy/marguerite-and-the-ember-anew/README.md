# Marguerite and the Ember Anew

*by B. M. Marchetti*

> Dominic Sterling, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
