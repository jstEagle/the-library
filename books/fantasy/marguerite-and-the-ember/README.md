# Marguerite and the Ember

*by A. M. Sterling*

> Edmund Reyes, a debt-bound temple scribe, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
