# Marguerite and the Hollow

*by Wendell Iversen*

> Lior Dunmore, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
