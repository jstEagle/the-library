# Marguerite and the Oath

*by Jonah Reyes*

> Coral Galloway, a debt-bound temple scribe, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
