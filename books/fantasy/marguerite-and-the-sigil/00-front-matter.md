# Marguerite and the Sigil

**by Quentin Marchetti**

> Lena Amberdale, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
