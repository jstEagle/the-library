# Mirabel and the Choir

*by Ulyana Sandoval*

> Coral Ishikawa, a cartographer of unmapped realms, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Ulyana Sandoval is a former choir alto who spent a decade cataloguing rare maps in a private archive before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about people who keep things — ledgers, secrets, fires — and what it costs to finally let go.
