# Mirabel and the Crown

*by Marguerite Vane*

> Odile Fairweather, the youngest member of a dying guild, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
