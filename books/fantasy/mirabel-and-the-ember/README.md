# Mirabel and the Ember

*by Petra Vane*

> Vesper Thackeray, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the long winter finishes falling.

Petra Vane is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing medieval prayer books before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about faith, inheritance, and the people history forgets to record.
