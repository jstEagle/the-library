# Mirabel and the Hollow

*by Emrys Eriksen*

> Wren Okafor, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
