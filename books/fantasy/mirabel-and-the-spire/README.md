# Mirabel and the Spire

*by Mirabel Kavanagh*

Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

Mirabel Kavanagh is the pen name of a former cartographer who spent a decade mapping mountain passes before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about borders, both the kind drawn on maps and the kind drawn around hearts.
