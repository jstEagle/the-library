## About the author

Isandro Lockridge is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters from families separated by border wars, an experience that haunts every found-family story they tell. They write fantasy about inheritance—the things we're given, the things we refuse, and the memories we choose to keep.
