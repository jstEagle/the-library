# Mirabel and the Veil

*by Isandro Lockridge*

> Jonah Fairweather, an exiled storm-caller, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Isandro Lockridge is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters from families separated by border wars, an experience that haunts every found-family story they tell. They write fantasy about inheritance—the things we're given, the things we refuse, and the memories we choose to keep.
