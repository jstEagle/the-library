*by Astrid Ashgrove*

> Juniper Thackeray, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
