# Noor and the Wolf Anew

*by August Iversen*

> Yara Eriksen, an exiled storm-caller, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
