# Noor and the Wolf

*by O. M. Blackwood*

> Theo Joubert, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
