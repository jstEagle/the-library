# Oath and Ember

*by Orsen Eastwick*

> Paloma Sterling, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Orsen Eastwick is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing broken contracts and unpaid debts in municipal records, an experience that seeps into every bargain his characters strike. He writes fantasy about people who keep their word even when it ruins them, and he insists oaths are the oldest magic there is.
