# Oath and Sigil

*by Wendell Whitlock*

> Ansel Ishikawa, a thief who steals memories, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
