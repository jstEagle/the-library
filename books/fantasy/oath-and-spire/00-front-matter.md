# Oath and Spire

**by Yara Sterling**

> Tamsin Blackwood, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
