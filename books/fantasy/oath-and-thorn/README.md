# Oath and Thorn

*by Elodie Montrose*

> Oscar Sandoval, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
