# Oath and Veil

*by Astrid Oyelaran*

> Marguerite Voss, a debt-bound temple scribe, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
