## About the author

Tamsin Thornbury is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing merchant guild records from coastal free cities, an obsession that seeps into her fiction's obsession with debts and ledgers. She writes fantasy about ordinary functionaries caught in mythic machinery from a drafty house overlooking a grey northern sea.
