# Odile and the Oath

*by Emrys Coldharbour*

> Vesper Ishikawa, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.
