# Odile and the Salt

*by Mirabel Galloway*

> Edmund Villalobos, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
