# Odile and the Spire

*by Emrys Sterling*

> Jude Joubert, the youngest member of a dying guild, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Emrys Sterling is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing medieval trade ledgers before turning to fantasy. They write stories about paperwork, prophecy, and the people caught between them.
