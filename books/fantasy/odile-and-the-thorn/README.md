# Odile and the Thorn

*by Ivo Ishikawa*

> Theo Thornbury, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
