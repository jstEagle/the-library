# Odile and the Veil

*by Sebastian Galloway*

> Sebastian Brightwater, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the long winter finishes falling.
