# Orsen and the Crown

*by W. M. Almeida*

> Sable Montrose, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
