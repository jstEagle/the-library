## About the author

R. M. Zhou is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters no one ever claimed, an experience that left a lasting fascination with what people choose to remember and what they pay to forget. Zhou writes fantasy about outsiders carrying dangerous truths, and lives quietly somewhere with unreliable weather.
