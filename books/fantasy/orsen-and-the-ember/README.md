# Orsen and the Ember

*by R. M. Zhou*

> Orsen Pemberton, a thief who steals memories, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.

## About the author

R. M. Zhou is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters no one ever claimed, an experience that left a lasting fascination with what people choose to remember and what they pay to forget. Zhou writes fantasy about outsiders carrying dangerous truths, and lives quietly somewhere with unreliable weather.
