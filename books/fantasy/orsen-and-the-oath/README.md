# Orsen and the Oath

*by Lior Amberdale*

> Kestrel Nakamura, a debt-bound temple scribe, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
