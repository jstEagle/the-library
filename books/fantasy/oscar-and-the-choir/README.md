# Oscar and the Choir

*by Juniper Crane*

> Ada Eastwick, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
