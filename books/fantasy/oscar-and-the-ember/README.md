# Oscar and the Ember

*by Ivo Nakamura*

> Silas Nakamura, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
