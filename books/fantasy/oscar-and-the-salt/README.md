# Oscar and the Salt

*by Odile Okafor*

> Isandro Hartwell, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Odile Okafor is a former archivist who spent six years cataloguing confiscated grimoires before turning to fiction. She writes from a coastal town whose name she declines to confirm, and claims her stories are all translations of books that no longer exist.
