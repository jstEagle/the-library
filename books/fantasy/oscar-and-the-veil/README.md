# Oscar and the Veil

*by Sable Montrose*

> Theo Iversen, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.
