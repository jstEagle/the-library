# Paloma and the Choir

*by Xanthe Montrose*

> Ada Brightwater, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
