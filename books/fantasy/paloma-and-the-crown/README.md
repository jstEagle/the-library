# Paloma and the Crown

*by Hazel Pemberton*

> Lena Vasquez, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Hazel Pemberton is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing folk charms and household blessings before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about ordinary people who inherit impossible things, and lives near the sea with an unreasonable number of houseplants.
