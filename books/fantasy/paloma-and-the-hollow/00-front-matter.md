## About the author

Dominic Sterling is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing merchant guild records in coastal port cities, an obsession that bleeds into every ledger, contract, and debt in his fiction. He writes fantasy about people trapped by paperwork and freed by conscience, and claims he has never once balanced his own accounts.
