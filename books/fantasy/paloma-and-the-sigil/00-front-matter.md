## About the author

Rosalind Whitlock is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing medieval charters before turning to fiction, a trade that left her obsessed with oaths, seals, and the people they bind. She writes fantasy about inheritance and obligation from a farmhouse full of rescued cats and unreliable clocks.
