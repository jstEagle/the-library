# Paloma and the Spire

*by A. M. Galloway*

> Emrys Ashgrove, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
