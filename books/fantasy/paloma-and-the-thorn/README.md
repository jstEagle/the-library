# Paloma and the Thorn

*by Mara Galloway*

> Quentin Nakamura, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Mara Galloway is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing trade ledgers in coastal port cities before turning to fantasy. She writes stories about paperwork, oaths, and the people who keep both from falling apart.
