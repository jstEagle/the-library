# Petra and the Crown

*by Q. M. Oyelaran*

> Mara Winterbourne, a thief who steals memories, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.
