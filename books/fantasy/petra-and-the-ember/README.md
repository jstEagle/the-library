# Petra and the Ember

*by Astrid Oyelaran*

> Gideon Brightwater, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
