## About the author

Dominic Nakamura is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging the correspondence of obscure nineteenth-century trading societies, an experience that seeps into every ledger and secret in his fiction. He writes from a drafty house in the Pacific Northwest, where he keeps a garden that refuses, stubbornly, to be impossible.
