# Petra and the Hollow

*by C. M. Kavanagh*

> Paloma Eriksen, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
