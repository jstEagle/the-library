# Petra and the Salt

*by I. M. Dunmore*

> August Villalobos, a bone-carver who hears the dead, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
