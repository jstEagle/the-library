# Petra and the Veil

*by Yara Dravenmoor*

> Lior Thackeray, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
