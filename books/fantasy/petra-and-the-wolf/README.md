# Petra and the Wolf

*by Lena Ravensworth*

> Jonah Whitlock, an exiled storm-caller, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
