## About the author

Hazel Pemberton is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing legal ledgers from abandoned mountain monasteries, an experience that taught her how much cruelty can hide in neat handwriting. She writes fantasy about ordinary people trapped in extraordinary paperwork, and lives near a river she suspects of remembering things.
