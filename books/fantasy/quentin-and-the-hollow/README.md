# Quentin and the Hollow

*by Lior Whitlock*

> Ulyana Zhou, a thief who steals memories, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Lior Whitlock is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing letters no one ever claimed, an experience that left a lasting fascination with what people choose to remember. They write quiet, aching fantasy about ordinary people caught in the machinery of history.
