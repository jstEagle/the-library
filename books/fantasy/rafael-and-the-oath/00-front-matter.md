# Rafael and the Oath

**by Lena Okonkwo**

> Gideon Almeida, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Lena Okonkwo is a former archivist who grew up between Lagos and Lisbon, collecting funeral songs from both cultures. She writes fantasy about grief, bargains, and the families we build when blood fails us.
