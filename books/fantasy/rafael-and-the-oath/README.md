# Rafael and the Oath

*by Lena Okonkwo*

> Gideon Almeida, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
