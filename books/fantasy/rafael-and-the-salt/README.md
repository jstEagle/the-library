# Rafael and the Salt

*by August Galloway*

> Yara Eriksen, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
