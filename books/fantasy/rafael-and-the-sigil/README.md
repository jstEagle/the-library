# Rafael and the Sigil

*by Freya Joubert*

> Mirabel Dunmore, a debt-bound temple scribe, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
