# Rafael and the Spire

*by Silas Reyes*

> Beatrix Ravensworth, a thief who steals memories, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
