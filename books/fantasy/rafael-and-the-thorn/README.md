To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Rafael Nakamura is the pen name of a former museum conservator who spent a decade restoring funerary art before turning to fiction. They live near a river valley they refuse to name, and write about borders, grief, and the things people carry out of burning houses.
