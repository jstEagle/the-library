*by Theo Villalobos*

> Odile Quintana, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
