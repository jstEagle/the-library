# Rosalind and the Choir

*by R. M. Oyelaran*

> Rosalind Hartwell, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the long winter finishes falling.
