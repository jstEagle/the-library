# Rosalind and the Hollow

*by Wren Zhou*

> Isandro Lockridge, a thief who steals memories, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Wren Zhou is the pen name of a former archivist who spent six years cataloguing the personal effects of displaced families before turning to fiction. They write fantasy about records, ruins, and the people history forgets to count.
