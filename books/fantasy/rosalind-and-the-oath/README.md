# Rosalind and the Oath

*by Kestrel Kavanagh*

> Juniper Vasquez, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
