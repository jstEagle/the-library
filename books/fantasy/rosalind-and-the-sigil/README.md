# Rosalind and the Sigil

*by Beatrix Ashgrove*

> Xanthe Thackeray, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
