# Rosalind and the Spire

*by Ulyana Eastwick*

> Kestrel Crane, a bone-carver who hears the dead, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ulyana Eastwick is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing medieval guild ledgers before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about bureaucracy, grief, and the small magics hidden in paperwork.
