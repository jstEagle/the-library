# Rosalind and the Veil

*by Ulyana Voss*

> Juniper Ravensworth, a bone-carver who hears the dead, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ulyana Voss is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the belongings of families displaced by border redraws. She writes fantasy about what survives when countries don't, and lives near a coastline that has changed hands four times.
