# Rosalind and the Wolf

*by Ivo Fairweather*

> Hazel Oyelaran, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
