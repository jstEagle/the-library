# Sable and the Oath

*by Jude Dunmore*

> Sebastian Ashgrove, the youngest member of a dying guild, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
