# Sable and the Sigil

*by Jonah Dunmore*

> Isandro Iversen, a debt-bound temple scribe, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
