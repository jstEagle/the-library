# Sable and the Thorn

*by Marguerite Yarrow*

> Ada Okafor, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
