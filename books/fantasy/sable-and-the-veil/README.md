# Sable and the Veil

*by Ada Nakamura*

> Juniper Brightwater, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
