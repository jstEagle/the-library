## About the author

W. M. Joubert is the pen name of a former surveyor who spent a decade charting mountain passes before turning to fiction, and whose maps still hang in three border-town taverns under someone else's name. They write about borders, exile, and the stubborn geography of belonging from a cabin at the edge of an unnamed range.
