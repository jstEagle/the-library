# Sable and the Wolf

*by W. M. Joubert*

> Marguerite Whitlock, a cartographer of unmapped realms, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

W. M. Joubert is the pen name of a former surveyor who spent a decade charting mountain passes before turning to fiction, and whose maps still hang in three border-town taverns under someone else's name. They write about borders, exile, and the stubborn geography of belonging from a cabin at the edge of an unnamed range.
