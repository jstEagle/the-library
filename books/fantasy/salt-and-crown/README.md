# Salt and Crown

*by Ada Kavanagh*

> Petra Blackwood, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
