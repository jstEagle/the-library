## About the author

G. M. Delacroix is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging trade ledgers from defunct merchant houses before turning to fiction. They write fantasy about paperwork, memory, and the violence hidden in fine print, and live somewhere with unreliable weather.
