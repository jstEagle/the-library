# Salt and Ember

*by G. M. Delacroix*

> Hazel Pemberton, an exiled storm-caller, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
