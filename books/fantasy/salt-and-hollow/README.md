# Salt and Hollow

*by Bram Sterling*

> Marguerite Villalobos, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
