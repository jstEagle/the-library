# Salt and Oath

*by Dominic Ashgrove*

> Freya Almeida, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.
