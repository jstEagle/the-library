# Salt and Spire Again

*by Lior Thornbury*

> Ines Oyelaran, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Lior Thornbury is the pen name of a former mountain rescue volunteer who spent six winters mapping disused trade routes across three countries before turning to fiction. They write fantasy about borders, belonging, and the people who live in the gaps between laws.
