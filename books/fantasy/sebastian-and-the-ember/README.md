# Sebastian and the Ember

*by Lior Reyes*

> Wendell Ishikawa, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
