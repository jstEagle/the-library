# Sebastian and the Garden

*by Juniper Villalobos*

> Cyrus Amberdale, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Juniper Villalobos grew up between her grandmother's orchard in Oaxaca and a drafty flat in Edinburgh, a childhood split between two languages and two kinds of weather. She writes quiet fantasies about people who talk to things that cannot answer back.
