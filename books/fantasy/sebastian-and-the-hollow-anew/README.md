# Sebastian and the Hollow Anew

*by Coral Thornbury*

> Rosalind Marchetti, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
