# Sebastian and the Oath

*by Cyrus Ravensworth*

> Quentin Dravenmoor, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
