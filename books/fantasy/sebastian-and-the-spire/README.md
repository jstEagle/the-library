# Sebastian and the Spire

*by Beatrix Vane*

> Orsen Whitlock, a bone-carver who hears the dead, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.
