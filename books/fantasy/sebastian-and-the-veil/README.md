# Sebastian and the Veil

*by Odile Sandoval*

> Zephyrine Delacroix, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
