# Sebastian and the Wolf

*by Petra Zhou*

> Ines Okafor, a cartographer of unmapped realms, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.

## About the author
