# Sigil and Choir Again

*by Sebastian Hartwell*

> Astrid Montrose, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
