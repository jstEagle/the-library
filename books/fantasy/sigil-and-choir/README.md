# Sigil and Choir

*by Wren Reyes*

> Emrys Delacroix, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the long winter finishes falling.
