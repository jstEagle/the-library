# Sigil and Crown

*by Noor Zhou*

> Felix Ravensworth, a bone-carver who hears the dead, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
