# Sigil and Garden

*by Rafael Kavanagh*

> Edmund Ravensworth, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
