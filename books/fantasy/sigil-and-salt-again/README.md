# Sigil and Salt Again

*by Xanthe Yarrow*

> Astrid Lockridge, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
