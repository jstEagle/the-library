# Sigil and Salt Anew

*by Mirabel Thornbury*

> Yara Villalobos, an exiled storm-caller, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
