# Sigil and Salt

*by Ansel Sandoval*

> August Coldharbour, a cartographer of unmapped realms, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
