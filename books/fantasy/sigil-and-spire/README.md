# Sigil and Spire

*by Jonah Villalobos*

> Isandro Galloway, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
