# Sigil and Thorn

*by Lena Ravensworth*

> Kestrel Underhill, an exiled storm-caller, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the long winter finishes falling.
