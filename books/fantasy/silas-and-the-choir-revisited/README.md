# Silas and the Choir Revisited

*by Sebastian Delacroix*

> Isandro Nystrom, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
