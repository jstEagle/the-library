# Silas and the Choir

*by Jude Coldharbour*

> Ansel Dunmore, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
