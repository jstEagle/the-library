## About the author

Rosalind Winterbourne is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the possessions of estates with no surviving heirs, an experience she describes as 'learning what objects remember.' She writes fantasy about grief, ledgers, and the stubborn persistence of small kindnesses.
