# Silas and the Crown

*by Rosalind Winterbourne*

> Wendell Hartwell, a bone-carver who hears the dead, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Rosalind Winterbourne is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the possessions of estates with no surviving heirs, an experience she describes as 'learning what objects remember.' She writes fantasy about grief, ledgers, and the stubborn persistence of small kindnesses.
