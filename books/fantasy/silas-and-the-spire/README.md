# Silas and the Spire

*by O. M. Eriksen*

> Lena Delacroix, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.

## About the author

O. M. Eriksen is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing trade ledgers in a northern port city before turning to fiction. They write fantasy about paperwork, oaths, and the people who fall through the cracks of both.
