## About the author

Coral Dunmore is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging trade ledgers from defunct merchant guilds before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about people whose work keeps them invisible, and lives near a harbor where the fog rolls in on schedule.
