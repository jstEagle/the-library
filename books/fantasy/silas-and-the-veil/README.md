# Silas and the Veil

*by Dominic Yarrow*

> Rafael Okonkwo, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
