# Spire and Choir

*by Astrid Blackwood*

> Astrid Sterling, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Astrid Blackwood is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing dead languages before turning to fiction. They write fantasy about people whose pasts refuse to stay buried, from a farmhouse office overlooking a valley much quieter than any in their books.
