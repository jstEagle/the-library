# Spire and Ember Again

*by Orsen Okonkwo*

> Edmund Villalobos, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Orsen Okonkwo is a former cartographer who spent a decade mapping trade routes across three continents before turning to fiction. He writes fantasy about the people history forgets—the couriers, smugglers, and keepers of small roads.
