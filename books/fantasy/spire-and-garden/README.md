# Spire and Garden

*by Petra Brightwater*

> Wren Iversen, a debt-bound temple scribe, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
