# Spire and Oath

*by Ansel Halloran*

> Paloma Nakamura, a debt-bound temple scribe, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the long winter finishes falling.
