Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Paloma Whitlock is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing medieval debt ledgers before turning to fiction full-time. She writes fantasy about ordinary people caught in machinery far larger than themselves, from a farmhouse in the northern hills.
