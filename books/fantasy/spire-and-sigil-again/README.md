# Spire and Sigil Again

*by Odile Blackwood*

> Ada Vane, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
