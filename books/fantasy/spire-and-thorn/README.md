# Spire and Thorn

*by Ansel Ashgrove*

> Quentin Halloran, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.
