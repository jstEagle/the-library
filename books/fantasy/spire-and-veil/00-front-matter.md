## About the author

Paloma Thornbury is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing damaged manuscripts in northern university libraries, where the winters shaped her imagination as much as her reading did. She writes fantasy about people who survive things they should not have survived, and what survival costs them.
