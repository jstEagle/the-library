# Spire and Wolf

*by Tamsin Dunmore*

> Zephyrine Thornbury, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Tamsin Dunmore is the pen name of a former wildlife rehabilitator who spent a decade listening to animals she could not answer. She writes fantasy about outsiders, wild places, and the bargains we make to stay alive.
