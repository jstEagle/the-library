# Tamsin and the Ember

**by Yara Pemberton**

Yara Pemberton is a former archivist from the Cornish coast who writes fantasy about ordinary people caught in extraordinary bargains. Her short fiction has appeared in several small-press anthologies; 'Tamsin and the Ember' is her first novel.
