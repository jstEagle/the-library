# Tamsin and the Ember

*by Yara Pemberton*

> Rafael Ashgrove, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
