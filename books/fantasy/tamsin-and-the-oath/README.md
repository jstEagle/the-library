# Tamsin and the Oath

*by Silas Crane*

> Zephyrine Iversen, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
