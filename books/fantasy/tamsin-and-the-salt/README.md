# Tamsin and the Salt

*by Vesper Dravenmoor*

> Noor Hartwell, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
