# Tamsin and the Thorn

*by Wendell Amberdale*

> Bram Vasquez, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
