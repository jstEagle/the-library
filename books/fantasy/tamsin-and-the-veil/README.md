# Tamsin and the Veil

*by Tamsin Nystrom*

> Edmund Galloway, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Tamsin Nystrom grew up on a failing sheep farm above the fog line, where she claims she learned to talk to animals out of boredom rather than magic. She writes fantasy about quiet people forced into loud destinies, and still keeps bees that do not, as far as anyone can prove, answer back.
