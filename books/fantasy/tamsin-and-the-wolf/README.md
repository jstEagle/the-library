# Tamsin and the Wolf

*by Cyrus Fairweather*

> Gideon Crane, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
