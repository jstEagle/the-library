# The Broken Ember Again

*by August Sandoval*

> Casper Hartwell, a debt-bound temple scribe, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

August Sandoval is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the papers of defunct trading companies before turning to fiction. They write about bureaucracy as magic and magic as debt, and live somewhere with too many locked drawers.
