# The Broken Garden

*by Rafael Sandoval*

> Yara Ravensworth, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
