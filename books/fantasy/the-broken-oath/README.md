# The Broken Oath

*by Petra Hartwell*

> Ansel Voss, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Petra Hartwell is the pen name of a former cartographer's apprentice who grew up hiking borderlands and collecting local legends of places that no longer appear on maps. She writes fantasy about people who keep things—gates, secrets, promises—and what happens when keeping is no longer enough.
