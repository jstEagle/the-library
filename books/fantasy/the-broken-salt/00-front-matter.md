## About the author

Astrid Okonkwo is a former marine cartographer turned novelist who grew up between Lagos and Reykjavik, a childhood she credits for her obsession with borders, tides, and the stories nations tell about their edges. She writes fantasy about people caught between loyalties, and she still keeps an unlabeled map on her study wall that she refuses to explain.
