# The Broken Thorn

*by Xanthe Sterling*

> Ulyana Quintana, an exiled storm-caller, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
