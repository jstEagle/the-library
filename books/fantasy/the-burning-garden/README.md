*by Cyrus Reyes*

> Ines Pemberton, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
