# The Burning Hollow Revisited

*by Yara Whitlock*

> Noor Halloran, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Yara Whitlock is the pen name of a former archivist who spent seven years cataloguing medieval debt records before turning to fantasy. They write about ledgers, oaths, and the people trapped between them from a drafty house on the northern coast.
