# The Burning Oath Revisited

*by Oscar Marchetti*

> Juniper Halloran, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
