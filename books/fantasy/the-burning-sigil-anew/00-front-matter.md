# The Burning Sigil Anew

**by Rosalind Villalobos**

> Beatrix Thornbury, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option.
