# The Burning Sigil Anew

*by Rosalind Villalobos*

> Beatrix Thornbury, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
