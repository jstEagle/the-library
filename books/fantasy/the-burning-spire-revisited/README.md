# The Burning Spire Revisited

*by Sebastian Crane*

> Sable Villalobos, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
