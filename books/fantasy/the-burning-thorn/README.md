# The Burning Thorn

*by Marguerite Amberdale*

> Jonah Eriksen, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
