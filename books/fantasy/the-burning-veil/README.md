# The Burning Veil

*by W. M. Reyes*

> Freya Marchetti, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
