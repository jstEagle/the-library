# The Burning Wolf

*by Theo Vane*

> Felix Hartwell, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
