# The Choir of Ashvale

*by Oscar Joubert*

> Petra Winterbourne, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Oscar Joubert is the pen name of a former archivist who spent eleven years cataloguing sealed church records before turning to fiction, a debt his novels repay in full. He writes from a coastal town where the fog arrives on schedule, and claims he can hear choirs in it.
