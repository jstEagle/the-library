# The Choir of Highmoor

*by O. M. Whitlock*

> Edmund Reyes, a debt-bound temple scribe, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
