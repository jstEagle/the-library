# The Choir of the Glass Steppe Anew

*by Xanthe Vane*

> Freya Nystrom, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
