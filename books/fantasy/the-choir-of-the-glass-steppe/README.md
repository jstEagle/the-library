# The Choir of the Glass Steppe

*by T. M. Reyes*

> Silas Whitlock, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
