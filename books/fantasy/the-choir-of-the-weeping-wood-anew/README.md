To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Ansel Reyes is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing damaged manuscripts in coastal libraries, an obsession that bleeds into every ledger-bound corner of their fiction. They write fantasy about people who keep records of things that should never have been written down.
