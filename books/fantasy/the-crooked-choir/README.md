# The Crooked Choir

*by Mara Ashgrove*

> Isandro Okonkwo, a cartographer of unmapped realms, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
