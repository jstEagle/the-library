# The Crooked Hollow Anew

*by Coral Winterbourne*

> Mara Quintana, a debt-bound temple scribe, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Coral Winterbourne is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging documents no one was allowed to read. She writes fantasy about people who keep records of things the world would rather forget.
