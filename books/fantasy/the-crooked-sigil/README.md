# The Crooked Sigil

*by J. M. Joubert*

> Felix Galloway, the youngest member of a dying guild, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
