# The Crooked Spire

*by Mara Delacroix*

> Silas Voss, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
