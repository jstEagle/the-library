# The Crooked Thorn

*by Jonah Halloran*

> Odile Voss, a thief who steals memories, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Jonah Halloran is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing letters from families separated by borders that no longer appear on maps. They write fantasy about people who keep records of things the world would rather forget.
