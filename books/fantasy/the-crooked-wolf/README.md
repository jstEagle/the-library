# The Crooked Wolf

*by R. M. Eriksen*

> Marguerite Dravenmoor, a cartographer of unmapped realms, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

R. M. Eriksen is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging maps of places that no longer appear in any atlas. They write from a house built on the site of a demolished railway station, which they insist still smells faintly of coal.
