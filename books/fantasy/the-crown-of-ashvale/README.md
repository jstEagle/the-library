# The Crown of Ashvale

*by Yara Marchetti*

> Yara Montrose, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.
