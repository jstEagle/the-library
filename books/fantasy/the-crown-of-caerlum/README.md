# The Crown of Caerlum

*by Wendell Kavanagh*

> Ivo Ravensworth, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
