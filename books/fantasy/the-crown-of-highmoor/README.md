# The Crown of Highmoor

*by Wren Thornbury*

> Wren Quintana, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Wren Thornbury grew up in a mining town where three dialects collided in every kitchen, and she credits her ear for untranslatable words to her grandmother's bedtime curses. She writes fantasy about people who belong to borders rather than kingdoms, and this is her second novel.
