# The Crown of Ironhollow

*by Jonah Nakamura*

> Lior Blackwood, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
