# The Crown of the Glass Steppe

*by Zephyrine Almeida*

> August Okafor, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Zephyrine Almeida is the pen name of a former cartographer who once mapped river deltas on three continents before turning to maps of imaginary places instead. She writes in a converted lighthouse, fueled by black tea and an abiding suspicion that every border is a story someone agreed to stop telling.
