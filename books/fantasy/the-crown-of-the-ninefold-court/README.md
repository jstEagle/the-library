# The Crown of the Ninefold Court

*by Mara Brightwater*

> Jonah Galloway, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Mara Brightwater is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing medieval charm-books before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about faith, bureaucracy, and the people caught between them from a rain-soaked coastal town.
