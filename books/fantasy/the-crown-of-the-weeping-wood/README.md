# The Crown of the Weeping Wood

*by S. M. Coldharbour*

> Felix Galloway, a thief who steals memories, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
