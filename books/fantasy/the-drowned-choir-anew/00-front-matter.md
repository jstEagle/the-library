## About the author

Orsen Marchetti is the pen name of a former river-ferry archivist who spent a decade cataloguing flood-damaged parish records along the northern coasts, an obsession that seeps into every drowned cathedral in their fiction. They write at dawn in a converted boathouse and claim their best plot ideas arrive as birdsong.
