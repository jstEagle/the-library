# The Drowned Choir

*by R. M. Winterbourne*

> Wendell Amberdale, a bone-carver who hears the dead, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
