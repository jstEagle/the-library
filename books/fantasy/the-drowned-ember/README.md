# The Drowned Ember

*by Vesper Lockridge*

> Paloma Halloran, the youngest member of a dying guild, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
