# The Drowned Garden

*by Mara Galloway*

> Tamsin Thornbury, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Mara Galloway is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing merchant ledgers in coastal port towns before turning to fiction. She writes fantasies about bookkeepers, smugglers, and the quiet violence of paperwork.
