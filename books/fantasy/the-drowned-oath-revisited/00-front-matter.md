## About the author

Rosalind Montrose is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging maritime trade records before turning to fiction, a past that seeps into her obsession with ledgers, oaths, and things lost to deep water. She writes fantasy about quiet people forced into loud destinies from a coastal cottage she shares with too many cats and one very opinionated heron.
