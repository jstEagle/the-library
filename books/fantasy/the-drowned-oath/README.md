# The Drowned Oath

*by Y. M. Montrose*

> Cyrus Almeida, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
