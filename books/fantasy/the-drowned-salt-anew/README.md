# The Drowned Salt Anew

*by Silas Voss*

> Bram Kavanagh, an exiled storm-caller, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
