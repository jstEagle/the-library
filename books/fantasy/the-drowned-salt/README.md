# The Drowned Salt

*by Elodie Joubert*

> Lior Oyelaran, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Elodie Joubert is the pen name of a former cartographer who spent five years mapping cave systems in the Pyrenees before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about borders, bargains, and the people who live in the gaps between them.
