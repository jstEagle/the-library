# The Drowned Spire

**by Paloma Crane**

> Wren Thornbury, a debt-bound temple scribe, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Paloma Crane is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing maritime folklore along the northern coasts. The Drowned Spire is their second novel, following the acclaimed novella Salt for the Ferryman.
