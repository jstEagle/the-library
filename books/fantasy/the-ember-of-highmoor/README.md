# The Ember of Highmoor

*by Y. M. Ashgrove*

> Zephyrine Whitlock, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
