# The Ember of the Glass Steppe

*by Wren Winterbourne*

> Ansel Quintana, a debt-bound temple scribe, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
