# The Forgotten Choir Again

*by Ivo Galloway*

> Noor Sandoval, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
