## About the author

Rosalind Galloway is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing medieval maps before turning to fiction, and whose love of marginalia and half-erased place names infuses every page of her work. She writes fantasy about people who carry too much history in too small a body, and lives somewhere deliberately unmapped.
