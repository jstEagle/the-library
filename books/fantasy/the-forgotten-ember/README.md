# The Forgotten Ember

*by Marguerite Dunmore*

> Kestrel Fairweather, a thief who steals memories, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Marguerite Dunmore is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing letters from people erased from official records, an obsession that bleeds into every page she writes. She lives in a drafty coastal house with too many candles and a firm belief that the best magic stories are about what we refuse to forget.
