# The Forgotten Hollow

*by Hazel Yarrow*

> Quentin Vasquez, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
