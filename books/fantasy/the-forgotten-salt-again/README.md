# The Forgotten Salt Again

*by R. M. Winterbourne*

> Zephyrine Ravensworth, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
