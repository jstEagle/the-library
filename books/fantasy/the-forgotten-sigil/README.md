# The Forgotten Sigil

*by Jonah Marchetti*

> Noor Nystrom, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
