# The Forgotten Thorn

*by Noor Reyes*

> Ada Almeida, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
