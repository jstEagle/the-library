# The Forgotten Veil

*by Lior Villalobos*

> Oscar Galloway, an exiled storm-caller, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
