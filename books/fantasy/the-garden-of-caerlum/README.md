# The Garden of Caerlum

*by F. M. Dravenmoor*

> Quentin Joubert, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the long winter finishes falling.
