# The Garden of Highmoor

*by Ivo Zhou*

> Casper Hartwell, a cartographer of unmapped realms, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the long winter finishes falling.
