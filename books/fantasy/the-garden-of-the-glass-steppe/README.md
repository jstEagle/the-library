# The Garden of the Glass Steppe

*by Bram Montrose*

> Bram Joubert, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
