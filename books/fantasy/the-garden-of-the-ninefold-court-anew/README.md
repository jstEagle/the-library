# The Garden of the Ninefold Court Anew

*by Beatrix Underhill*

> Sable Pemberton, a thief who steals memories, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
