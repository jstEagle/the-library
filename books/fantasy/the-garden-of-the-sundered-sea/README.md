# The Garden of the Sundered Sea

*by Xanthe Sandoval*

> Lena Reyes, a thief who steals memories, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
