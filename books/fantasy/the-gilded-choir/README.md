# The Gilded Choir

*by Bram Winterbourne*

> Paloma Thackeray, a debt-bound temple scribe, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Bram Winterbourne is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing medieval choir manuscripts before turning to fantasy. They write about debt, devotion, and the people history forgets to record.
