# The Gilded Garden Anew

*by Edmund Crane*

> Jonah Whitlock, an exiled storm-caller, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
