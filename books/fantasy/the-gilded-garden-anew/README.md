# The Gilded Garden Anew

*by Petra Blackwood*

> Noor Fairweather, the youngest member of a dying guild, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
