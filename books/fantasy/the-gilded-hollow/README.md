# The Gilded Hollow

*by Wendell Whitlock*

> Jude Dravenmoor, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.
