## About the author

Ansel Thornbury is the pen name of a former archivist who spent eleven years cataloguing medieval debt records before turning to fiction, a career that left him convinced every ledger is a ghost story. He writes fantasy about bureaucracy, faith, and the people who keep the world's impossible accounts balanced.
