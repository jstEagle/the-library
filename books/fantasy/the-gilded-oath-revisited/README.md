# The Gilded Oath Revisited

*by Ansel Thornbury*

> Ada Coldharbour, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Ansel Thornbury is the pen name of a former archivist who spent eleven years cataloguing medieval debt records before turning to fiction, a career that left him convinced every ledger is a ghost story. He writes fantasy about bureaucracy, faith, and the people who keep the world's impossible accounts balanced.
