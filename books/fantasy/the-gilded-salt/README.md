# The Gilded Salt

*by M. M. Winterbourne*

> Sebastian Kavanagh, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
