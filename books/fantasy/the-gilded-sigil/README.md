# The Gilded Sigil

*by Theo Thackeray*

> Beatrix Zhou, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
