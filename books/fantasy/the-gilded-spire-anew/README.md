## About the author

Xanthe Ravensworth is the pen name of a former trail surveyor who spent six years mapping mountain passes before turning to fiction, and whose love of half-finished maps bleeds into every story. They write fantasy about people who find doors where others see only walls, and they still carry a compass everywhere.
