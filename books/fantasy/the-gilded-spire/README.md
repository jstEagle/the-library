# The Gilded Spire

*by Quentin Whitlock*

> Mirabel Halloran, a debt-bound temple scribe, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Quentin Whitlock is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing medieval debt records and never quite recovered. He writes fantasy about people crushed by institutions, and lives in a drafty house full of unlabeled maps.
