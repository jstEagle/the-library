# The Gilded Thorn

*by Oscar Blackwood*

> Elodie Oyelaran, a thief who steals memories, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
