# the Glass Steppe Rising Anew

*by Beatrix Zhou*

> Rosalind Whitlock, an exiled storm-caller, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
