# The Hollow Crown

*by W. M. Blackwood*

> Isandro Yarrow, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author
