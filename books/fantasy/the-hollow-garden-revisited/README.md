# The Hollow Garden Revisited

*by Ansel Ravensworth*

> August Thackeray, an exiled storm-caller, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
