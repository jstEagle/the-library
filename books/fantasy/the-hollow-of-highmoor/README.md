# The Hollow of Highmoor

*by L. M. Dravenmoor*

> Ada Sterling, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
