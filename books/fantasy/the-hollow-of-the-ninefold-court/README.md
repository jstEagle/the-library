# The Hollow of the Ninefold Court

*by Theo Quintana*

> Wren Sterling, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Theo Quintana is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing debt records from vanished merchant houses, an experience that left a permanent fascination with what people will trade away. They write fantasy about small people inside large machinery, and live somewhere with too many stairs.
