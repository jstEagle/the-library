# The Hollow of the Sundered Sea

*by Ivo Almeida*

> Bram Brightwater, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the long winter finishes falling.
