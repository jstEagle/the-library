# The Hollow Spire Again

**by Petra Eriksen**

> Emrys Sandoval, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option.
