## About the author

Orsen Voss is the pen name of a former stonemason who spent a decade restoring cathedral gargoyles before turning to fiction. They write fantasy about laborers, exiles, and the stubborn afterlives of forgotten places from a farmhouse at the edge of a mountain range they refuse to name.
