# The Hollow Thorn

*by Petra Nystrom*

> Yara Thornbury, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
