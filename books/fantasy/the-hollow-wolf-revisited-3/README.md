# The Hollow Wolf Revisited

*by Wren Marchetti*

> Silas Sandoval, a debt-bound temple scribe, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
