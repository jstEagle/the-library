# The Last Choir Again

*by Casper Brightwater*

> Silas Nystrom, a cartographer of unmapped realms, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Casper Brightwater is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing medieval choir manuscripts before turning to fantasy. They write stories about people who map the impossible and the families they find along the way.
