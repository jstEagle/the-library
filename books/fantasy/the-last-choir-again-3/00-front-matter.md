## About the author

Edmund Winterbourne is the pen name of a former cartographer who spent a decade mapping mountain passes across three continents before turning to fiction. He writes fantasy obsessed with borders—geographic, moral, and mortal—from a farmhouse whose cellar allegedly connects to no smuggler's road whatsoever.
