# The Last Choir Again

*by Jude Zhou*

> August Okafor, the youngest member of a dying guild, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
