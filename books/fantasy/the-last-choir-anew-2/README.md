# The Last Choir Anew

*by Mirabel Iversen*

> Isandro Winterbourne, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
