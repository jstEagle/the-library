# The Last Choir Anew

*by L. M. Sandoval*

> Isandro Vane, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
