# The Last Crown Anew

**by Theo Nakamura**

> Lena Zhou, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Theo Nakamura is the pen name of a former archaeologist who spent a decade cataloguing dead languages across three continents before turning to fiction. They write fantasy about people caught between faiths they've outgrown and homes they can't return to, and live in a coastal city that inspires their fog-drowned ports.
