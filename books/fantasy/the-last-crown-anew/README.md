# The Last Crown Anew

*by Juniper Brightwater*

> Emrys Fairweather, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
