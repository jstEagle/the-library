# The Last Crown Revisited

*by Ada Okafor*

> Theo Dunmore, a debt-bound temple scribe, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Ada Okafor is a former archivist who spent six years cataloguing colonial-era maps before turning to fiction full-time. She writes fantasy about debts, borders, and the people who slip through both, and lives at the edge of a mountain range she refuses to name.
