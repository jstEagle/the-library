# The Last Crown Revisited

*by Sable Joubert*

> Orsen Zhou, a thief who steals memories, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Sable Joubert is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters from displaced communities before turning to fantasy. They write about memory, borders, and the families we build when the old ones fail us, from a small house at the edge of a large forest.
