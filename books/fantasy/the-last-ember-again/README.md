# The Last Ember Again

*by Zephyrine Montrose*

> Paloma Ashgrove, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Zephyrine Montrose is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing guild records in mountain towns before turning to fiction. She writes fantasies about paperwork, oaths, and the people who fall through the cracks of both.
