# The Last Ember Anew

*by Hazel Sandoval*

> Jude Ashgrove, an exiled storm-caller, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Hazel Sandoval is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging damaged manuscripts in coastal libraries, where salt air and half-legible marginalia taught her that every record is also an elegy. She writes fantasy about people who keep accounts with forces larger than themselves.
