## About the author

Gideon Zhou is a former line cook who spent six years working in hotel kitchens before turning to fantasy, a background that infuses his fiction with an obsessive attention to food, ritual, and the quiet violence of service. He lives near a river valley and writes at night, claiming the dark helps him hear old languages better.
