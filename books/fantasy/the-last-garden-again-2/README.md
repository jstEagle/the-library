# The Last Garden Again

*by Ulyana Brightwater*

> Mara Villalobos, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Ulyana Brightwater is the pen name of a former field botanist who spent a decade cataloguing endangered plants before turning to fantasy full-time. Her work is known for landscapes that behave like characters and for quiet, devastating choices made in gardens.
