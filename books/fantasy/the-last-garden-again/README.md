# The Last Garden Again

*by P. M. Hartwell*

> Quentin Ravensworth, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author
