# The Last Garden Anew

*by Freya Amberdale*

> Noor Iversen, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Freya Amberdale is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing medieval oath-scrolls before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about people bound by promises they never chose, from a farmhouse full of rescued orchard trees.
