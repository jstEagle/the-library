## About the author

Ansel Delacroix is the pen name of a former botanist who abandoned a decade of greenhouse research to write fantasy about gardens, memory, and the things that grow in the dark. They live in a drafty farmhouse with too many cats and a stubborn wisteria that refuses, much like their protagonists, to stay where it's planted.
