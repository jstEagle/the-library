# The Last Oath Again

*by Odile Okonkwo*

> Mirabel Oyelaran, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Odile Okonkwo is a Nigerian-British writer who grew up between Lagos and Manchester, collecting folktales from both grandmothers before learning to write her own. A former archivist, she writes fantasy about people history tried to forget.
