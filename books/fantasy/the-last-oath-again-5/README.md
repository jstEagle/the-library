*by Wren Galloway*

> Emrys Winterbourne, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Wren Galloway is the pen name of a former trail guide who spent six seasons leading expeditions through mountain passes before turning to fiction full-time. Their work blends folkloric fantasy with quiet character studies, and The Last Oath Again is their third novel.
