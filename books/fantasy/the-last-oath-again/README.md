# The Last Oath Again

*by Zephyrine Galloway*

> Ada Vane, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
