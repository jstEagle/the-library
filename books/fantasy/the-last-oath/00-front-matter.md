# The Last Oath

**by Tamsin Montrose**

> Ulyana Kavanagh, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Tamsin Montrose is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing confiscated grimoires before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about debts, oaths, and the people history forgets to record.
