# The Last Salt

*by Rosalind Coldharbour*

> Emrys Winterbourne, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
