# The Last Sigil Again

*by Ada Crane*

> Astrid Whitlock, an exiled storm-caller, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
