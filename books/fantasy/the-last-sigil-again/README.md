# The Last Sigil Again

*by August Delacroix*

> Odile Eriksen, an exiled storm-caller, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
