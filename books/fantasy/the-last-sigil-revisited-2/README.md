Now they must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.

Y. M. Zhou is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing family letters and estate records, an experience that seeded a lifelong fascination with what we inherit and what we forget. They write quiet, storm-soaked fantasy about outsiders rebuilding the bonds that exile broke.
