# The Last Sigil Revisited

*by B. M. Blackwood*

> Paloma Hartwell, a debt-bound temple scribe, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

B. M. Blackwood is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing condemned manuscripts before turning to fiction. They write fantasy about people trapped by systems—debt, law, memory—and the small illegal mercies that free them.
