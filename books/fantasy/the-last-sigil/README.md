# The Last Sigil

*by Coral Dunmore*

> Wendell Vane, a thief who steals memories, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Coral Dunmore is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters from people who vanished from official records. She writes about memory, borders, and the families we build from strangers.
