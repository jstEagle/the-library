# The Last Spire Again

*by Wren Iversen*

> Yara Lockridge, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
