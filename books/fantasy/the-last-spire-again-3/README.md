# The Last Spire Again

*by Felix Dunmore*

> Odile Blackwood, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Felix Dunmore is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing extinct languages before turning to fiction. They write fantasy about people outliving their own histories, and live quietly near a coastline that appears on no old maps.
