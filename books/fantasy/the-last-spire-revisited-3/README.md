# The Last Spire Revisited

*by Marguerite Montrose*

> Rafael Eriksen, a debt-bound temple scribe, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
