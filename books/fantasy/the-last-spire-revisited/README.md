# The Last Spire Revisited

*by Ines Blackwood*

> Wendell Kavanagh, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
