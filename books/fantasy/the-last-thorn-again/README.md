# The Last Thorn Again

*by Xanthe Halloran*

> Cyrus Dravenmoor, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Xanthe Halloran is the pen name of a former trail guide who spent six seasons mapping passes through real mountain ranges before turning them into fictional ones. Their work blends folkloric fantasy with intimate, unsentimental character studies of people who survive by being overlooked.
