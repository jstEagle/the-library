# The Last Veil

*by Kestrel Voss*

> Isandro Vasquez, an exiled storm-caller, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the long winter finishes falling.

Kestrel Voss is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing confiscated letters in a coastal city's forgotten records wing. They write fantasy about people the histories tried to erase, and they still cannot sleep through thunderstorms.
