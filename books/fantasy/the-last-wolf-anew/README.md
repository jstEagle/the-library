# The Last Wolf Anew

*by Isandro Marchetti*

> Paloma Marchetti, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
