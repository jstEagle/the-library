# The Midnight Crown Again

*by Wendell Lockridge*

> Ulyana Pemberton, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
