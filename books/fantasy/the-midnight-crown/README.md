*by Hazel Quintana*

> Dominic Almeida, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
