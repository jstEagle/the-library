# The Midnight Oath Revisited

*by W. M. Reyes*

> August Montrose, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
