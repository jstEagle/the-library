# The Midnight Salt

*by Tamsin Oyelaran*

> Juniper Brightwater, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
