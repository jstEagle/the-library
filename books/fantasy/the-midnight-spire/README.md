# The Midnight Spire

*by Noor Iversen*

> Oscar Voss, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
