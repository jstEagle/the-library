# The Midnight Veil Anew

*by Juniper Vasquez*

> Juniper Reyes, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.
