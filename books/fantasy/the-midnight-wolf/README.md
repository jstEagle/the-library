# The Midnight Wolf

*by Ada Dunmore*

> Marguerite Whitlock, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the long winter finishes falling.
