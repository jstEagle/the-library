# the Ninefold Court Rising Again

*by Paloma Joubert*

> Oscar Underhill, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
