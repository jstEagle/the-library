# the Ninefold Court Rising Again

*by Cyrus Underhill*

> Tamsin Villalobos, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
