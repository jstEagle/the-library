# the Ninefold Court Rising Again

*by Ulyana Ravensworth*

> Odile Eastwick, a thief who steals memories, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Ulyana Ravensworth is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing broken legal covenants before turning to fantasy, and it shows in every treaty, oath, and fine print her characters must survive. She writes in a draughty house full of maps and claims her cats are her first readers.
