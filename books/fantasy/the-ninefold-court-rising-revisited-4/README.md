# the Ninefold Court Rising Revisited

*by Yara Thackeray*

> Freya Galloway, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
