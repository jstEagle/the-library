# the Ninefold Court Rising

*by Yara Sterling*

> Theo Dunmore, a thief who steals memories, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author
