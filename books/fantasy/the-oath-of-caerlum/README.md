# The Oath of Caerlum

*by A. M. Iversen*

> Petra Hartwell, the youngest member of a dying guild, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

A. M. Iversen is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the paperwork of defunct institutions before turning to fiction. They write about record-keepers, small loyalties, and the strange weight of things that refuse to be forgotten, and live somewhere with long winters.
