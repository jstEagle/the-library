# The Oath of Highmoor

*by Rosalind Ravensworth*

> Bram Halloran, a debt-bound temple scribe, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Rosalind Ravensworth spent a decade cataloguing marginalia in mountain monasteries before turning to fiction, and her prose carries the dust and ink of that life. She writes fantasy about people the histories forgot, from a stone cottage she refuses to locate on any map.
