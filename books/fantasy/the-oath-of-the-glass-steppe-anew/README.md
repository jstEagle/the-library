# The Oath of the Glass Steppe Anew

*by Freya Quintana*

> Emrys Almeida, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
