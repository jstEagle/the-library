# The Oath of the Ninefold Court

*by Emrys Yarrow*

> Jonah Kavanagh, a debt-bound temple scribe, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
