## About the author

Ines Eriksen is a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the logbooks of ships that never came home, a habit that seeps into everything she writes. She lives on a cold northern coast, where she writes fantasy about oaths, drowned places, and the people who keep the books.
