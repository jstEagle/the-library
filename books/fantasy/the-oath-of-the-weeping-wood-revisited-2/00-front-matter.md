## About the author

E. M. Villalobos is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing broken contracts and unfulfilled wills in a coastal provincial records office. They write fantasy about people who keep accounts—of money, of promises, of wounds—and live somewhere damp with two cats named after failed treaties.
