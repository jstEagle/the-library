# The Oath of the Weeping Wood

*by Ansel Pemberton*

> Felix Fairweather, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
