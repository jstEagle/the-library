# The Salt of Ashvale

*by V. M. Kavanagh*

> Emrys Delacroix, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
