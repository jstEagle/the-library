# The Salt of Highmoor

*by Wren Kavanagh*

> Vesper Fairweather, the youngest member of a dying guild, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
