# The Salt of the Glass Steppe

*by Wren Okonkwo*

> Silas Kavanagh, a thief who steals memories, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
