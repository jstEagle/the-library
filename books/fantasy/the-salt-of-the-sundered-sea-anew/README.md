# The Salt of the Sundered Sea Anew

*by Ines Eriksen*

> Xanthe Oyelaran, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
