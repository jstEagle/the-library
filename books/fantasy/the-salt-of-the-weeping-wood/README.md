# The Salt of the Weeping Wood

*by L. M. Ashgrove*

> Paloma Okonkwo, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
