# The Sigil of Ashvale

*by J. M. Ashgrove*

> Wren Joubert, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
