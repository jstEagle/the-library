# The Sigil of Caerlum

*by Mirabel Amberdale*

> Wendell Yarrow, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
