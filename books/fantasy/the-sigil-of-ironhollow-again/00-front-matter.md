## About the author

Marguerite Hartwell is a former cathedral archivist who spent a decade cataloguing funeral records before turning to fiction, a habit that still haunts her prose. She writes in a converted bell tower in the north of England, where she keeps an extensive collection of carved animal bones and refuses, politely, to explain why.
