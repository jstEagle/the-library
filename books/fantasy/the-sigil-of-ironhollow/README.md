# The Sigil of Ironhollow

*by Kestrel Sterling*

> Wren Brightwater, an exiled storm-caller, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
