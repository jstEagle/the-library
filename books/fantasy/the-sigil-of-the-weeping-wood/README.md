# The Sigil of the Weeping Wood

*by Jonah Halloran*

> Isandro Coldharbour, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
