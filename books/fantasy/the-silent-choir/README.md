# The Silent Choir

*by I. M. Voss*

> Rosalind Winterbourne, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
