# The Silent Crown Anew

*by Mirabel Vasquez*

> Ada Villalobos, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
