# The Silent Crown

*by Isandro Iversen*

> Jude Zhou, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the long winter finishes falling.
