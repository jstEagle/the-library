## About the author

Gideon Dunmore is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing funerary carvings in remote parish crypts, an experience that seeps into every bone-carved detail of his fiction. He writes fantasy about quiet people dragged into loud histories, and claims he has never once left a draft unfinished — only unburied.
