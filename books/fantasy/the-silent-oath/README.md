# The Silent Oath

*by Rosalind Eriksen*

> Astrid Oyelaran, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
