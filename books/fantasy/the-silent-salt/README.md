# The Silent Salt

*by Ines Vane*

> Freya Almeida, an exiled storm-caller, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
