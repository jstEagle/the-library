# The Silent Sigil

*by Isandro Eastwick*

> Quentin Villalobos, a bone-carver who hears the dead, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
