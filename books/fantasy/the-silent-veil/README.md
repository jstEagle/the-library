# The Silent Veil

*by Casper Vasquez*

> Zephyrine Eriksen, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
