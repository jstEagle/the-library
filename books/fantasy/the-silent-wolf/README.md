# The Silent Wolf

*by Rosalind Ishikawa*

> Rafael Marchetti, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
