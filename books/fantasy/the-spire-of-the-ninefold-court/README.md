# The Spire of the Ninefold Court

*by Ada Amberdale*

> Mara Winterbourne, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
