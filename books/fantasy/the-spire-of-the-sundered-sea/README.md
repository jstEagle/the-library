# The Spire of the Sundered Sea

*by Coral Halloran*

> Tamsin Vane, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Coral Halloran is a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing drowned-town records along a fog-bound coast before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about stubborn people, unreliable maps, and the families we assemble from shipwreck.
