# the Sundered Sea Rising Anew

*by Ada Crane*

> Kestrel Sterling, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
