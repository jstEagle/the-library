# The Thorn of Ashvale Anew

*by Felix Underhill*

> Isandro Delacroix, the youngest member of a dying guild, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

Felix Underhill is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing broken oaths in municipal records before turning to fantasy. They write in a converted chapel in the north of England, where they keep a garden that stubbornly refuses to grow anything but thorns.
