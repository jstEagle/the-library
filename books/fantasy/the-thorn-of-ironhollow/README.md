# The Thorn of Ironhollow

*by Wendell Zhou*

> Petra Okafor, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
