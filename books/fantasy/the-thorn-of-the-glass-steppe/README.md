# The Thorn of the Glass Steppe

*by X. M. Lockridge*

> Edmund Almeida, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
