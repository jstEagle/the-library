# The Thorn of the Ninefold Court Again

*by Freya Vasquez*

> Wren Voss, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
