# The Thorn of the Ninefold Court Again

*by August Nakamura*

> August Blackwood, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
