# The Thorn of the Sundered Sea

*by Rosalind Iversen*

> Tamsin Reyes, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.
