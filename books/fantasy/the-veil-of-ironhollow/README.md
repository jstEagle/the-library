# The Veil of Ironhollow

*by Silas Pemberton*

> August Villalobos, an exiled storm-caller, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
