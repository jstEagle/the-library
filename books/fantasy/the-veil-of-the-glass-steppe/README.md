# The Veil of the Glass Steppe

*by Ada Joubert*

> Sebastian Winterbourne, the youngest member of a dying guild, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ada Joubert is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing border-town records in three languages before turning to fiction. She writes about places that exist between official maps and the people who refuse to be forgotten.
