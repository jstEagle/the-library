# The Veil of the Sundered Sea

**by Casper Okonkwo**

> August Hartwell, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Casper Okonkwo is a former maritime archivist who writes fantasy rooted in coastal folklore and the quiet violence of oaths. The Veil of the Sundered Sea is his third novel and the first set in the world of the Sundered Sea.
