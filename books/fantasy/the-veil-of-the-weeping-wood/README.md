# The Veil of the Weeping Wood

*by E. M. Voss*

> Zephyrine Joubert, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
