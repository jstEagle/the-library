## About the author

Petra Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing estate ledgers and probate records, an obsession that seeps into her fiction's fascination with debts, names, and what can legally be owned. She writes from a rain-dark coastal town, where she claims the fog does most of her drafting.
