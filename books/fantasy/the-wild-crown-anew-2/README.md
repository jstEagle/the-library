# The Wild Crown Anew

*by Sebastian Blackwood*

> Yara Villalobos, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
