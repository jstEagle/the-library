# The Wild Crown

*by J. M. Thornbury*

> Jude Fairweather, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
