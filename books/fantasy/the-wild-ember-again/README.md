# The Wild Ember Again

*by Kestrel Delacroix*

> Emrys Quintana, an exiled storm-caller, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
