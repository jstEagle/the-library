# The Wild Ember

*by Jonah Ishikawa*

> Isandro Okafor, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
