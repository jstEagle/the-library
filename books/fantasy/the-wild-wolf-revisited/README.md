# The Wild Wolf Revisited

*by Theo Joubert*

> Coral Blackwood, a debt-bound temple scribe, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
