# The Wolf of Ashvale Revisited

*by Ines Lockridge*

> Emrys Nystrom, a thief who steals memories, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
