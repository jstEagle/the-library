# The Wolf of Ashvale

*by Elodie Winterbourne*

> Beatrix Blackwood, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.
