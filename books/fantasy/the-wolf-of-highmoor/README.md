> Marguerite Delacroix, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
