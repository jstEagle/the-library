# The Wolf of Ironhollow

*by Kestrel Montrose*

> Felix Oyelaran, a debt-bound temple scribe, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Kestrel Montrose is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing medieval prayer books before turning to fantasy. They write about debt, faith, and the families we build when the ones we're given fail us.
