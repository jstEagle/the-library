*by Orsen Oyelaran*

Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Orsen Oyelaran is a former church archivist from Lagos who spent a decade cataloguing hymnals in three dead languages before turning to fiction. He writes fantasy about what communities choose to remember, and lives quietly with a parrot that refuses to learn any word older than itself.
