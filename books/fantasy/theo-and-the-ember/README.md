# Theo and the Ember

*by Hazel Galloway*

> Felix Zhou, a thief who steals memories, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the long winter finishes falling.

Hazel Galloway is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing folk charms and now writes fantasy about people caught between systems larger than themselves. She lives near a cold coast and insists the long winter in her books is entirely fictional.
