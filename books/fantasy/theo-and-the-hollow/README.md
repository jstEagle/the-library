# Theo and the Hollow

*by Mirabel Yarrow*

> Yara Coldharbour, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Mirabel Yarrow is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing medieval cookery manuscripts before turning to fantasy. She writes in a drafty attic full of mismatched teacups and insists every story should smell faintly of bread.
