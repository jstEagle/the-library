# Theo and the Salt

*by Kestrel Blackwood*

> Ada Joubert, a cartographer of unmapped realms, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Kestrel Blackwood is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent years cataloguing charts of seas that no longer exist. They write fantasy about the violence hidden in old paper and the people who keep records no one is meant to read.
