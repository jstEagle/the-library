# Thorn and Crown

*by Odile Ravensworth*

> Quentin Pemberton, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
