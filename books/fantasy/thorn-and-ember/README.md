# Thorn and Ember

*by P. M. Iversen*

> Freya Almeida, a bone-carver who hears the dead, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
