# Thorn and Oath

*by Silas Ravensworth*

> Noor Brightwater, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
