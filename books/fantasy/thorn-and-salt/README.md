# Thorn and Salt

*by O. M. Okonkwo*

> Freya Vane, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
