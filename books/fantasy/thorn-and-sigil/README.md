# Thorn and Sigil

*by Juniper Lockridge*

> Astrid Vasquez, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
