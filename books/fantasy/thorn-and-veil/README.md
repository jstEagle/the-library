# Thorn and Veil

*by August Halloran*

> Yara Dunmore, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

August Halloran is the pen name of a former surveyor who spent a decade drawing boundaries for land he never owned. Thorn and Veil is his second novel, written in the margins of old maps he refuses to sell.
