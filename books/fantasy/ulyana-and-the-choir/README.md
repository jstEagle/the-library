# Ulyana and the Choir

*by Beatrix Crane*

> Tamsin Zhou, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Beatrix Crane is the pen name of a former choir singer turned archivist who spent a decade cataloguing folk hymns in mountain villages before turning to fantasy. She writes about voices, vows, and the families we build in the margins of empires.
