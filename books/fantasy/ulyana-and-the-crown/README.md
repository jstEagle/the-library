# Ulyana and the Crown

*by Quentin Nakamura*

> Silas Fairweather, an exiled storm-caller, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
