# Ulyana and the Salt

*by Vesper Blackwood*

> Coral Brightwater, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
