# Ulyana and the Thorn

*by Beatrix Blackwood*

> Vesper Amberdale, a cartographer of unmapped realms, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
