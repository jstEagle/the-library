# Ulyana and the Veil

*by Paloma Nystrom*

> Ulyana Sterling, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Paloma Nystrom is the pen name of a former cartographer who spent six years mapping mountain passes across northern Scandinavia before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about borders, both drawn on maps and drawn in blood, from a cabin she refuses to geotag.
