# Ulyana and the Wolf

*by Vesper Okafor*

> Paloma Okonkwo, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
