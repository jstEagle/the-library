# Veil and Crown

*by Edmund Okafor*

> Jonah Thornbury, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Edmund Okafor is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the legal fictions by which vanished countries are declared to still exist. He writes fantasy about people trapped inside other people's paperwork.
