# Veil and Garden Again

*by Noor Amberdale*

> Kestrel Thackeray, a debt-bound temple scribe, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
