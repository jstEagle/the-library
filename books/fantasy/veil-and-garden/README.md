# Veil and Garden

*by Ivo Dravenmoor*

> Lior Oyelaran, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
