# Veil and Salt

*by Vesper Galloway*

> Edmund Coldharbour, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
