# Veil and Sigil

*by Paloma Voss*

> Astrid Joubert, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
