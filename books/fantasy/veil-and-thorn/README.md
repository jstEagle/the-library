# Veil and Thorn

*by Isandro Sandoval*

> Ada Joubert, a cartographer of unmapped realms, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Isandro Sandoval is a former trail surveyor who spent a decade mapping passes through remote mountain ranges before turning to fiction. He writes fantasy rooted in the texture of landscapes, believing every border on a map hides a story someone died to keep off it.
