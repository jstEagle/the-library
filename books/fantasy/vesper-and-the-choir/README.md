But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Zephyrine Vane is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the belongings of families displaced by border redraws, an experience that haunts every page of this debut. They live in a house full of unlabeled maps and insist they can still hear the previous owners.
