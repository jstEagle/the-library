# Vesper and the Garden

*by Wren Reyes*

> Tamsin Halloran, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
