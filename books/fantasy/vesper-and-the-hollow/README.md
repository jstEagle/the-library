# Vesper and the Hollow

*by Bram Underhill*

> Ines Ashgrove, a cartographer of unmapped realms, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
