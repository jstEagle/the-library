*by Sable Eastwick*

> Ines Montrose, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
