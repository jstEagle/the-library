# Vesper and the Spire

*by Isandro Marchetti*

> August Coldharbour, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

Isandro Marchetti is the pen name of a former cartographer's assistant who spent years redrawing borders that history had already erased. They write fantasy about people who survive by being unmemorable, and live quietly in a coastal town full of doors.
