# Vesper and the Thorn

*by Beatrix Almeida*

> Juniper Quintana, a debt-bound temple scribe, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Beatrix Almeida is a former archivist of municipal records who spent years cataloguing documents for towns that had been merged, renamed, or dissolved. She writes fantasy about paperwork, borders, and the people history forgets to file.
