# Wendell and the Oath

*by Theo Almeida*

> Beatrix Ashgrove, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
