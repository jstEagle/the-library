# Wendell and the Sigil

*by Astrid Villalobos*

> Rosalind Ishikawa, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.
