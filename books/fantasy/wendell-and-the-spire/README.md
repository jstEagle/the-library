# Wendell and the Spire

*by Sebastian Reyes*

> Jonah Thornbury, an exiled storm-caller, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
