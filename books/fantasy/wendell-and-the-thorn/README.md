# Wendell and the Thorn

*by Rafael Blackwood*

> Ansel Winterbourne, the youngest member of a dying guild, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Rafael Blackwood is the pen name of a former archivist who spent eleven years cataloguing estate sales before turning to fiction, a career that left him with an abiding love of old paper and old debts. He writes fantasy about ordinary people trapped inside systems older and larger than themselves, and lives near a border town whose name has changed four times.
