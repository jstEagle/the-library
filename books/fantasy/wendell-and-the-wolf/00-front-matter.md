## About the author

Ines Ishikawa is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters never meant to be read, an experience that taught her how memory and devotion entangle. She writes fantasy about ordinary people caught in machinery far larger than themselves, and lives near a forest she refuses to enter after dark.
