# When the Choir Falls Anew

*by Orsen Halloran*

> Juniper Almeida, the youngest member of a dying guild, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
