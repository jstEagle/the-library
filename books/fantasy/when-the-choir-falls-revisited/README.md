# When the Choir Falls Revisited

*by Noor Eastwick*

> Zephyrine Hartwell, an exiled storm-caller, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Noor Eastwick is the pen name of a former choir singer turned archivist who grew up in a family of refugees and has never quite stopped mapping exits. When the Choir Falls Revisited is their second novel, written largely at kitchen tables in borrowed houses.
