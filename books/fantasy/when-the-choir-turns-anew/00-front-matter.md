Cyrus Nakamura is a former cartographer who spent six years mapping remote mountain trade routes before turning to fiction, and his novels are prized for their lovingly rendered geography and morally tangled institutions. He writes in a converted waystation cabin and claims every story begins with a road that shouldn't exist.
