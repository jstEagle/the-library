# When the Choir Turns

**by Ada Okonkwo**

> Hazel Eriksen, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

Ada Okonkwo is a former church archivist from Lagos who writes fantasy about faith, inheritance, and the people history forgets. Her short fiction has appeared in several anthologies; When the Choir Turns is her debut novel.
