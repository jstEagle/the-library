# When the Choir Wakes

*by Dominic Ishikawa*

> Silas Almeida, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Dominic Ishikawa is the pen name of a former maritime cartographer turned novelist who writes fantasy about people who make maps of things that refuse to be mapped. They live in a coastal town with two opinionated cats and a wall of unfinished charts.
