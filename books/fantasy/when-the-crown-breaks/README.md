# When the Crown Breaks

*by Mirabel Iversen*

> Lena Ravensworth, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Mirabel Iversen is the pen name of a former cartographer who spent six years mapping remote mountain passes before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about borders, both geographic and emotional, from a farmhouse overlooking a valley she still can't quite name.
