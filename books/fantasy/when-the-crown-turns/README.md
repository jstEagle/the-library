# When the Crown Turns

*by Orsen Iversen*

> Astrid Voss, a bone-carver who hears the dead, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
