# When the Ember Breaks

*by Edmund Ravensworth*

> Rafael Almeida, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Edmund Ravensworth is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing folktales about talking animals before deciding to write his own. He lives somewhere rainy, keeps no pets he cannot argue with, and insists the fox in this book is only partly autobiographical.
