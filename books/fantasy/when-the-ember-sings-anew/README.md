# When the Ember Sings Anew

*by Rafael Zhou*

> Jonah Iversen, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
