# When the Garden Burns

*by Mara Lockridge*

> Paloma Galloway, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
