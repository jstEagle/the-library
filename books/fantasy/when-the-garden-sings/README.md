# When the Garden Sings

*by Oscar Zhou*

> Quentin Iversen, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
