# When the Hollow Breaks

*by E. M. Dunmore*

> Tamsin Thornbury, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
