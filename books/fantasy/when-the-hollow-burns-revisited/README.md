# When the Hollow Burns Revisited

*by Silas Okafor*

> August Amberdale, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the long winter finishes falling.
