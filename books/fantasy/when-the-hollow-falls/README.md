# When the Hollow Falls

*by Jude Kavanagh*

> Freya Hartwell, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
