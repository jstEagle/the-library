Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Juniper Vane is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing folk songs about animals that talked back. They write from a drafty cottage where the local crows are, they insist, taking notes.
