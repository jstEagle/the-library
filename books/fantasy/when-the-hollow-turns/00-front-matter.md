## About the author

Cyrus Oyelaran is a former cartographer who spent six years mapping remote mountain passes before turning to fiction, a background that infuses his landscapes with unsettling precision. He writes fantasy about memory, exile, and the geography of guilt from a home he refuses to locate on any map.
