# When the Hollow Wakes Again

*by Orsen Sterling*

> Kestrel Kavanagh, a thief who steals memories, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
