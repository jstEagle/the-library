# When the Hollow Wakes Anew

*by Rosalind Winterbourne*

> Ulyana Coldharbour, a debt-bound temple scribe, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Rosalind Winterbourne is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the ledgers of defunct merchant guilds before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about paperwork, memory, and the quiet violence of institutions.
