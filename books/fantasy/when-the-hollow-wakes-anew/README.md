# When the Hollow Wakes Anew

*by Ada Dravenmoor*

> Cyrus Amberdale, a debt-bound temple scribe, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
