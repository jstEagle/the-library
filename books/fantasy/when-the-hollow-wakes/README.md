# When the Hollow Wakes

*by Quentin Dunmore*

> Gideon Winterbourne, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
