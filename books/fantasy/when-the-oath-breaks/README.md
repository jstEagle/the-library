# When the Oath Breaks

*by Theo Lockridge*

> Juniper Okafor, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Theo Lockridge is the pen name of a former cartographer's apprentice who spent a decade drawing borders he now writes about erasing. They live in a house full of obsolete maps and insist that every fictional country deserves at least one unfinished war.
