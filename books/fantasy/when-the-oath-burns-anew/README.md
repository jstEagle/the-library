# When the Oath Burns Anew

*by Gideon Blackwood*

> Tamsin Okafor, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
