# When the Oath Burns

*by Astrid Dravenmoor*

> Ada Thackeray, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
