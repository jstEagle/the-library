# When the Oath Sings Again

*by Casper Voss*

> Juniper Hartwell, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Casper Voss is the pen name of a former archivist who spent six years cataloguing the marginalia of abandoned grimoires before turning to fiction. They write fantasy about people who were never supposed to be in the story, and they still cannot resist a book that predicts things.
