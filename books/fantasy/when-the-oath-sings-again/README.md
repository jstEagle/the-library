# When the Oath Sings Again

*by Rafael Ravensworth*

> Yara Crane, a debt-bound temple scribe, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the long winter finishes falling.
