# When the Oath Sings Revisited

*by Freya Sterling*

> Noor Delacroix, the youngest member of a dying guild, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
