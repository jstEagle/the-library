# When the Oath Sings

*by Astrid Blackwood*

> Beatrix Yarrow, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
