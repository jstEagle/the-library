# When the Oath Wakes Again

*by Ivo Whitlock*

> Tamsin Eastwick, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
