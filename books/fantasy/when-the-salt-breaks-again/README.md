# When the Salt Breaks Again

*by Sebastian Nakamura*

> Gideon Zhou, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.

## About the author

Sebastian Nakamura is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing maps of coastlines that no longer appear on modern charts. Their fiction blends cartographic obsession with quiet, aching fantasies about places the world insists on forgetting.
