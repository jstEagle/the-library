# When the Salt Breaks

*by Oscar Pemberton*

> Ivo Ashgrove, an exiled storm-caller, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Oscar Pemberton is the pen name of a former cartographer who spent a decade mapping coastlines that no longer appear on modern charts. He writes fantasy about borders, forgetting, and the people who live in the gaps between official histories.
