# When the Salt Burns

*by Petra Amberdale*

> Wendell Nystrom, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
