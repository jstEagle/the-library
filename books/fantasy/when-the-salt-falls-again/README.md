# When the Salt Falls Again

*by Silas Villalobos*

> Ansel Zhou, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
