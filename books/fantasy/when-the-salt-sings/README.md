# When the Salt Sings

*by Odile Villalobos*

> Ines Hartwell, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
