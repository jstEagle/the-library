## About the author

Ansel Ashgrove is the pen name of a former cartographer who spent a decade mapping mountain passes before turning to fiction, and whose love of borders — political, magical, and personal — saturates every page. Ashgrove writes at high altitude and claims no first draft has ever survived below the snowline.
