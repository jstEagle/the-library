# When the Sigil Burns Again

*by Hazel Brightwater*

> Felix Voss, a bone-carver who hears the dead, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
