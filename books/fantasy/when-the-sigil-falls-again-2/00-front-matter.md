# When the Sigil Falls Again

**by Wren Nakamura**

## About the author

Wren Nakamura is a former archivist who writes fantasy about people who keep things—gates, secrets, promises—that were never meant to be kept alone. She lives between two cities and one coastline, and claims the best plot ideas arrive on trains.
