# When the Sigil Falls Anew

*by Silas Marchetti*

> Edmund Dunmore, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
