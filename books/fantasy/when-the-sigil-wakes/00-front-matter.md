# When the Sigil Wakes

**by Mirabel Ashgrove**

## About the author

Mirabel Ashgrove is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the paperwork of defunct nations before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about people who keep records of things the world would rather forget.
