# When the Sigil Wakes

*by Mirabel Ashgrove*

> Paloma Brightwater, an exiled storm-caller, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
