# When the Spire Burns

**by Ada Oyelaran**

> Paloma Sterling, a debt-bound temple scribe, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Ada Oyelaran is a former archivist who spent years cataloguing colonial-era manuscripts before turning to fiction full-time. She writes fantasy about debt, memory, and the institutions people survive inside, and lives between Lagos and Lisbon.
