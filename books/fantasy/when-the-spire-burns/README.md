# When the Spire Burns

*by Ada Oyelaran*

> Paloma Sterling, a debt-bound temple scribe, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
