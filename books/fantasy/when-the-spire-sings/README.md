# When the Spire Sings

*by Gideon Kavanagh*

> Beatrix Dunmore, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
