# When the Spire Turns

*by S. M. Fairweather*

> Noor Halloran, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent fifteen years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
