# When the Spire Wakes Anew

*by Jude Galloway*

> Ivo Amberdale, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Jude Galloway is the pen name of a former shepherd turned archivist who claims to have learned storytelling from livestock and library dust alike. They live somewhere with poor phone reception and write about people who talk to things that shouldn't answer.
