# When the Thorn Burns

*by Mirabel Nakamura*

> Cyrus Crane, an exiled storm-caller, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
