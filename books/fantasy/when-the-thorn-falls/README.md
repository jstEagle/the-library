# When the Thorn Falls

*by Theo Quintana*

> Quentin Ashgrove, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Theo Quintana is the pen name of a former cartographer's apprentice who spent a decade drawing borders that no longer exist. They write fantasy about people caught between maps and the truths maps conceal, and live somewhere deliberately unmarked.
