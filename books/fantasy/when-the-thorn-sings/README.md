# When the Thorn Sings

*by Vesper Almeida*

> Beatrix Marchetti, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
