# When the Thorn Turns Again

*by Beatrix Yarrow*

> Dominic Thackeray, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Beatrix Yarrow is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing extinct languages before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about oaths, borders, and the people who fall between them from a cluttered study full of unlabeled maps.
