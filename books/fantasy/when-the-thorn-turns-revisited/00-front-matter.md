## About the author

Ansel Quintana is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging confiscated letters in a coastal city's municipal records, an experience that left a lasting fascination with what people choose to remember and what institutions choose to erase. When the Thorn Turns Revisited is their second novel.
