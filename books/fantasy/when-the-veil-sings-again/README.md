# When the Veil Sings Again

*by Ulyana Pemberton*

> Wendell Lockridge, a bone-carver who hears the dead, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
