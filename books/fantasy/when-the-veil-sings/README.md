# When the Veil Sings

*by Paloma Blackwood*

> Vesper Halloran, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Paloma Blackwood is the pen name of a former cartographer who spent six years mapping remote mountain passes before turning to fiction, a trade that left her with a lifelong obsession with borders and the things that cross them. She writes fantasy from a stone farmhouse where, she insists, the walls hum on windy nights.
