# When the Wolf Breaks Anew

*by Elodie Eastwick*

> August Underhill, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
