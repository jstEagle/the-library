# When the Wolf Sings

*by Astrid Crane*

> Lior Thackeray, an exiled storm-caller, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Astrid Crane is the pen name of a former folklorist who spent a decade collecting storm songs along northern coastlines before turning to fiction. Their debut novel draws on those field recordings and the oral traditions of exile communities.
