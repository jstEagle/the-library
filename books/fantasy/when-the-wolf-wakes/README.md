# When the Wolf Wakes

*by Astrid Winterbourne*

> Sebastian Kavanagh, an exiled storm-caller, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown, and time is already running out.
