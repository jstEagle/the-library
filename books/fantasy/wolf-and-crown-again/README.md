# Wolf and Crown Again

*by Lior Quintana*

> Paloma Blackwood, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
