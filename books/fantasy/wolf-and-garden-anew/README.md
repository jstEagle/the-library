# Wolf and Garden Anew

*by Sebastian Sandoval*

> Xanthe Whitlock, a bone-carver who hears the dead, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the old gods begin answering prayers again — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
