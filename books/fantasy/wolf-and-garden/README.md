# Wolf and Garden

*by Jonah Montrose*

> Quentin Eastwick, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.
