# Wolf and Salt

*by Gideon Galloway*

> Hazel Nakamura, the youngest member of a dying guild, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.
