# Wolf and Thorn

*by Sable Delacroix*

> Emrys Villalobos, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
