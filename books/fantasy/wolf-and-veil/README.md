# Wolf and Veil

*by Orsen Sterling*

> Bram Hartwell, a cartographer of unmapped realms, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
