# Wren and the Choir

*by Marguerite Yarrow*

> Coral Underhill, a thief who steals memories, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Marguerite Yarrow is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters no one ever claimed, an experience she describes as 'stealing memories legally.' She writes fantasy about people who survive by taking what others discard, and lives in a coastal town where the fog arrives like a debt collector.
