> Tamsin Kavanagh, the youngest member of a dying guild, knows every smuggler's road through the Thornfell mountains. Then a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author
