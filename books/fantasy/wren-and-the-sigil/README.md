# Wren and the Sigil

*by Casper Zhou*

> Emrys Zhou, an exiled storm-caller, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. Then tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the long winter finishes falling.

## About the author

Casper Zhou is the pen name of a former shipping clerk who spent nine years cataloguing oddities in a warehouse and now catalogs them in novels instead. They write cold, luminous fantasy about people who keep records of things that should not exist.
