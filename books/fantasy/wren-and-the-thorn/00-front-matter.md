## About the author

Orsen Okafor is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial land records in West Africa before turning to fiction about documents that refuse to stay quiet. He writes from a farmhouse outside Accra, where he keeps an unreadable book on his shelf as a reminder that some stories choose their readers.
