# Wren and the Thorn

*by Orsen Okafor*

> Oscar Halloran, a debt-bound temple scribe, has never left the valley where magic cannot follow. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Orsen Okafor is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial land records in West Africa before turning to fiction about documents that refuse to stay quiet. He writes from a farmhouse outside Accra, where he keeps an unreadable book on his shelf as a reminder that some stories choose their readers.
