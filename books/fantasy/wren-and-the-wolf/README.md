# Wren and the Wolf

*by Dominic Villalobos*

> Ulyana Pemberton, a thief who steals memories, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
