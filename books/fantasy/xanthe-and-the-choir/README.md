*by Juniper Coldharbour*

Then a child arrives at the door speaking a language that predates kingdoms — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the moon completes its thirteenth turn.

## About the author

Juniper Coldharbour is the pen name of a former choir librarian who spent a decade cataloguing hymnals in abandoned parish churches across the northern moors. They write fantasy about the things we inherit without being asked — languages, debts, and songs stuck in the head.
