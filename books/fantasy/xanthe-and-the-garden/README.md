# Xanthe and the Garden

*by Marguerite Zhou*

> Dominic Dravenmoor, a thief who steals memories, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.

## About the author

Marguerite Zhou is the pen name of a former botanical archivist who spent a decade cataloguing heirloom seeds before turning to fiction. She writes fantasy about gardens, grief, and the things we trade to survive, and lives with an unreasonable number of ferns.
