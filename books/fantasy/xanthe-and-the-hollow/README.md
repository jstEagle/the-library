# Xanthe and the Hollow

*by Cyrus Ashgrove*

> Coral Sandoval, an exiled storm-caller, has spent ten years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must find the door out of a country that no longer exists before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
