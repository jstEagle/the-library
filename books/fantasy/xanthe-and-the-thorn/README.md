# Xanthe and the Thorn

*by I. M. Dunmore*

> Edmund Thornbury, a thief who steals memories, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when a dying stranger presses a map into unwilling hands, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

I. M. Dunmore is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing documents belonging to nations that no longer exist. They write fantasy about memory, borders, and the things we trade away to stay safe, from a house full of unlabeled maps.
