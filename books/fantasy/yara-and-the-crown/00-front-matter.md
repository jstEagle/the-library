## About the author

Ivo Hartwell is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing guild records in a coastal trading city, where every ledger seemed to hold one entry that shouldn't exist. Yara and the Crown is their first novel, born from a fascination with paperwork, prophecy, and the people history forgets to name.
