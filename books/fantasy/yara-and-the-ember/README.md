# Yara and the Ember

*by Cyrus Galloway*

> Wendell Brightwater, the last oath-bound swordspeaker, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
