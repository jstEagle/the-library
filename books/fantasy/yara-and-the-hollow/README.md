# Yara and the Hollow

*by Ulyana Winterbourne*

> Bram Oyelaran, a mercenary sworn to a god that went silent, has spent seven years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must unbind a king from the thing wearing his crown before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
