# Yara and the Salt

*by Lior Hartwell*

> Orsen Kavanagh, a beast-speaker hiding a forbidden gift, has spent twenty years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must reforge a broken covenant between realms before the last dragon-fire gutters out.
