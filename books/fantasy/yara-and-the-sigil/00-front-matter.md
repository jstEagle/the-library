## About the author

Felix Sandoval is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters no one ever claimed, an experience that left him obsessed with what people choose to remember. He writes fantasy from a small apartment above a shuttered pawnshop, where he insists the previous owner still lends him ideas.
