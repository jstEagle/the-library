# Yara and the Sigil

*by Felix Sandoval*

> Kestrel Delacroix, a thief who steals memories, has spent five years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. But when the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.
