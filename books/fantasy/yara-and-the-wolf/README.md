# Yara and the Wolf

*by Juniper Ashgrove*

> Paloma Almeida, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when the old gods begin answering prayers again, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain, and time is already running out.
