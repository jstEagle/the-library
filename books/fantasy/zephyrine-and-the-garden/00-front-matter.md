## About the author

Dominic Sandoval is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters no one ever claimed, an experience that taught him how much of a life can live inside paper. He writes fantasy about people caught between what they remember and what they were told to forget, and lives near a coastline that appears on fewer maps each year.
