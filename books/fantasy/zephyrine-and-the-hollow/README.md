# Zephyrine and the Hollow

*by Petra Thackeray*

> Casper Ashgrove, the keeper of a gate that should stay shut, has spent three years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the border wards fall on the same night the stars go out — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must reforge a broken covenant between realms, and time is already running out.
