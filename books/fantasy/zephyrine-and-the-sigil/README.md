# Zephyrine and the Sigil

*by Ines Ishikawa*

> Xanthe Sandoval, a hedge-witch's runaway apprentice, has spent twelve years selling small charms in a city that outlawed magic. Then the crown announces a hunt for anyone with the gift — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must carry a secret that could reignite an ancient war, and time is already running out.

Ines Ishikawa is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters and diaries of people history forgot. Their fiction explores what we inherit from strangers, and their short work has appeared in several small-press fantasy anthologies.
