# Zephyrine and the Thorn

*by Tamsin Zhou*

> Theo Eriksen, a thief who steals memories, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must find the door out of a country that no longer exists, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Tamsin Zhou is the pen name of a former archivist who spent six years cataloguing confiscated books in a coastal city that has changed its name three times. They write fantasy about people the records forgot, and this is their second novel.
