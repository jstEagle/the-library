# Zephyrine and the Wolf

**by Wren Marchetti**

Wren Marchetti is a former apothecary's apprentice from the coastal town of Greyharbor who claims to have tasted everything once, on purpose. Zephyrine and the Wolf is their second novel, following the acclaimed The Ledger of Small Debts.
