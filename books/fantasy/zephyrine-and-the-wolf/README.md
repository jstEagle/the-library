# Zephyrine and the Wolf

*by Wren Marchetti*

> Coral Delacroix, a royal food-taster building immunity one dish at a time, keeps the ledgers of a guild that trades in impossible things. But when tonight's date appears in a book of prophecies written a century ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must steal back a soul sold to the court beneath the mountain before the usurper's armies reach the free cities.
