# A Atlas for the Compass

*by Isandro Crane*

> Sebastian Montrose, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, has spent three years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.
