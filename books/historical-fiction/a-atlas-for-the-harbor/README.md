# A Atlas for the Harbor

*by Edmund Thackeray*

> Felix Joubert, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
