# A Atlas for the Ledger

*by Orsen Blackwood*

> Ansel Pemberton, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the century turns.

## About the author

Orsen Blackwood is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing diplomatic correspondence from collapsed states before turning to fiction. They write from a farmhouse full of unlabeled maps, insisting that every empire is just a ledger waiting to be balanced.
