# A Atlas for the Letter

*by Casper Eriksen*

> Quentin Vane, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Casper Eriksen is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial trading-company records along the northern coast. Their novels excavate the lives of clerks, divers, and debtors whom official histories rendered as mere entries.
