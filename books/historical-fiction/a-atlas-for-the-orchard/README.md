# A Atlas for the Orchard

*by Jonah Blackwood*

> Ansel Nakamura, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, has spent three years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Jonah Blackwood is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing port records along the English coast before turning to fiction. He writes historical novels about the quiet people history overlooks, and lives near a working harbor where he still winds the town clock on Sundays.
