# A Bridge for the Atlas

*by H. M. Lockridge*

> Kestrel Dunmore, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must choose which family member gets the last berth before the century turns.

## About the author

H. M. Lockridge is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial shipping ledgers before turning to fiction. Their work explores how ordinary people are recorded, erased, and remembered by institutions that never learned their faces.
