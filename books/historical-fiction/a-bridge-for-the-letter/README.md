Now he must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Emrys Sandoval is a former cartographic archivist who spent a decade cataloguing smugglers' charts in coastal county records offices before turning to fiction. He writes historical novels about the documents people leave behind and the debts they encode.
