# A Bridge for the Orchard

*by Zephyrine Thackeray*

> Juniper Ashgrove, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent twelve years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.
