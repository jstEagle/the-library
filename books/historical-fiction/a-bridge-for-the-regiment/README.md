# A Bridge for the Regiment

*by Petra Okafor*

> Coral Voss, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, has spent ten years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the century turns.
