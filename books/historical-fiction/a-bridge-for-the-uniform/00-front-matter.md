## About the author

Edmund Dunmore is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing military correspondence from the Great War and became obsessed with the gaps between official reports and soldiers' letters. He writes from a converted signal station on the northern coast, where the fog still arrives on schedule.
