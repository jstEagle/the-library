# A Bridge for the Uniform

*by Edmund Dunmore*

> Sebastian Almeida, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, has spent fifteen years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Edmund Dunmore is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing military correspondence from the Great War and became obsessed with the gaps between official reports and soldiers' letters. He writes from a converted signal station on the northern coast, where the fog still arrives on schedule.
