# A Clock for the Atlas

*by Freya Dunmore*

> Ada Sandoval, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the census taker asks one question too many, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Freya Dunmore is the pen name of a former cartographic archivist who spent a decade cataloguing suppressed municipal maps in coastal archives. She writes historical fiction about the people history tried to leave off the page.
