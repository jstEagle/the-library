# A Clock for the Bridge

*by Yara Iversen*

> Freya Iversen, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the century turns.

## About the author

Yara Iversen is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unclaimed-person records in northern European municipal collections. She writes historical fiction about the people official histories leave off the page.
