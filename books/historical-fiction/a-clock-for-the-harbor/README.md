# A Clock for the Harbor

*by Sebastian Galloway*

> Juniper Vasquez, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ship sails on the morning tide.
