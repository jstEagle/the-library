# A Clock for the Lantern

*by Coral Okafor*

> Ansel Quintana, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Coral Okafor is the pen name of a former museum conservator who spent years restoring nineteenth-century instruments and now writes historical fiction about the people history overlooked. She lives between Lisbon and a small mountain town that inspired Vell, where she still winds a public clock every Sunday.
