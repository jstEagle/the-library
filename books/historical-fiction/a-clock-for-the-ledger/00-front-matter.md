# A Clock for the Ledger

**by Lena Ishikawa**

> Isandro Ishikawa, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Lena Ishikawa is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging displaced-persons records in coastal municipal libraries. She writes historical fiction about the paperwork of survival—the ledgers, maps, and manifests that decide who gets remembered.
