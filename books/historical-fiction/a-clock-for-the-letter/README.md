# A Clock for the Letter

*by Rafael Delacroix*

> August Hartwell, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.
