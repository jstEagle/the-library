# A Clock for the Orchard

*by Rafael Hartwell*

> Oscar Whitlock, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Rafael Hartwell is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing wartime correspondence in regional county records. A Clock for the Orchard is their third novel, drawn from the marginalia of confiscated letters they were never supposed to read.
