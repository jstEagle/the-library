# A Clock for the Uniform

**by Noor Eastwick**

> Marguerite Blackwood, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
