# A Compass for the Harbor

*by Quentin Sandoval*

> Marguerite Zhou, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the century turns.
