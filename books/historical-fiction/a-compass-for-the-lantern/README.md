# A Compass for the Lantern

*by L. M. Blackwood*

> Silas Blackwood, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the census taker asks one question too many, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

L. M. Blackwood is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse keepers' logs along the northern coast. Their fiction draws on the marginalia of those records—the debts, disguises, and quiet mercies that official histories leave out.
