# A Compass for the Orchard

*by August Villalobos*

> Dominic Marchetti, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

August Villalobos is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unsent letters in rural county collections. A Compass for the Orchard is their second novel, written in a farmhouse within sight of a river that floods every spring.
