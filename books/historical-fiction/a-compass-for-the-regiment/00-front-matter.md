# A Compass for the Regiment

**by Tamsin Lockridge**

> Emrys Dravenmoor, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Tamsin Lockridge is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing regimental records in provincial garrison towns, where she first encountered the paperwork of invented heroes. She writes historical fiction about ordinary people caught in machinery far larger than themselves.
