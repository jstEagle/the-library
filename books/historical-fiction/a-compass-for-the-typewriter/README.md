# A Compass for the Typewriter

*by Marguerite Galloway*

> Cyrus Voss, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Marguerite Galloway is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing displaced-persons records along the northern coast. She writes from a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage, where she keeps her grandmother's typewriter and an unfinished map of a town that no longer appears on any chart.
