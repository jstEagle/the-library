## About the author

Mara Thornbury spent a decade cataloguing maritime archives in coastal England before turning to fiction, where her love of tide charts and forgotten ledgers shapes every page. She writes from a converted pilot's cottage, convinced that the most ordinary lives hold the most extraordinary testimony.
