# A Harbor for the Bridge

*by A. M. Underhill*

> Lior Okafor, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, has spent fifteen years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

A. M. Underhill is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloging seized municipal records from contested border towns. They write historical fiction about the paperwork of atrocity and the people who refuse to let it stay filed.
