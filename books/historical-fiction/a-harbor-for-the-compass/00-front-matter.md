# A Harbor for the Compass

**by Tamsin Nakamura**

> Odile Almeida, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, has spent three years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Tamsin Nakamura is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing confiscated letters in municipal collections, where she first encountered the marginalia of ordinary people caught in official histories. She writes historical fiction about the records that almost didn't survive.
