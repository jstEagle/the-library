# A Harbor for the Lantern

*by Wren Crane*

> Wendell Galloway, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, has spent ten years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ship sails on the morning tide.
