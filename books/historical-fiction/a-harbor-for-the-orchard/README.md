*by Sable Crane*

> Kestrel Eriksen, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, has spent fifteen years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Sable Crane is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing salvaged charts from wrecked merchant vessels along the northern coast. Their fiction explores how ordinary people redraw their own borders when empires demand loyalty they never agreed to give.
