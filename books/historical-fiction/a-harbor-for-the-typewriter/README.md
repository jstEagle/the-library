Now she must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Lior Sterling is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing displaced-persons records from postwar Europe. Their fiction is drawn from the marginalia of those files — the notes, aliases, and unsent letters left between official lines.
