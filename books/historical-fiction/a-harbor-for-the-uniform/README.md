# A Harbor for the Uniform

*by Tamsin Reyes*

> Hazel Crane, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the century turns.

## About the author

Tamsin Reyes is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing diplomatic correspondence from interwar Europe before turning to fiction. She lives near a working harbor and writes about the quiet people who move history with their hands.
