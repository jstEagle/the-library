# A Lantern for the Atlas

*by Vesper Brightwater*

> Lior Oyelaran, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, has spent twenty years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Vesper Brightwater is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing displaced-persons records along the Atlantic seaboard. Their fiction draws on the cartographers, clerks, and quiet resisters whose names survive only in margins.
