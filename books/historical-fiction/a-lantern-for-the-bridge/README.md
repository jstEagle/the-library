# A Lantern for the Bridge

*by Petra Oyelaran*

> Hazel Vane, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the census taker asks one question too many, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must choose which family member gets the last berth before the century turns.

## About the author

Petra Oyelaran is a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century print shops across coastal Europe before turning to fiction. Her novels examine how ordinary hands—ink-stained, tired, precise—shape the documents that decide who belongs.
