# A Lantern for the Compass

*by Oscar Nakamura*

> Ulyana Iversen, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ice breaks on the river.
