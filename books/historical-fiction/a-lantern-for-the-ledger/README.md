# A Lantern for the Ledger

*by Rafael Ishikawa*

> Noor Eastwick, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.
