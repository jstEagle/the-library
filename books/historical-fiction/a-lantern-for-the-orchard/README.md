# A Lantern for the Orchard

*by Ulyana Coldharbour*

> Ines Halloran, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ulyana Coldharbour is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing disputed land surveys in municipal basements. She writes historical fiction about the documents that outlive the people who risked everything to keep them.
