# A Lantern for the Regiment

*by Ansel Underhill*

> Noor Zhou, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must choose which family member gets the last berth before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Ansel Underhill is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse keepers' logs along the northern coast. Their fiction is drawn from the marginalia keepers left between official entries.
