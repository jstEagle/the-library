# A Lantern for the Typewriter

*by Elodie Nystrom*

> Bram Winterbourne, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Elodie Nystrom is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment in Baltic port towns before turning to fiction. She writes historical novels about people whose work leaves marks on the world they cannot sign.
