# A Ledger for the Bridge

*by Vesper Almeida*

> Coral Okafor, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.
