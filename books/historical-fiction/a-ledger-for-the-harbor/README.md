# A Ledger for the Harbor

*by Paloma Delacroix*

> Freya Crane, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ice breaks on the river.
