# A Ledger for the Lantern

**by Hazel Joubert**

> Coral Ashgrove, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, has spent three years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it.
