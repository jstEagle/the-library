# A Ledger for the Lantern

*by Hazel Joubert*

> Coral Ashgrove, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, has spent three years learning which silences keep people safe. Now they must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Hazel Joubert is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing municipal records in small alpine towns before turning to fiction. She writes about the people history files under 'miscellaneous,' and about the objects that remember what paperwork forgets.
