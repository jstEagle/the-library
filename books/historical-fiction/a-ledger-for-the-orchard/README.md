# A Ledger for the Orchard

*by Gideon Delacroix*

> Dominic Hartwell, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent seven years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ice breaks on the river.
