# A Ledger for the Uniform

*by Quentin Pemberton*

> Yara Nakamura, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must choose which family member gets the last berth before the century turns.
