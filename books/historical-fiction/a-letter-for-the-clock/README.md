# A Letter for the Clock

*by Sebastian Nakamura*

> Zephyrine Oyelaran, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ship sails on the morning tide.
