## About the author

Mara Sterling is the pen name of a former field archivist who spent a decade cataloguing wartime correspondence in European national archives, where she first encountered ledgers kept in defiance of official histories. She writes historical fiction from a farmhouse in Normandy, where the war's paperwork is never more than a village memorial away.
