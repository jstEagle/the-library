# A Letter for the Regiment

**by B. M. Blackwood**

> Rosalind Nystrom, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
