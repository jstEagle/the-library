## About the author

Mirabel Blackwood is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unsent letters in provincial record offices, where she first encountered the quiet revolutionaries history forgot to file. She writes from a converted coach house, where a 1910 typewriter sits on her desk as both tool and muse.
