# A Orchard for the Bridge

*by Rafael Okafor*

> Kestrel Pemberton, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Rafael Okafor is a former cartographic archivist who spent a decade cataloguing confiscated maps in colonial port archives before turning to fiction. He writes historical novels about the people history records only as margins and marginalia.
