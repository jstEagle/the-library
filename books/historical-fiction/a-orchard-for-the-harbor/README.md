# A Orchard for the Harbor

*by Gideon Voss*

> Freya Fairweather, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the census taker asks one question too many, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Gideon Voss is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing confiscated letters from collapsed states. A Orchard for the Harbor is his second novel, drawn from a single unsigned promise found sewn into a customs ledger.
