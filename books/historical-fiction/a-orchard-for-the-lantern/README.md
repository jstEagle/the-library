# A Orchard for the Lantern

*by Rosalind Amberdale*

> Isandro Voss, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Rosalind Amberdale is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing diplomatic correspondence from collapsed states, work that taught her how much history turns on what couriers chose to carry. She writes from a farmhouse orchard, where she claims every tree was planted by someone breaking a rule.
