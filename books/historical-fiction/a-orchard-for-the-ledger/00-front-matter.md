## About the author

R. M. Okafor is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial trading ledgers before turning to fiction. Their work excavates the lives history filed under 'miscellaneous,' giving voice to the indentured, the drowned, and the deliberately forgotten.
