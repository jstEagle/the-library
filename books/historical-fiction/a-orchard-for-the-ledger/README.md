# A Orchard for the Ledger

*by R. M. Okafor*

> Mara Vane, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

R. M. Okafor is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial trading ledgers before turning to fiction. Their work excavates the lives history filed under 'miscellaneous,' giving voice to the indentured, the drowned, and the deliberately forgotten.
