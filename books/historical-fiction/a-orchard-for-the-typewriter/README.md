# A Orchard for the Typewriter

*by Jonah Marchetti*

> Xanthe Thackeray, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
