# A Orchard for the Uniform

*by Coral Almeida*

> Cyrus Reyes, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Coral Almeida is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing municipal records in small coastal towns, where she became fascinated by the documents ordinary people leave behind. She writes historical fiction about the quiet custodians of memory, and this is her third novel.
