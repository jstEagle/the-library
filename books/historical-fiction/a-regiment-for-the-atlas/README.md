# A Regiment for the Atlas

*by Orsen Pemberton*

> Freya Kavanagh, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent twenty years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Orsen Pemberton is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment in Baltic port towns. A Regiment for the Atlas draws on his fascination with the printers, smugglers, and clerks who moved revolutions one page at a time.
