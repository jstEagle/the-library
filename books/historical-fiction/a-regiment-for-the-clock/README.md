# A Regiment for the Clock

*by Ulyana Okafor*

> Ivo Yarrow, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the century turns.
