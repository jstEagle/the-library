# A Regiment for the Orchard

*by Juniper Villalobos*

> Edmund Hartwell, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the census taker asks one question too many, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Juniper Villalobos is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing Edwardian census records and letters before turning to fiction. She writes from a converted orchard shed in Somerset, where she keeps the unsent correspondence that inspired this novel pinned above her desk.
