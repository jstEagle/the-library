Now he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Theo Hartwell is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse logs and coastal customs records along the Italian Riviera. His novels are known for their salt-weathered realism and their conviction that ordinary keepers, clerks, and couriers are the true engines of history.
