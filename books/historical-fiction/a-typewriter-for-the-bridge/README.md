# A Typewriter for the Bridge

*by Oscar Montrose*

> Xanthe Fairweather, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.
