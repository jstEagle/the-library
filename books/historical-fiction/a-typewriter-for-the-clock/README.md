# A Typewriter for the Clock

*by Freya Sterling*

> Felix Eriksen, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, has spent twenty years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Freya Sterling is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse logbooks and census ledgers from the Baltic coast. She writes historical fiction about ordinary people caught between paper records and the truths those records erase.
