# A Typewriter for the Harbor

*by Quentin Thornbury*

> Juniper Eriksen, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ship sails on the morning tide.
