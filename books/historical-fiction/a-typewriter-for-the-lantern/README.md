*by Sebastian Quintana*

> Sebastian Lockridge, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.
