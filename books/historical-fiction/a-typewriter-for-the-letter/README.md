# A Typewriter for the Letter

*by Hazel Montrose*

> Elodie Whitlock, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the century turns.

## About the author

Hazel Montrose is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing small-town records across the mountain West before turning to fiction. She writes about the people history files under 'miscellaneous,' and the machines they trust with their truths.
