## About the author

Coral Nystrom spent a decade restoring coastal lighthouses along the Pacific Northwest before turning to fiction full-time, drawing her settings from fog-soaked winters and the oral histories of keeper families. She writes in longhand at a 1948 typewriter she claims was salvaged from a shipwreck, though she refuses to say which one.
