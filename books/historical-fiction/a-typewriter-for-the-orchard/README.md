# A Typewriter for the Orchard

*by Coral Nystrom*

> Wren Sandoval, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Coral Nystrom spent a decade restoring coastal lighthouses along the Pacific Northwest before turning to fiction full-time, drawing her settings from fog-soaked winters and the oral histories of keeper families. She writes in longhand at a 1948 typewriter she claims was salvaged from a shipwreck, though she refuses to say which one.
