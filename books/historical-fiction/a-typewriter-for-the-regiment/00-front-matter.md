## About the author

Oscar Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former typesetter who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing presses in river towns along the old imperial frontier. A Typewriter for the Regiment is the second novel in a planned trilogy about print, power, and the people who keep the record.
