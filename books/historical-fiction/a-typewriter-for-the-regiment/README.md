# A Typewriter for the Regiment

*by Oscar Dravenmoor*

> Quentin Vasquez, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Oscar Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former typesetter who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing presses in river towns along the old imperial frontier. A Typewriter for the Regiment is the second novel in a planned trilogy about print, power, and the people who keep the record.
