## About the author

Zephyrine Zhou is a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century print equipment in coastal museum archives across Northern Europe. Her fiction draws on the ledgers, broadsides, and marginalia she catalogued, giving voice to the invisible hands behind historical upheavals.
