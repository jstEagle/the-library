# A Uniform for the Harbor

*by Zephyrine Zhou*

> Oscar Eriksen, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Zephyrine Zhou is a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century print equipment in coastal museum archives across Northern Europe. Her fiction draws on the ledgers, broadsides, and marginalia she catalogued, giving voice to the invisible hands behind historical upheavals.
