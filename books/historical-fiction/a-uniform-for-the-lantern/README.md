# A Uniform for the Lantern

*by Sebastian Thackeray*

> Theo Hartwell, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Sebastian Thackeray is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing port ledgers and deportation manifests along the northern coast. His fiction draws on the quiet bureaucratic cruelties he found recorded in otherwise ordinary municipal paperwork.
