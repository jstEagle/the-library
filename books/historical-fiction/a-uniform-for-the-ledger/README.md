# A Uniform for the Ledger

*by Wren Hartwell*

> Yara Blackwood, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Wren Hartwell is a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing trading-company ledgers in coastal archives, where she first noticed how many lives were recorded only as cargo weights. She writes historical fiction about the people history filed under 'miscellaneous.'
