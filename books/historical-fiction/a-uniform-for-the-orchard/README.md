# A Uniform for the Orchard

*by Astrid Okonkwo*

> Astrid Almeida, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must choose which family member gets the last berth before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Astrid Okonkwo is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial trading-company ledgers from the Indian Ocean rim. She writes historical fiction about people the paperwork tried to erase.
