# Ada and the Bridge

**by Ines Winterbourne**

> Sable Dravenmoor, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, has spent twelve years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Ines Winterbourne is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing occupation-era records in Central European municipal collections. She writes historical fiction about ordinary people who keep accounts — literal and moral — that empires would prefer to lose.
