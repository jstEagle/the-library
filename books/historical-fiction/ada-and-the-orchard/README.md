# Ada and the Orchard

*by C. M. Halloran*

> Ivo Underhill, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the century turns.

## About the author

C. M. Halloran is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing wartime correspondence in county record offices. Their fiction is known for finding the extraordinary in the hands of ordinary letter-carriers.
