# Ansel and the Atlas

*by August Ashgrove*

> Sable Pemberton, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

August Ashgrove is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the letters of wartime evacuees. Their novels explore the quiet mathematics of sacrifice—what we count, what we carry, and what we leave behind.
