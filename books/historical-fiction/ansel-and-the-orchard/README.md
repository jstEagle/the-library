# Ansel and the Orchard

*by Xanthe Iversen*

> Sebastian Underhill, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.
