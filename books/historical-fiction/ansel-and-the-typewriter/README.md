# Ansel and the Typewriter

*by Ada Oyelaran*

> Paloma Pemberton, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, has spent five years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ada Oyelaran is a former horologist's apprentice who spent a decade restoring church tower clocks across northern England before turning to fiction. Her writing is shaped by her Nigerian-English heritage and a lifelong fascination with machines that keep time for people who cannot keep their promises.
