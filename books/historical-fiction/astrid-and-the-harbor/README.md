# Astrid and the Harbor

*by Emrys Marchetti*

> Zephyrine Whitlock, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
