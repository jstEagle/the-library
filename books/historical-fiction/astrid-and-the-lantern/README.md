# Astrid and the Lantern

*by Mara Underhill*

> Bram Montrose, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the census taker asks one question too many, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.
