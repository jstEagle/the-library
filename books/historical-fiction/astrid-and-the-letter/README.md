# Astrid and the Letter

*by Ulyana Pemberton*

> Jonah Vasquez, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the census taker asks one question too many, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Ulyana Pemberton is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring printing archives in Eastern European municipal libraries. Her novels examine how ordinary people become accomplices to history through the smallest acts of omission.
