# Astrid and the Orchard

*by J. M. Montrose*

> Dominic Delacroix, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

J. M. Montrose is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing provincial maps and municipal ledgers in the French countryside. Their fiction grew out of the marginalia—names, debts, and small rebellions—found in the records of people history forgot.
