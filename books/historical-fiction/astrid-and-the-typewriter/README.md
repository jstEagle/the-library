# Astrid and the Typewriter

*by Mirabel Eriksen*

> Gideon Brightwater, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the century turns.

## About the author

Mirabel Eriksen is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing confiscated Baltic manuscripts before turning to fiction. She writes historical novels about ordinary people caught between empires and their paperwork.
