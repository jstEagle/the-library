# Atlas and Bridge

*by Sebastian Montrose*

> Mara Winterbourne, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ice breaks on the river.
