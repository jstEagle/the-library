# Atlas and Clock

*by August Delacroix*

> Elodie Oyelaran, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

August Delacroix is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unclaimed wartime letters in European municipal collections. Atlas and Clock is their second novel, following a widely praised debut about railway porters during the armistice.
