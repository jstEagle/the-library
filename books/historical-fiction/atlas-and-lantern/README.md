# Atlas and Lantern

*by Jude Blackwood*

> Hazel Okafor, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the census taker asks one question too many, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ice breaks on the river.
