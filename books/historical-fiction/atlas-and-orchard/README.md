# Atlas and Orchard

*by Juniper Galloway*

> Noor Vasquez, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must choose which family member gets the last berth before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Juniper Galloway is the pen name of a former cartographic archivist who spent a decade restoring war-damaged maps from border regions. She writes historical fiction about the people who draw lines on paper and the people who live inside them.
