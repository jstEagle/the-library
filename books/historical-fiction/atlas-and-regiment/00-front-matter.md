# Atlas and Regiment

**by Lior Ravensworth**

> Lior Winterbourne, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ship sails on the morning tide.
