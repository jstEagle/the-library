# Atlas and Typewriter

*by Isandro Vasquez*

> Coral Lockridge, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Isandro Vasquez is a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring antique printing equipment in coastal trading towns before turning to fiction. He writes historical novels about the people who handle other people's words.
