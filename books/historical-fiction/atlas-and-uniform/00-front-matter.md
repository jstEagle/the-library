# Atlas and Uniform

**by Vesper Lockridge**

> Wendell Delacroix, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Vesper Lockridge is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unclaimed letters from European field hospitals. Atlas and Uniform is their first novel, drawn from the marginalia of ledgers no one thought worth saving.
