# August and the Bridge

*by Petra Yarrow*

> Elodie Nakamura, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Petra Yarrow is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing cartographic collections in coastal libraries, where she first encountered atlases with pages deliberately torn out. She writes historical fiction about the people history forgot to record.
