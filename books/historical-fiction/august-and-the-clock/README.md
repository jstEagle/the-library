Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

E. M. Ishikawa is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping ledgers and indenture contracts from colonial-era trading companies. Their fiction draws on the marginalia of those records — the names struck through, the deaths logged as weather.
