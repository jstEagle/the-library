## About the author

Xanthe Vane is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial shipping manifests in Southeast Asian port records. August and the Typewriter is her second novel, drawn from a salvaged ledger she once found sewn into the binding of an account book.
