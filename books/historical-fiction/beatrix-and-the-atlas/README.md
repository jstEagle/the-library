# Beatrix and the Atlas

*by Jude Vasquez*

> Orsen Okafor, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Jude Vasquez is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing displaced-persons records before turning to fiction. They write from a canal-side flat in Ghent, where they keep an unfinished atlas of towns that no longer exist.
