# Beatrix and the Bridge

*by Kestrel Crane*

> Paloma Iversen, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.
