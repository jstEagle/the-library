# Beatrix and the Harbor

*by Xanthe Sterling*

> Elodie Lockridge, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Xanthe Sterling is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing harbor court records along the northern coast. Their fiction is drawn from the marginalia of forgotten ledgers, where ordinary people fought to be remembered accurately.
