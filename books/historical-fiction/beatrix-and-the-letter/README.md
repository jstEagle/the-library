# Beatrix and the Letter

*by T. M. Amberdale*

> Felix Galloway, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.
