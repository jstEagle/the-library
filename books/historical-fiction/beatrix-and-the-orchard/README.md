# Beatrix and the Orchard

*by Emrys Delacroix*

> Marguerite Thornbury, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Emrys Delacroix is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial trading ledgers across three continents. They write historical fiction that recovers the voices ledger-keepers were paid to omit.
