# Beatrix and the Regiment

*by Quentin Reyes*

> Felix Halloran, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, has spent five years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.
