# Bram and the Bridge

*by L. M. Hartwell*

> Tamsin Ishikawa, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, has spent twelve years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.
