# Bram and the Clock

**by B. M. Okafor**

> Theo Fairweather, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ship sails on the morning tide.
