# Bram and the Harbor

**by Ulyana Voss**

> Ansel Eastwick, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, has spent twelve years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Ulyana Voss is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing port ledgers and indenture records along the northern coast. Her fiction draws on the faded ink of shipping manifests and the untold lives between their lines.
