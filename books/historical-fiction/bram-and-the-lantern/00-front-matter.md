## About the author

Casper Galloway is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing diplomatic correspondence from collapsed states, an experience that taught him how much history turns on what fits inside an envelope. Bram and the Lantern is their second novel, following a well-received debut about railway telegraph operators during a border war.
