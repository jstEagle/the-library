# Bram and the Regiment

*by Yara Halloran*

> Rafael Yarrow, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Yara Halloran is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing regimental records from the Great War, where she first noticed the small, deliberate gaps in official rolls. She writes historical fiction about the people history files under 'miscellaneous.'
