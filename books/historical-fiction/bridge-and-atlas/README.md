# Bridge and Atlas

*by Hazel Ishikawa*

> Casper Oyelaran, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Hazel Ishikawa is a former letterpress apprentice who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment in port towns before turning to fiction. She writes historical novels about the people history forgets to name — the hands that set the type behind great events.
