# Bridge and Harbor

*by August Kavanagh*

> Wendell Vane, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, has spent seven years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

August Kavanagh is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing port ledgers and intercepted letters from the postwar years. Bridge and Harbor is their second novel, drawn from the silences between official records.
