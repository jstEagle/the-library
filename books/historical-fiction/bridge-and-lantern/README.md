# Bridge and Lantern

*by Lior Marchetti*

> Ivo Okafor, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the century turns.

## About the author

Lior Marchetti is the pen name of a former field medic turned archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial-era hospital records in West African and Caribbean repositories. Bridge and Lantern is their first novel, drawn from marginalia found in a genuine casualty ledger.
