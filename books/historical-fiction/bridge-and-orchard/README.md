# Bridge and Orchard

*by Zephyrine Blackwood*

> Lior Nystrom, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Zephyrine Blackwood is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing merchant-company ledgers from coastal trading ports. Their fiction explores how ordinary clerks, divers, and dockworkers become the unlikely custodians of history.
