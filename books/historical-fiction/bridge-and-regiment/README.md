# Bridge and Regiment

*by Yara Galloway*

> Felix Lockridge, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the century turns.
