# Bridge and Typewriter

*by Orsen Yarrow*

> Oscar Ishikawa, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Orsen Yarrow is the pen name of a former museum conservator who spent years restoring mechanical instruments in small-town collections across the northern river valleys. They write historical fiction about the people history overlooks, one repaired object at a time.
