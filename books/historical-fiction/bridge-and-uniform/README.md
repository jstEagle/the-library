# Bridge and Uniform

*by Orsen Amberdale*

> Bram Okafor, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ship sails on the morning tide.
