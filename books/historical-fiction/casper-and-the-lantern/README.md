# Casper and the Lantern

*by Lior Sterling*

> Hazel Thornbury, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the century turns.

## About the author

Lior Sterling is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse keepers' logs along the English coast. Their fiction draws on the weathered voices of those records to explore quiet lives caught in loud histories.
