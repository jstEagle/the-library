# Casper and the Ledger

*by Rosalind Okafor*

> Rafael Marchetti, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Rosalind Okafor is a former archivist who spent six years cataloguing maritime court records from coastal garrisons, where she first encountered ledgers seized as evidence. She writes historical fiction about the paperwork of power and the people it was never meant to describe.
