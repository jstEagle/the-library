# Casper and the Letter

*by Hazel Ravensworth*

> Wendell Montrose, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Hazel Ravensworth is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing shipping ledgers from forgotten river towns. She writes historical fiction about the people those ledgers were never meant to remember.
