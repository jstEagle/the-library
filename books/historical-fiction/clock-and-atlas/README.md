# Clock and Atlas

*by Petra Iversen*

> Oscar Amberdale, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, has spent twelve years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ice breaks on the river.
