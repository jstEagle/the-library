# Clock and Bridge

**by Ada Brightwater**

> Wendell Almeida, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Ada Brightwater is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping ledgers from colonial river ports before turning to fiction. She writes about people history recorded only as line items.
