# Clock and Compass

*by Kestrel Joubert*

> Mara Montrose, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Kestrel Joubert is the pen name of a former military archivist who spent a decade cataloguing regimental records and the gaps between them. Fascinated by the clerks, nurses, and quartermasters who kept history's real books, Joubert writes fiction from the margins of official accounts.
