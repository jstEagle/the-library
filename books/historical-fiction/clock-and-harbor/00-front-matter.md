# Clock and Harbor

**by Ulyana Nystrom**

> Theo Nystrom, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent five years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Ulyana Nystrom is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century print equipment in Baltic port towns. Her fiction draws on family archives of confiscated pamphlets and the oral histories of printers' families who survived three occupations.
