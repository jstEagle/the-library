## About the author

Silas Ravensworth is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing suffrage-era letters in a Yorkshire county records office before turning to fiction. He writes in a converted tollhouse, where he keeps a 1911 census form framed above his desk as a reminder that every name was once a person.
