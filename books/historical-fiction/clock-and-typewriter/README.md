# Clock and Typewriter

*by Xanthe Oyelaran*

> Odile Whitlock, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must choose which family member gets the last berth before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Xanthe Oyelaran is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping manifests and indenture records from coastal trading houses before turning to fiction. She writes about people history filed away under 'miscellaneous,' and lives within earshot of a working harbor.
