# Compass and Clock

*by Bram Dunmore*

> Wendell Eriksen, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must choose which family member gets the last berth before the century turns.

## About the author

Bram Dunmore is the pen name of a former maritime museum archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse logs and horological ledgers along the North Sea coast. Compass and Clock is their second novel, following a well-received debut about a cartographer who maps a town that no longer exists.
