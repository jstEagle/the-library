**by Lior Hartwell**

## About the author

Lior Hartwell is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing wartime port records along the English coast. Their fiction is drawn from the marginalia of official documents—the names written in pencil, in grief, in the spaces the record keepers left blank.
