# Compass and Ledger

*by Beatrix Ravensworth*

> Ulyana Eriksen, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Beatrix Ravensworth is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing wartime printing presses and censored newspapers across Northern Europe. She writes historical fiction about the people whose hands actually made history — typesetters, couriers, and clerks.
