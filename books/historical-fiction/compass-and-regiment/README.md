# Compass and Regiment

*by Quentin Yarrow*

> Sable Sterling, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, has spent ten years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the century turns.

## About the author

Quentin Yarrow is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial-era maps before turning to fiction. They write about the people history forgot to name, and live within sight of a harbor that no longer appears on modern charts.
