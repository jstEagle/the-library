## About the author

Paloma Whitlock is a former archivist who spent six years cataloguing suffrage-era correspondence in regional historical societies, where she first encountered the anonymous couriers whose names never made the newspapers. She writes from a converted ferry house on the northern coast, where the bridge closes every winter.
