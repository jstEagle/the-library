# Coral and the Atlas

*by Oscar Eriksen*

> Zephyrine Joubert, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.
