# Coral and the Bridge

*by Jonah Pemberton*

> Ansel Sandoval, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, has spent fifteen years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the century turns.
