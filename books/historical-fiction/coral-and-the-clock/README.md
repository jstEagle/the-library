# Coral and the Clock

*by Vesper Vasquez*

> Wendell Vasquez, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, has spent five years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Vesper Vasquez is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging diplomatic correspondence from interwar Europe before turning to fiction. They write from a coastal town where the tide charts still hang in the post office, and where every letter feels like it might be the last one.
