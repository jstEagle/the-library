# Cyrus and the Letter

*by Emrys Sandoval*

> Edmund Sterling, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Emrys Sandoval is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unsent letters from the First World War in provincial English collections. Their fiction is drawn from the marginalia, ration books, and hospital ledgers that history forgot to index.
