# Cyrus and the Typewriter

**by Wendell Brightwater**

> Gideon Winterbourne, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Wendell Brightwater is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping ledgers from the dying days of the pearl trade. This is their second novel, written largely on a 1920s typewriter rescued from a flooded customs house.
