# Dominic and the Atlas

*by Yara Underhill*

> Wren Brightwater, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must choose which family member gets the last berth before the century turns.
