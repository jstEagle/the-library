# Dominic and the Bridge

*by Tamsin Vasquez*

> Mirabel Ravensworth, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the census taker asks one question too many, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.
