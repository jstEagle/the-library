# Dominic and the Harbor

*by Isandro Joubert*

> Edmund Ashgrove, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Isandro Joubert is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing tide ledgers and lighthouse logs along the northern coast. Their fiction explores how ordinary people carry unbearable knowledge across impossible distances.
