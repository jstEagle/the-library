# Dominic and the Ledger

*by Casper Almeida*

> Dominic Yarrow, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the century turns.
