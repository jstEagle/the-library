# Dominic and the Letter

**by Bram Almeida**

> Rosalind Pemberton, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it.
