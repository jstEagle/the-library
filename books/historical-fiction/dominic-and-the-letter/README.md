# Dominic and the Letter

*by Bram Almeida*

> Rosalind Pemberton, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ice breaks on the river.
