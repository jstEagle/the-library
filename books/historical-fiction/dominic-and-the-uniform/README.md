# Dominic and the Uniform

*by Edmund Pemberton*

> Jude Ravensworth, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ice breaks on the river.
