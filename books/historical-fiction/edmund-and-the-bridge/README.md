# Edmund and the Bridge

*by Sable Villalobos*

> Odile Brightwater, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ice breaks on the river.
