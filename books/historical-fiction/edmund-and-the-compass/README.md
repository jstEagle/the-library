# Edmund and the Compass

*by Felix Marchetti*

> Tamsin Thornbury, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.
