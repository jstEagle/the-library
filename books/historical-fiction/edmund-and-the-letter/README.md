# Edmund and the Letter

*by Petra Vane*

> Marguerite Sterling, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.
