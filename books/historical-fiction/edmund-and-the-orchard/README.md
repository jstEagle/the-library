# Edmund and the Orchard

*by Noor Almeida*

> Paloma Whitlock, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, has spent twenty years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.
