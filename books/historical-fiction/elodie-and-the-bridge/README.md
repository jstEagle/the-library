# Elodie and the Bridge

*by Theo Ashgrove*

> Orsen Coldharbour, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, has spent seven years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
