# Elodie and the Clock

*by Emrys Sandoval*

> Emrys Sandoval, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, keeps a low profile and busy hands. To get through this, she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
