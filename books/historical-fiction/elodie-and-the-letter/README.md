# Elodie and the Letter

*by August Blackwood*

> Kestrel Coldharbour, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the census taker asks one question too many, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the century turns.
