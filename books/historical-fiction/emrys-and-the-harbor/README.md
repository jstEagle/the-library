# Emrys and the Harbor

*by Oscar Eastwick*

> Orsen Crane, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ice breaks on the river.
