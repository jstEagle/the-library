# Emrys and the Lantern

*by P. M. Halloran*

> Cyrus Thornbury, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, has spent seven years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

P. M. Halloran is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing regimental records in provincial English libraries, where the gaps between documents proved more telling than the entries. Emrys and the Lantern is their second novel exploring the quiet people history forgets to write down.
