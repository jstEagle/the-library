# Emrys and the Orchard

*by Zephyrine Nakamura*

> Orsen Vasquez, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Zephyrine Nakamura is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing indenture ledgers from colonial trading ports. Their fiction excavates the small, unrecorded lives found in the margins of those documents.
