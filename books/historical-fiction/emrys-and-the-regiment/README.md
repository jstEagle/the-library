# Emrys and the Regiment

*by Xanthe Voss*

> Mara Dravenmoor, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, has spent five years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Xanthe Voss is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing military pension records and became obsessed with the gaps between official histories and lived ones. They write from a converted stable office in Yorkshire, surrounded by nineteenth-century ledgers and an ill-tempered cat named Quill.
