# Emrys and the Uniform

*by Lena Blackwood*

> Coral Ashgrove, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, has spent ten years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.

## About the author
