# Felix and the Bridge

*by Elodie Reyes*

> Hazel Almeida, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
