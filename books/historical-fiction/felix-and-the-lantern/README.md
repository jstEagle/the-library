Now they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Elodie Pemberton is the pen name of a former letterpress apprentice who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment in coastal mill towns. Their fiction explores the lives of people history recorded only in margins and misprints.
