# Felix and the Letter

*by Wren Zhou*

> Oscar Sterling, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the census taker asks one question too many, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the century turns.

## About the author

Wren Zhou is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing port-city shipping manifests and indenture ledgers before turning to fiction. They write about people history recorded only as line items.
