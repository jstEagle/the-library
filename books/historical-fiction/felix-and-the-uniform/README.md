# Felix and the Uniform

*by Emrys Oyelaran*

> Casper Nakamura, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Emrys Oyelaran is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing wartime correspondence in coastal municipal collections, where the gaps in the record fascinated her more than the entries. She writes from a house within sight of a decommissioned lighthouse, and Felix and the Uniform is her second novel.
