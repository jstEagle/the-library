## About the author

Sebastian Almeida is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping ledgers from colonial trading companies before turning to fiction. He writes from a coastal town whose harbor he can see from his window, and he insists every corruption in his novels is at least half true.
