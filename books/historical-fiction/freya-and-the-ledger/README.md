# Freya and the Ledger

*by Lena Galloway*

> Noor Eastwick, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Lena Galloway is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing displaced-persons records in northern Europe. She writes historical fiction about the paperwork of war—the ledgers, manifests, and letters that outlive the people they name.
