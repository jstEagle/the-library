# Freya and the Letter

**by Rosalind Yarrow**

> Rafael Fairweather, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Rosalind Yarrow is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing printers' ledgers and broadsides from the revolutionary era. She writes from a converted bindery on the coast, where she still sets type by hand.
