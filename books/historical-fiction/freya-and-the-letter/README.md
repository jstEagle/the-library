# Freya and the Letter

*by Rosalind Yarrow*

> Rafael Fairweather, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the century turns.
