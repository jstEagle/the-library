# Freya and the Uniform

*by Lena Thornbury*

> Coral Dravenmoor, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the century turns.

## About the author

Lena Thornbury is the pen name of a former cartographic archivist who spent a decade cataloguing disputed border maps before turning to fiction. She writes historical novels about the people who draw lines on paper and the people who die because of them.
