# Gideon and the Atlas

*by Vesper Montrose*

> Cyrus Eastwick, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ice breaks on the river.
