## About the author

Astrid Fairweather is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing diplomatic correspondence from collapsed interwar states, an obsession that seeps into every page she writes. She lives near a slow, ice-bound river and insists she began writing fiction only to give the forgotten couriers of history someone to carry their letters home.
