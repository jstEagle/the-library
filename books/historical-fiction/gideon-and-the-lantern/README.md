# Gideon and the Lantern

*by Mara Marchetti*

> Xanthe Thornbury, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must choose which family member gets the last berth before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
