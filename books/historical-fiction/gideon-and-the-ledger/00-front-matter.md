# Gideon and the Ledger

**by Ulyana Voss**

> Sebastian Halloran, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author
