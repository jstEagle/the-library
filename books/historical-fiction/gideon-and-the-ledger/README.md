# Gideon and the Ledger

*by Ulyana Voss*

> Sebastian Halloran, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.
