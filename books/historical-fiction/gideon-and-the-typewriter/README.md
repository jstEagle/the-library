# Gideon and the Typewriter

*by Noor Thornbury*

> Ines Thackeray, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ice breaks on the river.
