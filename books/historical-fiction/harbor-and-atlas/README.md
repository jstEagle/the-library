# Harbor and Atlas

*by Odile Delacroix*

> Cyrus Dunmore, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ship sails on the morning tide.
