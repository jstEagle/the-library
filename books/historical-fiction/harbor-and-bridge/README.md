# Harbor and Bridge

*by Orsen Lockridge*

> Elodie Winterbourne, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Orsen Lockridge is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse station logs along the northern coast. Harbor and Bridge is his third novel, drawn from a real requisition ledger he once found misfiled in a customs house basement.
