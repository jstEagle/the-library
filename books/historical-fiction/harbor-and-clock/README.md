# Harbor and Clock

*by Silas Kavanagh*

> Zephyrine Sterling, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Silas Kavanagh is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing confiscated letters from occupied port towns. They write from a house above a decommissioned lighthouse, where the foghorn still sounds once a year, on the anniversary of an armistice nobody celebrates.
