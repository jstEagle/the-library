Now they must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Wren Ashgrove is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing port ledgers and lighthouse logs along the northern coast. Their fiction draws on the rhythms of tide tables and the quiet heroism of tradespeople who keep cities running.
