## About the author

Zephyrine Brightwater is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing harbormasters' ledgers along the Atlantic coast, where she first encountered records deliberately altered to hide whole families from history. She writes historical fiction about the people time tried to erase, and lives in a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage with too many clocks.
