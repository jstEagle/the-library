# Harbor and Letter

## About the author

Tamsin Sterling is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping manifests and indenture records from forgotten port towns. Harbor and Letter is their second novel, drawn from a single marginal note found in a ledger: 'promised, in peacetime.'
