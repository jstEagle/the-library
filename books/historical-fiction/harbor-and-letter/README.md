# Harbor and Letter

*by Tamsin Sterling*

> Bram Thornbury, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Tamsin Sterling is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping manifests and indenture records from forgotten port towns. Harbor and Letter is their second novel, drawn from a single marginal note found in a ledger: 'promised, in peacetime.'
