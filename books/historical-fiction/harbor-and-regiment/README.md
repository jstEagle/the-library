# Harbor and Regiment

*by A. M. Hartwell*

> Hazel Blackwood, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, has spent twelve years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
