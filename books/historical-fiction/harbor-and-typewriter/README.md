# Harbor and Typewriter

*by Silas Ishikawa*

> Astrid Reyes, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent seven years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Silas Ishikawa is the pen name of a former newspaper compositor who spent a decade in coastal archives restoring water-damaged periodicals. Harbor and Typewriter is his third novel, drawn from his obsession with the printers who outlasted the regimes that employed them.
