# Harbor and Uniform

**by Gideon Nystrom**

> Hazel Underhill, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Gideon Nystrom is a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping ledgers in coastal repositories, where he first encountered the gaps left by victors' record-keeping. He writes historical fiction about the people history tried not to notice.
