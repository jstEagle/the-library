# Harbor and Uniform

*by Gideon Nystrom*

> Hazel Underhill, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Gideon Nystrom is a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping ledgers in coastal repositories, where he first encountered the gaps left by victors' record-keeping. He writes historical fiction about the people history tried not to notice.
