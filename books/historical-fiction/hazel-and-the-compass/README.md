# Hazel and the Compass

*by Paloma Underhill*

> Petra Voss, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Paloma Underhill is the pen name of a former letterpress apprentice who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century print-shop equipment in small German towns. She writes historical fiction about ordinary people who keep records when power would prefer they didn't.
