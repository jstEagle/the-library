# Ines and the Atlas

*by Tamsin Thornbury*

> Rosalind Ravensworth, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ship sails on the morning tide.
