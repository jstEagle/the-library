# Ines and the Compass

*by Oscar Halloran*

> Astrid Crane, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ship sails on the morning tide.
