# Ines and the Ledger

*by Mara Iversen*

> Rosalind Vasquez, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the census taker asks one question too many, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the century turns.
