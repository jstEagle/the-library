# Ines and the Typewriter

*by Mirabel Winterbourne*

> Odile Galloway, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the century turns.
