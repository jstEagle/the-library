# Isandro and the Lantern

*by Wendell Montrose*

> Rosalind Amberdale, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Wendell Montrose is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping ledgers from nineteenth-century river ports. Their fiction draws on the marginalia of those records — the names scrawled in margins by people history nearly forgot.
