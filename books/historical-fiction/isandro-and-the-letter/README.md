# Isandro and the Letter

*by M. M. Coldharbour*

> Hazel Sterling, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.
