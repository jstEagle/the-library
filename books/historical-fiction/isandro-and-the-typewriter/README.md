To get through this, he must choose which family member gets the last berth before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Xanthe Nystrom is the pen name of a former archival cartographer who spent a decade restoring water-damaged maps from occupied European towns. She writes historical fiction about people whose quiet work — ink, type, and paper — decides who lives.
