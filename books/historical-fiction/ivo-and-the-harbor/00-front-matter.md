# Ivo and the Harbor

**by A. M. Quintana**

> Sebastian Oyelaran, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, has spent twenty years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.

## About the author

A. M. Quintana is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing confiscated municipal records from port towns, work that seeded this novel's obsession with what paperwork remembers and what people survive. They live near a working harbor and write at dawn, when the fog still owns the water.
