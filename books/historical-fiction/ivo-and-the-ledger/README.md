# Ivo and the Ledger

*by Isandro Delacroix*

> Mirabel Dunmore, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the century turns.
