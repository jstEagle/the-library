# Ivo and the Letter

*by Noor Iversen*

> Rafael Halloran, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must choose which family member gets the last berth before the century turns.
