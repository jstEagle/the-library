# Ivo and the Orchard

*by Coral Iversen*

> Wendell Dravenmoor, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

Coral Iversen is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing provincial records before turning to fiction. Her work explores what ordinary people carry when institutions decide what history is worth keeping.
