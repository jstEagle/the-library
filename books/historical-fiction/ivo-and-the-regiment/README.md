# Ivo and the Regiment

*by Paloma Amberdale*

> Ivo Crane, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.
