## About the author

Casper Ishikawa is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing municipal records in small alpine towns, where he learned that the most dangerous documents are the ones nobody was meant to keep. He writes historical fiction about ordinary people caught holding evidence they cannot put down.
