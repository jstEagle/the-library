# Jonah and the Compass

*by L. M. Delacroix*

> Paloma Vane, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, has spent five years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the census taker asks one question too many, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

L. M. Delacroix is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial trading ledgers along the Pearl Coast. Their novels excavate the lives history recorded only as line items.
