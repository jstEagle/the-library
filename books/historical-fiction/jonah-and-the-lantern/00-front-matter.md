# Jonah and the Lantern

**by Zephyrine Winterbourne**

> Freya Halloran, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, has spent five years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Zephyrine Winterbourne is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial shipping records before turning to fiction. She writes from a coastal cottage where, she insists, every drawer contains at least one unfinished letter.
