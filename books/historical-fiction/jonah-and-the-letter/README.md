# Jonah and the Letter

*by August Dunmore*

> Gideon Montrose, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, has spent twenty years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ship sails on the morning tide.
