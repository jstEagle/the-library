# Jude and the Harbor

*by Ansel Eriksen*

> Orsen Delacroix, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ansel Eriksen is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse logs from the North Atlantic coast. Their fiction draws on the quiet heroism of keepers, smugglers, and the people history forgot to record.
