# Jude and the Ledger

*by Xanthe Thornbury*

> Quentin Okonkwo, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Xanthe Thornbury is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing maritime correspondence from coastal lighthouse stations before turning to fiction. She writes from a converted signal house on the northern coast, where she collects tide tables and unfinished letters.
