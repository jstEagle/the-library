# Jude and the Typewriter

*by Vesper Amberdale*

> Elodie Sterling, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Vesper Amberdale is the pen name of a former letterpress printer who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century presses in provincial archives. Their novels explore the people who made history with their hands rather than their names.
