# Juniper and the Atlas

*by Kestrel Nystrom*

> Odile Sandoval, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
