## About the author

Isandro Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial survey maps before turning to fiction. He writes from a coastal cottage, where he keeps an unfinished 19th-century atlas on his desk as a reminder that every line on a map was drawn by someone with something to gain.
