# Juniper and the Compass

*by Isandro Dravenmoor*

> Rosalind Okonkwo, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Isandro Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial survey maps before turning to fiction. He writes from a coastal cottage, where he keeps an unfinished 19th-century atlas on his desk as a reminder that every line on a map was drawn by someone with something to gain.
