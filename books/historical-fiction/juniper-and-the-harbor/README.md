# Juniper and the Harbor

*by Silas Villalobos*

> Cyrus Underhill, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent twelve years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the century turns.
