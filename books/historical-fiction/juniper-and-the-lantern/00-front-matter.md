# Juniper and the Lantern

**by Rafael Galloway**

> Hazel Villalobos, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, has spent ten years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
