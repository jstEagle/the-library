# Juniper and the Lantern

*by Rafael Galloway*

> Hazel Villalobos, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, has spent ten years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ship sails on the morning tide.
