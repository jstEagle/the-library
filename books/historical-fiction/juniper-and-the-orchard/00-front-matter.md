# Juniper and the Orchard

**by Cyrus Whitlock**

> Freya Coldharbour, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Cyrus Whitlock is a former horologist who spent a decade restoring church tower clocks across northern England before turning to fiction. He writes historical novels about machines, memory, and the people history forgets to record.
