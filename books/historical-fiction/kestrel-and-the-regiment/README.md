# Kestrel and the Regiment

*by Freya Eastwick*

> Gideon Crane, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, has spent five years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Freya Eastwick is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing military hospital records from the Peninsular War before turning to fiction. She writes from a converted tollhouse on the Yorkshire moors, where she keeps her own ledger of names found in margins.
