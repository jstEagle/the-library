# Lantern and Bridge

*by Mirabel Okonkwo*

> Rafael Voss, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ship sails on the morning tide.
