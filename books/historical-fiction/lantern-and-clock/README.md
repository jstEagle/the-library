# Lantern and Clock

*by Odile Amberdale*

> Theo Oyelaran, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Odile Amberdale is the pen name of a former triage nurse turned archivist who spent a decade cataloguing wartime field hospital records in three languages. Lantern and Clock is her second novel, written in a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage on the northern coast.
