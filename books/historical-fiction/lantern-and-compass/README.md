# Lantern and Compass

*by Freya Thackeray*

> Mirabel Marchetti, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must choose which family member gets the last berth before the century turns.

## About the author

Freya Thackeray is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing trading-company ledgers in colonial port archives. She writes historical fiction about the people the ledgers counted but never named.
