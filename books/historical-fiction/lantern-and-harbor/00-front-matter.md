# Lantern and Harbor

**by Rafael Whitlock**

> Lior Ishikawa, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must choose which family member gets the last berth before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Rafael Whitlock is the pen name of a former typesetter who spent a decade restoring letterpress equipment in coastal archives before turning to fiction. Their novels examine how ordinary trades become extraordinary witnesses to history.
