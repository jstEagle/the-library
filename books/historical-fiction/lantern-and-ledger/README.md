# Lantern and Ledger

*by Jude Montrose*

> Wendell Almeida, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Jude Montrose is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment before turning to fiction. They write historical novels about the people who made records—and the people who tried to erase them.
