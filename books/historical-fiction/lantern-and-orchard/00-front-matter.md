## About the author

Emrys Okonkwo is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing suppressed newspapers in coastal municipal collections. Lantern and Orchard is their second novel, drawn from a lifelong fascination with the people who kept ink flowing when it was dangerous to do so.
