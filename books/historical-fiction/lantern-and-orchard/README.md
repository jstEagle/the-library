# Lantern and Orchard

*by Emrys Okonkwo*

> Paloma Eriksen, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the census taker asks one question too many, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Emrys Okonkwo is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing suppressed newspapers in coastal municipal collections. Lantern and Orchard is their second novel, drawn from a lifelong fascination with the people who kept ink flowing when it was dangerous to do so.
