# Lantern and Typewriter

*by Vesper Vane*

> Freya Ashgrove, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Vesper Vane is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unsent letters from the First World War before turning to fiction. They write from a converted chapel on the coast, where they keep, they claim, one lamp lit at all times for the unnamed.
