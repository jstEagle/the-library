# Lantern and Uniform

**by Freya Okafor**

> Sable Eriksen, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the census taker asks one question too many, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Freya Okafor is a former archivist who spent six years cataloguing displaced-persons records in post-conflict regions before turning to fiction. She writes historical novels about ordinary people who refuse to let institutions finish their sentences for them.
