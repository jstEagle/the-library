# Ledger and Atlas

*by Wendell Hartwell*

> Ada Iversen, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ice breaks on the river.
