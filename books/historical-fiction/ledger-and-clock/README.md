# Ledger and Clock

*by Elodie Yarrow*

> Edmund Ashgrove, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must choose which family member gets the last berth before the century turns.
