# Ledger and Harbor

*by Cyrus Dunmore*

> Jonah Blackwood, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.
