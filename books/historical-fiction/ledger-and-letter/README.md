## About the author

N. M. Voss is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging private correspondence from the First World War era, an experience that seeded this novel's fascination with the lives folded inside envelopes. Voss writes under a pen name and lives quietly, claiming to prefer the company of dead letter writers to living ones.
