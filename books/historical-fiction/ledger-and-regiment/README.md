# Ledger and Regiment

*by Wendell Pemberton*

> Hazel Hartwell, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ship sails on the morning tide.
