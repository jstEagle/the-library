# Ledger and Uniform

*by Wendell Iversen*

> August Voss, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must choose which family member gets the last berth before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Wendell Iversen is a retired horological conservator who spent thirty years restoring tower clocks across northern Europe before turning to fiction. He writes in the margins of maintenance logs, convinced that every mechanism keeps time for someone who cannot.
