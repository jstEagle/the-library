# Lena and the Atlas

**by Beatrix Marchetti**

> Vesper Yarrow, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, has spent twelve years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must choose which family member gets the last berth before the century turns.
