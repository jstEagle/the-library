# Lena and the Compass

**by A. M. Halloran**

## About the author

A. M. Halloran is the pen name of a former typesetter's apprentice who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century letterpress equipment before turning to fiction. Halloran writes historical novels about ordinary hands caught in extraordinary machinery of history.
