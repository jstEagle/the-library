# Lena and the Ledger

*by August Ashgrove*

> Petra Halloran, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

August Ashgrove is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing maritime records from the First World War era. They write historical fiction about ordinary people whose small acts of record-keeping become acts of resistance.
