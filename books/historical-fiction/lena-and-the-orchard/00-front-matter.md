# Lena and the Orchard

**by Ada Fairweather**

## About the author

Ada Fairweather is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping manifests and indenture records from coastal trading ports. Her fiction is drawn from the marginalia — the names written small in the corners of contracts.
