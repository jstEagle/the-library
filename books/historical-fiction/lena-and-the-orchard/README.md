# Lena and the Orchard

*by Ada Fairweather*

> Odile Ishikawa, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the century turns.

## About the author

Ada Fairweather is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping manifests and indenture records from coastal trading ports. Her fiction is drawn from the marginalia — the names written small in the corners of contracts.
