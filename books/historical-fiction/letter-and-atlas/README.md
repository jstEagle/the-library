# Letter and Atlas

*by Paloma Crane*

> Coral Amberdale, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.
