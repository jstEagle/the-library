# Letter and Clock

*by Elodie Blackwood*

> Lena Okonkwo, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Elodie Blackwood is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing confiscated letters in municipal collections across Eastern Europe. She writes historical fiction about the people history filed away, and 'Letter and Clock' is her third novel.
