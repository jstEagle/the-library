# Letter and Compass

*by Gideon Delacroix*

> Elodie Blackwood, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, has spent twelve years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ice breaks on the river.
