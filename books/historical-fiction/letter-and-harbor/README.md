# Letter and Harbor

*by Kestrel Thackeray*

> Emrys Thackeray, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Kestrel Thackeray is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unclaimed letters from wartime field hospitals. They write from a coastal cottage, where they claim the tide brings in better stories than any archive.
