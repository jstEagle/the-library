# Letter and Lantern

*by Kestrel Blackwood*

> Bram Vane, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Kestrel Blackwood is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing suffragette correspondence in regional newspaper morgues. Letter and Lantern is their second novel, inspired by an unmarked parcel found sealed inside a 1914 ledger.
