# Letter and Ledger

*by Coral Almeida*

> Silas Crane, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.
