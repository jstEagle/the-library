# Letter and Orchard

**by Ada Ishikawa**

> Vesper Okonkwo, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, has spent ten years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ada Ishikawa is the pen name of a former archivist who spent six years cataloguing colonial trading ledgers before turning to fiction. She writes from a coastal town where pearling history is still contested, and her novels center on the people records leave out.
