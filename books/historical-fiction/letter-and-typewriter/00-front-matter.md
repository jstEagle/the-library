# Letter and Typewriter

**by Beatrix Ishikawa**

> Silas Underhill, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, has spent twelve years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Beatrix Ishikawa is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing military correspondence and became obsessed with the letters that never made it into official histories. She writes from a farmhouse full of typewriters, none of which agree with each other.
