# Letter and Uniform

*by E. M. Voss*

> Marguerite Sandoval, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.

## About the author

E. M. Voss is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial shipping ledgers before turning to fiction. Their work explores how ordinary people are recorded, erased, and remembered by institutions.
