But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Bram Ravensworth is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing Baltic maritime records before turning to fiction. His novels explore how ordinary tradespeople navigate history's collisions, drawing on his fascination with clocks, ledgers, and the paper trails people leave behind.
