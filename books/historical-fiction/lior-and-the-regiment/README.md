# Lior and the Regiment

*by Wren Hartwell*

> Mara Hartwell, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, has spent twenty years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the census taker asks one question too many, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Wren Hartwell is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging uncatalogued letters from regional suffrage chapters. They live in a river town where the old courthouse still smells of coal smoke, and they write about the people history files under 'miscellaneous.'
