# Lior and the Uniform

*by Elodie Fairweather*

> Sable Ishikawa, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Elodie Fairweather is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing wartime hospital records and became haunted by the names that appeared in no official document. She writes historical fiction about the people history files under 'miscellaneous,' and lives near a river that freezes every winter.
