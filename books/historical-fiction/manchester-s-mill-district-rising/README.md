*by Lior Yarrow*

> Marguerite Eastwick, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, has spent three years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.
