# Mara and the Compass

*by Paloma Sterling*

> Coral Villalobos, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.
