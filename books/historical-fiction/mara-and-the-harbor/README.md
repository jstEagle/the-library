# Mara and the Harbor

*by Gideon Crane*

> Mirabel Lockridge, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Gideon Crane is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse logs and Admiralty inquiry records along the northern coast. This is their third novel, written in a keeper's cottage rented through one full storm season.
