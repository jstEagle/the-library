# Mara and the Ledger

**by Mara Montrose**

> Dominic Quintana, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, has spent twelve years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
