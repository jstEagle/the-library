# Mara and the Ledger

*by Mara Montrose*

> Dominic Quintana, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, has spent twelve years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Mara Montrose is a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing trading-company ledgers in coastal archives, where she first encountered the marginalia of indentured clerks. She writes historical fiction about the people history filed under 'miscellaneous.'
