# Mara and the Letter

*by C. M. Underhill*

> Coral Ashgrove, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, has spent seven years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

C. M. Underhill is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse keepers' logs along the Atlantic coast. Their fiction draws on the unspoken bargains small coastal communities made with the sea and with the law.
