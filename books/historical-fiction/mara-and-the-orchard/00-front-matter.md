## About the author

Sable Thackeray is the pen name of a former horologist's assistant who spent a decade restoring tower clocks in river towns along the northern trade routes. Their fiction is drawn from repair ledgers, ferry manifests, and the stories clockkeepers told about the families who paid for the winding.
