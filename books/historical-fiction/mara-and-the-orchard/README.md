# Mara and the Orchard

*by Sable Thackeray*

> Isandro Nakamura, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Sable Thackeray is the pen name of a former horologist's assistant who spent a decade restoring tower clocks in river towns along the northern trade routes. Their fiction is drawn from repair ledgers, ferry manifests, and the stories clockkeepers told about the families who paid for the winding.
