# Marguerite and the Clock

**by Bram Halloran**

> August Villalobos, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, has spent twelve years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Bram Halloran is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing suffrage-era letters and learned that history is decided by whoever files first. This is their third novel of quiet people caught in loud histories.
