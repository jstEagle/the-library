# Marguerite and the Ledger

*by Juniper Zhou*

> Ivo Hartwell, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, has spent ten years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Juniper Zhou is the pen name of a former archivist who spent six years cataloguing displaced-persons records from interwar Europe before turning to fiction. They write about borders, paperwork, and the people history files under 'miscellaneous.'
