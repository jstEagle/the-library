# Marguerite and the Letter Revisited

*by Jonah Reyes*

> Ivo Joubert, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, has spent fifteen years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Jonah Reyes is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging seized civil records in coastal municipal libraries, where the margins of ledgers told truer stories than the entries. This is their third novel, all concerned with people history recorded only in passing.
