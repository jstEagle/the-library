## About the author

Rosalind Pemberton is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing provincial court records and horological ledgers from the 1790s. She writes historical fiction about ordinary people caught in the machinery of history, and lives near a decommissioned observatory she has never been allowed inside.
