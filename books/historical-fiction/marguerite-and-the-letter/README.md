# Marguerite and the Letter

*by Rosalind Pemberton*

> Silas Pemberton, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Rosalind Pemberton is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing provincial court records and horological ledgers from the 1790s. She writes historical fiction about ordinary people caught in the machinery of history, and lives near a decommissioned observatory she has never been allowed inside.
