# Mirabel and the Compass

*by Ines Vane*

> Juniper Lockridge, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, has spent twenty years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the century turns.
