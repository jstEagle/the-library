# Mirabel and the Harbor

*by Petra Eastwick*

> Ines Kavanagh, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.
