# Mirabel and the Letter

*by Vesper Marchetti*

> Paloma Voss, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Vesper Marchetti is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial trading company ledgers in coastal archives. Their fiction draws on the forgotten indentured laborers whose names appear only as marginalia in shipping manifests.
