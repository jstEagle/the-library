# Mirabel and the Uniform

*by Isandro Nakamura*

> Theo Joubert, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must choose which family member gets the last berth before the century turns.

## About the author

Isandro Nakamura is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the private letters of displaced families. They write historical fiction about the small, stubborn households history forgets to record.
