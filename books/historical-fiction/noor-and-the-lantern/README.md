# Noor and the Lantern

*by Wendell Crane*

> August Ashgrove, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Wendell Crane is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the confiscated letters of displaced families. He writes historical fiction from a farmhouse where, he insists, nothing of importance is ever kept.
