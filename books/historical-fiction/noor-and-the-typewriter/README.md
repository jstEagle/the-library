# Noor and the Typewriter

*by Ivo Delacroix*

> Odile Voss, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the census taker asks one question too many, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ivo Delacroix is the pen name of a former restorer of antique office machines who spent a decade repairing typewriters in archive basements across Northern Europe. Their fiction is drawn from family letters discovered sewn into the lining of a census ledger.
