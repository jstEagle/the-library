## About the author

Yara Reyes is a former bookbinder who spent a decade restoring damaged nineteenth-century ledgers and parish registers, an experience that taught her how easily records can be lost, altered, or quietly destroyed. She writes historical fiction about ordinary people who keep accounts — of grain, of names, of grief — and she lives near a river that freezes every winter.
