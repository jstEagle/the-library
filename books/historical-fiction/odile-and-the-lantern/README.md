# Odile and the Lantern

*by Yara Reyes*

> Ivo Eastwick, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Yara Reyes is a former bookbinder who spent a decade restoring damaged nineteenth-century ledgers and parish registers, an experience that taught her how easily records can be lost, altered, or quietly destroyed. She writes historical fiction about ordinary people who keep accounts — of grain, of names, of grief — and she lives near a river that freezes every winter.
