# Odile and the Orchard

*by August Almeida*

> Paloma Vasquez, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ship sails on the morning tide.
