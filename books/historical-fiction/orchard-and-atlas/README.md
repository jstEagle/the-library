# Orchard and Atlas

*by Paloma Almeida*

> Beatrix Fairweather, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Paloma Almeida is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing suppressed municipal records in coastal trading cities. Orchard and Atlas is her second novel, following a well-received debut about lighthouse keepers and debt.
