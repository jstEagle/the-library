# Orchard and Clock

*by Dominic Lockridge*

> Emrys Vane, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ship sails on the morning tide.
