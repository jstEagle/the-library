# Orchard and Compass

*by T. M. Underhill*

> Marguerite Lockridge, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, has spent fifteen years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the century turns.

## About the author

T. M. Underhill is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping ledgers from Pacific coast trading posts before turning to fiction. They write about the people history filed under 'miscellaneous' — the indentured, the interpreters, and the children who kept both sides' secrets.
