# Orchard and Harbor

*by Freya Winterbourne*

> Oscar Thackeray, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Freya Winterbourne is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing wartime cartographic collections in northern European libraries. She writes historical fiction about the people history forgot to name, and lives near a working harbor she refuses to romanticize.
