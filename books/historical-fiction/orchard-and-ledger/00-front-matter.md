## About the author

Felix Coldharbour is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the paperwork of collapsed governments before turning to fiction. Orchard and Ledger is his third novel, drawn from his obsession with the clerks, couriers, and census takers who outlive empires.
