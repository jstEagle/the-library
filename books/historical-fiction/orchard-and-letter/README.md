# Orchard and Letter

*by August Thornbury*

> Oscar Halloran, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, has spent five years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

August Thornbury is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing pearling ledgers and indenture records along the northwest Australian coast. Their fiction draws on the untold stories of divers' children whose names appear only as line items in company books.
