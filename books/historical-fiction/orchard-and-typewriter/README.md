## About the author

Juniper Halloran is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing wartime correspondence in municipal basements across northern Europe. Orchard and Typewriter is her second novel, written in a converted cider house where she keeps a 1912 typewriter that jams on the letter Q.
