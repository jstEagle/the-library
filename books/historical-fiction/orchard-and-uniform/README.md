# Orchard and Uniform

*by Noor Whitlock*

> Juniper Ashgrove, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.
