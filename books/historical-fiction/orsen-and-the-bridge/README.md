# Orsen and the Bridge

*by Quentin Vasquez*

> Silas Brightwater, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the ice breaks on the river.
