Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Rafael Fairweather is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing trading-company ledgers from the South Sea pearling era. They write historical fiction about the people those ledgers reduced to line items.
