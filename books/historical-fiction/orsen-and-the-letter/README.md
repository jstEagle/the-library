# Orsen and the Letter

*by Lena Dravenmoor*

> Isandro Galloway, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Lena Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing letters from the Great War's home front. She writes from a farmhouse in the river country, where she keeps a drawer of unsent letters of her own.
