# Oscar and the Clock

**by Vesper Pemberton**

> Lior Winterbourne, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, has spent ten years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Vesper Pemberton is the pen name of a former museum conservator who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century scientific instruments in northern river towns. Their fiction is drawn from the ledgers, repair logs, and court records of workshops that no longer exist.
