# Oscar and the Compass

*by Vesper Sandoval*

> Zephyrine Pemberton, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, has spent twenty years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author
