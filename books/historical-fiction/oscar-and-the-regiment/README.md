# Oscar and the Regiment

*by Elodie Ishikawa*

> Jude Kavanagh, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the ice breaks on the river.
