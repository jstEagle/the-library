# Paloma and the Atlas

*by Edmund Dunmore*

> Ada Ishikawa, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, has spent three years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
