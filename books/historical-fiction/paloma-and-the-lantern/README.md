# Paloma and the Lantern

*by Ulyana Vasquez*

> Rosalind Brightwater, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Ulyana Vasquez is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing trading-company ledgers from the late colonial era. Her fiction excavates the lives of laborers whose names appear only in margins and margin notes.
