## About the author

Sebastian Ishikawa is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging municipal records in river towns along the northern trade routes, where he first encountered the gaps left by victors' histories. He writes historical fiction that asks what ordinary witnesses owe to the record, and lives within earshot of a working clock tower.
