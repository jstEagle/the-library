# Paloma and the Typewriter

*by W. M. Brightwater*

> Odile Vane, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
