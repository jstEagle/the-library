# Paloma and the Uniform

*by Kestrel Kavanagh*

> Astrid Quintana, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ice breaks on the river.
