# Petra and the Atlas

*by Freya Nystrom*

> Paloma Delacroix, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the century turns.
