# Petra and the Clock

*by August Lockridge*

> Silas Blackwood, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.
