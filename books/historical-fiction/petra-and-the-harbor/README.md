# Petra and the Harbor

*by Ulyana Okafor*

> Paloma Kavanagh, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ulyana Okafor is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing war records across three continents. Petra and the Harbor is their second novel, inspired by the marginalia found in declassified field hospitals.
