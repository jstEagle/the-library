# Petra and the Lantern

*by Felix Reyes*

> Jude Oyelaran, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Felix Reyes is the pen name of a former shipping clerk who spent a decade cataloguing port ledgers in West African coastal archives before turning to fiction. He writes about people history recorded only as line items in other men's books.
