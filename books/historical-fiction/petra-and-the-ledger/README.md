# Petra and the Ledger

*by Coral Okonkwo*

> Jude Dunmore, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the ice breaks on the river.
