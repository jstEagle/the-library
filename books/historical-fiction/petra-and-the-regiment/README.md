# Petra and the Regiment

*by Noor Winterbourne*

> Beatrix Halloran, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, has spent five years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Noor Winterbourne is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing military survey records before turning to fiction. She writes historical novels about the people history forgot to name, and lives near a cold river she refuses to swim in.
