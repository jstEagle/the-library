# Port Mahon Rising

**by Mara Joubert**

> Mirabel Crane, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the century turns.

## About the author

Mara Joubert is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing naval correspondence from the Mediterranean campaigns. She writes from a stone house overlooking a harbor much like Port Mahon, where she first found a courier's ledger annotated in two hands.
