# Quentin and the Bridge

*by Ines Hartwell*

> Noor Nystrom, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, has spent five years learning which silences keep people safe. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ines Hartwell is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing border-town correspondence from the early twentieth century. She writes historical fiction about the people history forgets to name.
