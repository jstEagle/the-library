## About the author

G. M. Pemberton is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment in the American Midwest. Their fiction is drawn from marginalia found in the ledgers of defunct print shops, where ordinary people left traces of lives the official record omitted.
