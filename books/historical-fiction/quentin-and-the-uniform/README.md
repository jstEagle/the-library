# Quentin and the Uniform

*by G. M. Pemberton*

> Sebastian Vane, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

G. M. Pemberton is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment in the American Midwest. Their fiction is drawn from marginalia found in the ledgers of defunct print shops, where ordinary people left traces of lives the official record omitted.
