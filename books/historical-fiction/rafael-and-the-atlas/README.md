# Rafael and the Atlas

*by Zephyrine Okonkwo*

> Isandro Hartwell, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, has spent fifteen years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the census taker asks one question too many, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must choose which family member gets the last berth before the century turns.
