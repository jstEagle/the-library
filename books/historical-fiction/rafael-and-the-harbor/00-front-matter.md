## About the author

Beatrix Sandoval is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping ledgers from colonial trading posts. Her fiction is known for its salt-stained realism and its insistence that ordinary clerks and divers, not captains, are where history actually happens.
