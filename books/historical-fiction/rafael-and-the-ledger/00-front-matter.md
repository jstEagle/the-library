## About the author

L. M. Nystrom is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing colonial trading-company records in Southeast Asian ports. Their fiction draws on the ledgers, manifests, and marginalia of people whose names survive only as entries in someone else's accounting.
