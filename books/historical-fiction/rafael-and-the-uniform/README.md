# Rafael and the Uniform

*by Edmund Nystrom*

> Noor Crane, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, has spent three years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.
