# Regiment and Bridge

**by Rosalind Marchetti**

> Orsen Ravensworth, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Rosalind Marchetti is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing suffrage-era letters in northern England before turning to fiction. She writes about the quiet people history forgot—the drivers, laundresses, and doorkeepers who made other people's courage possible.
