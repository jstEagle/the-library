# Regiment and Harbor

**by Orsen Halloran**

> Oscar Nakamura, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Orsen Halloran is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent fifteen years restoring printing equipment in coastal museum workshops before turning to fiction. He writes historical novels about ordinary tradespeople caught inside large machinery of state, and lives within sight of a working harbor.
