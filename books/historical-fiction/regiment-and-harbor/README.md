## About the author

Orsen Halloran is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent fifteen years restoring printing equipment in coastal museum workshops before turning to fiction. He writes historical novels about ordinary tradespeople caught inside large machinery of state, and lives within sight of a working harbor.
