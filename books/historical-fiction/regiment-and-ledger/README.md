# Regiment and Ledger

*by Ivo Galloway*

> Theo Delacroix, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ship sails on the morning tide.
