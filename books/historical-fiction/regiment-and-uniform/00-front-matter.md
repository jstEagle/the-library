## About the author

Astrid Sandoval is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse logbooks and assize records from the North Atlantic coast. Regiment and Uniform is her third novel, drawing on real cases of posthumous pardons and the forgotten women who kept remote lights burning.
