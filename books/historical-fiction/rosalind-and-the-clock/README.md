# Rosalind and the Clock

*by Theo Montrose*

> Xanthe Iversen, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, has spent twenty years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.
