# Rosalind and the Lantern

*by Mara Marchetti*

> Orsen Amberdale, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.
