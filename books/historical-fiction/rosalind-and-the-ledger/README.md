# Rosalind and the Ledger

*by Beatrix Ravensworth*

> Sable Almeida, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Beatrix Ravensworth is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing maritime correspondence from coastal lighthouse stations. She writes historical fiction about ordinary people caught in the machinery of other people's crimes.
