# Sable and the Atlas

*by Emrys Ishikawa*

> Rosalind Nakamura, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Emrys Ishikawa is the pen name of a former horologist's apprentice who spent a decade restoring tower clocks in coastal towns before turning to fiction. Their work explores what instruments — and people — remember when institutions would rather they forget.
