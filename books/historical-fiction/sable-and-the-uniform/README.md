# Sable and the Uniform

*by Felix Delacroix*

> Ansel Kavanagh, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Felix Delacroix is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping ledgers from forgotten river towns. Their fiction excavates the lives of people history recorded only as line items.
