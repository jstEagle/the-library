# Sebastian and the Clock

*by Oscar Hartwell*

> August Crane, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Oscar Hartwell is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse logs and requisition records from nineteenth-century coastal towns. Hartwell writes historical fiction about ordinary people caught in the machinery of official decisions.
