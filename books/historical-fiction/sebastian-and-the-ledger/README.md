# Sebastian and the Ledger

*by Gideon Thackeray*

> Wendell Almeida, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, has spent five years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
