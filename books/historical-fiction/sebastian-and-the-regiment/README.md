# Sebastian and the Regiment

*by Gideon Nystrom*

> Casper Ashgrove, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ship sails on the morning tide.
