# Silas and the Letter

*by P. M. Eriksen*

> Marguerite Quintana, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

P. M. Eriksen is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing trading-company ledgers from the late colonial pearl fisheries. This is their second novel, drawn from a single unsigned letter found sewn into an indenture book.
