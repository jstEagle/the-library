# Silas and the Orchard

*by August Dunmore*

> Wendell Winterbourne, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

August Dunmore is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent fifteen years restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment in New England mill towns. Their novels explore labor, secrecy, and the people history forgets to count.
