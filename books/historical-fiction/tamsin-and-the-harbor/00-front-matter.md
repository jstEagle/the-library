## About the author

Ivo Marchetti is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping ledgers and diver compensation claims along the northern coast. Their fiction draws on the half-erased lives found in marginalia, water-stained paybooks, and the silences between official entries.
