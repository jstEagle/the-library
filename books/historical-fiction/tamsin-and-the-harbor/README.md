# Tamsin and the Harbor

*by Ivo Marchetti*

> Wendell Pemberton, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ivo Marchetti is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping ledgers and diver compensation claims along the northern coast. Their fiction draws on the half-erased lives found in marginalia, water-stained paybooks, and the silences between official entries.
