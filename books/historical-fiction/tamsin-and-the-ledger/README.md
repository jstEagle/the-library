# Tamsin and the Ledger

*by Astrid Crane*

> Astrid Nystrom, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.
