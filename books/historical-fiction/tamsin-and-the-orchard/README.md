# Tamsin and the Orchard

*by Freya Coldharbour*

> Tamsin Ashgrove, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Freya Coldharbour is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing municipal records in river towns, where she learned that every official history has a shadow ledger. She writes historical fiction about the people who keep records — and the people the records were built to forget.
