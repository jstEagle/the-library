# Tamsin and the Typewriter

*by Coral Okonkwo*

> Beatrix Eastwick, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Coral Okonkwo is a former letterpress apprentice who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment in riverside mill towns. She writes historical fiction about the people history forgot to credit, and teaches a yearly workshop on setting type by hand.
