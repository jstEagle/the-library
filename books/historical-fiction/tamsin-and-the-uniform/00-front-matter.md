## About the author

Beatrix Hartwell is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing private letters from the First World War, an experience that taught her history is written by whoever keeps the paperwork. She writes from a cottage in the Cotswolds, where she still reads other people's mail—only now, with permission.
