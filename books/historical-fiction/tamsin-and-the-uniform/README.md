# Tamsin and the Uniform

*by Beatrix Hartwell*

> Jonah Montrose, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Beatrix Hartwell is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing private letters from the First World War, an experience that taught her history is written by whoever keeps the paperwork. She writes from a cottage in the Cotswolds, where she still reads other people's mail—only now, with permission.
