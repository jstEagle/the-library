# The Atlas of the Cape Route

*by Edmund Nakamura*

> Xanthe Marchetti, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.
