# The Atlas of the Lower Danube

**by Sebastian Iversen**

> Ivo Delacroix, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent five years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Sebastian Iversen is the pen name of a former typesetter who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century letterpress equipment in workshops along the Danube. This is their third novel, and the first written entirely by hand before a single line touched a keyboard.
