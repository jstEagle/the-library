# The Atlas of Vilna

*by Wren Zhou*

> Lior Lockridge, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, has spent twenty years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the census taker asks one question too many, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must finish the work while the workshop still stands before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Wren Zhou is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing displaced-persons records across Eastern Europe before turning to fiction. They write about the small, dangerous acts of record-keeping that history forgets to thank.
