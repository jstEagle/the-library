# The Borrowed Atlas

*by Marguerite Vane*

> Tamsin Fairweather, a mapmaker's apprentice finishing the atlas the master died for, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the century turns.
