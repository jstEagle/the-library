*by Quentin Winterbourne*

> Ulyana Iversen, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.
