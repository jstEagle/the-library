# The Borrowed Compass

*by Marguerite Coldharbour*

> Odile Delacroix, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Marguerite Coldharbour is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing contested wills in provincial French records offices. She writes historical fiction about the people history tried to leave out.
