# The Borrowed Ledger

**by Astrid Okafor**

> Jonah Nakamura, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. ## About the author

Astrid Okafor is a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring colonial-era printing archives in West African port cities. She writes historical fiction about the people who keep records when power would prefer they didn't.
