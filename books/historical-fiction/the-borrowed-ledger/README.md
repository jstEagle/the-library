# The Borrowed Ledger

*by Astrid Okafor*

> Jonah Nakamura, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Astrid Okafor is a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring colonial-era printing archives in West African port cities. She writes historical fiction about the people who keep records when power would prefer they didn't.
