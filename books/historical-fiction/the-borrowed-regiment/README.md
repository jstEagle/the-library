# The Borrowed Regiment

*by Marguerite Galloway*

> Zephyrine Fairweather, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy, and time is already running out.
