# The Borrowed Uniform

*by Felix Delacroix*

> Lior Crane, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent seven years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must finish the work while the workshop still stands, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Felix Delacroix is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment in provincial archives. Their novels explore the quiet people history forgets—the hands that set the type for other people's revolutions.
