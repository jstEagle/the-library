*by Emrys Ashgrove*

> Bram Ishikawa, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent twenty years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Emrys Ashgrove is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing industrial-era coroners' records in northern England. Their fiction is drawn from the silences between official documents.
