# The Clock of Vilna

**by Emrys Sandoval**

> August Dravenmoor, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Emrys Sandoval is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing displaced records from Eastern European observatories and municipal clocks. The Clock of Vilna grew out of a single marginal note found in a repair log dated 1919.
