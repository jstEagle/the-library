## About the author

Lior Eriksen is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse keepers' logs along the North Atlantic coast. The Copper Atlas is a debut novel inspired by the weathered ledgers, unsent letters, and unexplained gaps she found in the official records.
