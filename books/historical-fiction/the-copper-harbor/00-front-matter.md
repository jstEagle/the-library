# The Copper Harbor

**by Yara Galloway**

> Sable Montrose, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author
