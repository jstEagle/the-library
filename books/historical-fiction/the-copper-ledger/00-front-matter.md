# The Copper Ledger

**by C. M. Amberdale**

> Orsen Winterbourne, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must choose which family member gets the last berth before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

C. M. Amberdale is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing military hospital records from forgotten coastal towns. Their fiction is drawn to the small, unrecorded lives that official history leaves in the margins.
