# The Faithful Compass

**by Ada Ravensworth**

> Sebastian Montrose, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the census taker asks one question too many, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the century turns.

## About the author

Ada Ravensworth is the pen name of a former museum conservator who spent a decade restoring timepieces and municipal archives across Northern Europe. She writes historical fiction about the small mechanisms—clocks, ledgers, stamps—by which empires remember and forget.
