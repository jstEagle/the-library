## About the author

Hazel Fairweather grew up in a coastal town where the smell of brine and rope was as constant as weather, and she credits the dockworkers' stories for her lifelong obsession with ordinary people caught in extraordinary tides. She writes historical fiction from a converted boathouse, where she keeps her grandfather's brass compass on the desk as a reminder that every story needs a true bearing.
