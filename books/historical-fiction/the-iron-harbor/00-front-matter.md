## About the author

Petra Villalobos is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing military correspondence from coastal garrisons, where she first encountered ledgers kept by unofficial record-keepers. She writes historical fiction from a restored harbor house, drawn to stories of people history tried to leave out.
