## About the author

Zephyrine Delacroix is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse keepers' logs along the North Atlantic coast. The Iron Lantern is her third novel, inspired by the unsent letters she once found sealed inside a decommissioned lamp room wall.
