## About the author

Ansel Fairweather is the pen name of a former horologist's apprentice who spent a decade restoring tower clocks in Rhineland churches before turning to fiction. Their work draws on family letters recovered from an eighteenth-century Palatine emigration, and on the quiet conviction that history is mostly kept by people no census bothered to name.
