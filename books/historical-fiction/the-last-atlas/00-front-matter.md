# The Last Atlas

**by R. M. Okafor**

> Petra Hartwell, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when a child is left at the door with instructions to forget the face, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ice breaks on the river.
