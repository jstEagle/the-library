# The Last Bridge

**by Tamsin Nakamura**

> Cyrus Sandoval, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author
