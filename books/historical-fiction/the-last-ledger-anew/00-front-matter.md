## About the author

E. M. Sandoval is the pen name of a former typesetter and archivist who spent a decade cataloguing nineteenth-century print shops in central Europe. Their debut novel was inspired by a single surviving ledger of forbidden pamphlets found sewn into the binding of a hymnal.
