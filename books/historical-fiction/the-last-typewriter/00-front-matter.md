# The Last Typewriter

**by Tamsin Yarrow**

> Elodie Ravensworth, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
