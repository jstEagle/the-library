## About the author

Noor Nakamura is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing wartime correspondence in coastal port cities. Their fiction explores the quiet paperwork of history—the ledgers, manifests, and unsent letters through which ordinary people resist being forgotten.
