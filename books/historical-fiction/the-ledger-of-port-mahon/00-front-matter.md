# The Ledger of Port Mahon

**by Isandro Eriksen**

> Tamsin Kavanagh, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Isandro Eriksen is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing naval correspondence from the Mediterranean garrisons. The Ledger of Port Mahon is his second novel, drawn from a misfiled requisition order he found in 2009.
