# The Ledger of the Palatinate

**by Quentin Okafor**

> Freya Delacroix, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, keeps a low profile and busy hands. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the century turns.

## About the author

Quentin Okafor is a former military archivist turned novelist who writes about the paperwork of war—the ledgers, manifests, and omissions that decide whose lives count. He lives in Lisbon with his partner and an unruly collection of nineteenth-century field notebooks.
