## About the author

Oscar Thackeray is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment in port cities from Callao to Cardiff. The Ledger of Valparaíso grew out of a salvaged Chilean jobbing press and the unanswered questions of everyone whose names never made it into official histories.
