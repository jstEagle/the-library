# The Long Bridge

**by August Blackwood**

> Casper Blackwood, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the census taker asks one question too many, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the century turns.

## About the author

August Blackwood is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing confiscated private papers before turning to fiction. The Long Bridge draws on that experience with documents that outlive the regimes that tried to bury them.
