# The Long Clock

**by P. M. Whitlock**

> Coral Joubert, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent twenty years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the bridge — the only road out — closes for the season, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must smuggle the evidence past three checkpoints before the ice breaks on the river.
