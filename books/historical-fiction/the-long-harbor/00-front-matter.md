# The Long Harbor

**by Lena Villalobos**

> Rosalind Kavanagh, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, has spent ten years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must choose which family member gets the last berth, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Lena Villalobos is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing private correspondence in Irish estate collections, where she first encountered the unread letters of servants and drivers. She writes historical fiction about the people history recorded only in margins.
