# The Long Letter

**by Odile Okonkwo**

> Casper Okonkwo, a widowed lighthouse keeper during the year of the great storms, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

Odile Okonkwo is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing coastal municipal records from the First World War era. She writes historical fiction about ordinary people caught between paperwork and conscience.
