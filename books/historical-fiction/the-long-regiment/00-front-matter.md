## About the author

Silas Almeida is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing port records and indenture ledgers along the old trade coasts. Their fiction is drawn from the marginalia of those documents — the unsigned letters, the corrected entries, the names struck through but never quite erased.
