## About the author

Edmund Hartwell is the pen name of a former merchant-marine radio operator who spent three years in postwar Japanese ports and never stopped writing letters home about what he saw there. He lives in Cornwall, where he grafts apple trees descended from cuttings he carried back in 1949.
