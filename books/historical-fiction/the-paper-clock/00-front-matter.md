## About the author

Juniper Nakamura is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the letters of wartime refugees before turning to fiction. She writes from a small coastal town, where she keeps a broken clock on her desk that stopped the day her grandmother's family fled their homeland.
