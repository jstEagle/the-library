## About the author

Orsen Quintana is the pen name of a former archival clerk who spent a decade cataloging the confiscated papers of collapsed governments before turning to fiction. They write from a small coastal town, where they keep an orchard of paper-mulberry trees grown from cuttings of uncertain provenance.
