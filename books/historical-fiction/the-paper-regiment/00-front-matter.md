# The Paper Regiment

**by Wendell Blackwood**

> Quentin Winterbourne, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the century turns.

## About the author

Wendell Blackwood is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing private papers of Edwardian political families before turning to fiction. They write from a converted coach house in Kent, where they keep a wall of unsent letters.
