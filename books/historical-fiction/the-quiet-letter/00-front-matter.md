**by E. M. Amberdale**

> Oscar Montrose, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.

## About the author

E. M. Amberdale is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing disputed border-town records across Central Europe. The Quiet Letter is their first novel, drawn from a single unclaimed envelope found misfiled in a 1919 census ledger.
