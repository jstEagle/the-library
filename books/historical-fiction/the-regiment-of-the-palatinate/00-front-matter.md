# The Regiment of the Palatinate

**by Quentin Halloran**

> Wren Joubert, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. ## About the author

Quentin Halloran is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment in Alsace before turning to fiction. His novels are known for their tactile precision and their obsession with how paper outlasts power.
