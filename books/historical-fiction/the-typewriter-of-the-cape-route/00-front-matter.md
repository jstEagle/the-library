**by Theo Ishikawa**

> Elodie Zhou, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Theo Ishikawa is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse logs and harbor court records along northern trade routes. The Typewriter of the Cape Route is their second novel, following a well-received debut about a cartographer who maps a city that no longer exists.
