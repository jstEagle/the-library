# The Unwritten Letter

**by Dominic Underhill**

> Freya Galloway, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the century turns.

## About the author

Dominic Underhill is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing displaced-persons records in postwar Europe. He writes historical fiction about the small, carryable objects — letters, keys, ledgers — through which ordinary people hold power to account.
