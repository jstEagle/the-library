**by Mara Marchetti**

## About the author

Mara Marchetti is the pen name of a former conservator of scientific instruments who spent a decade restoring tower clocks across Central Europe. Her fiction draws on the ledgers, repair logs, and unsent letters she found sealed inside the machines.
