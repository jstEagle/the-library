# The Winter Regiment

**by Wren Okafor**

> Ada Winterbourne, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, keeps a low profile and busy hands. Then a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Wren Okafor is a former archivist who spent years cataloguing suffragist correspondence in regional collections, where the margins told louder stories than the letters themselves. The Winter Regiment is a debut novel born from a single misfiled envelope and the question of who keeps secrets for people they love.
