# The Winter Typewriter

**by Gideon Thackeray**

> Casper Thackeray, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the census taker asks one question too many, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Gideon Thackeray is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment in provincial museums. The Winter Typewriter draws on archives of underground pamphlets and census records discovered during that work.
