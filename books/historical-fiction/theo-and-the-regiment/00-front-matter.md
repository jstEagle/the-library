## About the author

W. M. Brightwater is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the private letters of soldiers and civil servants from a forgotten border war. Theo and the Regiment is a debut novel born from one uncatalogued envelope found sewn into the lining of a regimental ledger.
