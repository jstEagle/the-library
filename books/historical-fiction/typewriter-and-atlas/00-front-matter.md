# Typewriter and Atlas

**by M. M. Galloway**

> Oscar Dravenmoor, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent twenty years learning which silences keep people safe. ## About the author

M. M. Galloway is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment in Baltic port towns. Their fiction explores what working hands remember that official histories forget.
