## About the author

Mara Dunmore is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing diplomatic correspondence from interwar Europe, where she first encountered the marginalia of couriers who never made it home. She writes from a converted mill in Vermont, where she keeps her grandmother's typewriter and an unsynchronized collection of clocks.
