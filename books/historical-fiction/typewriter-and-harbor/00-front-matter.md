# Typewriter and Harbor

**by Jude Okonkwo**

> Vesper Iversen, a suffragist's driver who reads every letter before delivering it, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the census taker asks one question too many, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
