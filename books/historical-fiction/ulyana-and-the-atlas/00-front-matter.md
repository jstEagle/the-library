## About the author

Emrys Hartwell is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing maritime charts and the letters folded inside them. Hartwell writes historical fiction about the quiet keepers of knowledge, and lives near a harbor where the tide still keeps better time than any clock.
