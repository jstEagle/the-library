# Ulyana and the Clock

**by Dominic Brightwater**

> Felix Montrose, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent ten years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Dominic Brightwater is the pen name of a former letterpress printer who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century press equipment in small-town museums. Their fiction draws on family stories of border towns caught between armies, and on a lifelong fascination with the people history forgets to record.
