## About the author

Noor Ashgrove is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the private letters of border families displaced by forgotten wars. Writing from a farmhouse surrounded by half-wild orchards, Ashgrove turns those fragments into fiction about ordinary people caught in history's machinery.
