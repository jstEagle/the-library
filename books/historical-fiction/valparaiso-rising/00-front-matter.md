## About the author

Ines Yarrow is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping manifests from Pacific trade routes. Valparaíso Rising is her second novel, drawn from the marginalia she found in indenture ledgers — the names written small, in the corners, where no one was meant to read them.
