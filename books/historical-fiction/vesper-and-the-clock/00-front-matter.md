# Vesper and the Clock

**by Gideon Galloway**

> Sebastian Sterling, a battlefield nurse keeping a ledger of the names officers omit, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. But when the census taker asks one question too many, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ice breaks on the river.

## About the author

Gideon Galloway is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing Civil War hospital records in the Ohio River Valley. Their novels are known for excavating the small, stubborn documents through which ordinary people contradicted official history.
