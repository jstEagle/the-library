## About the author

Petra Eriksen is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse keepers' logs along the North Atlantic coast. Her novels are known for their salt-bleached prose and their insistence that ordinary record-keepers are the quiet engines of history.
