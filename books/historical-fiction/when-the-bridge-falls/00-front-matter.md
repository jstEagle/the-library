# When the Bridge Falls

**by Isandro Halloran**

> Isandro Okafor, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent five years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Isandro Halloran is the pen name of a former letterpress apprentice who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century print shops across river towns now erased from most maps. Their novels examine how ordinary tradespeople become accidental archivists of suppressed histories.
