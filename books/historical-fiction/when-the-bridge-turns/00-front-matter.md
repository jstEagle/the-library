# When the Bridge Turns

**by Oscar Dunmore**

> Vesper Almeida, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent three years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the ship sails on the morning tide.

## About the author

Oscar Dunmore is the pen name of a former typesetter's apprentice who spent a decade restoring letterpress equipment in coastal archives before turning to fiction. He writes about ordinary people caught between history's machinery and its ink.
