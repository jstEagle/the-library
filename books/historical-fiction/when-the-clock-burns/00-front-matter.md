# When the Clock Burns

**by Beatrix Zhou**

> Odile Eastwick, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must keep a promise made in peacetime to someone now declared enemy before the ice breaks on the river.
