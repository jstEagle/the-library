# When the Clock Turns

**by Coral Kavanagh**

## About the author

Coral Kavanagh is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing disputed border treaties and the people erased from them. She writes historical fiction about the clerks, mapmakers, and record-keepers who fight wars with ink instead of guns.
