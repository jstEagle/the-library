## About the author

Petra Iversen is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the displaced papers of small nations that no longer appear on maps. She writes from a converted pilot house on the northern coast, where she keeps a wall of unsent letters to the republics her grandfather fled.
