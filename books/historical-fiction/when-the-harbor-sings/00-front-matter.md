## About the author

Beatrix Villalobos is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing lighthouse logs and coastal requisition records from the early twentieth century. She writes historical fiction about women whose labor kept the edges of the world running and whose names rarely made the ledgers.
