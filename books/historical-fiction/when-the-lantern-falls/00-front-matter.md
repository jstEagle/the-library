# When the Lantern Falls

**by Dominic Thornbury**

> Marguerite Quintana, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent three years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Dominic Thornbury is the pen name of a former archival restorer who spent a decade cataloguing confiscated printing records from provincial European archives. Their novels examine how ordinary tradespeople become accidental custodians of history.
