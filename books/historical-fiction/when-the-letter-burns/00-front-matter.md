# When the Letter Burns

**by Edmund Yarrow**

> Freya Reyes, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, has spent three years learning which silences keep people safe. But when the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Edmund Yarrow is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring printing equipment in Eastern European museum archives. His novels explore how ordinary craftspeople become unwilling witnesses to history.
