# When the Regiment Breaks

**by E. M. Blackwood**

> Noor Delacroix, a code-carrying courier on the last diplomatic mission of a doomed republic, has spent twelve years learning which silences keep people safe. Then the army requisitions the town and the ledger with all the names — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the armistice is signed — or isn't.
