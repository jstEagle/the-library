# When the Uniform Breaks

**by Jonah Delacroix**

> Lena Underhill, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, has spent fifteen years learning which silences keep people safe. But when a sealed letter names the family in a plot no one wants a part of, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must get the truth on the record ahead of the victors' historians before the century turns.

## About the author

Jonah Delacroix is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing municipal records in port cities, where he developed a fascination with the small clerical lies that history runs on. He writes historical fiction about ordinary tradespeople caught in machinery far larger than themselves.
