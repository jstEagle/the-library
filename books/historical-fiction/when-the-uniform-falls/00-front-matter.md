# When the Uniform Falls

**by Ines Ravensworth**

> Mara Ravensworth, an apprentice clockmaker summoned to repair the observatory's master clock, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. But when a stranger arrives carrying unmistakable handwriting from a supposed corpse, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
