# Xanthe and the Clock

**by Lena Nakamura**

> Wren Ashgrove, a typeface cutter's daughter in a print shop on the eve of a revolution, measures the world in what can be carried when the orders come. Then the census taker asks one question too many — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author
