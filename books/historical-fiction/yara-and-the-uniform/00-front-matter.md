## About the author

Sable Amberdale is the pen name of a former letterpress compositor who spent a decade restoring nineteenth-century printing equipment in provincial archives. Their fiction is drawn from marginalia, apprenticeship ledgers, and the quiet lives of tradespeople caught in history's margins.
