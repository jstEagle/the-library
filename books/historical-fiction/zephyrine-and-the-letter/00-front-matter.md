**by L. M. Eastwick**

> August Marchetti, a pearl diver's child indentured to a trading company, believes history happens to other people, elsewhere. ## About the author

L. M. Eastwick is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipping ledgers in coastal municipal collections, where the gaps in the records proved more instructive than the entries. Zephyrine and the Letter is their second novel, following a well-reviewed debut about lighthouse keepers and the lies families tell to survive.
