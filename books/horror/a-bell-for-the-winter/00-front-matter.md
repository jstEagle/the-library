# A Bell for the Winter

**by Xanthe Winterbourne**

> Vesper Hartwell, a property surveyor appraising a house that appraises back, documents everything, because documentation is a kind of prayer. Then the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911 — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must finish the ritual the founders started before the last resident signs the papers.
