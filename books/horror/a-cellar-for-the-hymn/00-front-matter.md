## About the author

Orsen Ashgrove is the pen name of a former rural schoolteacher who spent nine years in one-room schoolhouses across the northern counties before turning to writing. They collect hymnals, and their fiction draws on the folk rituals and quiet cruelties of small communities that protect themselves at any cost.
