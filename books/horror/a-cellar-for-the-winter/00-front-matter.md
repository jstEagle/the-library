## About the author

Wendell Hartwell is the pen name of a former land registry clerk from coastal Maine who spent eleven years cataloguing deeds to houses that burned, flooded, or simply stopped appearing on maps. Their fiction explores the paperwork of haunting: what we sign, what we inherit, and what refuses to be filed away.
