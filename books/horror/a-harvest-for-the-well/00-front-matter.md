# A Harvest for the Well

**by U. M. Oyelaran**

> Mirabel Montrose, a property surveyor appraising a house that appraises back, keeps the lights on and the doors counted. But when all the photographs from grandma's house now include an extra chair, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must hold out until sunrise with the truth about what was invited in, and time is already running out.

## About the author

U. M. Oyelaran is a Nigerian-British writer of quiet horror whose stories explore what families owe to the places that raised them. A former land surveyor, Oyelaran brings an appraiser's precision to the geography of dread.
