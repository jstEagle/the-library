## About the author

Freya Quintana is the pen name of a former archival assistant who spent six years cataloguing estate sales and unclaimed property, an experience that taught her how objects remember. She writes horror about the tenderness that survives inside haunted places and lives in a drafty farmhouse she insists is only a house.
