## About the author

E. M. Delacroix is the pen name of a former rural schoolteacher who taught for eleven years in towns too small to appear on maps, collecting the stories colleagues only told after dark. Delacroix now writes full-time from a farmhouse whose cellar door has been locked since before they moved in.
