## About the author

Cyrus Dravenmoor spent a decade as an overnight radio operator in small-market stations across the Rust Belt, collecting the strange calls and dead air that haunt his fiction. He writes horror about institutions—churches, stations, hospitals—that outlive the people they were built to protect.
