# A Smile for the Hymn

**by Noor Ishikawa**

## About the author

Noor Ishikawa is a former archivist of regional hymnals who grew up between Osaka and rural Vermont, where harvest festivals were held in churches that no longer had congregations. Her fiction explores what survives of faith when only the songs remain.
