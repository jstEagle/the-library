## About the author

Astrid Nystrom is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing estate sales in rural New England, where she developed a fascination with houses that outlive their families. She writes horror about inheritance—of property, of guilt, and of names—and lives quietly in a farmhouse she refuses to describe in detail.
