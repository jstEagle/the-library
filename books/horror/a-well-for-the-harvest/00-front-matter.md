## About the author

Gideon Winterbourne is the pen name of a former rural radio engineer who spent fifteen years maintaining transmitter towers across the American heartland and collecting the stories nobody would say on air. A Well for the Harvest is their third novel, following two critically ignored books about small towns that refuse to stay buried.
