# A Winter for the Bell

**by Rafael Coldharbour**

> Ansel Eriksen, a folklorist recording songs the village refuses to sing twice, has spent twenty years telling themselves the noises have explanations. But when the sleep recordings capture a second voice answering questions, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Rafael Coldharbour is the pen name of a former archivist of Nordic folk collections who spent a decade cataloguing songs too dangerous or shameful to perform twice. They write horror from a farmhouse where, they insist, the floorboards only creak in patterns.
