# Ada and the Hymn

**by Lior Dravenmoor**

> Mara Amberdale, a hospice nurse who hears the dying count backwards, has spent twenty years telling themselves the noises have explanations. But when the sleep recordings capture a second voice answering questions, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must learn the song's last verse without singing along before the last resident signs the papers.

## About the author

Lior Dravenmoor spent seven years as a night-shift caregiver in long-term care facilities across the English coast before turning to writing full-time. Their work explores the quiet horrors of institutions, the ethics of memory, and the families people build in the hours no one else wants to work.
