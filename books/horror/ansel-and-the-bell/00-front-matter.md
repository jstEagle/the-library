# Ansel and the Bell

**by Petra Whitlock**

> Ansel Brightwater, the new teacher at a school with a missing-class problem, documents everything, because documentation is a kind of prayer. But when the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must learn the song's last verse without singing along, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Petra Whitlock is the pen name of a former parish archivist who spent eleven years cataloguing church records in coastal England before turning to fiction. She writes horror about paperwork, small towns, and the terrible intimacy of inherited obligations.
