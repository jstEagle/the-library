# Astrid and the Room

**by Casper Okonkwo**

> Xanthe Ashgrove, a property surveyor appraising a house that appraises back, has spent five years telling themselves the noises have explanations. Then the excavation crew unearths a door, sealed from the inside — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it.
