# August and the Bell

**by Mara Joubert**

> Wendell Sterling, a folklorist recording songs the village refuses to sing twice, has spent ten years telling themselves the noises have explanations. Then all the photographs from grandma's house now include an extra chair — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must hold out until sunrise with the truth about what was invited in before the last resident signs the papers.
