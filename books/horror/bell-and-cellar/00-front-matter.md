## About the author

G. M. Reyes is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing funeral hymns and lullabies across remote coastal villages, collecting the songs people were afraid to repeat. Bell and Cellar is their debut novel, drawn from a single field recording no singer would ever perform twice.
