## About the author

Sebastian Dunmore is the pen name of a former parish archivist who spent a decade cataloguing church records across rural England before turning to fiction. His work is known for quiet dread rooted in bureaucratic horror—the contracts, ledgers, and small-town complicity behind the supernatural.
