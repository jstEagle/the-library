# Bell and Winter

**by Ada Quintana**

> August Oyelaran, the new teacher at a school with a missing-class problem, documents everything, because documentation is a kind of prayer. But when the excavation crew unearths a door, sealed from the inside, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must hold out until sunrise with the truth about what was invited in before the well refills.

## About the author

Ada Quintana is a former rural schoolteacher who spent six years collecting oral histories from towns that preferred to be forgotten. She writes horror about institutions, ledgers, and the things communities agree not to see.
