## About the author

Sebastian Amberdale is the pen name of a former rural surveyor who spent a decade mapping farmsteads across the upper Midwest and cataloguing the things landowners asked him not to write down. Bram and the Visitor is his second novel, following the critically quiet but cult-beloved The Long Acre.
