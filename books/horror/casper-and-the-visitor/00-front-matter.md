## About the author

Mirabel Vane spent a decade as a municipal records clerk in rural England before turning to horror, and her fiction remains obsessed with ledgers, permits, and the terrible things filed neatly between lines. She writes from a converted tithe barn where she keeps, she insists, a complete inventory of every room.
