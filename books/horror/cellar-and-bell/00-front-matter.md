# Cellar and Bell

**by Noor Eastwick**

> Cyrus Nakamura, a pest controller called to something with fingerprints, keeps the lights on and the doors counted. But when the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must hold out until sunrise with the truth about what was invited in before the well refills.
