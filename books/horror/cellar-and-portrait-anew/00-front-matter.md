## About the author

Ulyana Thackeray is the pen name of a former architectural conservator who spent a decade cataloguing condemned buildings across the northern counties before turning to fiction. Her debut novel, Cellar and Portrait Anew, was written in a rented farmhouse whose previous tenant left behind forty years of unopened survey reports.
