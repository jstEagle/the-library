## About the author

Isandro Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former land surveyor from the chalk hills of southern England who spent fifteen years cataloguing houses that no one wanted to buy twice. He now writes full-time in a converted tithe barn, where he insists the drafts get colder after midnight.
