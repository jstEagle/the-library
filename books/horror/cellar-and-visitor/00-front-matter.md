**by Dominic Galloway**

## About the author

Dominic Galloway is the pen name of a former building inspector who spent fifteen years cataloguing condemned properties across rural England, collecting the strange things owners whisper about their own houses. He writes horror from a converted chapel with no mirrors in it, which he insists is a coincidence.
