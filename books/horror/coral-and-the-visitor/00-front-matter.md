# Coral and the Visitor

**by M. M. Vasquez**

> August Delacroix, a folklorist recording songs the village refuses to sing twice, has spent twenty years telling themselves the noises have explanations. But when every mirror in the house starts reflecting the room one second late, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must get the family out ahead of the count before the harvest moon rises.
