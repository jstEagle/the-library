## About the author

Gideon Whitlock is the pen name of a former church organist from coastal Maine who spent a decade transcribing dying musical traditions in isolated communities before turning to horror. His work is known for its scholarly dread, treating folklore not as decoration but as a living contract with terrible fine print.
