## About the author

Edmund Almeida is the pen name of a former rural schoolteacher who spent nine years in small-town classrooms before turning to horror full-time. His work draws on the quiet dread of declining farming communities, where empty houses outnumber occupied ones and everyone remembers who left and who stayed.
