## About the author

Oscar Kavanagh is a former rural schoolteacher who spent a decade collecting local legends about wells, harvests, and disappearances across the English countryside. He writes horror rooted in domestic inheritance, insisting that the scariest houses are the ones we're given rather than the ones we choose.
