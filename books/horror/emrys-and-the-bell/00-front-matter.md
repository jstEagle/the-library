# Emrys and the Bell

**by Coral Dunmore**

> Vesper Okafor, a folklorist recording songs the village refuses to sing twice, keeps the lights on and the doors counted. But when the childhood imaginary friend is waiting on the porch, unchanged, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
