# Emrys and the Cellar

**by Juniper Nystrom**

> Beatrix Yarrow, a radio host taking calls from listeners who died last week, documents everything, because documentation is a kind of prayer. ## About the author

Juniper Nystrom is a former community radio volunteer from coastal Maine who spent three years archiving dead-air recordings before turning to fiction. 'Emrys and the Cellar' is her second novel, written mostly between midnight and four in the morning.
