# Felix and the Visitor

**by Yara Villalobos**

> Ansel Okonkwo, a radio host taking calls from listeners who died last week, has spent ten years telling themselves the noises have explanations. ## About the author

Yara Villalobos is a former community-radio producer from the Gulf Coast who writes horror rooted in family archives, inheritance, and the things houses remember. 'Felix and the Visitor' is her second novel, inspired by a box of unlabeled photographs left to her by an aunt.
