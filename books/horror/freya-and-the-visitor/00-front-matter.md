# Freya and the Visitor

**by Beatrix Lockridge**

> Bram Ravensworth, a hospice nurse who hears the dying count backwards, documents everything, because documentation is a kind of prayer. But when the sleep recordings capture a second voice answering questions, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must hold out until sunrise with the truth about what was invited in before the last resident signs the papers.

## About the author

Beatrix Lockridge is the pen name of a former palliative care chaplain who spent eleven years listening to last words and now writes horror about what people say when no one is supposed to be listening. She lives in coastal Maine, where she keeps a tape recorder running at night purely out of professional habit.
