# Hazel and the House

**by Elodie Eriksen**

> Wren Yarrow, a property surveyor appraising a house that appraises back, has spent twelve years telling themselves the noises have explanations. But when all the photographs from grandma's house now include an extra chair, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Elodie Eriksen is a former building inspector from coastal Maine who spent a decade cataloguing structurally unsound houses before turning to fiction. Her stories explore how places remember the people who tried to leave them.
