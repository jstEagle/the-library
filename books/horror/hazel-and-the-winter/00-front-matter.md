# Hazel and the Winter

**by Edmund Vasquez**

> Odile Pemberton, the new teacher at a school with a missing-class problem, has spent three years telling themselves the noises have explanations. Then the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911 — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must hold out until sunrise with the truth about what was invited in, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Edmund Vasquez is the pen name of a former rural schoolteacher who taught for eleven years in isolated mountain communities across the Pacific Northwest. He writes horror rooted in the quiet complicity of small towns, and Hazel and the Winter is his fourth novel.
