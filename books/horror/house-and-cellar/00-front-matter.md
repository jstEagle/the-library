# House and Cellar

**by Hazel Okonkwo**

> Edmund Dunmore, a hospice nurse who hears the dying count backwards, has spent twelve years telling themselves the noises have explanations. Then the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911 — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Hazel Okonkwo is a former palliative care volunteer from the Norfolk fens who writes horror about the quiet bargains ordinary people make. House and Cellar is her third novel, following the critically acclaimed The Tallyman's Daughter.
