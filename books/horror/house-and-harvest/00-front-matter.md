## About the author

Petra Whitlock is the pen name of a former land surveyor who spent a decade mapping properties in the Appalachian foothills, where every deed tells a story the owners would rather forget. Their work has appeared in several small-press horror anthologies, and they live, deliberately, in a house with no basement.
