# House and Room

**by Kestrel Iversen**

> Quentin Galloway, a radio host taking calls from listeners who died last week, has spent ten years telling themselves the noises have explanations. Then the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911 — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author
