# House and Visitor

**by Emrys Fairweather**

> Jude Vane, a property surveyor appraising a house that appraises back, has spent twelve years telling themselves the noises have explanations. But when every mirror in the house starts reflecting the room one second late, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must hold out until sunrise with the truth about what was invited in before the last resident signs the papers.

## About the author

Emrys Fairweather is the pen name of a former building inspector who spent fifteen years cataloguing structural decay in Victorian houses across the English countryside. They write horror about places that remember more than the people who live in them.
