# Hymn and Well

**by Ada Brightwater**

> Noor Dunmore, the new teacher at a school with a missing-class problem, keeps the lights on and the doors counted. But when the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must get the family out ahead of the count before the well refills.
