# Jonah and the Bell

**by Oscar Eastwick**

> August Voss, a radio host taking calls from listeners who died last week, keeps the lights on and the doors counted. Then the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911 — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must learn the song's last verse without singing along, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Oscar Eastwick is the pen name of a former night-shift radio engineer from coastal East Anglia who spent years maintaining transmitters nobody admitted still received signals. He writes horror about small communities, old machinery, and the things frequencies carry after their owners are gone.
