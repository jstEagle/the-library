# Jonah and the House

**by Gideon Lockridge**

> Odile Joubert, a hospice nurse who hears the dying count backwards, has spent seven years telling themselves the noises have explanations. Then the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911 — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it.
