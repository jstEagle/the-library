# Jude and the Harvest

**by E. M. Montrose**

> Casper Marchetti, a folklorist recording songs the village refuses to sing twice, has spent five years telling themselves the noises have explanations. But when the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must return what was taken in 1974 before the last resident signs the papers.

## About the author

E. M. Montrose is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging oral histories in rural England, where more than one interview subject asked to have their words struck from the record. Jude and the Harvest is their second novel, written in a house they admit is probably haunted.
