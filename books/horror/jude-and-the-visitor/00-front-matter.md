# Jude and the Visitor

**by M. M. Nystrom**

> Noor Lockridge, a folklorist recording songs the village refuses to sing twice, has spent ten years telling themselves the noises have explanations. But when the excavation crew unearths a door, sealed from the inside, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must learn the song's last verse without singing along before the well refills.

## About the author

M. M. Nystrom is the pen name of a former field archivist who spent years collecting oral histories in remote coastal and moorland communities, an experience that left a lasting unease about what people choose not to record. Nystrom now writes horror from a house with too many doors, insisting the extra one was on the survey.
