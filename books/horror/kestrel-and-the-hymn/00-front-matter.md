## About the author

Mara Hartwell is the pen name of a former rural schoolteacher who spent seven years in a boarding school with genuinely strange acoustics before turning to horror full-time. She writes about institutions, belonging, and the things that hum beneath old floors, and lives with two cats who refuse to enter her study after dark.
