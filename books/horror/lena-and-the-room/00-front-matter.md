# Lena and the Room

**by Juniper Eriksen**

> Oscar Ashgrove, a radio host taking calls from listeners who died last week, has spent fifteen years telling themselves the noises have explanations. But when every mirror in the house starts reflecting the room one second late, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must return what was taken in 1974 before the last resident signs the papers.

## About the author

Juniper Eriksen is the pen name of a former night-shift radio producer from coastal Maine who spent years collecting listeners' stories about houses that refused to let them go. Lena and the Room is their second novel, written mostly between midnight and four a.m.
