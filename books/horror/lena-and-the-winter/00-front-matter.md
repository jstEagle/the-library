## About the author

Lior Joubert is the pen name of a former land registry clerk from the Loire Valley who spent eleven years cataloguing deeds for houses that burned, collapsed, or simply stopped appearing on maps. Their fiction draws on the bureaucratic horror of paperwork that outlives the people it describes.
