# Lior and the Portrait

**by Marguerite Sterling**

> August Voss, a folklorist recording songs the village refuses to sing twice, documents everything, because documentation is a kind of prayer. But when every mirror in the house starts reflecting the room one second late, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must hold out until sunrise with the truth about what was invited in, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Marguerite Sterling is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing oral histories in disappearing rural communities before turning to horror. She writes about the violence of being forgotten and lives, deliberately, somewhere without mirrors.
