## About the author

Cyrus Delacroix is the pen name of a former overnight radio producer who spent years logging calls from strangers at 3 a.m. and swears half of them were wrong about being alive. They write horror from a farmhouse in Vermont where the photographs, they note cheerfully, are all normal.
