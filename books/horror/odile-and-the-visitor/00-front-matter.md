# Odile and the Visitor

**by Quentin Joubert**

> Beatrix Reyes, a folklorist recording songs the village refuses to sing twice, documents everything, because documentation is a kind of prayer. But when all the photographs from grandma's house now include an extra chair, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
