## About the author

Cyrus Underhill is the pen name of a former boarding-school caretaker who spent eleven winters on the grounds of a shuttered New England academy. His fiction draws on institutional archives, oral histories of vanished students, and a personal refusal to explain away what he saw in the north stairwell.
