## About the author

Jonah Quintana is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing estate sales and unclaimed inheritances, an experience that left him suspicious of every family that keeps its records locked. He writes horror from a farmhouse in upstate New York, where the well water, he insists, tastes fine.
