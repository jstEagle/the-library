# Room and Bell

**by G. M. Marchetti**

> Marguerite Voss, a property surveyor appraising a house that appraises back, doesn't believe, has never believed, will not begin believing now. Then all the photographs from grandma's house now include an extra chair — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

G. M. Marchetti is a former building inspector who spent eleven years cataloguing condemned properties across the Rust Belt before turning to fiction full-time. They write horror about structures that remember, and live in a farmhouse they refuse to have appraised.
