# Room and Harvest

**by Odile Crane**

> Ines Sandoval, the new teacher at a school with a missing-class problem, keeps the lights on and the doors counted. But when all the photographs from grandma's house now include an extra chair, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must hold out until sunrise with the truth about what was invited in before the harvest moon rises.

## About the author

Odile Crane is the pen name of a former rural schoolteacher who spent nine years in towns too small to appear on maps. She writes horror about institutions, inheritance, and the quiet violence of belonging.
