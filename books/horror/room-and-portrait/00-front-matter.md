# Room and Portrait

**by Ines Galloway**

> Ivo Crane, a folklorist recording songs the village refuses to sing twice, has spent seven years telling themselves the noises have explanations. Then the sleep recordings capture a second voice answering questions — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must hold out until sunrise with the truth about what was invited in before the last resident signs the papers.
