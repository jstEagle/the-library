# Silas and the House

**by Ada Sandoval**

> Lior Ravensworth, a hospice nurse who hears the dying count backwards, has spent five years telling themselves the noises have explanations. But when the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must learn the song's last verse without singing along before the last resident signs the papers.

## About the author

Ada Sandoval is a former hospice volunteer from the Pacific Northwest who writes horror about the tenderness required at the edges of life. Her work has appeared in small-press anthologies, and Silas and the House is her first novel.
