## About the author

Lena Brightwater grew up above her grandmother's antique shop, where she was forbidden to look into the Victorian mirrors after dark—an instruction she obeyed for twenty years before writing this book. She lives alone, keeps all mirrors covered at night, and insists this is only a habit.
