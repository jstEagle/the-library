**by Dominic Fairweather**

> Edmund Voss, a folklorist recording songs the village refuses to sing twice, doesn't believe, has never believed, will not begin believing now. But when the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Dominic Fairweather is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing parish records in rural England before turning to fiction. His novels blend meticulous historical detail with folk horror, exploring what communities bury to survive.
