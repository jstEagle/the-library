## About the author

Paloma Okonkwo is the pen name of a Nigerian-British writer who grew up between Lagos and a rain-lashed coastal town in Cornwall, a duality that haunts her fiction. She writes horror about houses that remember, drawing on her years as a schoolteacher and her family's stories of inheritances that arrive with instructions.
