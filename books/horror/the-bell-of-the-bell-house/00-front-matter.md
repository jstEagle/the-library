# The Bell of the Bell House

**by Elodie Okonkwo**

> Edmund Villalobos, a hospice nurse who hears the dying count backwards, doesn't believe, has never believed, will not begin believing now. But when the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must hold out until sunrise with the truth about what was invited in before the last resident signs the papers.

## About the author

Elodie Okonkwo is a former palliative care volunteer from coastal Maine whose years sitting with the dying taught her that the last words are rarely the truest ones. She writes horror about institutions, inheritance, and the quiet violence of belonging.
