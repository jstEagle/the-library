## About the author

Jude Thackeray is the pen name of a former overnight radio producer who spent six years answering calls from strangers at 3 a.m. and never quite stopped wondering who was on the other end. The Endless House is their second novel, written in a rented cottage whose doors they counted nightly out of habit.
