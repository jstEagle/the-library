## About the author

Mirabel Almeida is the pen name of a former small-market radio producer who grew up in rural Pennsylvania coal country, where abandoned mine shafts and dry wells dotted every childhood summer. Their work blends oral-history intimacy with slow-burn folk horror, exploring what families agree never to say out loud.
