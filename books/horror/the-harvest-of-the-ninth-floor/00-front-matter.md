## About the author

August Sterling is the pen name of a former night-shift school custodian who spent eleven years convinced the boiler room had a tenth floor. They write horror about institutions that remember too much and families that remember too little. Sterling lives in a coastal town where the fog arrives on schedule and is, they insist, only fog.
