## About the author

Ivo Delacroix is the pen name of a former land surveyor who spent a decade mapping decommissioned infrastructure across the American Rust Belt before turning to fiction. Their work draws on abandoned-place archives and family oral histories, and they claim to have never entered a basement they didn't immediately regret.
