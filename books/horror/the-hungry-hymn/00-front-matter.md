## About the author

Marguerite Galloway is the pen name of a former church archivist who spent a decade cataloguing rural parish records across the American interior, where she collected the hymnals, ledgers, and burial disputes that seed her fiction. She writes horror that treats bureaucracy and belief as equally haunted, and she still cannot hear a bell without counting the strokes.
