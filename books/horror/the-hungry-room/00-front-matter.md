## About the author

Jude Crane is the pen name of a former rural schoolteacher who spent nine years in small-town classrooms before turning to horror full-time. They write about institutions that fail children and houses that remember, drawing on Appalachian folklore and their own habit of counting exits in every unfamiliar building.
