## About the author

Astrid Montrose is the pen name of a former overnight radio producer who spent a decade answering calls from insomniacs, grief-stricken strangers, and the occasional voice she could never quite explain. She writes horror about small towns and the debts they bury, and lives somewhere with very good cell reception.
