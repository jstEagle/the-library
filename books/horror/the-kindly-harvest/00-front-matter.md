## About the author

Rosalind Villalobos is the pen name of a former agricultural inspector who spent a decade documenting abandoned farmsteads across the borderlands, where locals refused to walk certain fields after dark. She writes horror about inheritance, rural silence, and the bargains families make that outlive them.
