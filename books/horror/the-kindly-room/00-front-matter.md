# The Kindly Room

**by Quentin Amberdale**

> Ada Voss, a property surveyor appraising a house that appraises back, keeps the lights on and the doors counted. But when every mirror in the house starts reflecting the room one second late, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must return what was taken in 1974 before the harvest moon rises.

## About the author

Quentin Amberdale is the pen name of a former building inspector who spent fifteen years cataloguing empty houses across northern England before turning to horror. Their fiction explores the quiet dread of places that remember their occupants better than the occupants remember themselves.
