## About the author

Silas Thornbury spent eleven years as a county archivist before turning to fiction, a career that left him with a professional reverence for records and a private distrust of what they omit. He writes in a converted surveyor's office in upstate New York, where he claims the filing cabinets are load-bearing in more ways than one.
