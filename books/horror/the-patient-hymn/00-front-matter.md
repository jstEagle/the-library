## About the author

Elodie Hartwell is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing deeds and estate records in the English countryside, where she became convinced that houses remember more than the people who live in them. She writes horror about bureaucracy, inheritance, and the terrible patience of old buildings.
