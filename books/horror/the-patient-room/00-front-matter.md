## About the author

Odile Sterling is the pen name of a former hospice night nurse who spent a decade writing down what patients said in their last hours. Her debut novella 'Count the Doors' won the Ashgrove Prize for quiet horror, and she lives in a farmhouse with more rooms than the deed allows.
