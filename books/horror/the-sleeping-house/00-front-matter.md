## About the author

Jonah Oyelaran is a Nigerian-British writer who grew up between Lagos and a Yorkshire mill town, collecting his grandmother's lullabies and his neighbors' ghost stories with equal seriousness. He writes horror about the places that mistake memory for ownership.
