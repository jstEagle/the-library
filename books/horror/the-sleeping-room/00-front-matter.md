## About the author

Ada Underhill is the pen name of a former county archivist who spent a decade cataloguing probate disputes and unsolved disappearances in rural Virginia. Her horror novels are known for their obsession with paperwork, inheritance law, and the quiet terror of official records that don't match the people they describe.
