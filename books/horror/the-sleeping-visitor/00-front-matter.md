# The Sleeping Visitor

**by Theo Joubert**

> Kestrel Sandoval, an urban explorer mapping tunnels that aren't on any survey, documents everything, because documentation is a kind of prayer. Then the sleep recordings capture a second voice answering questions — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author
