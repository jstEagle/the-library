## About the author

Beatrix Reyes is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing estate sales in the Hudson Valley, where she collected the town legends that refused to stay filed. She writes horror about houses that keep receipts, and lives, deliberately, somewhere with no basement.
