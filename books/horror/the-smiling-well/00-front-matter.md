## About the author

Tamsin Almeida is the pen name of a former archivist who spent nine years cataloguing estate papers in coastal New England, where she developed a professional unease about family records that end too neatly. She writes horror about houses that remember, and claims she has never once looked into an antique mirror after midnight — on principle.
