## About the author

Gideon Zhou is the pen name of a former land registry clerk who spent eleven years cataloguing properties that no one would buy twice. He writes horror from a rented flat above a decommissioned fire station, and claims his best ideas arrive whenever the building's old alarm tests itself at 3 a.m.
