# The Visitor of Old Chapel Road

**by Lior Ishikawa**

> Sable Montrose, a property surveyor appraising a house that appraises back, has spent twelve years telling themselves the noises have explanations. But when the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must hold out until sunrise with the truth about what was invited in, and time is already running out.
