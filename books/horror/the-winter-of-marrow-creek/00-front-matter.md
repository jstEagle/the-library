# The Winter of Marrow Creek

**by Ada Okonkwo**

> Paloma Coldharbour, an urban explorer mapping tunnels that aren't on any survey, has spent ten years telling themselves the noises have explanations. Then the excavation crew unearths a door, sealed from the inside — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must hold out until sunrise with the truth about what was invited in before the frost writes its final pattern.
