## About the author

P. M. Oyelaran is the pen name of a former palliative care volunteer turned writer who grew up in a house near an old churchyard and never quite stopped listening for bells. They write horror about tenderness under pressure, insisting that the scariest rooms are the ones where someone is trying to take care of you.
