## About the author

E. M. Vasquez is the pen name of a former overnight radio producer who spent years answering calls from strangers at 3 a.m. and never quite shook the feeling that some of them weren't among the living. They write horror about grief, small towns, and the terrible intimacy of voices in the dark.
