# Visitor and Well

**by Kestrel Zhou**

> Ivo Okonkwo, a property surveyor appraising a house that appraises back, doesn't believe, has never believed, will not begin believing now. Then the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911 — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must learn the song's last verse without singing along before the last resident signs the papers.
