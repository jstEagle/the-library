# Well and Visitor

**by Xanthe Kavanagh**

## About the author

Xanthe Kavanagh is the pen name of a former archivist of oral traditions who spent a decade recording folk songs in coastal and moorland villages before turning to fiction. Their work is known for horror built from patience — the dread of things documented too carefully.
