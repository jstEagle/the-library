# Wendell and the Harvest

**by Beatrix Montrose**

## About the author

Beatrix Montrose is the pen name of a former rural hospice worker who spent a decade collecting the things patients say in their final hours. She writes horror about small communities keeping large secrets, and lives somewhere with a well she refuses to drink from.
