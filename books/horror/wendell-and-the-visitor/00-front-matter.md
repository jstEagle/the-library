## About the author

Edmund Thackeray is the pen name of a former overnight radio engineer from the Ohio Valley who spent twenty years logging dead air and claims the quiet hours taught him everything he knows about what listens back. He writes horror rooted in small-town bargains, vanished children, and the machinery of forgetting.
