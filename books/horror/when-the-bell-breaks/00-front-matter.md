## About the author

Rosalind Ravensworth is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing estate sales in coastal New England, where she developed a professional unease about houses that seem to remember their occupants. She writes horror about belonging, bureaucracy, and the quiet violence of being unnoticed.
