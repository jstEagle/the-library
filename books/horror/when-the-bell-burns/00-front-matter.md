# When the Bell Burns

**by Dominic Marchetti**

> Rosalind Eriksen, a property surveyor appraising a house that appraises back, keeps the lights on and the doors counted. But when every mirror in the house starts reflecting the room one second late, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must finish the ritual the founders started before the last resident signs the papers.

## About the author
