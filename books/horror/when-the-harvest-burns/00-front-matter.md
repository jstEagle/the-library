## About the author

Vesper Vasquez grew up in rural farm country where abandoned homesteads outnumbered streetlights, and spent years collecting local legends about wells, harvests, and things that wait. Their fiction blends folk horror with intimate family secrets, exploring how small towns survive by agreeing not to look too closely.
