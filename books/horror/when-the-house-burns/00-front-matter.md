# When the House Burns

**by Freya Dravenmoor**

> Rosalind Quintana, a property surveyor appraising a house that appraises back, documents everything, because documentation is a kind of prayer. But when the excavation crew unearths a door, sealed from the inside, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must finish the ritual the founders started before the last resident signs the papers.

## About the author

Freya Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former land registry clerk from coastal Maine who spent eleven years cataloguing deeds to houses that no longer exist. Her fiction explores what buildings remember when their owners are gone.
