# When the Hymn Breaks

**by Isandro Crane**

> Ines Thornbury, a property surveyor appraising a house that appraises back, documents everything, because documentation is a kind of prayer. But when the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must learn the song's last verse without singing along, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Isandro Crane is the pen name of a former archivist who spent nine years cataloguing condemned buildings across northern England before turning to fiction. They write horror about paperwork, place, and the things that outlast both, and they still keep a surveyor's notebook by their bed.
