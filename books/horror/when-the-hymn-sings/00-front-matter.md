# When the Hymn Sings

**by Lena Blackwood**

> Marguerite Thornbury, a radio host taking calls from listeners who died last week, documents everything, because documentation is a kind of prayer. But when the childhood imaginary friend is waiting on the porch, unchanged, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must get the family out ahead of the count before the last resident signs the papers.

## About the author

Lena Blackwood spent a decade as a small-market radio producer before turning to horror, and she still can't sleep without static in the background. She writes from a farmhouse in upstate New York where, she insists, the phone lines are perfectly ordinary.
