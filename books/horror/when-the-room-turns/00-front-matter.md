# When the Room Turns

**by Hazel Quintana**

> Wren Montrose, a property surveyor appraising a house that appraises back, documents everything, because documentation is a kind of prayer. Then every mirror in the house starts reflecting the room one second late — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Hazel Quintana is a former building inspector from coastal Maine who spent a decade cataloguing condemned houses before turning to fiction. Her horror draws on real estate records, family ledgers, and the quiet dread of places that remember more than people do.
