# When the Smile Burns

**by Mirabel Ravensworth**

> Sable Eastwick, the new teacher at a school with a missing-class problem, doesn't believe, has never believed, will not begin believing now. But when the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must hold out until sunrise with the truth about what was invited in before the frost writes its final pattern.

## About the author

Mirabel Ravensworth is the pen name of a former rural schoolteacher who spent nine years in villages where everyone knew everyone, and one winter where she began to suspect that was the problem. She writes horror about the violence of belonging from a house with excellent locks.
