# When the Visitor Falls

**by Lior Thornbury**

> Theo Marchetti, a folklorist recording songs the village refuses to sing twice, has spent twenty years telling themselves the noises have explanations. Then the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911 — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must return what was taken in 1974 before the harvest moon rises.
