# Winter and Bell

**by Astrid Kavanagh**

## About the author

Astrid Kavanagh is a former palliative care chaplain who spent eleven years sitting with the dying in coastal Norfolk before turning to fiction. She writes horror about tenderness, insisting that grief is simply love with nowhere left to go.
