# Wren and the Visitor

**by Xanthe Underhill**

> Ines Galloway, a folklorist recording songs the village refuses to sing twice, keeps the lights on and the doors counted. But when the village's church bell rings although the bell was melted down in 1911, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must learn the song's last verse without singing along before the well refills.

## About the author

Xanthe Underhill is the pen name of a former archivist of oral traditions who spent a decade collecting folk songs across coastal England before turning to fiction. Their work blends fieldwork's patience with a conviction that every old song is a door left deliberately ajar.
