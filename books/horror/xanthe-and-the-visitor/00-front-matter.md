## About the author

Elodie Vasquez is a former funeral home archivist from coastal Maine who spent a decade cataloguing estate inventories of houses no one wanted to inherit. Her fiction draws on the quiet dread of family property, and 'Xanthe and the Visitor' is her third novel.
