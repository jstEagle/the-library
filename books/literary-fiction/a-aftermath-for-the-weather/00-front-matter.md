## About the author

Silas Underhill is the pen name of a former civil engineer who spent eleven years inspecting truss bridges across the Ohio Valley before turning to fiction. His work examines the load-bearing structures of ordinary lives — marriages, friendships, debts — and what happens when one member quietly fails.
