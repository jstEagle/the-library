# A Blue Hour for the Inventory

**by Tamsin Vasquez**

> Petra Pemberton, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Tamsin Vasquez is a former estate appraiser who spent a decade cataloguing other people's belongings before turning to fiction. She writes about inheritance, memory, and the objects we mistake for people, and lives near the coast with an unreliable record player.
