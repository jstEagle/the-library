# A Blue Hour for the Postcards

**by Paloma Vasquez**

> Ivo Sandoval, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Paloma Vasquez is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up between two languages and one long-defunct bus route. She writes about families that keep their most important sentences untranslated.
