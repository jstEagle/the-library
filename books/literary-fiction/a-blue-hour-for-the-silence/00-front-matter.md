# A Blue Hour for the Silence

**by Cyrus Eriksen**

> Isandro Dunmore, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, has spent ten years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Cyrus Eriksen is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloging other people's unfinished correspondence before turning to fiction. Raised between a fishing town and a university city, Eriksen writes about inheritance, silence, and the slow cost of keeping a family's story intact.
