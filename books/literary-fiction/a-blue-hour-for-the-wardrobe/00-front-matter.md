# A Blue Hour for the Wardrobe

**by Ivo Marchetti**

> Silas Blackwood, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ivo Marchetti is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unclaimed letters in a municipal dead-letter office. He writes quietly devastating fiction about the objects people leave behind, and lives near the sea with a piano he refuses to tune.
