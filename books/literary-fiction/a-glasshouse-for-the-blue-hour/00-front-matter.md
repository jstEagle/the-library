# A Glasshouse for the Blue Hour

**by Elodie Nakamura**

> Jonah Reyes, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent five years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a childhood home goes up for auction, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Elodie Nakamura is a former archivist who spent years cataloging damaged photographs from coastal towns, an experience that taught her how much of love lives in what almost got said. She writes from a converted greenhouse on the Pacific coast, where she keeps every postcard anyone has ever sent her.
