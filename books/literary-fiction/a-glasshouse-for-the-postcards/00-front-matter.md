# A Glasshouse for the Postcards

**by Wren Lockridge**

> Lena Galloway, a former prodigy returning to the conservatory as a janitor, has spent five years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a childhood home goes up for auction, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Wren Lockridge is the pen name of a former conservatory custodian who wrote this debut over four years of night shifts, drawing on a childhood spent in houses that were always being sold. They live in a coastal town where the last bus route was rerouted in 2019.
