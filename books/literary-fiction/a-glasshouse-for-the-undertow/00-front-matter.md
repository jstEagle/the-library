# A Glasshouse for the Undertow

**by U. M. Blackwood**

## About the author

U. M. Blackwood is the pen name of a former conservatory archivist who spent a decade cataloguing other people's unfinished letters before writing her own. Her fiction explores the quiet violence of things left unsaid, and she lives within earshot of the sea.
