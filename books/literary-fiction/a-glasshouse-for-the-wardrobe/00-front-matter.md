# A Glasshouse for the Wardrobe

**by Freya Thornbury**

> Coral Reyes, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must forgive the stranger in the old photographs, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Freya Thornbury is the pen name of a former postal clerk who spent a decade sorting dead letters in a coastal town, where undeliverable mail became an obsession. She writes about the things people almost say, and lives within walking distance of a ruined conservatory.
