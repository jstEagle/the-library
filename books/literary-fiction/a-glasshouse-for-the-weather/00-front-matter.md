# A Glasshouse for the Weather

**by Ulyana Oyelaran**

> Mara Blackwood, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Ulyana Oyelaran is a former court stenographer who spent six years documenting testimony in small-town courthouses across the American Midwest. She now lives near Lake Michigan, where she teaches community classes in endangered-alphabet calligraphy.
