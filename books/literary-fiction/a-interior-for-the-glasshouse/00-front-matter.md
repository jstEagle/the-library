## About the author

Paloma Amberdale is the pen name of a former estate auctioneer who spent a decade cataloguing the contents of other people's lives before turning to fiction. She writes from a converted conservatory in coastal Oregon, where she keeps an unsent letter of her own in a drawer she opens exactly once a year.
