# A Inventory for the Blue Hour

**by Jonah Whitlock**

> Theo Sandoval, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent fifteen years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Jonah Whitlock is the pen name of a former small-town postmaster who spent two decades sorting other people's undeliverable letters before turning to fiction. He writes about the things people mail instead of say, and lives within sight of a decommissioned lighthouse.
