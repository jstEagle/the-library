# A Inventory for the Postcards

**by Astrid Oyelaran**

> Rafael Okafor, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. Then a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must return to the place where the story went wrong before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Astrid Oyelaran is a former archivist for a municipal records office who spent years cataloguing condemned properties and the mail that kept arriving for them. She writes from Lagos and Lisbon about families that survive by forgetting.
