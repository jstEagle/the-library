## About the author

Juniper Ravensworth is the pen name of a former line cook who spent a decade in professional kitchens before turning to fiction, and whose stories often explore appetite as a form of power. She lives near the coast, keeps an annotated collection of vintage menus, and claims she can still taste every meal she has ever eaten.
