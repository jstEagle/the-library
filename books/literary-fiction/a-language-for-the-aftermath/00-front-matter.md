# A Language for the Aftermath

**by Astrid Fairweather**

> Astrid Vane, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has spent three years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Astrid Fairweather is the pen name of a former court stenographer who spent eleven years transcribing other people's confessions before writing her own fiction. She lives in a drafty house on the Maine coast whose radiators she has learned, like her protagonist, to read.
