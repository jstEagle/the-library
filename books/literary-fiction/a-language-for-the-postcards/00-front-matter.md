# A Language for the Postcards

**by Juniper Okonkwo**

> Jude Crane, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, has spent fifteen years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Juniper Okonkwo is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging endangered-language recordings before turning to fiction. She writes from a house full of unsent postcards, and this is her second novel.
