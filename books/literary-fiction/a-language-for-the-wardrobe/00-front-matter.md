# A Language for the Wardrobe

**by Y. M. Underhill**

> Yara Brightwater, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Y. M. Underhill is the pen name of a former night-shift archivist who spent eleven years cataloguing endangered-language recordings in municipal collections. Their fiction explores what institutions keep, and what they quietly discard.
