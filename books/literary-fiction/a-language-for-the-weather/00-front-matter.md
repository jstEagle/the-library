# A Language for the Weather

**by Theo Vane**

> Yara Montrose, a restaurant critic who has forgotten how food tastes, has spent twenty years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Theo Vane is the pen name of a former line cook who spent eleven years in professional kitchens before turning to fiction. He writes about appetite, silence, and the families people assemble out of shift schedules and shared bread.
