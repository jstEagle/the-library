# A Postcards for the Blue Hour

**by Ada Nystrom**

> Jonah Montrose, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has spent five years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ada Nystrom is the pen name of a former interpreter who grew up between a coastal fishing village and a northern city, writing about the erosion of small languages and the people who carry them. This is their second novel; the first was shortlisted for a regional literary prize.
