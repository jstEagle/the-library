# A Postcards for the Interior

**by Kestrel Amberdale**

> Paloma Galloway, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must return to the place where the story went wrong before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Kestrel Amberdale is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up between two languages and now writes about the ones that get away. They live near a decommissioned bus depot and collect unsent letters at estate sales.
