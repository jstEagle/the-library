# A Postcards for the Language

**by Cyrus Nystrom**

> Ansel Villalobos, a bridge inspector measuring the marriage in expansion joints, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a childhood home goes up for auction, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Cyrus Nystrom is a former municipal surveyor who spent eleven years inspecting infrastructure across the upper Midwest before turning to fiction. He writes about the quiet mathematics of ordinary lives from a farmhouse he shares with his partner and an unreasonable number of field notebooks.
