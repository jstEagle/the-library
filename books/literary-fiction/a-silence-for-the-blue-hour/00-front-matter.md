# A Silence for the Blue Hour

**by Edmund Okonkwo**

> Marguerite Okonkwo, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Edmund Okonkwo is the pen name of a Nigerian-British writer who worked for years as a court interpreter in London before turning to fiction. His writing explores the quiet violence of assimilation and the languages families keep losing on purpose.
