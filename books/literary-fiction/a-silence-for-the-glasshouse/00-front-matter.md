# A Silence for the Glasshouse

**by Marguerite Okonkwo**

> Ada Hartwell, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, has spent twenty years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Marguerite Okonkwo is the pen name of a former archivist who spent fifteen years cataloguing private papers in institutional libraries across West Africa and northern England. She writes quiet, forensic fiction about families that mistake silence for love.
