## About the author

Astrid Sandoval is a former municipal engineer who spent a decade inspecting bridges across the industrial Midwest before turning to fiction. Her work examines infrastructure as inheritance — the structures we maintain, the ones we abandon, and the people we fail to maintain along the way.
