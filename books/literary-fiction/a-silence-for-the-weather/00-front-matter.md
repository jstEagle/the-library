# A Silence for the Weather

**by Orsen Blackwood**

> Ivo Kavanagh, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Orsen Blackwood is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent two decades listening to other people's truths before writing any of his own. He lives near a bus depot and insists that all his books are translated from a language that no longer exists.
