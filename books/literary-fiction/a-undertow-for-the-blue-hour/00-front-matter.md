# A Undertow for the Blue Hour

**by Ansel Dravenmoor**

> Dominic Underhill, a former prodigy returning to the conservatory as a janitor, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a childhood home goes up for auction, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Ansel Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former conservatory custodian who spent eleven years sweeping practice rooms before publishing short fiction in small literary journals. A Undertow for the Blue Hour is their first novel, written on the last buses of a decommissioned transit line.
