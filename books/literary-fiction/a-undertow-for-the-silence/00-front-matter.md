# A Undertow for the Silence

**by Mirabel Ishikawa**

> Bram Marchetti, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has spent three years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a childhood home goes up for auction — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the demolition date arrives.
