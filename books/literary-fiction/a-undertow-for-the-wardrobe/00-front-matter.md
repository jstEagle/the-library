# A Undertow for the Wardrobe

**by Mara Dravenmoor**

> Marguerite Eastwick, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has spent ten years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a childhood home goes up for auction, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must return to the place where the story went wrong before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Mara Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent eleven years translating testimony in coastal towns where everyone knew everyone and no one said anything. This is her second novel; the first was quietly withdrawn after a libel scare she now calls her apprenticeship.
