# A Wardrobe for the Blue Hour

**by Orsen Eastwick**

> Odile Iversen, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. Then a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the mother's memory goes entirely.
