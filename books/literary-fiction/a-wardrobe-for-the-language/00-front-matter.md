# A Wardrobe for the Language

**by Ulyana Zhou**

> Petra Marchetti, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must return to the place where the story went wrong before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Ulyana Zhou is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing undeliverable mail in a northern port city. Their fiction explores how families survive their own silences, and this is their second novel.
