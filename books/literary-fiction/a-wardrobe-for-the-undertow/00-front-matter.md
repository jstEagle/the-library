# A Wardrobe for the Undertow

**by F. M. Okafor**

> Felix Underhill, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around, and time is already running out.

## About the author

F. M. Okafor is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing undelivered mail in a coastal municipal records office. They write literary fiction about the objects people leave behind when they cannot leave words.
