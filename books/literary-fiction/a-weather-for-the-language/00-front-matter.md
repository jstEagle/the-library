# A Weather for the Language

**by L. M. Brightwater**

> Noor Marchetti, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

L. M. Brightwater is the pen name of a former court transcriber who grew up in a multilingual port city and spent a decade documenting endangered dialects for community archives. Their fiction explores the grammar of grief and the sentences we leave unfinished.
