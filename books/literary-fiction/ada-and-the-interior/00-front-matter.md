# Ada and the Interior

**by Hazel Vasquez**

> Lior Winterbourne, a former prodigy returning to the conservatory as a janitor, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Hazel Vasquez is a former conservatory custodian turned novelist whose essays on music, labor, and memory have appeared in small literary journals across the Pacific Northwest. She writes from a converted practice room in Portland, where she keeps a piano she refuses to tune.
