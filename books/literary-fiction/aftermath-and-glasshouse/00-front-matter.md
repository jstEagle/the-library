# Aftermath and Glasshouse

**by Ada Pemberton**

> Wren Ishikawa, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must return to the place where the story went wrong before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Ada Pemberton is a former architectural conservator who spent a decade restoring heritage houses in northern England before turning to fiction full-time. Her work is preoccupied with buildings as witnesses, and she lives in a converted glasshouse annex where she writes by natural light.
