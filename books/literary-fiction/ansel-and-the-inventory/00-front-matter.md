# Ansel and the Inventory

**by Freya Vane**

> Elodie Dravenmoor, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Freya Vane is the pen name of a former estate appraiser who spent a decade cataloguing other people's inheritances before writing her own. She lives in a coastal town full of houses waiting to be emptied, and writes about the objects we keep and the words we don't.
