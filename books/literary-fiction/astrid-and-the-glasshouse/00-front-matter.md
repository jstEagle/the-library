# Astrid and the Glasshouse

**by Noor Ashgrove**

But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Noor Ashgrove is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unclaimed letters in a provincial records office. Astrid and the Glasshouse is her second novel, written in a rented cottage within sight of the sea.
