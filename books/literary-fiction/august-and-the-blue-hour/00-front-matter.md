## About the author

Sebastian Thornbury is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unclaimed letters in a provincial postal dead-letter office, an experience that haunts every page of their fiction. They write quietly devastating novels about the things people almost say, and live near the sea with a dog named after a typewriter.
