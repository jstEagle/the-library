## About the author

Felix Yarrow is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing municipal records in small industrial towns before turning to fiction. Their work explores how ordinary documents—deeds, ledgers, obituaries—conceal the private histories of people who were never meant to be remembered.
