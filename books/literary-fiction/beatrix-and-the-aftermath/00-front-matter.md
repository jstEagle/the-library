## About the author

Felix Eriksen is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging unclaimed estates in coastal New England, where he first encountered boxes of letters the dead never sent. He writes quietly devastating fiction about the paperwork of grief and lives in a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage with two opinionated cats.
