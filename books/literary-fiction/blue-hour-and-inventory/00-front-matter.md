# Blue Hour and Inventory

**by Petra Oyelaran**

> Lena Yarrow, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Petra Oyelaran is a Nigerian-British writer and former archivist whose work explores inheritance, diaspora, and the things families leave unsaid. She lives between London and Ibadan, where she collects recorded voices of elders for a digital language archive.
