## About the author

Wendell Montrose is the pen name of a former postal clerk who spent eleven years sorting undeliverable mail in a coastal town, collecting the stories of letters that never arrived. Blue Hour and Language is his second novel, following a well-received debut about lighthouse keepers and their unsent confessions.
