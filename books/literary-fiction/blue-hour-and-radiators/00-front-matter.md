# Blue Hour and Radiators

**by Cyrus Fairweather**

> Beatrix Lockridge, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent three years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Cyrus Fairweather is the pen name of a former small-town obituary writer who spent a decade collecting the letters people never mailed. He writes from a rented room above a defunct radio station, where he claims the static still talks back.
