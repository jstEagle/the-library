## About the author

E. M. Marchetti is the pen name of a former structural engineer who spent a decade inspecting bridges across the American Midwest before turning to fiction. Their work explores the architecture of families—the load-bearing lies, the hairline fractures—and they live, deliberately unlisted, near water.
