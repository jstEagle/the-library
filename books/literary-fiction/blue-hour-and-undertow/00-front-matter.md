# Blue Hour and Undertow

**by Vesper Reyes**

> Rosalind Winterbourne, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Vesper Reyes is the pen name of a former postal archivist who spent a decade cataloguing undeliverable mail in a coastal city's dead letter office. Their fiction explores what people confess only to paper, and their stories have appeared in small literary journals under three other names they no longer answer to.
