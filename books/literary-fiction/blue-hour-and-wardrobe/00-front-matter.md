# Blue Hour and Wardrobe

**by Casper Vasquez**

> Ada Iversen, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must return to the place where the story went wrong before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Casper Vasquez is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unclaimed letters in municipal storage before turning to fiction. They write quietly devastating stories about people who mistake solitude for safety.
