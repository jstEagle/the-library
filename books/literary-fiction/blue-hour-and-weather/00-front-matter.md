# Blue Hour and Weather

**by Astrid Marchetti**

> Ada Montrose, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Astrid Marchetti is the pen name of a former court stenographer who spent two decades transcribing other people's confessions before writing her own fiction. She lives near a lake in upstate New York, where she collects unsent letters at estate sales.
