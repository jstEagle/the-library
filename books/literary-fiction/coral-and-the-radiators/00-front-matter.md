# Coral and the Radiators

**by Ivo Pemberton**

> Edmund Delacroix, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has spent fifteen years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must return to the place where the story went wrong before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Ivo Pemberton is the pen name of a former piano tuner's apprentice who spent a decade in coastal mill towns collecting the stories houses tell through their pipes. This is their second novel, written mostly on night buses.
