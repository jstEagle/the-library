# Cyrus and the Silence

**by Jonah Reyes**

## About the author

Jonah Reyes is the pen name of a former small-town radio archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unsung local songwriters before turning to fiction. Cyrus and the Silence is their second novel, following a well-reviewed debut about a lighthouse keeper's letters.
