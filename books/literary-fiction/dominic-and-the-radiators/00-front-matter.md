# Dominic and the Radiators

**by Rosalind Blackwood**

> Ada Crane, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, has spent twenty years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Rosalind Blackwood is the pen name of a former night-shift archivist who spent eleven years cataloguing unclaimed recordings in a municipal library. She writes about grief, cities, and the small illegalities of love, and lives near a radiator she refuses to fix.
