**by Tamsin Hartwell**

## About the author

Tamsin Hartwell is the pen name of a former orchestral librarian who spent fifteen years cataloguing other people's masterpieces before turning to fiction. Her work explores institutions as characters, and the quiet violence of things left unsaid.
