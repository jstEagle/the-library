# Dominic and the Undertow

**by Silas Marchetti**

> Gideon Ishikawa, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent seven years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Silas Marchetti is the pen name of a former municipal archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the records of demolished buildings in coastal Pacific towns. This is a second novel, written in the margins of a day job translating property deeds.
