# Elodie and the Inventory

**by Casper Sterling**

> Wren Iversen, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the mother's memory goes entirely.
