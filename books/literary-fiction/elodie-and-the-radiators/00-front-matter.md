**by Cyrus Iversen**

## About the author

Cyrus Iversen is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging other people's unopened correspondence before turning to fiction. They write quietly devastating novels about inheritance, secrecy, and the families we assemble when the given ones fail.
