# Felix and the Silence

**by Mirabel Brightwater**

> Casper Blackwood, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Mirabel Brightwater is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing other people's unfinished correspondence before turning to fiction. She writes at night, in libraries, about the quiet violence families commit in the name of love.
