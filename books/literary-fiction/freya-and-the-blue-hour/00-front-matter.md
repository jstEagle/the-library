## About the author

Isandro Brightwater is the pen name of a former night-shift archivist who spent a decade cataloguing orphaned recordings in a coastal city that never fully sleeps. Their fiction explores how love survives institutional forgetting, and 'Freya and the Blue Hour' is their second novel.
