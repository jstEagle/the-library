# Freya and the Silence

**by Emrys Sterling**

> Sebastian Kavanagh, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has spent five years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Emrys Sterling is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up between two languages and never fully belonged to either. They write about families that keep their most important sentences unfinished.
