## About the author

Sebastian Pemberton is the pen name of a former structural engineer who spent a decade inspecting bridges along the northern coast before turning to fiction. His work examines the quiet architectures of concealment—what families build, what they load-bearing-ly hide, and what collapses when the inspection comes due.
