# Gideon and the Inventory

**by Noor Dravenmoor**

## About the author

Noor Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing endangered-language recordings in coastal communities before turning to fiction. They write quietly devastating novels about the things we preserve and the people we fail to.
