# Gideon and the Radiators

**by Bram Underhill**

> Tamsin Brightwater, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Bram Underhill is the pen name of a former estate auctioneer who spent a decade cataloguing the belongings of the recently bereaved. They write literary fiction about the objects and silences families leave behind, and live in a converted chapel on the Yorkshire coast.
