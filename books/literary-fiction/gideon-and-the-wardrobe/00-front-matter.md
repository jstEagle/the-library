# Gideon and the Wardrobe

**by B. M. Fairweather**

> Hazel Vasquez, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.
