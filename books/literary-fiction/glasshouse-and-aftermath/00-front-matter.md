# Glasshouse and Aftermath

**by Juniper Almeida**

> Astrid Voss, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Juniper Almeida is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing undeliverable mail in a coastal municipal records office. Their fiction explores the objects people leave behind when they cannot leave words.
