# Glasshouse and Postcards

**by Silas Ishikawa**

> Theo Okafor, a bridge inspector measuring the marriage in expansion joints, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Silas Ishikawa is a former structural engineer who spent nine years inspecting bridges along the Ohio River before turning to fiction full-time. His work examines the quiet mechanics of things that hold — marriages, families, steel — and the precise moment they fail.
