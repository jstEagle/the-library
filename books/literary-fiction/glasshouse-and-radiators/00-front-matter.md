## About the author

Wren Yarrow is a former structural drafter who spent a decade inspecting bridges across the northern counties before turning to fiction, and the precision of that work still shows in every sentence. Their stories examine how people build lives the way they build spans—assuming the load will hold—and have appeared in several small literary journals.
