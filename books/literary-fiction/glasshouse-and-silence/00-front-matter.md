# Glasshouse and Silence

**by Jude Kavanagh**

> Ansel Galloway, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Jude Kavanagh is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unclaimed correspondence in municipal storage. Their fiction explores what people confess to paper that they cannot say aloud.
