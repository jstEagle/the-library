# Hazel and the Interior

**by Sebastian Amberdale**

> Quentin Yarrow, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the house sale closes.
