# Hazel and the Radiators

**by Casper Vasquez**

> Coral Ishikawa, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must forgive the stranger in the old photographs, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Casper Vasquez is the pen name of a former building superintendent who spent a decade listening to the pipes of pre-war apartment houses. Their essays on sound, tenancy, and family debt have appeared in small literary journals under three other names.
