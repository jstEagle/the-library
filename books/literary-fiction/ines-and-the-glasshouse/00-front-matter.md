**by C. M. Thornbury**

## About the author

C. M. Thornbury is the pen name of a former food writer who spent a decade reviewing restaurants before leaving the trade to write fiction about families and the meals they avoid sharing together. They live near a botanical garden and claim to have never successfully grown anything.
