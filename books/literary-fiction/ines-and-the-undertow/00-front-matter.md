# Ines and the Undertow

**by Elodie Sterling**

> Bram Okonkwo, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, has spent twenty years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the mother's memory goes entirely.
