# Interior and Aftermath

**by Zephyrine Iversen**

> Zephyrine Almeida, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Zephyrine Iversen is a former court interpreter who grew up between two languages and one long family silence. 'Interior and Aftermath' is her second novel, written in rented rooms across three cities.
