# Interior and Glasshouse

**by W. M. Coldharbour**

> Emrys Pemberton, a former prodigy returning to the conservatory as a janitor, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

W. M. Coldharbour is the pen name of a former conservatory caretaker who spent a decade sweeping practice rooms before publishing short fiction in small literary journals. Interior and Glasshouse is their first novel, written largely in the margins of maintenance logs.
