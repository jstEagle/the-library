# Interior and Inventory

**by Kestrel Thackeray**

> Casper Brightwater, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Kestrel Thackeray is the pen name of a former interpreter who grew up between two languages and one silence. Their work examines how families curate their own histories, and what survives when the archivists begin to forget.
