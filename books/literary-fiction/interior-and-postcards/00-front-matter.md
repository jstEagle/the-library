# Interior and Postcards

**by Emrys Galloway**

> Xanthe Lockridge, a restaurant critic who has forgotten how food tastes, has spent five years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Emrys Galloway is the pen name of a former food writer who left restaurant journalism after a decade of reviews he no longer believes. They live near a decommissioned bus line and write about inheritance, appetite, and the things families postmark but never send.
