# Interior and Undertow

**by Zephyrine Eastwick**

> Theo Galloway, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has spent ten years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Zephyrine Eastwick is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up between two coastal towns and two grammars of silence. Her fiction explores how communities keep what empires cannot translate.
