# Interior and Wardrobe

**by A. M. Montrose**

> Ulyana Fairweather, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must return to the place where the story went wrong before the house sale closes.

## About the author

A. M. Montrose is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up between two languages and claims fluency in neither. They live near the sea and write about the words people bury in order to belong.
