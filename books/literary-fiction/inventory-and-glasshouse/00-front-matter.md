# Inventory and Glasshouse

**by R. M. Okafor**

> Rosalind Zhou, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. But when a childhood home goes up for auction, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

R. M. Okafor is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up between Lagos, Manchester, and Singapore, and writes about the quiet violence of translation. Their short fiction has appeared in several small journals; Inventory and Glasshouse is their first novel.
