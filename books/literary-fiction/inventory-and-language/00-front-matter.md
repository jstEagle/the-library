# Inventory and Language

**by Rosalind Joubert**

> Zephyrine Blackwood, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author
