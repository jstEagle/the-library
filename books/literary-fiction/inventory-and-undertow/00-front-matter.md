# Inventory and Undertow

**by Bram Brightwater**

> Casper Reyes, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Bram Brightwater spent a decade tuning pianos in decommissioned schools and boarding houses across the Rust Belt, collecting the stories buildings tell through their heating systems. Inventory and Undertow is his second novel, written mostly in the basements of houses about to be sold.
