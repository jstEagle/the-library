# Ivo and the Silence

**by Oscar Oyelaran**

> Ines Yarrow, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, has spent three years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Oscar Oyelaran is a Nigerian-British writer who spent a decade as an archivist before turning to fiction, a career that taught him how much of a life lives in what was never said. He writes about inheritance, houses, and the families we fail to leave.
