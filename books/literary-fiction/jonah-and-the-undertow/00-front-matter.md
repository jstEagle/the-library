# Jonah and the Undertow

**by Wendell Almeida**

> Jonah Joubert, a former prodigy returning to the conservatory as a janitor, has spent three years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Wendell Almeida is the pen name of a former conservatory custodian who spent eleven years sweeping the halls where he had once been a scholarship student. His fiction concerns the people institutions forget on purpose, and this is his third novel.
