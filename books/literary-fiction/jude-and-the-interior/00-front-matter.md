**by Mara Dunmore**

## About the author

Mara Dunmore is the pen name of a former food writer from the Pacific Northwest who spent a decade reviewing restaurants before turning to fiction about the things we cannot swallow. Her work examines inheritance — of houses, of silences, of appetites — with a critic's precision and a daughter's tenderness.
