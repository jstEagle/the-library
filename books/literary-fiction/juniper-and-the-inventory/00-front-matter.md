# Juniper and the Inventory

**by Felix Ravensworth**

## About the author

Felix Ravensworth is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent fifteen years translating other people's confessions before writing any of his own. He lives in a coastal town where the old language survives mainly in place names and arguments.
