# Language and Blue Hour

**by Theo Coldharbour**

> Kestrel Halloran, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must return to the place where the story went wrong before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Theo Coldharbour is the pen name of a former court transcriptionist who spent a decade recording other people's words before writing fiction about the ones left unsaid. They live near a decommissioned bus depot and collect dictionaries of extinct languages they cannot read.
