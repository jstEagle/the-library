## About the author

I. M. Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging unclaimed letters in municipal storage, a job that taught her how much of a life fits on a postcard. Language and Interior is her second novel, following a widely praised debut about a translator who loses her mother tongue.
