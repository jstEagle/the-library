# Language and Weather

**by Felix Sterling**

> Theo Montrose, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, has spent five years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Felix Sterling is the pen name of a former night-shift archivist who spent a decade cataloguing private letters donated to public collections. Language and Weather is their second novel, written mostly between midnight and dawn.
