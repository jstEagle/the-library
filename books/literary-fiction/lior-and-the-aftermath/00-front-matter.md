# Lior and the Aftermath

**by B. M. Reyes**

> Marguerite Eastwick, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the demolition date arrives.
