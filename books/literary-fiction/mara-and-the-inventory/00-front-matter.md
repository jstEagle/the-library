## About the author

Wren Lockridge is the pen name of a former municipal engineer who spent eleven years inspecting bridges across the Rust Belt before turning to fiction. Her work examines the quiet infrastructures of obligation—family, debt, memory—that hold communities up long after their official supports have failed.
