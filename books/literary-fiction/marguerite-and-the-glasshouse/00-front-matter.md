# Marguerite and the Glasshouse

**by Petra Voss**

But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Petra Voss is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent fifteen years translating testimony in immigration hearings before turning to fiction. She writes about language, exile, and the families we invent to survive our real ones.
