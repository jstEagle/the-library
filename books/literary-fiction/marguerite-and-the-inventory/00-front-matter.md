# Marguerite and the Inventory

**by Quentin Sandoval**

> Isandro Thornbury, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Quentin Sandoval is the pen name of a former night-shift archivist who spent six years cataloging oral histories for a university that later lost funding for the collection. This is their second novel; the first was written entirely on the backs of discarded inventory slips.
