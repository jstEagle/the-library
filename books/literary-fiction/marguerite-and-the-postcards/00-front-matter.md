# Marguerite and the Postcards

**by Hazel Underhill**

> Coral Crane, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, has spent twenty years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must return to the place where the story went wrong, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Hazel Underhill is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging other people's unfinished correspondence before turning to fiction. She writes about inheritances of every kind—the ones we receive, and the ones we refuse.
