**by Hazel Dravenmoor**

> Odile Blackwood, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. ## About the author

Hazel Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former estate appraiser who spent a decade cataloguing other people's inheritances before writing about her own. She lives in a converted schoolhouse where, she admits, there are still two wardrobes she hasn't opened.
