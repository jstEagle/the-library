# Mirabel and the Glasshouse

**by Coral Winterbourne**

> Wren Vane, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent three years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a childhood home goes up for auction, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Coral Winterbourne is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing other people's unsent letters before turning to fiction. She writes about quiet families, coastal towns, and the things we almost say, and lives within sight of an abandoned greenhouse.
