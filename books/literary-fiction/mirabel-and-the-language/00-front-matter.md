# Mirabel and the Language

**by Jonah Dravenmoor**

> Petra Okonkwo, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has spent ten years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Jonah Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent years listening to what people almost said. They write about inheritance, silence, and the families we build around unspeakable things, and live between two cities and three languages.
