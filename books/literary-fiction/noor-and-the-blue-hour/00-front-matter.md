# Noor and the Blue Hour

**by Lena Blackwood**

> Kestrel Marchetti, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, has spent fifteen years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Lena Blackwood is the pen name of a former overnight radio producer who spent a decade collecting the stories of people who call in at 3 a.m. She writes about the hours when cities pretend to sleep and the things people only say when they believe no one is listening.
