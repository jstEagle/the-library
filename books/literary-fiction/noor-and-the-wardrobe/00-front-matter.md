## About the author

Sebastian Sandoval is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent a decade transcribing other people's truths before writing his own. He lives near a coastal town whose library keeps a shelf of dying-language grammars, and he claims the wardrobe in his childhood home still smells of smoke.
