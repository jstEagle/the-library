# Noor and the Weather

**by W. M. Galloway**

> Theo Amberdale, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the house sale closes.
