# Odile and the Glasshouse

**by Edmund Voss**

> Paloma Zhou, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Edmund Voss is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the estates of families that no longer exist, a job that taught him how much of a life is stored in what gets left out. He writes from a coastal town where the fog arrives earlier every year, and where, he insists, the mail is unreliable in instructive ways.
