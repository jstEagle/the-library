# Odile and the Postcards

**by Vesper Marchetti**

> Quentin Coldharbour, a bridge inspector measuring the marriage in expansion joints, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Vesper Marchetti is the pen name of a former municipal archivist who spent a decade cataloguing undelivered mail from condemned buildings. Their stories examine how ordinary people survive by disappearing, and what it costs to come back.
