# Orsen and the Postcards

**by Rosalind Ashgrove**

> Quentin Dravenmoor, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Rosalind Ashgrove is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing undelivered correspondence in municipal storage. She writes about language, houses, and the things families seal behind walls.
