# Orsen and the Undertow

**by O. M. Delacroix**

> Yara Iversen, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent seven years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

O. M. Delacroix is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing undeliverable mail from coastal towns. They write quietly devastating fiction about people who communicate everything except the thing that matters.
