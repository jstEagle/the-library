# Paloma and the Radiators

**by Ines Nakamura**

> Odile Coldharbour, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Ines Nakamura is the pen name of a former building superintendent who spent fifteen years listening to the plumbing of prewar apartment houses before turning to fiction. Her stories about urban memory have appeared in small literary journals, and this is her first novel.
