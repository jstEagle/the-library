# Paloma and the Weather

**by Emrys Thackeray**

> Mara Eriksen, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a childhood home goes up for auction, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Emrys Thackeray is the pen name of a former piano restorer who spent two decades listening to houses before writing about them. Paloma and the Weather is his second novel, written in a rented cottage whose radiators he refuses to fix.
