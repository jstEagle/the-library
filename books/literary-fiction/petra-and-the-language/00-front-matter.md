# Petra and the Language

**by Cyrus Yarrow**

> Xanthe Delacroix, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Cyrus Yarrow is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent a decade transcribing other people's confessions before writing his own fiction. He lives near a river that changes its name every fifty miles, and writes in the margins of demolition notices.
