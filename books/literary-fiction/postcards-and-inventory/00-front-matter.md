# Postcards and Inventory

**by Quentin Quintana**

> Sable Brightwater, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent seven years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Quentin Quintana is a former estate auctioneer from the Pacific Northwest whose novels are known for their quiet obsessions with objects, letters, and the families that mislabel them. He writes in longhand on postcards he never sends.
