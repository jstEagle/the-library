# Postcards and Silence

**by A. M. Ravensworth**

> Oscar Amberdale, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, has spent three years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must return to the place where the story went wrong before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

A. M. Ravensworth is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing other people's unfinished letters before turning to fiction. Raised between a coastal town and its inland twin, they write about inheritance, place, and the words families refuse to say aloud.
