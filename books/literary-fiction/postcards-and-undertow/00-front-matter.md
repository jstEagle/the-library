**by Juniper Thackeray**

## About the author

Juniper Thackeray is the pen name of a former piano restorer who spent fifteen years listening to other people's houses before writing about them. She lives on a cold coast, keeps an unfinished correspondence of her own, and maintains that every radiator tells the truth eventually.
