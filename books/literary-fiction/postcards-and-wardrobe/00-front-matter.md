# Postcards and Wardrobe

**by Oscar Nakamura**

> Sable Oyelaran, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Oscar Nakamura is the pen name of a former postal archivist who spent twelve years cataloguing undeliverable mail before turning to fiction. They write quietly devastating stories about the objects people leave behind and the words they never send.
