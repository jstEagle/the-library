**by Wren Ravensworth**

## About the author

Wren Ravensworth is the pen name of a former conservatory librarian who spent a decade cataloguing other people's manuscripts before writing about the ones that got away. Postcards and Weather is the second novel, written in the margins of night shifts and dedicated to the last speakers of small things.
