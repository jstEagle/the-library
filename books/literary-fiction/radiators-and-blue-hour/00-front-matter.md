# Radiators and Blue Hour

**by Ada Eastwick**

> Zephyrine Yarrow, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, has spent seven years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Ada Eastwick is the pen name of a former radio archivist who spent a decade cataloging orphaned recordings and the people who made them. She writes from a house with too many radiators, all of which clank at blue hour.
