# Radiators and Inventory

**by Petra Hartwell**

> Astrid Brightwater, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent ten years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a childhood home goes up for auction, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Petra Hartwell is a former estate auctioneer from coastal Maine who spent fifteen years cataloguing other people's abandoned belongings before turning to fiction. Her debut novel draws on her obsession with the objects people cannot bring themselves to throw away.
