# Radiators and Postcards

**by Quentin Okonkwo**

> Isandro Sandoval, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Quentin Okonkwo is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up between three languages and claims to dream in none of them. Radiators and Postcards is their second novel, written in the margins of a decade spent translating other people's testimony.
