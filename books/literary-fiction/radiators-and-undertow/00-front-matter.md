## About the author

Odile Lockridge spent a decade working as a literary translator in port cities before turning to fiction, and her prose carries the salt-stain of borrowed vocabularies and half-remembered lullabies. She writes from a narrow flat above a laundromat, where she keeps an inventory notebook of every word she has forgotten.
