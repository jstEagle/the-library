# Rosalind and the Glasshouse

**by Tamsin Halloran**

> Astrid Vasquez, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Tamsin Halloran is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing oral histories in coastal cities before turning to fiction. She writes about inheritance, quiet love, and the places we lose to timetables.
