# Rosalind and the Inventory

**by Edmund Ravensworth**

> Paloma Blackwood, a restaurant critic who has forgotten how food tastes, has spent fifteen years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a childhood home goes up for auction, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Edmund Ravensworth is the pen name of a former line cook who spent a decade writing menu copy before turning to fiction. Rosalind and the Inventory is their second novel, written largely on night buses between service shifts.
