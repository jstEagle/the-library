# Sable and the Aftermath

**by Bram Dravenmoor**

> Freya Villalobos, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent seven years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Bram Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former postal archivist who spent fifteen years cataloguing undeliverable mail in a northern port city. They write quietly devastating literary fiction about the words people save for too late.
