# Sebastian and the Glasshouse

**by Rosalind Hartwell**

> Vesper Crane, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Rosalind Hartwell is a former court interpreter who spent fifteen years listening to other people's truths before beginning to write her own. She lives near a decommissioned botanical garden and writes exclusively in the early morning, before the words have time to hide.
