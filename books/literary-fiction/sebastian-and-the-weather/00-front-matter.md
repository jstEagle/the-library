# Sebastian and the Weather

**by Oscar Blackwood**

> Rafael Vasquez, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent five years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Oscar Blackwood is the pen name of a former court translator who spent a decade rendering testimony for families torn between two languages. He writes literary fiction about the silences immigrants carry, and lives within sight of a coastline he refuses to name.
