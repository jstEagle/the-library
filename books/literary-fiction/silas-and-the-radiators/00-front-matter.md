# Silas and the Radiators

**by Theo Hartwell**

> Lior Hartwell, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has spent seven years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must return to the place where the story went wrong before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.
