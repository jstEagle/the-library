# Silas and the Silence

**by Ivo Eriksen**

> August Oyelaran, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, has spent twenty years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ivo Eriksen is the pen name of a Nigerian-Danish writer raised between Lagos and Aarhus, whose work examines inheritance, exile, and the grammar of things left unsaid. Silas and the Silence is their second novel, written over seven years in rented rooms on two continents.
