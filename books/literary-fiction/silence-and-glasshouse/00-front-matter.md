# Silence and Glasshouse

**by Ivo Oyelaran**

> August Joubert, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Ivo Oyelaran is a former archivist who spent years cataloging other people's unfinished letters before turning to fiction. He writes about inheritance, music, and the things families keep in glasshouses — visible, protected, and never touched.
