**by Q. M. Amberdale**

## About the author

Q. M. Amberdale is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent fifteen years rendering other people's truths in borrowed words before turning to fiction. They live near a coastal archive of endangered languages and write at dawn, when the dictionaries are still asleep.
