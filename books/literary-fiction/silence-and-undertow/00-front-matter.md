# Silence and Undertow

**by M. M. Galloway**

> Mirabel Okafor, a restaurant critic who has forgotten how food tastes, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

M. M. Galloway is the pen name of a former food writer who spent a decade covering restaurant culture before turning to fiction about the things families refuse to say at the table. They live near a coastal city whose oldest bus line was recently decommissioned, an event that inspired this novel.
