## About the author

Astrid Yarrow is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing private correspondence donated to regional libraries, an experience that left her convinced every family is a locked room with one key. She writes from a converted glasshouse on the coast, where she keeps her own unsent letters in a box she swears she will one day post.
