## About the author

Y. M. Eriksen grew up between two languages on a fjord that was dammed for hydroelectric power, an experience they describe as 'learning to grieve in someone else's grammar.' They live in Trondheim, where they work as a court interpreter and write novels about what justice looks like when it arrives too late for the law.
