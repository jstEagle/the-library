# The Blue Hour of Milton Flats

**by Odile Marchetti**

> Ivo Halloran, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, has spent ten years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the demolition date arrives.
