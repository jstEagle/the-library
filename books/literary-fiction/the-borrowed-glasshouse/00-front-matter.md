# The Borrowed Glasshouse

**by Casper Brightwater**

> Casper Iversen, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, has spent twenty years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud, and time is already running out.
