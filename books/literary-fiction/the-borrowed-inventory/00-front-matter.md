## About the author

Sebastian Yarrow is the pen name of a former line cook who spent a decade writing menu copy before turning to fiction. He lives in a coastal town he refuses to name, and claims he can still remember what his grandmother's kitchen smelled like, which he considers his greatest literary asset.
