# The Borrowed Postcards

**by Mara Iversen**

> Xanthe Okafor, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Mara Iversen is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging other people's unmailed letters in coastal Norway and northern England. She writes about inheritance, quiet loves, and the objects that outlive their owners.
