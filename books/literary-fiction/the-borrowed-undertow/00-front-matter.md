# The Borrowed Undertow

**by Ada Okonkwo**

## About the author

Ada Okonkwo is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up between Lagos and a fading coastal town, writing about the words families lose in transit. Their work has appeared in several literary journals, and this is their first novel.
