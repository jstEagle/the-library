## About the author

Sebastian Crane is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing undelivered letters in a municipal records office before turning to fiction. He writes from a coastal town where fog arrives earlier than the mail, and claims every novel begins with a sentence he was too afraid to send.
