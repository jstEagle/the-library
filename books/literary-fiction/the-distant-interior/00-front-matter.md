# The Distant Interior

**by Astrid Almeida**

> Petra Whitlock, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has spent three years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must return to the place where the story went wrong, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Astrid Almeida is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up between two languages and one silence. She writes about translation as an act of witness and lives near a river whose original name she is still learning.
