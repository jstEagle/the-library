# The Distant Inventory

**by Yara Sandoval**

> Jonah Eriksen, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Yara Sandoval grew up between two languages and one disappearing dialect, working as a court interpreter before turning to fiction. She writes about people who carry other people's words for a living.
