# The Distant Postcards

**by Jonah Thornbury**

> Oscar Delacroix, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent five years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must return to the place where the story went wrong, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Jonah Thornbury is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing other people's unsent correspondence before writing fiction of his own. He lives near the sea and claims he has never mailed a letter he didn't first read aloud to an empty room.
