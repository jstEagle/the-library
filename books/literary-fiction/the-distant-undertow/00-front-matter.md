# The Distant Undertow

**by Paloma Marchetti**

> Silas Pemberton, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must return to the place where the story went wrong before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Paloma Marchetti worked for nine years as an archivist in a coastal municipal library before turning to fiction full-time. Her writing explores quiet people caught in loud inheritances, and she lives above a defunct ferry terminal with her dog, Bricole.
