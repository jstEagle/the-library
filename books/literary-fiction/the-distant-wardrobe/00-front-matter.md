# The Distant Wardrobe

**by Wendell Nystrom**

> Cyrus Villalobos, a restaurant critic who has forgotten how food tastes, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Wendell Nystrom is the pen name of a former obituary writer who spent a decade composing other people's goodbyes before turning to fiction about the ones we fail to deliver. He lives near a house scheduled for demolition and visits it often.
