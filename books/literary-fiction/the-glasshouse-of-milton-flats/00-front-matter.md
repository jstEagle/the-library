# The Glasshouse of Milton Flats

**by Lena Okonkwo**

> Paloma Dunmore, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, has spent five years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Lena Okonkwo is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing oral histories from disappearing communities before turning to fiction. She writes about language, memory, and the quiet violence of families from a small apartment above a laundromat.
