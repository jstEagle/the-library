# The Half-Light Blue Hour

**by Rafael Nakamura**

> Juniper Villalobos, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Rafael Nakamura is the pen name of a former legal interpreter who worked a decade in immigration and asylum courts before turning to fiction. Their stories about language, silence, and the machinery of justice have appeared in several small literary journals.
