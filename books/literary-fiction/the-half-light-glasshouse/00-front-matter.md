# The Half-Light Glasshouse

**by Zephyrine Blackwood**

> Isandro Marchetti, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Zephyrine Blackwood is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up between three languages and claims none of them entirely. She writes from a coastal apartment full of unlabeled jars of borrowed words.
