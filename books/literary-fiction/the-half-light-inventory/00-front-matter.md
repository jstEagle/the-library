# The Half-Light Inventory

**by Ulyana Dravenmoor**

> Ines Quintana, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has spent twenty years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Ulyana Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former radio archivist who spent a decade recording disappearing dialects across coastal cities before turning to fiction. She writes from a converted tram depot, where she claims the heating pipes still carry conversations from 1974.
