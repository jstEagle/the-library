# The Half-Light Language

**by Ansel Eriksen**

> Silas Almeida, a restaurant critic who has forgotten how food tastes, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must return to the place where the story went wrong, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ansel Eriksen is the pen name of a former line cook who spent six years in kitchens along the northern Atlantic before turning to writing. Their essays on food, memory, and silence have appeared in small literary journals, and 'The Half-Light Language' is their first novel.
