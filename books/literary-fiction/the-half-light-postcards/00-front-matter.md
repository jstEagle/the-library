# The Half-Light Postcards

**by I. M. Winterbourne**

> Dominic Pemberton, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

I. M. Winterbourne is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloging private letters in municipal collections across northern England. They write quietly devastating fiction about inheritance, language, and the things families refuse to say aloud.
