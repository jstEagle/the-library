# The Half-Light Silence

**by Xanthe Montrose**

> Juniper Pemberton, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Xanthe Montrose is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unclaimed letters in a coastal county records office. Their fiction explores the quiet violence of family secrets and the objects that outlast the people who owned them.
