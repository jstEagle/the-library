# The Half-Light Undertow

**by Sable Fairweather**

> Felix Eriksen, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Sable Fairweather is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up between two languages on the northern coast and has spent decades watching words migrate, die, and return. Their fiction explores what survives translation—and what deliberately doesn't.
