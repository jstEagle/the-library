# The Inventory of Anderlecht

**by Ada Sandoval**

> Sable Halloran, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Ada Sandoval is the pen name of a former hospital archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the belongings of the deceased before turning to fiction. She writes at night, in cities that refuse to sleep, and claims every novel begins as an unrecorded song.
