# The Inventory of the coast road

**by Emrys Zhou**

> Yara Amberdale, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent ten years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Emrys Zhou is the pen name of a former archivist who spent seven years cataloguing endangered-language recordings along the northern coast before turning to fiction. Their work explores how families survive their own histories, and what gets lost in translation between silence and speech.
