## About the author

Gideon Villalobos is a former building inspector who spent fifteen years cataloguing condemned structures in post-industrial river towns, work that taught him how houses remember what their occupants try to forget. He writes literary fiction about inheritance, labor, and the acoustics of memory from a restored row house he refuses to fully renovate.
