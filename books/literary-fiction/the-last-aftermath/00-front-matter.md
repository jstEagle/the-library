# The Last Aftermath

**by Casper Sterling**

> August Coldharbour, a former prodigy returning to the conservatory as a janitor, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Casper Sterling is the pen name of a former conservatory custodian who spent eleven years listening to practice rooms at night before writing a word. The Last Aftermath is a debut novel about the languages we lose and the ones we refuse to speak.
