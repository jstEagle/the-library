# The Last Blue Hour

**by Ulyana Fairweather**

> Odile Whitlock, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Ulyana Fairweather is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent a decade translating testimony in immigration tribunals before turning to fiction. She lives near the sea in a house whose radiators, she admits, tell her nothing at all.
