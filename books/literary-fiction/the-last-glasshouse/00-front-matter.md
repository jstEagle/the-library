# The Last Glasshouse

**by Tamsin Eriksen**

> Silas Fairweather, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a childhood home goes up for auction, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Tamsin Eriksen is a former piano restorer who spent a decade working in historic conservatories across northern England before turning to fiction. Her writing explores the quiet violence of family inheritance and the objects that outlive us.
