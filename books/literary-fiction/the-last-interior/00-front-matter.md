# The Last Interior

**by Xanthe Eastwick**

> Petra Eastwick, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. Then a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Xanthe Eastwick is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up trilingual in a port town and now writes about the words families lose on purpose. She lives near a river that changes its name every century, and this is her second novel.
