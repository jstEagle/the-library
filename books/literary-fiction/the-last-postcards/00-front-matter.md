# The Last Postcards

**by Sable Reyes**

> Beatrix Dravenmoor, a restaurant critic who has forgotten how food tastes, has spent fifteen years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Sable Reyes is the pen name of a former food writer who left restaurant journalism after a decade of reviews she no longer believes. She lives near a decommissioned lighthouse and writes about families, memory, and the things we archive instead of say.
