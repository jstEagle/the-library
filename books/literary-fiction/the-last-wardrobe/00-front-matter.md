**by P. M. Winterbourne**

> Lior Lockridge, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then a childhood home goes up for auction — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

P. M. Winterbourne is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent a decade transcribing other people's most important words before turning to fiction. They write from a farmhouse full of inherited furniture, much of it untranslatable in the emotional sense.
