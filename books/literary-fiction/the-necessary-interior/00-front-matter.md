# The Necessary Interior

**by Vesper Winterbourne**

> Silas Quintana, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Vesper Winterbourne is the pen name of a former archivist who spent eleven years cataloguing oral histories in municipal libraries before turning to fiction. They write at night, claiming the hours belong to the people and stories that daytime forgets.
