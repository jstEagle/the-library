# The Necessary Language

**by Silas Voss**

> Noor Ravensworth, a former prodigy returning to the conservatory as a janitor, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Silas Voss is the pen name of a former conservatory custodian who spent eleven years sweeping concert halls before turning to fiction. Their work explores the quiet lives of people the institutions they serve have forgotten.
