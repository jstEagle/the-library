## About the author

U. M. Villalobos is the pen name of a former court stenographer from the Oregon coast who spent two decades transcribing other people's confessions before writing any of her own. 'The Necessary Wardrobe' is her second novel, following a well-received debut about a lighthouse keeper's widow.
