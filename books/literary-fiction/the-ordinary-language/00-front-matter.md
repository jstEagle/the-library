**by Jonah Thackeray**

## About the author

Jonah Thackeray is the pen name of a former estate appraiser who spent fifteen years cataloging other people's unfinished lives before turning to fiction. He writes from a rented farmhouse where he keeps, deliberately unlabeled, one box of his own family's papers.
