# The Ordinary Weather

**by Petra Joubert**

> Zephyrine Dunmore, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Petra Joubert is a former piano restorer from Cape Town who now writes full-time in a house with notoriously loud pipes. The Ordinary Weather is her second novel, following the acclaimed The Long Way Down the Stairs.
