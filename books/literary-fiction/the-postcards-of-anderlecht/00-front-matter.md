# The Postcards of Anderlecht

**by Yara Sandoval**

> Ada Underhill, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Yara Sandoval is the pen name of a former translator who spent a decade in Brussels before returning to coastal Portugal to write. Her work explores language as inheritance and the quiet violence of belonging to no one.
