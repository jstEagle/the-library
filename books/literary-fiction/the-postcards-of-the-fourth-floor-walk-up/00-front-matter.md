## About the author

Felix Oyelaran is the pen name of a former postal clerk who spent a decade sorting undeliverable mail in a mid-sized city, where he began collecting the stories of letters that arrived too late. He writes quietly devastating fiction about the small architectures of grief and lives near a river he refuses to name.
