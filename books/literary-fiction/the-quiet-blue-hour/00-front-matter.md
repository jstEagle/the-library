## About the author

Sebastian Nystrom is the pen name of a former night-shift archivist who wrote this novel over four winters in a coastal city where the streetlights never fully go out. He lives quietly, keeps his family's photograph albums in chronological disorder, and insists the best hours for reading are the ones no one else is using.
