# The Quiet Glasshouse

**by Lior Eastwick**

> Coral Ishikawa, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has spent twenty years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the mother's memory goes entirely.
