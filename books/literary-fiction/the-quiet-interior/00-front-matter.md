# The Quiet Interior

**by N. M. Okonkwo**

> Lena Sandoval, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, has spent seven years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud, and time is already running out.

## About the author

N. M. Okonkwo is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing private correspondence collections in Lagos and Lisbon before turning to fiction. They write quietly devastating stories about the things families refuse to say and the strangers who end up saying them.
