# The Quiet Language

**by Elodie Nakamura**

> Dominic Voss, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has spent fifteen years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a childhood home goes up for auction, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Elodie Nakamura is a former court interpreter who grew up between three languages and two continents, and whose fiction examines what families say by refusing to speak. She lives in Rotterdam, where she teaches translation workshops for writers working across inherited tongues.
