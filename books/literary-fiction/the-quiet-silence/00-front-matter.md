**by Oscar Delacroix**

But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Oscar Delacroix is the pen name of a former piano restorer from upstate New York who spent fifteen years tuning instruments in houses with more past than present. The Quiet Silence is his second novel, written in the margins of repair invoices.
