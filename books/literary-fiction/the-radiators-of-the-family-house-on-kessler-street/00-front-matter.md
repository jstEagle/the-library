## About the author

Noor Villalobos is a former building conservator who spent a decade documenting houses slated for demolition, interviewing the families who left them behind. Her fiction is known for finding enormous feeling in small domestic machinery — hinges, boilers, and the quiet rooms where people almost said what they meant.
