# The Slow Glasshouse

**by J. M. Zhou**

## About the author

J. M. Zhou is the pen name of a former civil engineer who spent eleven years inspecting bridges across the American Midwest before turning to fiction. Their stories about infrastructure and intimacy have appeared in several small literary journals.
