# The Slow Inventory

**by Rafael Galloway**

> Edmund Ashgrove, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a childhood home goes up for auction, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must return to the place where the story went wrong before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Rafael Galloway is the pen name of a former estate appraiser who spent a decade cataloguing the belongings of the dead and the departed. The Slow Inventory is his second novel, drawn from the letters he was never asked to send.
