**by Y. M. Nystrom**

## About the author

Y. M. Nystrom is a former building conservator who spent a decade documenting pre-war houses slated for demolition, work that taught her how buildings keep accounts of their own. She writes in a converted schoolhouse in northern Norway and has published two previous novels about inheritance, silence, and the ethics of memory.
