# The Slow Radiators

**by Edmund Ishikawa**

> Jonah Dunmore, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Edmund Ishikawa is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up between two languages in a coastal working-class family. He writes about translation not as craft but as inheritance, and lives within earshot of a bus depot.
