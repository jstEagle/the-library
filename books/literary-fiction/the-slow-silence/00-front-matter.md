# The Slow Silence

**by Juniper Amberdale**

> Ansel Yarrow, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, has spent seven years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Juniper Amberdale is the pen name of a former night-shift archivist who spent a decade cataloguing endangered-language recordings in a coastal city library. The Slow Silence is their second novel, written mostly between midnight and dawn.
