# The Small Aftermath

**by Gideon Brightwater**

Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Gideon Brightwater is the pen name of a former piano restorer who spent fifteen years tuning instruments in inherited houses across the Pacific Northwest before turning to fiction. Their work explores how objects hold memory, and they still cannot pass an old radiator without listening to it.
