# The Small Radiators

**by Cyrus Kavanagh**

> Quentin Ishikawa, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then a childhood home goes up for auction — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Cyrus Kavanagh is the pen name of a former building superintendent who spent twenty years listening to the pipes of old apartment blocks before turning to fiction. He writes about inheritance, architecture, and the quiet violence of families from a rented flat he refuses to buy.
