# The Small Weather

**by Hazel Halloran**

> August Brightwater, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, has spent three years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must forgive the stranger in the old photographs, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Hazel Halloran is a former court transcriptionist who grew up in a coastal mill town and now writes about inheritance, silence, and the small weathers of ordinary injustice. She lives with her partner and an archive of other people's letters.
