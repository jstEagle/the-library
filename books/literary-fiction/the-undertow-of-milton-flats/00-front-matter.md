## About the author

Sebastian Dravenmoor spent a decade as a building superintendent in fading mill towns before turning to fiction, and his prose still carries the smell of boiler rooms and old varnish. He writes about the quiet inheritances—debts, names, and loyalties—that families pass down without ever saying them aloud.
