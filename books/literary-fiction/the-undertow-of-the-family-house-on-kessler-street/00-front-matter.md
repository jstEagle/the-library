# The Undertow of the family house on Kessler Street

**by Z. M. Okafor**

> Dominic Crane, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent twenty years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Z. M. Okafor is a former estate appraiser who spent years cataloguing the belongings of the recently departed before turning to fiction. They write quietly devastating stories about the things people leave unsaid, and live in a rented flat full of other people's abandoned postcards.
