# The Unsaid Undertow

**by Odile Eriksen**

> Casper Blackwood, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must return to the place where the story went wrong before the demolition date arrives.
