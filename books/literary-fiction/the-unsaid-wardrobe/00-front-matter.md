## About the author

Coral Quintana is a former structural engineer who spent eleven years inspecting aging infrastructure across the Rust Belt before turning to fiction full-time. Her work examines how families, like bridges, fail slowly and then suddenly—and she writes from a converted tollbooth on the Delaware River.
