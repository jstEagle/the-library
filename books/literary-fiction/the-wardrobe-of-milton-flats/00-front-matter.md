## About the author

Odile Ashgrove is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unclaimed letters in a northern postal dead-letter office. She writes about families who communicate best through objects, and lives above a shuttered tailor's shop with a wardrobe she refuses to open.
