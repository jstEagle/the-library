# The Wardrobe of the family house on Kessler Street

**by Felix Yarrow**

> Tamsin Villalobos, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has spent five years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must return to the place where the story went wrong before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Felix Yarrow is the pen name of a former piano restorer who spent a decade listening to houses before writing about them. Their work explores how families keep secrets the way buildings keep heat—in the walls, in the pipes, in the things left behind.
