# The Weather of Anderlecht

**by Marguerite Yarrow**

> Gideon Villalobos, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has spent ten years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then the last speaker of a language asks for one final student — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Marguerite Yarrow is the pen name of a former radio archivist who spent a decade recording disappearing accents across Belgium. She writes from a converted tram depot, where she keeps her late grandmother's recipe box annotated in three half-forgotten languages.
