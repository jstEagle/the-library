# The Weather of the coast road

**by Mara Eriksen**

> Lior Lockridge, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. Then a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Mara Eriksen grew up between two fishing towns whose names changed on every new map, and worked for years as an interpreter before turning to fiction. She writes about borders that live inside families rather than on maps.
