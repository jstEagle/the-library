# Ulyana and the Interior

**by Lior Thornbury**

> Ivo Villalobos, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Lior Thornbury is the pen name of a former interpreter who worked in immigration courts for eleven years before leaving to write. They live near a border town where three languages are spoken in the same kitchen, and none of them is their first.
