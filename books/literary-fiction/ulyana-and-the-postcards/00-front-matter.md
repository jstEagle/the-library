# Ulyana and the Postcards

**by Xanthe Ravensworth**

> Freya Brightwater, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Xanthe Ravensworth is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent eleven years listening to other people's truths before beginning to write her own. Her short fiction has appeared in several small literary journals, and 'Ulyana and the Postcards' is her first novel.
