# Undertow and Blue Hour

**by Elodie Almeida**

> Beatrix Vasquez, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has spent three years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a childhood home goes up for auction — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Elodie Almeida is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up between two languages on a working harbor and has never quite left it. Her fiction examines what families keep in translation, and what they lose when they finally say it plainly.
