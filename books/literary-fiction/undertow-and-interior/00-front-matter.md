# Undertow and Interior

**by Astrid Eriksen**

> Emrys Ashgrove, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Astrid Eriksen grew up between a fishing town in western Norway and her grandmother's farm in Carmarthenshire, a childhood spent translating herself for both households. She worked as a literary translator for fifteen years before writing fiction of her own; Undertow and Interior is her second novel.
