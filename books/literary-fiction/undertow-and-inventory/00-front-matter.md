# Undertow and Inventory

**by Felix Ishikawa**

> Xanthe Okonkwo, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Felix Ishikawa is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent a decade translating testimony for people who could not speak for themselves. They live near a coastline that keeps erasing its own letters, and this is their first novel.
