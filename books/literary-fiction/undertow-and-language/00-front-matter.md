## About the author

O. M. Thackeray spent fifteen years as a building conservator in coastal towns before turning to fiction, and still claims old houses talk if you press your ear to the right pipe. Undertow and Language is their third novel, following two quietly acclaimed books about inheritance, work, and the things families refuse to say.
