# Undertow and Postcards

**by Rosalind Eastwick**

> Paloma Eriksen, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. Then a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must return to the place where the story went wrong before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Rosalind Eastwick is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent a decade translating testimony in coastal fishing communities. She writes about the words people lose on purpose, and lives within earshot of a working harbor.
