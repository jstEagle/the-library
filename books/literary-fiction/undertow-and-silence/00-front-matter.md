# Undertow and Silence

**by Jonah Ishikawa**

> Vesper Nakamura, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Jonah Ishikawa is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unclaimed letters in coastal municipal records. He writes quietly devastating fiction about inheritance, migration, and the things families agree never to say.
