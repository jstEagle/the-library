# Undertow and Weather

**by Felix Brightwater**

But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Felix Brightwater is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing other people's unopened letters before turning to fiction. They write at night, in libraries, about families who mistake silence for peace.
