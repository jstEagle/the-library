# Vesper and the Undertow

**by Yara Sterling**

> Oscar Thackeray, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent twenty years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Yara Sterling is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing undelivered mail for a coastal postal service. She writes literary fiction about the objects people leave behind and the words they couldn't say in time.
