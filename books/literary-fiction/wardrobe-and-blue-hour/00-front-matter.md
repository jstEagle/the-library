# Wardrobe and Blue Hour

**by Ines Halloran**

> Kestrel Joubert, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, has spent ten years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ines Halloran is the pen name of a former archivist who spent seven years cataloguing other people's unopened correspondence. She writes about quiet people and the loud consequences of their restraint.
