# Wardrobe and Glasshouse

**by Lior Whitlock**

> Orsen Amberdale, a restaurant critic who has forgotten how food tastes, has spent fifteen years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now she must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Lior Whitlock is the pen name of a former food writer who spent a decade covering restaurant culture before turning to fiction. Wardrobe and Glasshouse is their second novel, following a widely praised debut about a family of sign-language interpreters.
