# Wardrobe and Language

**by Bram Winterbourne**

> Dominic Voss, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must return to the place where the story went wrong before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Bram Winterbourne is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent a decade listening to testimony in three languages. They write about the people justice forgets and the words that outlive them.
