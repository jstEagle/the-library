# Wardrobe and Postcards

**by Kestrel Pemberton**

> Theo Marchetti, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must return to the place where the story went wrong, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Kestrel Pemberton is the pen name of a former postal archivist who spent a decade cataloguing undeliverable mail in a northern coastal town. Wardrobe and Postcards is their second novel, following the widely praised debut The Weight of Returned Letters.
