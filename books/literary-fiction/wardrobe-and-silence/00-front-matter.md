# Wardrobe and Silence

**by Rosalind Quintana**

> Theo Nystrom, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. Then a childhood home goes up for auction — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Rosalind Quintana is a former architectural conservator who spent a decade documenting buildings slated for demolition, interviewing the families who once lived in them. She writes in a converted schoolhouse in upstate New York, where she keeps a piano she refuses to have tuned.
