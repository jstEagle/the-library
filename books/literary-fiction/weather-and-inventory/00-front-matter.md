# Weather and Inventory

**by Ada Voss**

> Zephyrine Vane, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. Then a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Ada Voss is the pen name of a former night-shift archivist who spent eleven years cataloguing unclaimed property in a coastal city. She writes about the small bureaucratic tendernesses that hold families together and apart.
