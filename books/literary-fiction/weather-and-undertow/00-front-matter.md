# Weather and Undertow

**by Lena Nystrom**

> Bram Hartwell, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, has spent twenty years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Lena Nystrom grew up in a fishing town on the Baltic coast, where she spent her childhood listening to adults talk around the things they meant. She writes quiet, atmospheric fiction about families and the weather they live inside; Weather and Undertow is her third novel.
