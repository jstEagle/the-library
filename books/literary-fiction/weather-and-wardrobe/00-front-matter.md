# Weather and Wardrobe

**by Quentin Okonkwo**

> Zephyrine Oyelaran, a former prodigy returning to the conservatory as a janitor, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must return to the place where the story went wrong before the demolition date arrives.
