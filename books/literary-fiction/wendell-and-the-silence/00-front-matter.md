# Wendell and the Silence

**by Ada Kavanagh**

> Rosalind Joubert, a restaurant critic who has forgotten how food tastes, has spent twenty years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a childhood home goes up for auction — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Ada Kavanagh is the pen name of a former food writer who spent a decade covering restaurant culture before turning to fiction. She lives in coastal Maine, where she is at work on a second novel about families and the things they agree not to say.
