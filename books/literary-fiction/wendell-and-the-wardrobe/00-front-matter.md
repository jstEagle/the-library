## About the author

Ivo Oyelaran is a former structural engineer who left the profession after a decade of inspecting bridges in three countries, and now writes fiction about the weight-bearing points of human lives. He lives between Lagos and Liverpool, where he still cannot walk across a bridge without touching the railing.
