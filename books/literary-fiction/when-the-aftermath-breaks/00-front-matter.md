# When the Aftermath Breaks

**by Orsen Sandoval**

> Gideon Halloran, a night librarian in a city that sleeps less every year, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Orsen Sandoval is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing other people's unclaimed correspondence before turning to fiction. He writes quietly devastating novels about ordinary people and the documents that outlive them.
