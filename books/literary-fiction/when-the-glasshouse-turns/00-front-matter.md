# When the Glasshouse Turns

**by Yara Brightwater**

> Quentin Vasquez, a former prodigy returning to the conservatory as a janitor, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Yara Brightwater is the pen name of a former conservatory archivist who spent years cataloguing recordings of endangered languages. She writes literary fiction about the quiet inheritances we refuse and the ones we cannot put down.
