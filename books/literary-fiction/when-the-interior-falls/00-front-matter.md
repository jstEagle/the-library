# When the Interior Falls

**by Wendell Fairweather**

> Silas Delacroix, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
