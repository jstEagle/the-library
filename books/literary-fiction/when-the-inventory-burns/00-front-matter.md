# When the Inventory Burns

**by Cyrus Hartwell**

> Noor Halloran, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, has spent five years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when the last speaker of a language asks for one final student, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Cyrus Hartwell is the pen name of a former piano technician who spent a decade tuning instruments in inherited houses along the Great Lakes before turning to fiction. Their work explores quiet people, endangered languages, and the justice families administer when the law looks away.
