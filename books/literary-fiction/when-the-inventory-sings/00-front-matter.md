# When the Inventory Sings

**by Edmund Winterbourne**

> Orsen Nystrom, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a childhood home goes up for auction — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the house sale closes.

## About the author

Edmund Winterbourne is the pen name of a former estate auctioneer who spent two decades cataloguing the belongings of the departed. He writes quiet novels about the objects people leave behind and the words they never said.
