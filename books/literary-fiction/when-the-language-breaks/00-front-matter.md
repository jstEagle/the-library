# When the Language Breaks

**by Ada Dunmore**

> Oscar Nystrom, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, keeps a careful inventory of everything unsaid. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must ask the question the whole family arranged itself around before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Ada Dunmore is the pen name of a former legal transcriptionist who spent twenty years listening to testimony in small-town courthouses across the upper Midwest. She writes about the people whose truths never made it into the record.
