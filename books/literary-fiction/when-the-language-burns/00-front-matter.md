# When the Language Burns

**by Mirabel Ishikawa**

> Ivo Whitlock, a piano tuner who hears the house's history in its radiators, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Mirabel Ishikawa is the pen name of a former piano restorer who spent a decade tuning instruments in condemned buildings across the industrial North. She writes about sound, inheritance, and the small justices people make when institutions fail them.
