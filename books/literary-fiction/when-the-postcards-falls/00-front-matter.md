# When the Postcards Falls

**by Astrid Brightwater**

> Noor Villalobos, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, has spent twelve years practicing the art of almost saying it. Then a song on the radio is one that was written and never recorded — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must forgive the stranger in the old photographs before the demolition date arrives.

## About the author

Astrid Brightwater is the pen name of a former postal clerk who spent a decade sorting undeliverable mail in a coastal town. Their fiction explores the letters we send to places that no longer exist.
