## About the author

Astrid Amberdale is the pen name of a former conservatory librarian who spent a decade cataloguing other people's unfinished manuscripts before writing her own. She lives in a coastal town in Maine, where she teaches a single writing workshop a year and keeps a piano she admits she never learned to play properly.
