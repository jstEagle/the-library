# When the Radiators Falls

**by Petra Joubert**

> Cyrus Pemberton, a restaurant critic who has forgotten how food tastes, has spent seven years practicing the art of almost saying it. But when a box of undelivered letters surfaces behind a wardrobe, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must translate the untranslatable sentence and live with it before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Petra Joubert is the pen name of a former line cook who spent eleven years in restaurant kitchens before turning to fiction. She writes about appetite, inheritance, and the things families swallow instead of saying.
