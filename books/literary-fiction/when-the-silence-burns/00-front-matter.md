# When the Silence Burns

**by Ines Marchetti**

> August Almeida, a translator losing a childhood language one borrowed word at a time, has perfected the smile that ends conversations. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Ines Marchetti is the pen name of a former court interpreter who grew up between two languages and one long family silence. She writes about diaspora, memory, and the small betrayals of translation from a coastal town where she keeps her grandmother's dictionary within arm's reach.
