# When the Silence Falls

**by Zephyrine Winterbourne**

> Odile Thornbury, an heir cataloging a parent's silence after the funeral, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. But when a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must return to the place where the story went wrong before the mother's memory goes entirely.

## About the author

Zephyrine Winterbourne is the pen name of a former archivist who spent fifteen years cataloging unclaimed estates and the letters people never sent. Their fiction explores how families become museums of things unsaid.
