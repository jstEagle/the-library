## About the author

Emrys Fairweather is the pen name of a former civil engineer who spent fifteen years inspecting bridges along the Atlantic coast before turning to fiction full-time. Their work explores the quiet architectures of family life, and they live in a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage with two rescue dogs.
