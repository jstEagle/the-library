## About the author

Theo Sandoval is the pen name of a former court interpreter who spent a decade translating testimony and grew obsessed with everything language refuses to carry across. They write literary fiction about grief, inheritance, and the small treacheries of survival, and live near a coastline that keeps erasing its own maps.
