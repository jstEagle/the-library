# Yara and the Language

**by Yara Okafor**

> Ansel Amberdale, a letter-writer sending postcards to an address that burned down years ago, moves through the days like a tenant, not an owner. Then a stranger at the funeral knows the family by a name nobody uses — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must say goodbye properly, at last, out loud before the last bus to the old neighborhood is rerouted forever.

## About the author

Yara Okafor is a former court interpreter who grew up between Lagos and Manchester, and whose fiction examines how families keep secrets in the gaps between languages. She writes at kitchen tables in other people's houses, collecting the stories strangers tell when they think no one is keeping record.
