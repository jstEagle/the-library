## About the author

Sebastian Okonkwo is a former archivist who spent six years cataloging the unclaimed letters of a decommissioned postal depot, an experience that taught him how much of a life can fit inside an envelope. He writes from a rented room above a shuttered laundrette, where he is at work on a second novel about families that mistake silence for peace.
