# A Alibi for the Cipher

**by K. M. Delacroix**

> Paloma Quintana, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has spent ten years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must decode a ledger hidden in plain sight before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.
