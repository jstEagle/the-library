# A Alibi for the Confession

**by Mara Crane**

> Felix Sandoval, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has spent fifteen years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. ## About the author

Mara Crane is the pen name of a former small-town paralegal who spent a decade watching secrets move through probate files and swore some of them deserved daylight. She writes mysteries about ordinary people whose tidy lives come undone by paper trails.
