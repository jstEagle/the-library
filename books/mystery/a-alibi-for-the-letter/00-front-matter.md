# A Alibi for the Letter

**by Coral Zhou**

> Quentin Brightwater, a retired detective running a failing bookshop, has spent three years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when twin letters arrive: one confessing, one promising more, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the witness everyone swears is dead before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.
