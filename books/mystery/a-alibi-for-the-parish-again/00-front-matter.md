# A Alibi for the Parish Again

**by Ivo Delacroix**

> Kestrel Crane, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has spent twelve years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
