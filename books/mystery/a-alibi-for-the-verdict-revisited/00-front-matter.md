## About the author

Petra Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former court stenographer who spent fifteen years transcribing testimony before turning to fiction. She writes cold-case mysteries from a farmhouse in rural Vermont, where she keeps a wall of unsolved files 'for inspiration.'
