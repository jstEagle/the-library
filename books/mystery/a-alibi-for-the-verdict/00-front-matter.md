# A Alibi for the Verdict

**by Paloma Whitlock**

> August Pemberton, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, has spent seven years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when the town's beloved founder is exhumed and the coffin is empty, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Paloma Whitlock spent a decade answering late-night phones for a regional transit authority, where she collected the strange confessions strangers make when they think no one is listening. She writes mysteries about small towns and the people paid to know everything and say nothing.
