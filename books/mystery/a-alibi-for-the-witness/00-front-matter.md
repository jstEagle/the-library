# A Alibi for the Witness

**by Mara Crane**

> Ansel Sandoval, an insurance investigator with a photographic memory, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when twin letters arrive: one confessing, one promising more, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.

## About the author
