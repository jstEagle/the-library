## About the author

Sable Yarrow is the pen name of a former parish records clerk who spent a decade cataloguing centuries of births, deaths, and disappearances in English cathedral towns. Their fiction is drawn to quiet people who keep other people's secrets—and to the moment those secrets refuse to stay buried.
