## About the author

Rafael Okonkwo spent a decade as a records clerk in county courthouses before turning to fiction, and his mysteries are known for their obsession with paper trails and the dead who leave none. He lives in a converted funeral parlor on the coast of Maine, where he claims the filing cabinets still whisper.
