## About the author

Lena Sandoval is a former parish records clerk from the Cornish coast who spent a decade cataloguing centuries of baptismal registers before turning to fiction. Her mysteries are known for their quiet ecclesiastical settings and protagonists who believe justice is a craft practiced in archives, not courtrooms.
