## About the author

O. M. Amberdale is the pen name of a former small-town court reporter who spent fifteen years watching justice bend in real time before turning to fiction. They live above a secondhand bookshop and insist, unprompted, that every plot twist in their novels has happened at least once in an actual courtroom.
