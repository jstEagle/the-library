## About the author

Juniper Underhill spent fifteen years as a small-town court reporter before turning to fiction, giving her mysteries an insider's ear for the quiet corruptions of ordinary institutions. She writes from a converted chapel in the Cotswolds, where she keeps a shelf of unsolved cases that inspire her plots.
