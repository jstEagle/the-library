## About the author

Petra Winterbourne is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing small-town records before turning to fiction, a background that infuses her mysteries with the texture of paper trails and buried ledgers. She writes from a converted ferry house on the northern coast, where she collects obituaries and insists every town she invents deserves its own history no one wants read aloud.
