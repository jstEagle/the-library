# A Cipher for the Witness Anew

**by Petra Okafor**

> Kestrel Whitlock, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when the town's beloved founder is exhumed and the coffin is empty, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Petra Okafor spent a decade as a probate paralegal in small-town New England before turning her case files into fiction. She writes mysteries about the paperwork people leave behind and the lives it quietly governs.
