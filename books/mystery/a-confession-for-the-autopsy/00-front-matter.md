## About the author

Sebastian Kavanagh spent seven years answering phones at a small-town cab depot before turning to fiction, a job he credits for teaching him that every call carries a second story underneath. He writes mysteries from a rented room above a shuttered garage, where he still keeps an old radio scanner running out of habit.
