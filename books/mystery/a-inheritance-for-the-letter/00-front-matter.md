## About the author

Theo Dravenmoor spent a decade working night desks in grand European hotels before turning to crime fiction, collecting the small betrayals and quiet kindnesses of transient lives. He writes from a coastal apartment he refuses to photograph, believing every good mystery begins with someone watching the lobby.
