# A Ledger for the Confession

**by Wendell Nystrom**

> Bram Blackwood, a small-town coroner who knows every grave by name, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must untangle alibis woven thirty years ago before the statute expires.

## About the author

Wendell Nystrom is a former small-town records clerk who spent fifteen years cataloguing deaths, deeds, and disappearances in rural county archives. His novels are known for their patient, forensic intimacy and their affection for communities that keep their secrets in ledgers rather than words.
