## About the author

Freya Okonkwo spent a decade cataloguing parish records in northern England before turning to crime fiction, a career that taught her that every archive hides a body. She writes from a converted vestry office, where she keeps an unopened ledger on her desk as a reminder that some accounts are never settled.
