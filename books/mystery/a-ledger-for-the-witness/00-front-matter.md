## About the author

Astrid Galloway spent a decade transcribing coroner's inquests in coastal market towns before turning to fiction, and her prose carries the cadence of sworn testimony. She writes from a converted tithe barn, where she keeps an unopened ledger she claims was left on her doorstep with no return address.
