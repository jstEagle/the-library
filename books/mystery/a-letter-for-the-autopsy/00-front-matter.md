# A Letter for the Autopsy

**by Mara Crane**

> Astrid Hartwell, a retired detective running a failing bookshop, prides themselves on never taking a case they can't close. ## About the author

Mara Crane is a former small-town newspaper reporter who spent ten years covering cold cases along the northern coast before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries about memory, tide, and the families we build when the ones we're given fail us.
