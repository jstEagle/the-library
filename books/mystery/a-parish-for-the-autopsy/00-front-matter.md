# A Parish for the Autopsy

**by Ivo Marchetti**

> Paloma Kavanagh, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. Then a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must untangle alibis woven thirty years ago before the trial begins.

## About the author

Ivo Marchetti is the pen name of a former parish records clerk from Trieste who spent a decade cataloguing deaths in church registries before turning to fiction. They write mysteries about institutions, archives, and the quiet violence of respectability.
