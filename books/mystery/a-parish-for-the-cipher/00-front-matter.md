# A Parish for the Cipher

**by E. M. Ishikawa**

> Freya Kavanagh, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must find the witness everyone swears is dead, and time is already running out.

## About the author

E. M. Ishikawa is a former emergency-services dispatcher turned novelist who writes mysteries steeped in small-town memory and moral fog. Known for meticulous plotting and quiet, haunted prose, Ishikawa lives in a coastal town that inspires the fictional parishes of the Freya Kavanagh series.
