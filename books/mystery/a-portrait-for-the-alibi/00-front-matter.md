# A Portrait for the Alibi

**by Xanthe Ishikawa**

> Oscar Voss, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. ## About the author

Xanthe Ishikawa is the pen name of a former night-shift radio operator who spent eleven years listening to a city talk itself to sleep before turning those voices into fiction. They write mysteries about people who watch carefully and are watched too late.
