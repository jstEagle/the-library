# A Portrait for the Inheritance

**by Ulyana Brightwater**

> Ines Hartwell, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, has spent twenty years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Ulyana Brightwater is the pen name of a former small-town court stenographer who spent eleven years listening to other people's confessions before writing her own stories. She lives in a converted firehouse with two cats named after cold cases.
