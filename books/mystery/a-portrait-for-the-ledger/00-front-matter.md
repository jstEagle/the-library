## About the author

Sable Quintana spent a decade as a court stenographer before turning to fiction, and her prose carries the cadence of testimony. She lives in a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage on the Maine coast, where she writes mysteries about people who can't stop digging into other people's pasts.
