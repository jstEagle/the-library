## About the author

Rosalind Iversen spent fifteen years as a court stenographer in northern England before turning to fiction, a career that taught her how much people reveal when they think no one is writing it down. She writes mysteries about quiet women who notice everything, and lives near the coast with an untidy archive of her own making.
