**by Noor Coldharbour**

## About the author

Noor Coldharbour is the pen name of a former small-town archivist who spent fifteen years cataloguing wills, deeds, and unsolved inquests before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries from a converted chapel bookshop on the coast, where the fog does half her plotting for her.
