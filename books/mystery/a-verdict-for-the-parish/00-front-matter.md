# A Verdict for the Parish

**by Wendell Coldharbour**

> Ulyana Eriksen, a retired detective running a failing bookshop, has spent three years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Wendell Coldharbour is the pen name of a former small-town court reporter who spent a decade listening to verdicts from the back benches before turning to fiction. He writes mysteries about places that remember too much and people who remember selectively.
