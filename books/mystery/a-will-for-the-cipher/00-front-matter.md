# A Will for the Cipher

**by Ivo Whitlock**

> Isandro Marchetti, an insurance investigator with a photographic memory, has spent three years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the trial begins.

## About the author

Ivo Whitlock is the pen name of a former claims adjuster who spent eleven years reading other people's disasters in triplicate before turning to fiction. They live in a small coastal town much like the one in this book, where they are politely suspected of knowing too much.
