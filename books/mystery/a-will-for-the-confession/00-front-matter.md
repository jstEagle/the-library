# A Will for the Confession

**by Lena Quintana**

## About the author

Lena Quintana spent a decade as a claims adjuster in small-town New England before turning to fiction, where she developed a reputation for mysteries built on paperwork, probate, and quiet menace. She writes from a converted records office and admits she still alphabetizes her research notes.
