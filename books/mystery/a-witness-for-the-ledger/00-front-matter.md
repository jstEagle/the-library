## About the author

Coral Whitlock spent a decade working night desks at coastal hotels before turning to crime fiction, collecting the overheard confessions of travelers who mistook anonymity for safety. She writes from a rented room above a lighthouse keeper's cottage, where the tide dictates her working hours.
