# A Witness for the Letter

**by Coral Quintana**

> Marguerite Lockridge, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has spent fifteen years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then twin letters arrive: one confessing, one promising more — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Coral Quintana is a former parish records clerk who spent a decade cataloguing diocesan archives in coastal New England before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries obsessed with the quiet violence of paperwork and the families we build in old buildings.
