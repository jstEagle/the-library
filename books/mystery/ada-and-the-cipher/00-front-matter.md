# Ada and the Cipher

**by O. M. Almeida**

## About the author

O. M. Almeida is the pen name of a former insurance fraud analyst who spent a decade reading other people's disasters for a living. Almeida writes mysteries about people whose careful, ordered lives are cracked open by the past they thought they'd filed away.
