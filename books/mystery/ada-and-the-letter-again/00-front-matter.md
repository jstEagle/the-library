## About the author

Cyrus Thackeray is the pen name of a former hotel night manager who spent two decades collecting the confessions strangers make to concierges at 3 a.m. He writes mysteries from a rented room above a shuttered seaside inn, where he claims the tide still delivers letters no one sent.
