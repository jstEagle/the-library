# Ada and the Will

**by Emrys Thornbury**

> Cyrus Nakamura, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has spent seven years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when the missing person's diary continues past the date she disappeared, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the tide gives up what it took.
