## About the author

Gideon Kavanagh is the pen name of a former rural paramedic who spent a decade answering calls in towns too small for their own morgue. They write mysteries about the quiet rot beneath picturesque places, drawing on the strange intimacy of knowing everyone's secrets before they're confessed.
