# Alibi and Cipher

**by Astrid Underhill**

> Wren Ashgrove, a small-town coroner who knows every grave by name, has spent seven years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must untangle alibis woven thirty years ago before the tide gives up what it took.

## About the author

Astrid Underhill is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing coroners' inquest records from small coastal towns before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries about the paperwork of death and the people who keep it.
