# Alibi and Confession

**by Mirabel Okonkwo**

> Lena Almeida, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, prides themselves on never taking a case they can't close. But when a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must untangle alibis woven thirty years ago, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Mirabel Okonkwo is a former probate clerk in Lagos and Lisbon who spent a decade reading other people's final wishes before writing her own stories. She writes mysteries about paperwork, secrets, and the families we assemble from strangers.
