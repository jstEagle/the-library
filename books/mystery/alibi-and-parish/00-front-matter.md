## About the author

Wendell Nakamura is a former small-town archivist who spent a decade cataloguing municipal records before turning to fiction, giving his mysteries an unnerving authenticity about paper trails and buried secrets. He lives on the Pacific coast, where he records ambient soundscapes of coastal towns that often find their way into his atmospheric prose.
