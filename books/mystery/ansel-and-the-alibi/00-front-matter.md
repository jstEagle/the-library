# Ansel and the Alibi

**by Rafael Ashgrove**

> Ulyana Nystrom, a true-crime podcaster chasing the coldest case on record, has spent seven years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Rafael Ashgrove is the pen name of a former court stenographer who spent two decades transcribing small-town trials before turning to fiction. Ashgrove writes mysteries obsessed with what official records leave out, and lives near Lake Superior with an aging bloodhound named Verdict.
