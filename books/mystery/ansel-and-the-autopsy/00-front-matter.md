# Ansel and the Autopsy

> Elodie Halloran, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. ## About the author

Zephyrine Nystrom is the pen name of a former coroner's office transcriptionist who spent a decade listening to other people's endings before writing her own. She lives in a coastal town much like the one in this book, where she keeps a meticulous file of unsolved local legends she promises are fiction.
