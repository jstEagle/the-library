# Ansel and the Parish

**by Cyrus Thackeray**

> Paloma Amberdale, a retired detective running a failing bookshop, has spent fifteen years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must untangle alibis woven thirty years ago before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.

## About the author

Cyrus Thackeray spent a decade as a parish records clerk in coastal Norfolk before turning to crime fiction, a career he describes as 'reading other people's endings for a living.' He writes in a converted vestry and claims he can date any lie by its ink.
