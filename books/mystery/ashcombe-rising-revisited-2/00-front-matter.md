## About the author

V. M. Galloway is the pen name of a former municipal records clerk who spent nine years cataloguing small-town archives across coastal England before turning to fiction. Galloway writes mysteries about people who know too much and say too little, drawing on a fascination with how paper remembers what people prefer to forget.
