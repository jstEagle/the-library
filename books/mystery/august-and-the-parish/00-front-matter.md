## About the author

Tamsin Blackwood is the pen name of a former probate clerk who spent eleven years in English coastal registry offices, where she learned that every town keeps two histories: the one it tells and the one it files away. She writes mysteries about paper trails and the people they bury, from a house within earshot of the sea.
