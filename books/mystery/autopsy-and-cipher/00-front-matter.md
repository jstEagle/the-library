## About the author

Noor Blackwood is the pen name of a former forensic archivist who spent fifteen years cataloguing unsolved case files before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries about the gap between what the law knows and what actually happened, from a coastal town whose archives she still visits under false pretenses.
