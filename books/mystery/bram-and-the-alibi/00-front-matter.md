# Bram and the Alibi

**by U. M. Iversen**

> Vesper Ishikawa, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the tide gives up what it took.

## About the author

U. M. Iversen is the pen name of a former parish records clerk who spent a decade cataloguing centuries of quiet scandals in English cathedral archives. They write mysteries about people who know too much and say too little.
