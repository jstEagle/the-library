## About the author

Rafael Iversen spent nine years answering phones for a livery service in a mid-sized harbor city before turning to fiction full-time. His novels are known for their nocturnal rhythms, working-class tenderness, and mysteries that hinge on what ordinary people choose to keep quiet.
