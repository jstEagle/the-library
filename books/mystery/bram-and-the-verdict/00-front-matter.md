**by Quentin Sandoval**

## About the author

Quentin Sandoval is the pen name of a former parish records clerk who spent a decade cataloguing centuries of births, deaths, and quiet scandals in English cathedral archives. That experience left an enduring fascination with the secrets towns agree not to see.
