# Cipher and Alibi

**by Tamsin Quintana**

> Isandro Blackwood, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has spent twenty years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when the missing person's diary continues past the date she disappeared, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Tamsin Quintana spent a decade as a probate clerk in a coastal town before turning to fiction, a career that taught her that every family's fortune has a filing error somewhere. She writes mysteries about paper trails, small towns, and the people who keep both in order.
