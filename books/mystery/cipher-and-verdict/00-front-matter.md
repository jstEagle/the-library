## About the author

Coral Dunmore is the pen name of a former rural hospice nurse who spent a decade listening to the last words of small-town families before turning those confessions into fiction. She lives in a converted funeral parlor on the coast of Maine, where she writes at night and keeps bees she refuses to name.
