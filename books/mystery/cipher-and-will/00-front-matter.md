# Cipher and Will

**by Wren Kavanagh**

> Dominic Joubert, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has spent twelve years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.
