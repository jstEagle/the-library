## About the author

Sebastian Dravenmoor spent fourteen years as a night auditor in grand European hotels before turning to crime fiction, collecting the confessions of insomniacs and the secrets of service corridors. He writes from a rented room above a shuttered railway station, where he claims the trains still stop for him alone.
