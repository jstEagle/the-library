# Confession and Ledger

**by Tamsin Brightwater**

> Sebastian Pemberton, a hotel concierge who notices everything, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
