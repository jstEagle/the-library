## About the author

Bram Nystrom is the pen name of a former parish archivist from the Norfolk coast who spent fifteen years cataloguing wills, deeds, and disputes before turning to crime fiction. Their novels are known for quiet villages with loud secrets and detectives who carry their past cases like stones in their pockets.
