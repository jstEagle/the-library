## About the author

Astrid Amberdale spent a decade as a probate clerk in coastal New England before turning to fiction, giving her mysteries an insider's eye for wills, deeds, and the secrets they bury. She writes from a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage, where the fog does half her plotting for her.
