## About the author

Kestrel Ravensworth spent seven years answering phones at a harbor-town cab company before turning the voices in her headset into fiction. She writes mysteries about ordinary workers who stumble into extraordinary crimes, drawn from the graveyard shift's strange intimacy with a city after dark.
