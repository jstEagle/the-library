# Coral and the Verdict

**by Ulyana Nakamura**

> Sebastian Ravensworth, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, prides themselves on never taking a case they can't close. Then a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must close the one case everyone swore was closed for good before the statute expires.

## About the author

Ulyana Nakamura is a former parish archivist who spent seven years cataloguing ecclesiastical records before turning to crime fiction full-time. She writes mysteries steeped in liturgical ritual, quiet English cities, and the moral weight of secrets kept for good reasons.
