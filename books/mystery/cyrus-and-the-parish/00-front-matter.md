# Cyrus and the Parish

**by Astrid Marchetti**

> Paloma Okafor, a true-crime podcaster chasing the coldest case on record, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. Then a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it.
