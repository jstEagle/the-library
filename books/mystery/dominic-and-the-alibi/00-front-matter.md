# Dominic and the Alibi

**by Ines Iversen**

> Wendell Fairweather, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has spent three years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must find the witness everyone swears is dead before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.

## About the author

Ines Iversen is the pen name of a former parish records volunteer who spent years alphabetizing other people's absences. She writes mysteries about quiet institutions and the documents that outlive the people they were meant to protect.
