## About the author

Yara Amberdale spent a decade as a probate clerk in coastal New England before turning to fiction, and her mysteries are known for their uncanny fidelity to the paperwork of death. She writes from a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage, where she keeps her late grandmother's ledgers for inspiration.
