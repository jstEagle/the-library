# Edmund and the Confession

**by Mirabel Whitlock**

> Odile Marchetti, an insurance investigator with a photographic memory, has spent fifteen years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must untangle alibis woven thirty years ago before the tide gives up what it took.

## About the author

Mirabel Whitlock is a former coastal archivist whose novels are known for their patient, tide-like pacing and characters haunted by what they chose to forget. She writes from a converted boathouse on the northern Atlantic coast, where she keeps every letter she has ever received.
