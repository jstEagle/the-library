## About the author

Ulyana Ravensworth is the pen name of a former rural claims adjuster who spent fifteen years documenting other people's losses before turning them into fiction. They write from a converted tithe barn in East Anglia, where they keep meticulous notes on everything—including, they admit, things better forgotten.
