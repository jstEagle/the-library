# Emrys and the Autopsy

**by Xanthe Okafor**

## About the author

Xanthe Okafor spent a decade as a probate paralegal in coastal New England before turning to fiction, drawing on the strange human dramas hidden inside estate files. She writes mysteries about quiet people who notice too much, and lives with an uncooperative cat named Verdict.
