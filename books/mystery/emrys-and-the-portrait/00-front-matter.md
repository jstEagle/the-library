## About the author

Ada Sterling is the pen name of a former forensic archivist who spent fifteen years cataloguing cold-case evidence before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries from a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage on the Cornish coast, where the fog does half her plotting for her.
