# Felix and the Alibi

**by Rafael Okafor**

> Felix Eriksen, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, thought they had seen every way a life can end. But when a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must untangle alibis woven thirty years ago before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.
