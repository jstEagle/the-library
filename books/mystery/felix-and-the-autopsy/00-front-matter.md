## About the author

Juniper Brightwater is the pen name of a former small-town court reporter who spent fifteen years transcribing other people's worst days before turning them into fiction. They write mysteries about ordinary people forced to choose between safety and conscience, and live quietly near the coast with an ill-tempered cat named Verdict.
