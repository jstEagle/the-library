**by Sable Eriksen**

## About the author

Sable Eriksen is the pen name of a former claims adjuster from coastal Maine who spent fifteen years reading other people's disasters for a living. Their debut novels are known for quiet towns hiding loud secrets and investigators who remember everything except how to let go.
