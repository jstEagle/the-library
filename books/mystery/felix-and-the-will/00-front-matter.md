## About the author

Orsen Halloran spent eleven years driving night shifts in a mid-sized city before turning to fiction, and his mysteries are known for their insider texture of radio static, dead runs, and drivers who see everything. He writes under a pen name because, as he puts it, 'a dispatcher's best tool is being nobody.'
