# Gideon and the Alibi

**by Lena Montrose**

> Edmund Ravensworth, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, thought they had seen every way a life can end. But when a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the statute expires.

## About the author

Lena Montrose is the pen name of a former probate clerk turned novelist who spent a decade reading wills that read like confessions. She writes mysteries about paperwork, secrets, and the people who keep both.
