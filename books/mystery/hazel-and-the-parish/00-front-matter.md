# Hazel and the Parish

**by Marguerite Okonkwo**

> August Coldharbour, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, prides themselves on never taking a case they can't close. But when the missing person's diary continues past the date she disappeared, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must close the one case everyone swore was closed for good before the trial begins.

## About the author

Marguerite Okonkwo is the pen name of a former court stenographer who spent eleven years transcribing small-town trials before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries about people the justice system overlooks, from a farmhouse office full of case files she probably shouldn't own.
