# Ines and the Autopsy

**by Marguerite Amberdale**

> Theo Fairweather, a true-crime podcaster chasing the coldest case on record, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must decode a ledger hidden in plain sight before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.

## About the author

Marguerite Amberdale is the pen name of a former hospital archivist who spent fifteen years cataloguing unclaimed belongings and the stories they refused to tell. She writes mysteries from a converted chapel on the coast, where the fog does half her plotting.
