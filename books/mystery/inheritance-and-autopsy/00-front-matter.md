## About the author

Tamsin Voss spent a decade as a rural records clerk before turning to fiction, a job she credits for teaching her how small towns keep their dead organized and their living complicit. She writes mysteries from a farmhouse full of filing cabinets, where every character is filed under a name they may not deserve.
