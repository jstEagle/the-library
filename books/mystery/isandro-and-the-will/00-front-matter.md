# Isandro and the Will

**by Gideon Oyelaran**

> Odile Villalobos, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. Then a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Gideon Oyelaran is a former court clerk from Ibadan who now writes mysteries from a small apartment above a laundromat in Lisbon. He claims his plots come from reading other people's discarded legal paperwork, which he insists he found legally.
