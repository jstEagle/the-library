# Jonah and the Autopsy

**by Gideon Okafor**

> Sable Lockridge, a small-town coroner who knows every grave by name, has spent three years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do, and time is already running out.
