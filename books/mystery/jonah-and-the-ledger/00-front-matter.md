## About the author

Orsen Blackwood is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent fifteen years cataloguing shipwreck records along the northern coast before turning to fiction. His novels are known for their tide-worn atmospheres and protagonists who speak more fluently to the dead than to the living.
