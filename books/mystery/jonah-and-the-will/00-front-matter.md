## About the author

Rafael Voss spent a decade cataloguing ecclesiastical archives across northern England before turning to fiction, a career that left him with an abiding fascination for what institutions choose to lock away. He writes mysteries from a converted vestry in York, where he insists the drafts are better for the draftiness.
