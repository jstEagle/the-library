## About the author

Freya Dravenmoor spent a decade working night desks in small-town hotels, where she learned that every lobby keeps two histories: the one on display and the one in the ledger. She writes mysteries about people who notice too much, from a farmhouse office lined with other people's abandoned luggage tags.
