# Jude and the Witness

**by Elodie Reyes**

> Dominic Iversen, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, prides themselves on never taking a case they can't close. Then a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Elodie Reyes is a former paralegal from coastal Maine who spent a decade drafting wills for families with more secrets than assets. She writes mysteries about paper trails, quiet witnesses, and the people the legal system forgets.
