## About the author

Rafael Villalobos spent a decade as a probate clerk in coastal New England before turning to fiction, where he developed a reputation for mysteries built from paperwork, inheritance law, and the lies families tell to keep their money clean. He lives in a converted boathouse and claims he still reads strangers' wills for pleasure.
