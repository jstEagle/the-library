# Kestrel and the Alibi

**by Astrid Montrose**

> August Ravensworth, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must untangle alibis woven thirty years ago, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Astrid Montrose is the pen name of a former cathedral tour guide who spent a decade listening to tourists ask what was behind locked doors. She writes mysteries about quiet people who notice everything, from a cottage within earshot of church bells.
