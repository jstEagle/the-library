# Kestrel and the Witness

**by Lena Hartwell**

> Silas Voss, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, prides themselves on never taking a case they can't close. Then a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it.
