## About the author

Theo Ravensworth is the pen name of a former court stenographer who spent eleven years transcribing testimony in small-town courthouses before turning to fiction. Known for procedurally precise mysteries steeped in moral ambiguity, Ravensworth writes from a farmhouse in upstate New York.
