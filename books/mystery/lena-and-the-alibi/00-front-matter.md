# Lena and the Alibi

**by Freya Vane**

> Ansel Sterling, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when the missing person's diary continues past the date she disappeared, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Freya Vane is the pen name of a former parish administrator who spent a decade among vestry minutes, burial registers, and things no one wanted written down. She writes mysteries about quiet people who notice too much.
