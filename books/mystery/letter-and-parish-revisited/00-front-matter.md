## About the author

Isandro Blackwood spent a decade working night dispatch for coastal transport companies before turning to fiction, a job he credits with teaching him how much people confess to strangers on the phone. He writes mysteries from a rented room above a shuttered chandlery, where the fog arrives earlier than the news does.
