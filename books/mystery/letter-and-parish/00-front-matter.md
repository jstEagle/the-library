## About the author

Emrys Brightwater is the pen name of a former rural newsroom reporter who spent seven years covering small-town courts and estate disputes before turning to fiction. They write mysteries about people who live at the edges of tight-knit communities, drawing on a childhood spent moving between foster homes in the English countryside.
