# Little Hawley Rising Anew

**by A. M. Zhou**

> Ada Winterbourne, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has spent twelve years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when the town's beloved founder is exhumed and the coffin is empty, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.

## About the author

A. M. Zhou is the pen name of a former parish records volunteer who spent three years cataloguing a decommissioned English church's archives before turning to fiction. They write mysteries about quiet people who know too much, from a drafty house full of unlabeled boxes.
