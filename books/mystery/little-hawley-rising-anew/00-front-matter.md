# Little Hawley Rising Anew

**by Odile Delacroix**

> Silas Crane, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has spent ten years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the trial begins.

## About the author

Odile Delacroix is the pen name of a former small-town paralegal who spent years cataloguing probate files in New England and never stopped wondering what the quietest clients were hiding. She writes mysteries about paper trails, small towns, and the people who believe order can hold back the dark.
