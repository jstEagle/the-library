## About the author

Kestrel Sterling is the pen name of a former coastal claims adjuster who spent eleven years reading other people's disasters for a living before turning to fiction. They write mysteries from a houseboat office, insisting that every alibi they've ever audited taught them something about how stories are built to be believed.
