## About the author

Lena Whitlock spent a decade answering phones for a coastal taxi company before turning to fiction, and her dialogue still carries the cadence of 3 a.m. radio chatter. She lives in a fog-bound harbor town where she keeps, she admits, an unseemly interest in old cemetery records.
