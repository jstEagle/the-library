## About the author

Isandro Blackwood is the pen name of a former records clerk who spent a decade inside municipal archives before turning to fiction. Known for mysteries built on paperwork, memory, and the lies towns tell themselves, Blackwood writes from a coastal New England town much like the one in this novel.
