## About the author

Ulyana Coldharbour is the pseudonym of a former probate clerk who spent eleven years cataloguing the unclaimed estates of the wealthy. They write mysteries from a converted tithe barn on the Yorkshire moors, where they claim every will tells a lie if you read it closely enough.
