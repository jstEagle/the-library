# Mirabel and the Alibi

**by Petra Nakamura**

> Yara Okafor, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must close the one case everyone swore was closed for good before the trial begins.

## About the author

Petra Nakamura is a former parish records clerk who spent a decade cataloguing centuries of births, marriages, and quiet scandals before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries about people who know too much because they were never supposed to be noticed.
