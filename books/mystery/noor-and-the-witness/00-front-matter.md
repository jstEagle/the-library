## About the author

Wendell Whitlock spent a decade as a probate clerk in small-town Ohio before turning to fiction, a career that left him with an archivist's love of paper trails and a skeptic's view of official justice. He writes mysteries about people who keep secrets for a living, and lives quietly where the county records room still smells of toner and dust.
