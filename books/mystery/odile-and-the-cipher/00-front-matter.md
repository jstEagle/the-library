## About the author

Orsen Pemberton is the pen name of a former small-town newspaper reporter who spent seven years covering municipal archives and cold cases across the Great Lakes region. Pemberton now writes mysteries full-time from a farmhouse overlooking water, insisting that every fictional town be built from real minutes of real town council meetings.
