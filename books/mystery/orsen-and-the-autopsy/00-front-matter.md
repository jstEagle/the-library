# Orsen and the Autopsy

**by August Joubert**

> Casper Nystrom, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has spent five years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. ## About the author

August Joubert is the pen name of a former probate clerk from the upper Midwest who spent a decade filing wills for people who preferred not to be found. This is the second novel in the Casper Nystrom series.
