# Orsen and the Ledger

**by Edmund Coldharbour**

> Freya Dunmore, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has spent seven years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Edmund Coldharbour is the pen name of a former parish records clerk who spent a decade cataloguing small-town archives across northern England. He writes mysteries about paper trails, buried secrets, and the people paid to keep both tidy.
