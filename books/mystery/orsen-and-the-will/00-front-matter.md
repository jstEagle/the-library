## About the author

Ulyana Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former rural registrar who spent a decade cataloguing births and deaths in small-town municipal archives before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries about the quiet violence hidden inside close-knit communities, and lives somewhere with too many cemeteries and not enough questions asked.
