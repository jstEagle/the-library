## About the author

Rafael Joubert is the pen name of a former night auditor at a grand seaside hotel, where he collected a decade of overheard confessions and unsolved disappearances. He writes mysteries from a rented room above a laundromat, insisting that every plot he invents is less strange than what guests actually confess at 3 a.m.
