# Oscar and the Cipher

**by Gideon Oyelaran**

> Silas Marchetti, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, prides themselves on never taking a case they can't close. ## About the author

Gideon Oyelaran spent six years driving nights in Lagos and Chicago before turning his logbooks into fiction, and he still can't sleep before midnight. He writes mysteries about people whose jobs keep them invisible to the cities they know best.
