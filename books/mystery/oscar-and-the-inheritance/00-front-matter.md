# Oscar and the Inheritance

**by E. M. Sterling**

> Wren Sandoval, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, has spent twenty years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when twin letters arrive: one confessing, one promising more, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do, and time is already running out.

## About the author

E. M. Sterling is the pen name of a former municipal court clerk who spent fifteen years transcribing small-town depositions before turning to fiction. Sterling writes mysteries about the people who keep records—and the records that keep people.
