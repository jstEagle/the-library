# Oscar and the Parish

**by Coral Sterling**

> August Yarrow, a small-town coroner who knows every grave by name, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. ## About the author

Coral Sterling spent a decade as a rural court reporter before turning to fiction, drawing on the quiet secrets of small-town death records. She writes from a farmhouse where, she insists, nothing mysterious has ever happened.
