# Paloma and the Autopsy

**by W. M. Okafor**

> Emrys Okafor, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has spent fifteen years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
