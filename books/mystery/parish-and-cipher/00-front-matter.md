# Parish and Cipher

**by Theo Zhou**

> Silas Halloran, a retired detective running a failing bookshop, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. Then a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.

## About the author

Theo Zhou is the pen name of a former court stenographer who spent fifteen years transcribing testimony before turning to fiction. They live above a secondhand bookshop and write mysteries about people who know too much.
