## About the author

M. M. Thornbury is the pen name of a former parish archivist who spent fifteen years cataloguing birth, death, and burial registers in the English countryside before turning to fiction. They write mysteries about the lies small communities tell to survive, and they still cannot pass a churchyard without checking the dates on the stones.
