# Parish and Verdict

**by Lior Vasquez**

> Orsen Pemberton, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. ## About the author

Lior Vasquez is the pen name of a former parish records volunteer who spent five years transcribing Victorian burial registers in northern England. They write mysteries where the archive itself becomes a witness, and where belonging is always conditional.
