# Petra and the Inheritance

**by Elodie Voss**

> Wren Ishikawa, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must decode a ledger hidden in plain sight before the statute expires.

## About the author

Elodie Voss spent a decade as a probate paralegal in coastal Oregon before turning to fiction, where her fascination with wills, secrets, and the paperwork of dying became her signature. She writes mysteries about people who fix what institutions won't.
