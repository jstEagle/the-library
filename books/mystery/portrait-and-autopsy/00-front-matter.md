## About the author

Mirabel Almeida is a former parish records clerk who spent a decade cataloguing centuries of births, deaths, and quiet scandals before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries from a narrow stone house near the coast, where she insists the best plots are found in archives, not imaginations.
