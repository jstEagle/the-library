# Quentin and the Witness

**by Juniper Reyes**

> August Thackeray, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, has spent three years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Juniper Reyes is a former small-town radio operator who spent a decade listening to strangers' voices in the dark before turning those nights into fiction. She writes mysteries about ordinary people who know too much and say too little.
