## About the author

Tamsin Ishikawa spent a decade as a rural correspondent covering cold cases that never made national news, and it shows in her patient, unglamorous attention to small-town complicity. She writes from a farmhouse in North Yorkshire, where she claims the best research material is simply listening to what neighbors don't say.
