Then the town's beloved founder is exhumed and the coffin is empty — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Elodie Montrose is a former parish records clerk from the Yorkshire coast who spent a decade cataloguing centuries of municipal secrets before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries about people who keep other people's secrets, and lives above a decommissioned library with an unreasonably large card catalogue.
