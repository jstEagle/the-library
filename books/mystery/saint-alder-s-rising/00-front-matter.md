## About the author

Coral Yarrow is the pen name of a former night-desk clerk who spent a decade in small coastal hotels, collecting the stories guests thought no one was listening to. Saint Alder's Rising is her second novel, drawn from a real unsolved disappearance she could never stop filing away.
