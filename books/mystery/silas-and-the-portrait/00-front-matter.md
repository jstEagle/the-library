## About the author

Rafael Vasquez spent fifteen years as a probate clerk in coastal New England before turning to fiction, drawing on the strange, half-buried lives he encountered in dusty estate files. He writes mysteries about paper trails, inherited secrets, and the people who read other people's endings for a living.
