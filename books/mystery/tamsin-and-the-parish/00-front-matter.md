## About the author

Marguerite Thackeray spent a decade as a probate clerk in rural England before turning to fiction, giving her mysteries an insider's knowledge of wills, estates, and the secrets families file away. She writes from a converted tithe barn in Dorset, where she claims every village has one closed case too many.
