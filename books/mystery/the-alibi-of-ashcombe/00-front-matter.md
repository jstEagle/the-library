## About the author

Sebastian Okonkwo is a former legal clerk turned novelist who spent a decade handling probate files in coastal England, where he collected the strange, half-finished stories the dead leave behind. He writes quiet mysteries about paperwork, memory, and the violence that respectable towns prefer to forget.
