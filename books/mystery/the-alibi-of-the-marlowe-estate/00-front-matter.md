# The Alibi of the Marlowe Estate

**by Astrid Thackeray**

> Kestrel Galloway, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has spent twelve years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then the missing person's diary continues past the date she disappeared — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Astrid Thackeray is the pen name of a former small-town paralegal who spent a decade reading wills in courthouse basements before turning to crime fiction. She writes mysteries about the secrets towns keep and the people who keep them, from a farmhouse full of rescued animals.
