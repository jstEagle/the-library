## About the author

O. M. Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former coastal coroner's assistant who spent eleven years cataloguing unexplained deaths along the northern seaboard before turning to fiction. They write in a lighthouse-keeper's cottage and claim every novel begins with a real case file they were never allowed to close.
