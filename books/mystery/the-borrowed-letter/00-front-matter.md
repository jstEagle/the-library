## About the author

Noor Ravensworth is a former small-town archivist who spent years cataloguing the paper trails people leave behind, an obsession that now fuels her fiction. She writes mysteries about quiet places where old secrets compound like interest, and lives somewhere with unreliable weather and excellent secondhand bookshops.
