## About the author

Edmund Okafor is a former night auditor at coastal hotels across three continents, where he collected the small betrayals and quiet kindnesses of hospitality workers. He writes mysteries from a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage, insisting that every locked room is just a lobby nobody watched closely enough.
