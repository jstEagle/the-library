## About the author

E. M. Iversen is the pen name of a former parish records clerk who spent a decade cataloguing English cathedral archives before turning to fiction. Known for quiet, meticulous mysteries, Iversen writes about people who find belonging in old buildings and dangerous truths in old paper.
