## About the author

Wendell Quintana is a former rural records clerk who spent fifteen years cataloguing small-town deaths before turning to fiction. He writes mysteries about the secrets communities keep in plain sight, drawing on the ledgers, obituaries, and half-truths of the places he has lived.
