# The Confession of Saint Alder's

**by Dominic Dunmore**

> Dominic Iversen, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, has spent twenty years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the tide gives up what it took.

## About the author

Dominic Dunmore spent eleven years driving night cabs in a coastal town before turning to fiction, and still keeps his old dispatch logs for inspiration. He writes mysteries about small places that keep large secrets, and lives within earshot of the sea.
