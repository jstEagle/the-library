## About the author

Tamsin Eastwick spent eight years working front desks in small coastal hotels before turning to fiction, a career she credits for her ear for what guests say when they think no one is listening. She lives in a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage with two indifferent cats and an archive of other people's postcards.
