## About the author

Mirabel Amberdale is the pen name of a former hotel night manager who spent eleven years watching guests come and go, collecting the stories they left behind. She writes mysteries from a small flat above a shuttered tea room in Bath, where she insists every character deserves a secret worth keeping.
