## About the author

Orsen Vane is the pen name of a former small-town newspaper reporter who spent a decade covering courtrooms across the American Midwest before turning to fiction. Known for meticulously researched mysteries about secrets that outlive their keepers, Vane writes from a farmhouse where, allegedly, nothing has ever happened.
