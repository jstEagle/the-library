## About the author

Quentin Ishikawa is a former claims adjuster from coastal Maine whose fascination with paper trails and small-town silences informs his slow-burn mysteries. He writes under a pen name to protect the privacy of the families whose legal disputes first taught him how much truth hides inside paperwork.
