## About the author

Sable Okafor is a former forensic technician turned novelist who grew up in a river town where everyone claimed to know everyone's business and no one knew anything at all. Her fiction explores the quiet rot beneath small-town civility, drawing on her fascination with the people whose job it is to look at the dead.
