## About the author

Kestrel Ashgrove spent eleven years working night desks in grand old hotels before turning to crime fiction, and their novels are known for elevators, lobbies, and stairwells as places where secrets change hands. They live above a bookshop in a coastal city and claim they can read a guest's whole biography from their luggage.
