# The Last Confession Anew

**by Ivo Galloway**

> Beatrix Yarrow, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has spent five years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then the missing person's diary continues past the date she disappeared — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.

## About the author

Ivo Galloway is the pen name of a former small-town court clerk who spent a decade watching wills, deeds, and case files decide what people were allowed to remember. They write mysteries about paper trails and the people who learn too much from them.
