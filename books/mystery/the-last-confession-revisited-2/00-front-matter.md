# The Last Confession Revisited

**by Ulyana Eriksen**

> Edmund Hartwell, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has spent twelve years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.

## About the author

Ulyana Eriksen is the pen name of a former parish records clerk who spent a decade cataloguing centuries of small-town secrets before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries about people who mistake order for safety.
