# The Last Parish

**by Jude Delacroix**

> Lena Montrose, a true-crime podcaster chasing the coldest case on record, prides themselves on never taking a case they can't close. Then a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Jude Delacroix is the pen name of a former court reporter who grew up in small-town Louisiana, where everyone knew which doors not to knock on. Their debut novel draws on decades of listening to testimony that never made it into verdicts.
