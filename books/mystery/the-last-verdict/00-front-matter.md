# The Last Verdict

**by Freya Oyelaran**

> Sebastian Winterbourne, a true-crime podcaster chasing the coldest case on record, has spent twenty years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must decode a ledger hidden in plain sight, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Freya Oyelaran is a former court stenographer from coastal Maine who spent a decade transcribing small-town trials before turning to fiction. She writes about communities that keep their own ledgers, and the people who finally refuse to balance them.
