## About the author

Mara Iversen spent a decade as a claims adjuster in the Pacific Northwest before turning to fiction, drawing on the ledgers, lies, and quiet desperation she witnessed in the industry. She writes mysteries about ordinary people whose meticulous minds become instruments of reckoning.
