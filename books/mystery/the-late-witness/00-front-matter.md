**by Hazel Brightwater**

> Beatrix Ashgrove, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has spent three years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then the town's beloved founder is exhumed and the coffin is empty — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Hazel Brightwater is the pen name of a former parish records clerk who spent a decade cataloguing centuries of small-town secrets before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries about quiet people who keep dangerous archives, from a stone cottage she shares with an ill-tempered cat named Folio.
