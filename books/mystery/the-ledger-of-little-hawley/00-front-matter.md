**by Petra Kavanagh**

> Ada Villalobos, a retired detective running a failing bookshop, has spent seven years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. ## About the author

Petra Kavanagh is the pen name of a former small-town bookseller who spent a decade listening to gossip over the register before turning it into fiction. She writes mysteries about places that keep secrets the way other towns keep gardens — carefully, and out of sight.
