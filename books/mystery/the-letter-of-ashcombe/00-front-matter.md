## About the author

Felix Vasquez spent a decade working overnight shifts in small-town radio before turning to crime fiction, a background that gives his novels their intimate knowledge of who is awake at 3 a.m. He lives in coastal New England, where he collects antique ledgers and insists every mystery he writes could plausibly be solved from behind a desk.
