# The Letter of Windrow House

**by Xanthe Halloran**

> Kestrel Amberdale, a retired detective running a failing bookshop, has spent twenty years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Xanthe Halloran is the pen name of a former archivist who spent fifteen years cataloging estate papers in the English countryside before turning to crime fiction. She writes mysteries about small towns where the past is never as neatly filed as it appears.
