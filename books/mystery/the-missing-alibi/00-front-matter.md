# The Missing Alibi

**by Astrid Thornbury**

> Ivo Dravenmoor, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. Then a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the tide gives up what it took.

## About the author

Astrid Thornbury spent a decade answering phones for a coastal cab company before turning her ear for late-night confessions into fiction. She writes mysteries about ordinary people who notice too much, from a house above the tide flats.
