## About the author

Vesper Vasquez is a former small-town newspaper archivist who spent a decade cataloguing obituaries, wills, and the quiet crimes between them. She writes mysteries from a converted funeral parlor office, insisting that every town keeps two ledgers — the one it shows, and the one it buries.
