## About the author

Mirabel Kavanagh is a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing unsolved case files before turning to fiction, and her novels are known for their forensic attention to detail and quiet, aching emotional undercurrents. She writes from a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage on the Atlantic coast, where she claims every story begins with a document that shouldn't exist.
