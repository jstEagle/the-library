## About the author

Edmund Thackeray is the pen name of a former hotel night manager who spent a decade collecting the secrets guests left behind in lobby conversations. Writing from a converted boathouse on the Norfolk Broads, Thackeray specializes in mysteries where the past refuses to stay politely buried.
