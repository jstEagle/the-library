## About the author

Felix Ravensworth is the pen name of a former court stenographer who spent two decades transcribing trials before turning to fiction, giving his thrillers an ear for the exact moment testimony cracks. He lives in a coastal town he refuses to name, citing an unsolved case he swears he'll write about someday.
