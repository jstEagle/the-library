# The Painted Verdict

**by Emrys Brightwater**

> Xanthe Ravensworth, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has spent twenty years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Emrys Brightwater is the pen name of a former parish records clerk who spent eleven years transcribing centuries-old ledgers before turning to fiction. He writes mysteries about quiet people who know too much, and lives somewhere within sight of an old church tower.
