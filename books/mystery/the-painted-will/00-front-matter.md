## About the author

Theo Joubert is a former small-town newspaper reporter from the Atlantic coast of Canada who covered coroners' inquests for a decade before turning to fiction. He lives in a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage with his wife and an ill-tempered gull he refuses to name.
