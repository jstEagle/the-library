# The Painted Witness

**by Lior Ashgrove**

> Oscar Marchetti, a hotel concierge who notices everything, has spent twenty years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when the town's beloved founder is exhumed and the coffin is empty, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.

## About the author

Lior Ashgrove is the pen name of a former night-desk clerk who spent a decade in grand hotels listening to what guests thought no one heard. The Painted Witness is their third novel, drawn from a lifelong fascination with the invisible people who keep institutions running.
