## About the author

Emrys Dunmore is the pen name of a former parish records clerk who spent nine years cataloguing centuries of baptisms, marriages, and burials along England's eastern coast. They write mysteries steeped in ecclesiastical archives, tidal landscapes, and the quiet moral compromises of small communities.
