# The Parish of Little Hawley

**by J. M. Delacroix**

> Juniper Crane, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must close the one case everyone swore was closed for good before the tide gives up what it took.

## About the author

J. M. Delacroix is the pen name of a former emergency dispatcher who spent eleven years on the night shift in a coastal town much like Little Hawley, listening to the dark so others could sleep. They now write mysteries about quiet people who notice everything.
