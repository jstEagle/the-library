## About the author

Jonah Okafor is the pen name of a former rural claims adjuster who spent a decade walking the waterlogged byways of eastern England and never stopped wondering about the cases that closed too neatly. The Parish of the Fens Again is the second novel to feature investigator Kestrel Coldharbour.
