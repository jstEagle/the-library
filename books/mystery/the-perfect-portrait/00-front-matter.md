## About the author

Kestrel Sandoval is the pen name of a former forensic archivist who spent a decade cataloguing disputed artworks for European insurers before turning to fiction. They live off-grid on the Atlantic coast, where they restore antique cameras and write mysteries about memory, money, and the lies people preserve like paintings.
