# The Perfect Witness

**by Rosalind Brightwater**

> Theo Sterling, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has spent five years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must close the one case everyone swore was closed for good before the statute expires.

## About the author

Rosalind Brightwater spent fifteen years as a probate clerk in small-town New England before turning to fiction, where she learned that every filing cabinet holds at least one body. She writes mysteries about ordinary professionals who discover the paperwork of power.
