# The Portrait of Little Hawley

**by Yara Thornbury**

> Zephyrine Okafor, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, thought they had seen every way a life can end. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Yara Thornbury is the pen name of a former parish records clerk who spent eleven years cataloguing births, deaths, and the silences between them in northern England. They write mysteries about people history tried to file away.
