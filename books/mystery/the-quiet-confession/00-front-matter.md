**by Quentin Kavanagh**

## About the author

Quentin Kavanagh is the pen name of a former claims adjuster who spent eleven years reconstructing other people's worst days from paperwork. They write mysteries about memory, complicity, and the small towns that keep their secrets like savings accounts.
