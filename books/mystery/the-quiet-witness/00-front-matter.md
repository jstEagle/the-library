## About the author

Marguerite Zhou is a former court stenographer who spent fifteen years transcribing testimony before turning to fiction, giving her thrillers an uncanny ear for what people say when they think no one is listening. She lives in a coastal town where fog rolls in early and everyone remembers who left and why.
