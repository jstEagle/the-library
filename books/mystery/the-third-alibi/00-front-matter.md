# The Third Alibi

**by Jude Zhou**

> Yara Thornbury, a true-crime podcaster chasing the coldest case on record, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. Then a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.

## About the author

Jude Zhou is the pen name of a former court stenographer who spent eleven years transcribing testimony in small-town courthouses before turning to fiction. They write mysteries about people who remember too much and communities that remember too little.
