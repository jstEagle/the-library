# The Unquiet Alibi

**by Ada Eriksen**

> Emrys Crane, a small-town coroner who knows every grave by name, thought they had seen every way a life can end. But when a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Ada Eriksen is the pen name of a former coastal records clerk who spent a decade cataloguing unclaimed deaths along the northern seaboard. She writes mysteries about the people towns prefer to forget.
