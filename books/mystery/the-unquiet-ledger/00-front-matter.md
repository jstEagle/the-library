# The Unquiet Ledger

**by Jude Crane**

## About the author

Jude Crane spent a decade as a paralegal in small-town probate courts before turning to fiction, and their mysteries are known for legal procedural texture and quietly ferocious protagonists. They live in a converted courthouse with two cats named after dismissed verdicts.
