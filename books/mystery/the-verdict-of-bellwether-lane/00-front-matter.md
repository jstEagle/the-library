## About the author

Zephyrine Blackwood is the pen name of a former maritime claims adjuster who spent eleven years investigating deaths along the English coast before turning to fiction. She writes in a converted pilot's cottage, where the tide chart on her kitchen wall is said to be more accurate than the one published by the admiralty.
