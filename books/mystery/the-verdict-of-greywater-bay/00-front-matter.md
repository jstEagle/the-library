# The Verdict of Greywater Bay

**by Noor Okafor**

> Dominic Thornbury, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, has spent ten years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do, and time is already running out.
