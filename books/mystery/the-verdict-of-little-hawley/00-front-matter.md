## About the author

Astrid Ravensworth spent a decade as a court reporter in small-market towns before trading the courtroom for the night desk, a vantage point she claims is the best seat in the house for watching what people do when they think no one is listening. She writes mysteries about ordinary people who keep extraordinary secrets.
