# The Will of Little Hawley

**by R. M. Dravenmoor**

> Casper Coldharbour, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must close the one case everyone swore was closed for good before the tide gives up what it took.

## About the author

R. M. Dravenmoor spent a decade as a probate clerk in coastal England before turning to fiction, and their mysteries are known for wills that lie and tides that remember. They live above a shuttered solicitor's office with a deaf terrier named Verdict.
