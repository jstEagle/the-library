## About the author

Ansel Okafor is a former night-desk clerk at a historic coastal hotel who spent a decade collecting the strange, sad, and occasionally criminal stories guests leave behind at 3 a.m. He writes mysteries in which the people who watch — porters, cleaners, clerks — see everything the important people miss.
