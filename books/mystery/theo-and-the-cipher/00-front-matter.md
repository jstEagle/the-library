## About the author

Rosalind Galloway spent fifteen years as a probate clerk in coastal Maine before turning to fiction, and her mysteries are known for their forensic attention to paperwork and inheritance law. She lives in a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage with two indifferent cats and an ever-growing archive of real estate deeds she swears are 'for research.'
