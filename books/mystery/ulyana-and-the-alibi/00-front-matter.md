# Ulyana and the Alibi

**by Emrys Eriksen**

> Tamsin Crane, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has spent twenty years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it.
