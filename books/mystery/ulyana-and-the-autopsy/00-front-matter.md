## About the author

Silas Kavanagh is a former parish records clerk from the Yorkshire coast who spent eleven years cataloguing documents no one was meant to read. Ulyana and the Autopsy is his fourth novel, written in a converted tithe barn with a view of a churchyard he refuses to discuss.
