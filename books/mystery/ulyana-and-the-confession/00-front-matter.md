## About the author

Lior Thackeray spent a decade working overnight shifts in municipal transit before turning to crime fiction, a background that gives the book its insomnia-accurate texture of radio static and empty streets. The author lives in a coastal city and is known for refusing interviews, saying only that 'the best confessions happen at 3 a.m.'
