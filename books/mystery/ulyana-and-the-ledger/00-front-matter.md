# Ulyana and the Ledger

**by B. M. Dunmore**

> August Brightwater, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, prides themselves on never taking a case they can't close. Then a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.

## About the author

B. M. Dunmore is the pen name of a former parish records clerk who spent eleven years cataloguing births, deaths, and disappearances in English cathedral towns before turning to fiction. They write mysteries about people whose lives are filed away in someone else's drawers.
