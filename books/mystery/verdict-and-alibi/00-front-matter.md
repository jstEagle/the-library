# Verdict and Alibi

**by Jude Blackwood**

> Paloma Coldharbour, a true-crime podcaster chasing the coldest case on record, has spent twelve years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must untangle alibis woven thirty years ago before the tide gives up what it took.

## About the author

Jude Blackwood is the pen name of a former court reporter who spent a decade transcribing small-town trials before turning to fiction. They live on a fog-bound coastline that supplies most of their atmosphere and none of their alibis.
