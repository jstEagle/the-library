## About the author

Lena Okonkwo is a former parish records clerk who spent a decade cataloguing centuries of births, deaths, and quiet scandals before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries about people history overlooked, drawing on her fascination with archives as places where truth waits patiently to be found.
