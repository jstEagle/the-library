## About the author

Juniper Crane is the pen name of a former hotel night manager who spent a decade collecting the secrets guests left behind in lobby conversations and unclaimed mail. She writes mysteries about the people who keep other people's secrets, and lives in a coastal town that never fully recovered from its own history.
