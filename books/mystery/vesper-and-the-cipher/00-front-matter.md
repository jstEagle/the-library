**by Cyrus Brightwater**

> Ivo Whitlock, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, thought they had seen every way a life can end. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Cyrus Brightwater spent fifteen years as a probate clerk in coastal New England before turning to fiction, drawing on the strange inheritances and quiet grudges he witnessed firsthand. He writes from a converted boathouse where, he insists, nothing suspicious has ever washed ashore.
