# Wendell and the Alibi

**by Ulyana Thornbury**

## About the author

Ulyana Thornbury is the pen name of a former claims adjuster who spent eleven years reading other people's disasters for a living before turning them into fiction. They write mysteries from a farmhouse in upstate New York, where they keep meticulous notes and trust almost no one.
