# Wendell and the Ledger

**by Gideon Dunmore**

> August Vane, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has spent seven years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Gideon Dunmore is the pen name of a former small-town court clerk who spent a decade handling probate files in coastal New England before turning to fiction. His debut draws on years of watching how paper can outlive, and outmaneuver, the people it describes.
