# Wendell and the Parish

**by Elodie Thackeray**

## About the author

Elodie Thackeray is the pen name of a former rural claims adjuster who spent a decade reading other people's disasters on paper. She writes mysteries about paper trails, small parishes, and the people history forgets to index.
