# Wendell and the Will

**by Petra Voss**

> Silas Galloway, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has spent seven years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must untangle alibis woven thirty years ago before the trial begins.

## About the author

Petra Voss is a former small-town court clerk who spent a decade transcribing depositions in rural county courthouses before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries about paper trails, buried confessions, and the people who keep secrets professionally.
