# When the Alibi Turns

**by Felix Dravenmoor**

> Paloma Villalobos, a retired detective running a failing bookshop, prides themselves on never taking a case they can't close. Then a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author
