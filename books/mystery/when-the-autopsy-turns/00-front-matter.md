# When the Autopsy Turns

**by Ivo Vasquez**

> Beatrix Okonkwo, a retired detective running a failing bookshop, has spent twenty years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must trap a killer who knows the case file better than the investigators do before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.

## About the author

Ivo Vasquez is a former forensic archivist who spent a decade cataloguing cold cases before turning to fiction. He writes mysteries about people who thought they had outrun their pasts, from a farmhouse in upstate New York.
