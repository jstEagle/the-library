## About the author

W. M. Nystrom is the pen name of a former night-desk clerk who spent a decade watching strangers check in and out of coastal hotels, collecting the small betrayals people think no one notices. They write mysteries from the staff side of the front desk, where every guest is a suspect and every smile is a locked door.
