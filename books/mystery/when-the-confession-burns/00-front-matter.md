# When the Confession Burns

**by Isandro Dunmore**

> Juniper Hartwell, a small-town coroner who knows every grave by name, has spent twelve years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Isandro Dunmore is the pen name of a former rural court reporter who spent a decade transcribing small-town trials before turning to fiction. Known for procedurals steeped in grief and civic silence, Dunmore writes from a farmhouse where the nearest neighbor's porch light is a rumor.
