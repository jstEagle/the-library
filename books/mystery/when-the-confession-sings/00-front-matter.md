## About the author

Marguerite Eriksen is a former court stenographer from the Yorkshire coast who spent twenty years transcribing confessions she was never allowed to comment on. 'When the Confession Sings' is her third novel, drawing on her fascination with what people say when they believe no one is keeping score.
