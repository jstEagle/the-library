# When the Inheritance Falls

**by F. M. Voss**

> Tamsin Winterbourne, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, thought they had seen every way a life can end. But when a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must find the witness everyone swears is dead before the anniversary dinner raises its glass.

## About the author

F. M. Voss spent a decade answering phones for a night-shift taxi dispatch before turning to fiction, and the voices in this book are all ones she heard through a headset. She writes from a rented room above a depot, where the radios never quite go silent.
