## About the author

I. M. Vane spent a decade working front desks in grand hotels across two continents, collecting the small betrayals and large kindnesses that guests assume go unnoticed. Now writing from a rented room above a seaside inn, Vane specializes in mysteries where the most dangerous secrets hide behind impeccable service.
