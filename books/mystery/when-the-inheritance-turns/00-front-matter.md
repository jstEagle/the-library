## About the author

Lior Villalobos grew up in a coastal fishing town where every family kept one story they never told, an inheritance of silence that shaped their obsession with unsolved deaths. A former court stenographer turned novelist, they write mysteries about people who take justice into their own hands because the law arrived too late.
