# When the Ledger Burns Anew

**by Emrys Eastwick**

> Rafael Sterling, an estate lawyer reading a will that shouldn't exist, has spent twelve years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it.
