# When the Parish Breaks

**by Astrid Coldharbour**

> Marguerite Marchetti, a small-town coroner who knows every grave by name, has spent twenty years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must find the witness everyone swears is dead before the statute expires.

## About the author

Astrid Coldharbour spent a decade transcribing inquest records in coastal New England before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries about small towns where the dead are better documented than the living.
