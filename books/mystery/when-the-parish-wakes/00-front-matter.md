# When the Parish Wakes

**by Gideon Eriksen**

> Coral Yarrow, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, has spent five years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. Then twin letters arrive: one confessing, one promising more — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it.
