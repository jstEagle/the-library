# When the Verdict Falls

**by V. M. Nystrom**

## About the author

V. M. Nystrom spent a decade as a paralegal in a small coastal probate firm before turning to fiction full-time, and their mysteries are known for legal procedural detail worn lightly. They live in a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage with two indifferent cats and an ever-growing shelf of unread wills research.
