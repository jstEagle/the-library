# When the Verdict Turns

**by Freya Eriksen**

> Wendell Eastwick, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when the missing person's diary continues past the date she disappeared, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Freya Eriksen grew up in a Norwegian fishing village where every family kept its secrets below the waterline, an upbringing that taught her how much a small town will forgive and what it never forgets. She now writes mysteries from a converted boathouse, drawn always to quiet observers who see more than they say.
