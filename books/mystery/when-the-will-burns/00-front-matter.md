## About the author

Jonah Pemberton is the pen name of a former night-shift hotel manager who spent a decade collecting the strange, sad, and quietly criminal stories of luxury hospitality. He writes mysteries about people the world overlooks, from an undisclosed coastal town he refuses to name.
