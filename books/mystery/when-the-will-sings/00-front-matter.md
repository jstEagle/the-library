## About the author

Petra Amberdale is the pen name of a former rural registrar who spent a decade recording births and deaths in a coastal village, where every file seemed to hide a second story. She now writes mysteries from a drafty cottage, aided by an opinionated cat and a weakness for unsolved local legends.
