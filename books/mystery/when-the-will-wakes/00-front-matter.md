# When the Will Wakes

**by Tamsin Hartwell**

> Kestrel Fairweather, a true-crime podcaster chasing the coldest case on record, has spent twelve years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must close the one case everyone swore was closed for good before the statute expires.

## About the author

Tamsin Hartwell is a former court stenographer from rural Vermont who spent a decade transcribing small-town trials before turning to fiction. She writes mysteries about the secrets communities keep and the price of saying them out loud.
