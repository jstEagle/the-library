## About the author

Coral Coldharbour spent a decade cataloguing parish records in coastal England before turning to crime fiction, and her novels are known for their reverence for archives and their suspicion of institutions. She writes in a converted vestry on the North Norfolk coast, where the tide keeps its own schedule of secrets.
