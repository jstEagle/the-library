## About the author

Isandro Kavanagh is a former claims adjuster from the coastal town of Greyharrow who turned to writing after a decade of reading other people's misfortunes in paperwork. Known for spare prose and morally tangled investigators, Kavanagh writes mysteries where memory is both a gift and a wound.
