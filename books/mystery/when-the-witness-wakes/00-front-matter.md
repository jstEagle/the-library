# When the Witness Wakes

**by Lena Ravensworth**

> Cyrus Iversen, an insurance investigator with a photographic memory, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when a body turns up dressed in clothes stolen from the witness's own closet, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author
