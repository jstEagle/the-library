# Will and Inheritance

**by Freya Delacroix**

> Ulyana Coldharbour, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when twin letters arrive: one confessing, one promising more, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must decode a ledger hidden in plain sight before the tide gives up what it took.

## About the author

Freya Delacroix is the pen name of a former parish records clerk who spent a decade transcribing centuries of baptisms, marriages, and burials in coastal England. Her fascination with what official documents leave unsaid fuels her quiet, archival brand of mystery.
