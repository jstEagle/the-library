# Witness and Cipher

**by Jonah Oyelaran**

## About the author

Jonah Oyelaran is a former night-desk clerk at a coastal grand hotel who spent a decade collecting the stories guests left behind in ledgers and lost-and-found boxes. He writes mysteries about the people who keep other people's secrets for a living.
