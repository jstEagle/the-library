# Witness and Inheritance

**by Ulyana Fairweather**

> Dominic Nystrom, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, prides themselves on never taking a case they can't close. But when twin letters arrive: one confessing, one promising more, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must untangle alibis woven thirty years ago before the tide gives up what it took.

## About the author

Ulyana Fairweather is a former parish records volunteer who spent a decade cataloguing unclaimed letters in coastal church archives. She writes mysteries about people who hide inside institutions, and about the documents that refuse to let them.
