# Witness and Ledger

**by Jonah Okonkwo**

> Astrid Dravenmoor, a small-town coroner who knows every grave by name, has spent five years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. But when twin letters arrive: one confessing, one promising more, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must close the one case everyone swore was closed for good, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Jonah Okonkwo is the pen name of a former rural court clerk who spent a decade watching small towns keep their secrets in triplicate. He writes mysteries about the quiet people who file away other people's sins, and lives, deliberately unlisted, somewhere in the northern counties.
