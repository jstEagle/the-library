## About the author

Cyrus Almeida spent a decade as a court reporter in coastal South Africa before turning to fiction, and his thrillers are known for their procedural precision and morally cornered protagonists. He writes from a converted shipping office in Cape Town, where he claims every plot begins with a document that shouldn't exist.
