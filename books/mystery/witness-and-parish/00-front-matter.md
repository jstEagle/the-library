## About the author

Cyrus Yarrow is the pen name of a former claims adjuster turned novelist who spent eleven years investigating maritime insurance fraud along the English coast. Their fiction is known for procedural precision, unreliable memory as a narrative engine, and quiet seaside towns that keep terrible secrets.
