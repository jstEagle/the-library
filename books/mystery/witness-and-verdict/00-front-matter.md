## About the author

Bram Eriksen is the pen name of a former parish records clerk from the Norfolk coast who spent eleven years cataloguing church archives before turning to crime fiction. His novels are known for their quiet ecclesiastical settings, meticulous archival detail, and detectives who listen more than they speak.
