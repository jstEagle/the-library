**by Sebastian Ishikawa**

## About the author

Sebastian Ishikawa is a former night-shift hotel auditor who spent a decade listening to what guests said when they thought no one was writing it down. He lives on the Norwegian coast, where he collects tide tables and writes mysteries about the people towns prefer to forget.
