## About the author

Wendell Nystrom spent fifteen years as a rural paramedic before turning to fiction, drawing on the quiet devastations he witnessed in county morgues and kitchen-table vigils. He writes from a farmhouse in upstate New York, where he keeps his late father's coroner's field manual on the nightstand.
