# Wren and the Confession

**by Coral Sandoval**

> Lior Yarrow, an insurance investigator with a photographic memory, has spent twelve years keeping the town's secrets filed and alphabetized. ## About the author

Coral Sandoval is a former claims adjuster from a small coastal town who spent a decade reading other people's misfortunes in triplicate before turning them into fiction. She writes mysteries about ordinary people whose meticulous lives come apart at the seams.
