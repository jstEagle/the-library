# Xanthe and the Autopsy

**by Sable Quintana**

> Astrid Montrose, an insurance investigator with a photographic memory, has quietly solved three deaths the police called accidents. But when a confession letter arrives postmarked the day after the funeral, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
