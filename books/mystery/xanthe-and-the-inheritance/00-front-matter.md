# Xanthe and the Inheritance

**by Ines Fairweather**

> Quentin Villalobos, the archivist of a cathedral with a locked crypt, prides themselves on never taking a case they can't close. ## About the author

Ines Fairweather is a former parish records clerk who spent a decade cataloguing wills, letters, and lies in English coastal towns. She writes mysteries about the secrets institutions keep and the people who keep them anyway.
