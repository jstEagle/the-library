## About the author

Zephyrine Montrose is the pen name of a former court stenographer who spent fifteen years transcribing verdicts before deciding to write the stories transcripts couldn't hold. They live in a coastal town much like Larkspur Hollow, where the fog keeps everyone's secrets.
