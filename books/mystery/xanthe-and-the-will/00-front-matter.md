## About the author

Zephyrine Brightwater is the pen name of a former parish records clerk who spent eleven years cataloguing wills, deeds, and disputes in a northern cathedral city. Their fiction is known for quiet protagonists, ecclesiastical settings, and mysteries where the archives themselves become the crime scene.
