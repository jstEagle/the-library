## About the author

Zephyrine Marchetti spent a decade cataloguing ecclesiastical archives in southern France before turning to fiction, where her love of marginalia and misfiled documents shapes every mystery she writes. She lives above a former sacristy with two opinionated cats and an unshakable belief that paper remembers what people forget.
