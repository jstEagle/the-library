## About the author

Juniper Dunmore is the pen name of a former parish records clerk who spent a decade cataloguing centuries of births, deaths, and quiet scandals in English cathedral archives. Their mysteries are known for ecclesiastical settings, unreliable paper trails, and protagonists who love institutions enough to break them.
