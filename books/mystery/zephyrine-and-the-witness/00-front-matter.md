# Zephyrine and the Witness

**by Ulyana Sandoval**

> Dominic Kavanagh, a night-shift dispatcher for a taxi company, prides themselves on never taking a case they can't close. Then a client pays in advance to prove a murder hasn't happened — yet — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must close the one case everyone swore was closed for good before the tide gives up what it took.

## About the author

Ulyana Sandoval spent six years answering phones for a coastal cab company before turning the voices in her headset into fiction. She writes mysteries about people whose jobs begin where the law's paperwork ends.
