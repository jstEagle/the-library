## About the author

Dominic Amberdale is the pen name of a former small-town baker who traded early mornings at the oven for late nights at the keyboard, writing romances about people who rebuild themselves one honest conversation at a time. He lives above a bookshop with his partner and an opinionated cat, and still measures every draft by whether it rises.
