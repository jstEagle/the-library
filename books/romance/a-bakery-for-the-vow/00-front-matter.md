## About the author

Isandro Ishikawa is the pen name of a former pastry cook turned novelist who writes love stories set in kitchens, concert halls, and the spaces between grief and second chances. They live near the sea with a temperamental sourdough starter and an ever-growing stack of unread sheet music.
