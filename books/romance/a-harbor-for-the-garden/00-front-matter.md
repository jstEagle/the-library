# A Harbor for the Garden

**by Edmund Brightwater**

> Noor Ishikawa, a cellist recovering from one catastrophic final performance, swears the heart is a muscle like any other: trainable, containable. But when a lost letter surfaces, postmarked eleven years ago, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Edmund Brightwater is the pen name of a former orchestral librarian who spent a decade cataloguing other people's music before writing his own stories. He lives in a coastal town where the ferry schedule dictates the rhythm of the year, and much of the plot.
