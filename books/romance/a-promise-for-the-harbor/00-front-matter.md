## About the author

Ulyana Joubert spent a decade working seasonal jobs in coastal towns from Nova Scotia to Cornwall, collecting the salt-worn voices that populate her fiction. She writes romances about people who rebuild themselves the way harbors rebuild after storms — slowly, and with a lot of stubborn hope.
