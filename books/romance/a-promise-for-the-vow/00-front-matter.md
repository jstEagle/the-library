## About the author

Dominic Zhou is a former land surveyor who spent a decade mapping coastal properties before turning to fiction, giving his romances an unusual grounding in deeds, boundaries, and the lines people draw. He writes from a converted boathouse and insists every love story is, at bottom, a property dispute between two hearts.
