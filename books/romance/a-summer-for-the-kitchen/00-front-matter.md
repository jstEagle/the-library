## About the author

Ada Crane is the pen name of a former line cook who traded double shifts for double-spaced manuscripts and now writes love stories set in kitchens, bookshops, and the long silences between. She lives in a coastal town much like Marrow Bay, where she still burns the occasional batch of shortbread on purpose.
