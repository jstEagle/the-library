## About the author

Rosalind Ishikawa spent three summers living in a decommissioned lighthouse keeper's cottage on the northern coast, where she collected local storm lore and the letters people never sent. She writes romances about stubborn hearts and salt air from a small studio overlooking a harbor that never quite sleeps.
