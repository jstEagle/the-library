## About the author

Ines Underhill is the pen name of a former lighthouse keeper's assistant who now writes romance from a rented room above a chandlery on the Cornish coast. Their fiction is known for salt-weathered settings, slow-burning tenderness, and characters who learn that safety is a place you build with other people.
