## About the author

Casper Joubert is the pen name of a former seasonal farmhand turned novelist who spent three autumns picking fruit in the Cape winelands before writing full-time. Known for quiet romances about people who mistake control for safety, Joubert lives near an orchard he refuses to own outright.
