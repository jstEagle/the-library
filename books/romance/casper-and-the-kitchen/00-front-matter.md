## About the author

Dominic Nystrom is a former lighthouse tender's assistant who turned to fiction after a decade spent cataloguing maritime letters for a coastal historical society. He writes quiet romances about people who repair themselves the way they repair old things: slowly, honestly, and with salt-stained hands.
