## About the author

Zephyrine Voss is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years restoring water-damaged letters in coastal town libraries, where she became obsessed with what people confess only to strangers. She writes slow-burn romances about small towns, inherited burdens, and the courage of ordinary sentences.
