## About the author

Freya Dravenmoor is a former pastry cook who traded early-morning ovens for late-night manuscripts, writing love stories set in kitchens, coastal towns, and the spaces between almost and always. She lives in a drafty seaside house with a sourdough starter older than her career and a firm belief that every good romance deserves a deadline.
