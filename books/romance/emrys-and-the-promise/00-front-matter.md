## About the author

Zephyrine Joubert is the pen name of a former lighthouse keeper's daughter who spent a decade writing letters to strangers before turning her obsession with correspondence and coastlines into fiction. She lives on a tidal island reachable only at low tide, where she keeps a standing appointment with the last ferry of every season.
