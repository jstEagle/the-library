## About the author

Edmund Thackeray is the pen name of a former orchestral librarian turned novelist who grew up in coastal Norway and now writes from a converted boathouse on the Skagerrak. Known for quietly devastating love stories set against northern landscapes, Thackeray has a devoted readership who describe the books as 'storms you want to be caught in.'
