## About the author

Quentin Lockridge is the pseudonym of a former event coordinator who spent a decade behind the scenes of other people's weddings before turning those observations into fiction. Writing from a coastal town where lighthouses outnumber traffic lights, Lockridge specializes in romances about people who insist they are done with love.
