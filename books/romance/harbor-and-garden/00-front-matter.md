## About the author

Isandro Almeida is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent fifteen years cataloging ship manifests and love letters seized in customs, a job that taught him how often people lie in official documents and tell the truth in margins. He writes from a rented room above a seed shop, where he keeps one stubborn fig tree that refuses to fruit.
