## About the author

Freya Winterbourne is the pen name of a former lighthouse keeper's assistant who spent seven winters on the Maine coast writing letters she never sent. She now lives inland but keeps a postcard of a lighthouse pinned above her desk, and writes love stories about people who arrive too late and stay anyway.
