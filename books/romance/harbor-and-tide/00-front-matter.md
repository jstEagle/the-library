## About the author

Beatrix Sandoval spent a decade as an uncredited ghostwriter for celebrity memoirs before turning her pen toward fiction about people who live in the margins of other people's stories. She writes from a converted fruit-packing shed on the coast, where the tide keeps better time than she does.
