## About the author

Wendell Villalobos is a former sommelier who traded restaurant cellars for a writing desk in wine country, where he now chronicles second chances among the vines. His fiction blends agricultural realism with slow-burn romance, drawing on years of listening to growers argue about weather, money, and regret.
