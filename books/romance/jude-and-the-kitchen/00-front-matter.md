## About the author

Mirabel Eastwick is a former pastry chef who traded early-morning ovens for late-night manuscripts, writing love stories set in coastal towns she can no longer resist visiting. She lives on a windswept peninsula with two rescue dogs and a firm belief that every romance deserves a good bread metaphor.
