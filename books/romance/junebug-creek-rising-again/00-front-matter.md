## About the author

Marguerite Brightwater is the pen name of a former Gulf Coast journalist who covered small-town courtrooms for fifteen years before turning to fiction. She writes romances about people who find love on the far side of hard truths, and she still keeps a battered notebook of overheard sentences from courthouse steps.
