## About the author

O. M. Blackwood is the pen name of a former pastry chef turned novelist who writes stories about people who rebuild their lives in kitchens. Blackwood lives in the Pacific Northwest with a temperamental sourdough starter and a deep suspicion of second chances that never quite holds.
