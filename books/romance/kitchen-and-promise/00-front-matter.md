# Kitchen and Promise

**by Noor Iversen**

## About the author

Noor Iversen is a former pastry chef turned novelist who writes love stories set in kitchens, harbors, and the quiet hours between them. She lives on a windswept island where the ferries keep imperfect time, much like her characters.
