# Lena and the Tide

**by Mara Coldharbour**

> Yara Zhou, a ghostwriter secretly penning a rival's memoirs, swears the heart is a muscle like any other: trainable, containable. But when two estranged names appear on the same impossibly stubborn orchard deed, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must choose between the career that saved everything and the person who sees clearly at last before the bakery lease runs out.
