## About the author

Rosalind Nakamura spent seven years as a ghostwriter before publishing under her own name, an experience that taught her how much of a life can live between someone else's lines. She writes from a converted fishing cottage on the Oregon coast, where the tide charts on her kitchen wall are more decorative than practical.
