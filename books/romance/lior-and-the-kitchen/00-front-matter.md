## About the author

Kestrel Iversen is the pen name of a former line cook who spent six winters on a remote Scottish island before trading her chef's knife for a fountain pen. She writes romance from a converted boathouse, insisting that every good love story needs salt air, bad weather, and someone brave enough to speak first.
