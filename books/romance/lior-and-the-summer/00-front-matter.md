# Lior and the Summer

**by Ines Dravenmoor**

> August Delacroix, a cellist recovering from one catastrophic final performance, has spent twelve years rebuilding a life measured in small safe steps. But when a fake date to survive a family reunion feels too real by dessert, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must let someone stay after the last train leaves before the bakery lease runs out.

## About the author
