## About the author

Hazel Delacroix is the pen name of a former sommelier turned novelist who spent a decade working harvest seasons in coastal vineyard towns before trading tasting notes for love stories. She writes romances about people who mistake control for safety, from a drafty farmhouse office overlooking rows of grapes she probably should be pruning.
