## About the author

Lior Okafor is the pen name of a Nigerian-British writer who spent five years working seasonally in cider orchards across Kent while drafting novels at night. Their fiction explores how people rebuild identity after public failure, and they credit a decades-long correspondence with a stranger as the inspiration for this book.
