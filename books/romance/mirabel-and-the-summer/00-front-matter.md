# Mirabel and the Summer

**by Mirabel Oyelaran**

> Emrys Marchetti, a cellist recovering from one catastrophic final performance, has spent twenty years rebuilding a life measured in small safe steps. Then a snowstorm strands two rivals with the best mistake of their twenties — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must choose between the career that saved everything and the person who sees clearly at last before the last ferry of the season departs.
