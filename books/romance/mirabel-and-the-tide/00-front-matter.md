## About the author

Odile Yarrow is the pen name of a former line cook who spent seven seasons on a Puget Sound island, writing love stories in the margins of order tickets. She now lives one ferry ride from anywhere, where she bakes bread badly and believes every small town keeps at least one unsent letter.
