## About the author

Wendell Ishikawa is a former orchestral librarian who spent a decade backstage before turning to fiction, and his novels are known for their unsentimental portraits of musicians and the institutions that consume them. He lives in a coastal town where he writes in the mornings and repairs string instruments in the afternoons.
