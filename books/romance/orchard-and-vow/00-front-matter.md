## About the author

Ansel Voss is the pen name of a former line cook who spent a decade writing love stories on the backs of order tickets in coastal towns much like Salt Hollow. Known for romances where forgiveness costs something real, Voss lives off-season on a peninsula with a stubborn dog and an even more stubborn sourdough starter.
