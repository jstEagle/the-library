## About the author

Yara Pemberton is a former estate lawyer turned novelist who writes about inheritance, salt air, and second chances from a converted boathouse in Maine. Her debut, 'Oscar and the Harbor,' was inspired by an orchard deed she once probated with two names she never forgot.
