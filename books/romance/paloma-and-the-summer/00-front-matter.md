## About the author

Astrid Halloran is a former maritime archivist who spent seven years cataloguing lighthouse logs and undelivered letters along the Pacific coast before turning to fiction full-time. She writes from a converted boathouse on the Oregon shore, where she keeps a small vineyard that stubbornly refuses to die.
