## About the author

Astrid Nystrom is a former orchestral librarian who spent fifteen years touring with chamber ensembles before turning to fiction. She writes love stories set in the spaces where music, memory, and regret collide, and lives above a bookshop in Bergen with a temperamental cat named Adagio.
