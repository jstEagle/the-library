# Promise and Kitchen

**by Theo Underhill**

> Kestrel Thackeray, a lighthouse keeper's granddaughter and part-time novelist, has spent seven years rebuilding a life measured in small safe steps. But when a snowstorm strands two rivals with the best mistake of their twenties, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must risk the whole heart instead of lending pieces before the last ferry of the season departs.

## About the author

Theo Underhill is the pen name of a former coastal radio archivist who spent a decade collecting storm logs and keeper's diaries from decommissioned lighthouses along the North Atlantic. Promise and Kitchen is their third novel, written in winter, in sight of water.
