## About the author

Mirabel Oyelaran is a former orchestra librarian who spent six summers working ticket booths on a coastal ferry line, collecting the love stories of strangers who missed their boats on purpose. She writes from a converted boathouse, where she keeps a broken metronome on her desk as a reminder that timing is everything.
