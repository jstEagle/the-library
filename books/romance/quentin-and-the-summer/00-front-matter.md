## About the author

Orsen Nystrom is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloging lighthouse logs along the northern coast before turning to fiction. Known for romances steeped in salt air, family secrets, and second chances, Nystrom writes from a restored keeper's cottage with an unreliable view of the sea.
