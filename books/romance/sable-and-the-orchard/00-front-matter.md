## About the author

Coral Thackeray is the pen name of a former small-town newspaper editor who covered zoning disputes and bake sales with equal solemnity before turning to romance. She lives above a bookshop she refuses to name, and writes her first drafts longhand in bakeries that let her stay past closing.
