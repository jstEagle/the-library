# Sebastian and the Summer

**by A. M. Quintana**

> Ines Winterbourne, a cellist recovering from one catastrophic final performance, swears the heart is a muscle like any other: trainable, containable. But when two estranged names appear on the same impossibly stubborn orchard deed, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
