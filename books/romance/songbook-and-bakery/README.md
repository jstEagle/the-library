# Songbook and Bakery

*by Wendell Blackwood*

But when the person behind a decade of letters moves in next door, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must say the sentence that has been rehearsed for a decade before the festival ends.

## About the author

Wendell Blackwood is the pen name of a former small-town bookseller who spent fifteen years recommending other people's love stories before writing one. He lives above a bakery, insists the smell of yeast is the only acceptable alarm clock, and answers letters—always.
