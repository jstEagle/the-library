## About the author

I. M. Halloran is the pen name of a former lighthouse keeper's assistant who wrote love letters for illiterate sailors on a remote Atlantic island. They now live in a converted ferry terminal with a dog named Margin and an unreasonable collection of secondhand hymnals.
