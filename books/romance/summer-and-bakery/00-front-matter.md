# Summer and Bakery

**by Xanthe Kavanagh**

## About the author

Xanthe Kavanagh is a former line cook turned novelist who writes love stories set in kitchens, believing that sugar, like forgiveness, works best under pressure. She lives in a coastal town where she tests every recipe that appears in her books.
