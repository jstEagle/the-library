## About the author

Noor Coldharbour is the pen name of a former coastal researcher who traded tide charts for manuscripts after a decade spent studying sea turtle nesting grounds in Brittany and the Azores. Their fiction explores how people rebuild themselves after institutional betrayal, drawing on years of watching small creatures fight impossible odds.
