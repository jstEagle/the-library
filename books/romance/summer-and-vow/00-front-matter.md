## About the author

Edmund Amberdale is the pen name of a former lighthouse archivist who spent seven years cataloguing correspondence salvaged from coastal stations along the English seaboard. Summer and Vow is a second novel inspired by an unsigned bundle of letters found sealed in a keeper's logbook.
