## About the author

Gideon Vane is the pen name of a former small-town newspaper reporter who spent a decade covering coastal communities and the quiet wars over their main streets. Vane writes slow-burn romances about people who mistake safety for happiness, and bakes an admittedly mediocre sourdough.
