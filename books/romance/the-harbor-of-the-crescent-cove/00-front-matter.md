# The Harbor of the Crescent Cove

**by Petra Eastwick**

> Wren Brightwater, a single parent running the town's only bookshop, has spent three years rebuilding a life measured in small safe steps. But when the anonymous pen-pal assignment pairs enemies instead of strangers, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Petra Eastwick is a former lighthouse keeper's daughter who grew up in coastal towns and writes about the families we build when the ones we're given fall away. She lives above a bookshop she refuses to name, with two cats and an unreasonable number of tide charts.
