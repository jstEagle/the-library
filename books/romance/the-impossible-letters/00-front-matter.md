## About the author

A. M. Ravensworth is the pen name of a former line cook turned novelist who writes love stories set in kitchens, train stations, and other places where people pretend they aren't waiting for someone. They live in a snowy northern city with an aging espresso machine and a firm belief that dessert is a language.
