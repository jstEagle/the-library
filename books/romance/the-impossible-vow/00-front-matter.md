# The Impossible Vow

**by Ivo Brightwater**

But when a lost letter surfaces, postmarked eleven years ago, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Ivo Brightwater grew up in a family of coastal archivists and claims to have learned storytelling from reading other people's unsent letters. The Impossible Vow is their fourth novel and the first written entirely during winter storms.
