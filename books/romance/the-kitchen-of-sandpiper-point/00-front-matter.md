## About the author

Tamsin Quintana spent a decade managing a coastal bookshop in Northern California before turning to writing full-time, where she developed a reputation for warm, food-scented small-town romances. She lives with her partner and an ill-tempered cat named Hemingway, and still judges towns by the quality of their bakeries.
