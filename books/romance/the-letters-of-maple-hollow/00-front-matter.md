**by Ansel Ishikawa**

## About the author

Ansel Ishikawa is the pen name of a former small-town journalist who spent a decade covering courtrooms in wine country before turning to fiction. Their novels examine how ordinary people carry extraordinary guilt, and what it costs to finally set it down.
