## About the author

A. M. Pemberton is the pen name of a former orchestral librarian who spent fifteen years touring with chamber ensembles before trading concert halls for a writing desk overlooking a grey northern sea. Pemberton writes romances about people who missed their cue and are learning, late, to come in on time.
