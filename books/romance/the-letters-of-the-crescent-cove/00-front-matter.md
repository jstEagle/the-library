## About the author

Astrid Dunmore is the pen name of a former orchestral librarian who spent fifteen years touring with chamber ensembles before turning to fiction full-time. She writes love stories set in coastal towns, drawn to characters who must choose between the lives they've carefully built and the ones that terrify them.
