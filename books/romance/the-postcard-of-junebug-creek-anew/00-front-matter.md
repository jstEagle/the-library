## About the author

P. M. Eastwick is the pen name of a former orchestra librarian who spent a decade backstage watching musicians fall in and out of love with their art. Now writing from a porch overlooking a creek not unlike Junebug's, Eastwick crafts romances about second chances and the courage of unguarded hearts.
