# The Promise of Port Alder

**by E. M. Whitlock**

> Rosalind Delacroix, a vineyard owner who inherited debts instead of grapes, believes second chances are for people with better timing. But when a fake date to survive a family reunion feels too real by dessert, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
