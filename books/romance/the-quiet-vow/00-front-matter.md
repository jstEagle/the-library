## About the author

Edmund Ravensworth is the pen name of a former lighthouse keeper's assistant who spent fifteen years writing obituaries for a small coastal newspaper before turning to fiction. He writes romances about people who mistake silence for safety, drawing on the fog-bound towns of his childhood.
