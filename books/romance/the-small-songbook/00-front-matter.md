# The Small Songbook

**by Rosalind Blackwood**

## About the author

Rosalind Blackwood is the pen name of a former orchestral librarian who spent a decade cataloguing other people's music before writing her own stories. She lives on a cold northern coast, where she keeps an untidy garden and an even more untidy notebook of things people confess to strangers on ferries.
