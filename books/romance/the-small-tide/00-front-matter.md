## About the author

Gideon Ravensworth is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing undelivered correspondence from decommissioned British lighthouses. They write slow-burn romances about people who mistake solitude for safety, and live within earshot of the sea they refuse to romanticize.
