## About the author

N. M. Eriksen is the pen name of a former orchestral librarian who spent a decade cataloguing other people's music before writing love stories set where the tide decides the schedule. They live on a coastal island much like the one in this book, where the last ferry of the season is a genuine annual heartbreak.
