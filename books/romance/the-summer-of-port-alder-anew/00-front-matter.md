## About the author

A. M. Nakamura is the pen name of a former tide-pool docent who spent three summers counting sea turtle nests along the Pacific coast before trading field notebooks for fiction. Their romances are known for salt-stung settings, stubborn scientists, and communities that refuse to let anyone heal alone.
