## About the author

Ivo Brightwater is the pen name of a former lighthouse keeper's assistant who spent a decade writing letters for sailors before turning to fiction. Known for storm-swept romances set on northern coasts, Ivo writes from a cottage that leans, like its owner, toward the sea.
