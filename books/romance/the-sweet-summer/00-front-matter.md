## About the author

Rafael Kavanagh is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent five years cataloguing lighthouse keepers' logs along the Atlantic coast before turning to fiction full-time. The Sweet Summer is his fourth novel and the first written from his grandmother's kitchen table overlooking a working light.
