## About the author

Paloma Thackeray spent a decade as a ghostwriter for celebrity memoirs before publishing under her own name, drawing on the strange intimacies of writing in another person's voice. She lives in a coastal town much like Bellbird Bay, where she keeps a wall of unsent letters she swears will one day become a novel.
