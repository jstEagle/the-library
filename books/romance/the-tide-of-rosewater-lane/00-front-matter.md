## About the author

Juniper Vane is the pen name of a former coastal archivist who spent seven years cataloguing lighthouse keeper logs along the Atlantic seaboard. She writes romance for readers who believe love is less a lightning strike than a tide—patient, insistent, and impossible to argue with.
