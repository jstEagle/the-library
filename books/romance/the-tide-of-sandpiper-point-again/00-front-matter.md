## About the author

Bram Sterling is the pen name of a former lighthouse keeper's assistant who now writes coastal romances from a converted bait shop on the Atlantic seaboard. Their novels are known for salt-weathered settings, slow-burning second chances, and heroines who know more about the ocean than about their own hearts.
