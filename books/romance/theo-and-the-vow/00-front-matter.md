## About the author

Lior Vasquez is the pen name of a former field researcher who traded tide charts for typewriters and now writes love stories set where the land meets the sea. They live in a coastal town much like the one in this book, where every summer they volunteer to count hatchlings and pretend it isn't research for their next novel.
