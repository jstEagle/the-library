# Tide and Orchard

**by Gideon Okafor**

> Ada Okafor, a ghostwriter secretly penning a rival's memoirs, believes second chances are for people with better timing. But when the person behind a decade of letters moves in next door, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must risk the whole heart instead of lending pieces before the last ferry of the season departs.

## About the author

Gideon Okafor is a former maritime archivist who spent years cataloguing letters recovered from coastal post offices before turning to fiction. He writes about people who love quietly and too late, and lives within earshot of a ferry terminal.
