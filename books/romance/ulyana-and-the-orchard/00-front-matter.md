## About the author

Gideon Thackeray is the pen name of a former orchard worker turned novelist who spent six seasons grafting apples in the Kentish Weald before writing full-time. Known for quiet, sensory romances about people who mistake discipline for safety, Thackeray lives beside a working orchard tended by a cooperative of neighbors.
