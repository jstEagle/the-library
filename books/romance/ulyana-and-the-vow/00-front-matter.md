## About the author

Rafael Iversen is the pen name of a former literary archivist who spent years cataloguing unsent letters in estate collections before turning to fiction. Iversen writes romances about people who mistake silence for safety, and lives quietly in a coastal town where, reportedly, no one knows their real name either.
