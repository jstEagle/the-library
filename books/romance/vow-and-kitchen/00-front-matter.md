## About the author

Bram Vasquez is the pen name of a former line cook turned novelist who spent six years writing between dinner services before trading the pass for the page. Known for romances steeped in food, family obligation, and salt air, Vasquez lives near the coast with an unreasonable number of cast-iron pans.
