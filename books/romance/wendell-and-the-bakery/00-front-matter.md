## About the author

Ines Brightwater is a former pastry chef who traded pre-dawn ovens for pre-dawn manuscripts and now writes love stories set in small towns that smell like cardamom and rain. She lives in a converted creamery with her partner, a retired orchestra librarian, and a cat named Proof.
