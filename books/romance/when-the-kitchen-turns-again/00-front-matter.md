## About the author

Ulyana Voss is a former pastry chef turned novelist who writes love stories set in kitchens, because that's where she learned that people tell the truth when their hands are busy. She lives above her own café with a dog named Brioche and a firm belief that every ending should smell faintly of burnt sugar.
