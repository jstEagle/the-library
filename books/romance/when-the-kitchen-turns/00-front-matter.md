## About the author

K. M. Okafor is the pen name of a former restaurant line cook turned novelist who writes love stories set in kitchens, ferries, and the spaces between what people mean and what they say. Raised between Lagos and coastal Maine, Okafor believes every grudge is just an unfinished conversation.
