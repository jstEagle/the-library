# When the Songbook Breaks

**by Coral Eriksen**

> Silas Eastwick, a wedding planner who doesn't believe in weddings anymore, has spent seven years rebuilding a life measured in small safe steps. Then the person behind a decade of letters moves in next door — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it.
