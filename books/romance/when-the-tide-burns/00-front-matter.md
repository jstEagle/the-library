## About the author

Gideon Delacroix is the pen name of a former small-town bookseller who spent a decade watching love stories walk through the door before deciding to write them. Known for slow-burn romances laced with moral reckoning, Delacroix writes from a coastal cottage with a rescue dog and an unreasonable number of unread paperbacks.
