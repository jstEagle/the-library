# When the Vow Breaks

**by Kestrel Almeida**

## About the author

Kestrel Almeida is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing other people's undelivered letters before writing her own stories. She lives on a windswept peninsula with her partner, two opinionated cats, and a sourdough starter older than most of her manuscripts.
