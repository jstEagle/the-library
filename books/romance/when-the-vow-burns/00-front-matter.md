## About the author

Quentin Fairweather is the pen name of a former rare-book cataloguer who spent a decade in auction houses before turning to fiction, and who claims to have fallen in love twice: once in a reading room, once in a bindery. Fairweather writes small-town romances about people who mistake caution for character, and lives quietly with a dog named Marginalia.
