## About the author

Kestrel Okonkwo is the pen name of a former archivist who spent years cataloguing the letters of families who never said what they meant. Writing from a small house above a shuttered cinema, Okonkwo crafts romances where love arrives late, on purpose, and costs exactly what it is worth.
