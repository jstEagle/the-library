# Zephyrine and the Tide

**by Ines Lockridge**

> Hazel Ravensworth, a marine biologist counting turtles instead of heartbreaks, has spent twelve years rebuilding a life measured in small safe steps. Then a lost letter surfaces, postmarked eleven years ago — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must choose between the career that saved everything and the person who sees clearly at last before the vineyard's first harvest comes in.

## About the author
