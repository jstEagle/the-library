## About the author

Wendell Dunmore is the pen name of a former agricultural archivist who spent a decade cataloguing heirloom seeds before turning to fiction. Writing from a converted grain silo in rural Manitoba, Dunmore crafts quiet, humane science fiction about people who keep things running after everyone else leaves.
