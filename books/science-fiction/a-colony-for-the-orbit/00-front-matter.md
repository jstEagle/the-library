# A Colony for the Orbit

**by Jude Galloway**

> Theo Eriksen, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, has spent fifteen years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart, and time is already running out.
