**by Wren Fairweather**

## About the author

Wren Fairweather is the pen name of a former orbital logistics analyst who spent six years writing shipping manifests before writing stories about what ships carry. They live somewhere with unreliable gravity and excellent coffee, and write about people who move things they shouldn't.
