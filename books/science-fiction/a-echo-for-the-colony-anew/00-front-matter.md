# A Echo for the Colony Anew

**by Yara Thackeray**

> Coral Reyes, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it before Earth's last transmission loop ends.
