# A Echo for the Colony

**by Rosalind Ishikawa**

> Silas Brightwater, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Rosalind Ishikawa is a former shipping-route dispatcher who spent a decade listening to pilots' logs from the outer colonies before turning to fiction. She writes about language, distance, and the people who carry messages no one asked for.
