# A Echo for the Gravity

**by Casper Yarrow**

> Vesper Ravensworth, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, has spent twelve years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option.
