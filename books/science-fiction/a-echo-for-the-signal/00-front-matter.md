# A Echo for the Signal

**by Sebastian Eastwick**

> August Eastwick, the last human technician on an automated relay station, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Sebastian Eastwick is the pen name of a former radio engineer who spent six years maintaining remote communications posts in the Arctic before turning to fiction. His work explores loneliness, machine memory, and the ethics of systems that outlive their makers.
