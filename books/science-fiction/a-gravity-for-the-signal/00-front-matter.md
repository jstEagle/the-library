# A Gravity for the Signal

**by Zephyrine Oyelaran**

> Ulyana Galloway, the last human technician on an automated relay station, has spent twenty years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Zephyrine Oyelaran is a former orbital logistics dispatcher who turned to fiction after two decades of logging cargo manifests in low Earth orbit. She writes near-future science fiction about the people machines forget, and lives between Lagos and a decommissioned weather station she refuses to name.
