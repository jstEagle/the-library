# A Horizon for the Archive

**by B. M. Yarrow**

> Vesper Vane, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, has spent seven years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

B. M. Yarrow is the pen name of a former merchant-marine radio officer who now writes science fiction from a houseboat on the Baltic coast. Their work explores lawlessness at the edges of empire, where people must invent the justice they cannot petition for.
