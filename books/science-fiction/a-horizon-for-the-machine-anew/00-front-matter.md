# A Horizon for the Machine Anew

**by Yara Lockridge**

> Xanthe Ishikawa, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the fleet's truce expires.
