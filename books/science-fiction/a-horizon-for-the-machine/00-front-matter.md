## About the author

Xanthe Lockridge is a former agricultural archivist who spent a decade cataloguing heirloom seeds before turning to fiction, and her work is haunted by what institutions choose to forget. She writes from a converted grain silo on the northern coast, where she keeps a garden of varieties no one has planted in a century.
