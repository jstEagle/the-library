## About the author

Noor Oyelaran is a former agricultural engineer who spent six years on hydroponic projects in extreme climates before turning to fiction. Their work explores how ordinary people carry extraordinary secrets, and they write from a small coastal town where they keep a stubborn, thriving garden of their own.
