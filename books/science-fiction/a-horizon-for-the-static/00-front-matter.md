## About the author

Ulyana Brightwater is a former radio astronomer who left observatory life to write about the people who listen at the edges of things. She lives aboard a refitted survey vessel with her partner and a temperamental coffee machine, writing novels about duty, distance, and the voices we invent to survive the quiet.
