# A Orbit for the Colony

**by Rosalind Hartwell**

> Rosalind Okonkwo, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option.
