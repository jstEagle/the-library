# A Orbit for the Signal

**by Lior Okonkwo**

> Silas Zhou, a decommissioned war android paying off its debts, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Lior Okonkwo is a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years relaying messages between ships that officially had no reason to speak. They write science fiction about machines that inherit human guilt, from a houseboat that moves between three harbors.
