# A Protocol for the Horizon

**by Tamsin Underhill**

> Odile Montrose, the last human technician on an automated relay station, has spent three years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart before the jump window closes.

## About the author

Tamsin Underhill is a former maritime radio operator who spent six years alone on remote North Atlantic weather stations before turning to fiction. She writes about isolation, machinery, and the strange tenderness between people who only exist to each other as voices.
