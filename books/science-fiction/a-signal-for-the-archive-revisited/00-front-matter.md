## About the author

Dominic Okonkwo is a former orbital logistics analyst who spent six years writing technical manuals for deep-space cargo contracts before turning to fiction. He writes from Lagos and Lisbon, drawing on his Yoruba and Portuguese heritage to explore what migrants carry when they can carry nothing else.
