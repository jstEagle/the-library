# A Signal for the Machine Again

**by M. M. Winterbourne**

> Xanthe Sandoval, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, has spent three years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal, and time is already running out.

## About the author

M. M. Winterbourne is the pen name of a former orbital logistics analyst who spent six years writing shipping manifests before writing stories about the people behind them. They live somewhere with real weather and write about gardens on worlds that don't have any yet.
