# A Signal for the Protocol

**by Freya Thackeray**

> Casper Ashgrove, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before Earth's last transmission loop ends.

## About the author

Freya Thackeray is a former shipping-log archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the paperwork of routes that no longer exist. She writes science fiction about the languages we abandon and the families we assemble from the wreckage.
