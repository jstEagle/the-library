# A Static for the Gravity Anew

**by Ivo Eriksen**

> Wren Nystrom, a clone who inherited the original's murder case, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it.
