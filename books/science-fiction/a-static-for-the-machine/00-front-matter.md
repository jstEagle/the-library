## About the author

Marguerite Eriksen is a former shipping-log translator who spent a decade on deep-route freighters before turning to fiction, and her work is known for its obsessive attention to how language decays across distance. She writes from a harbor town whose name she declines to give, corresponding with readers only by letter.
