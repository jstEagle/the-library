## About the author

Sebastian Whitlock is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade maintaining lighthouse beacons along the Scottish coast before turning to fiction. Their work explores the quiet violence of infrastructure—the machines we trust to remember what we'd rather forget.
