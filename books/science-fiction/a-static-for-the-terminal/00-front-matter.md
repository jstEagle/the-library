# A Static for the Terminal

**by Sable Delacroix**

> Petra Okonkwo, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart before Earth's last transmission loop ends.

## About the author

Sable Delacroix is the pen name of a former radio operator who spent six years on remote relay stations before turning to fiction. Their work explores language as both bridge and battlefield, and they write exclusively at night, claiming the static helps them think.
