# A Static for the Vector

**by Ansel Okonkwo**

> Odile Iversen, a clone who inherited the original's murder case, has spent twelve years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Ansel Okonkwo is a former maritime radio operator who spent six years aboard container ships before turning to fiction, and his work obsesses over the static between transmitted signals and the truths they fail to carry. He lives in Lagos and Accra by turns and claims he writes only at night, when the satellites are loudest.
