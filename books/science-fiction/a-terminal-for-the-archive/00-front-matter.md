# A Terminal for the Archive

**by Ines Ishikawa**

> Noor Almeida, the last human technician on an automated relay station, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Ines Ishikawa is a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade relaying weather and distress calls across the North Pacific before turning to fiction. She writes about the people who keep signals alive long after anyone remembers what they were for.
