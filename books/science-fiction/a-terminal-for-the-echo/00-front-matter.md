# A Terminal for the Echo

**by Odile Delacroix**

> Freya Lockridge, the last human technician on an automated relay station, has spent five years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Odile Delacroix is the pen name of a former radio astronomer who spent a decade listening to dead frequencies before turning to fiction. She writes about lonely people in loud universes from a coastal town where the fog does most of the talking.
