## About the author

E. M. Ravensworth is the pen name of a former merchant-marine radio operator who spent eleven years on deep-space supply runs before turning to fiction. Known for melancholic, machine-eyed science fiction, Ravensworth writes about debt, distance, and the things we carry so others don't have to.
