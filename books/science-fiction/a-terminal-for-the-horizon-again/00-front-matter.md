# A Terminal for the Horizon Again

**by Edmund Ishikawa**

> August Coldharbour, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, has spent five years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise, and time is already running out.
