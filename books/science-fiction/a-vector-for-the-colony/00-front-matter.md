## About the author

Ulyana Whitlock is a former maritime claims adjuster who spent eleven years investigating losses at sea before turning to fiction, a career she credits for her obsession with paperwork as evidence of human folly. She writes from a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage on the Atlantic coast, where she lives with her partner and an ill-tempered rescue cat named Actuary.
