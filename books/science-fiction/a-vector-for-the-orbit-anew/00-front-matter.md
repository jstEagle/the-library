# A Vector for the Orbit Anew

**by Mirabel Coldharbour**

> Juniper Coldharbour, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, has spent five years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Mirabel Coldharbour is the pen name of a former flight dispatcher who spent a decade listening to pilots talk to their ships. She writes from a converted cargo container on the coast, where she insists the tides keep better time than any AI.
