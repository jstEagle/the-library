# A Vector for the Static

**by Paloma Okonkwo**

> Noor Fairweather, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Paloma Okonkwo is a former field linguist who spent six years documenting dying languages across three continents before turning to fiction. She writes about people the record forgot, from a small apartment full of archived cassette tapes.
