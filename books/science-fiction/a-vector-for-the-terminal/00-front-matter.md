## About the author

A. M. Galloway spent eleven years as a marine salvage clerk before turning to fiction, a job that taught her how paperwork remembers what people prefer to forget. She writes from a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage on the Atlantic coast, where she collects defunct shipping manifests.
