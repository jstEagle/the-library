# Ada and the Signal

**by Ines Nystrom**

> Juniper Fairweather, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ines Nystrom is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years relaying messages between ships that rarely answered. They write about language, distance, and the people who carry words across the dark.
