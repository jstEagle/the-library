# Ada and the Static

**by Orsen Eastwick**

> Sable Dunmore, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, has spent three years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option.
