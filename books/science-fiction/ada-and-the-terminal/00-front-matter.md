# Ada and the Terminal

**by Ines Pemberton**

> Gideon Nakamura, a decommissioned war android paying off its debts, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before Earth's last transmission loop ends.
