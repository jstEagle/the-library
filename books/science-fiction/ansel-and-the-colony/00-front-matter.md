# Ansel and the Colony

**by August Blackwood**

> Hazel Sandoval, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise, and time is already running out.
