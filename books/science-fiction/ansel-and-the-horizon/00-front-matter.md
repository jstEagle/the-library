# Ansel and the Horizon

**by Wren Marchetti**

> Ansel Quintana, a clone who inherited the original's murder case, maintains the beacon nobody remembers commissioning. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before Earth's last transmission loop ends.
