# Ansel and the Orbit

**by Theo Dravenmoor**

> Odile Marchetti, a decommissioned war android paying off its debts, has spent ten years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before Earth's last transmission loop ends.
