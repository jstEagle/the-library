## About the author

Mirabel Galloway is a former orbital logistics analyst who spent six years writing technical manuals before turning her hand to fiction. She writes science fiction about people who inherit other people's problems, and lives in a coastal town where she keeps a telescope pointed at traffic she insists is ordinary.
