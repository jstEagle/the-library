## About the author

August Thornbury is a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade relaying messages between ships that never saw each other, an experience that haunts every page of their fiction. They write from a coastal town where the fog rolls in on schedule, and where they insist the best stories are the ones someone paid to have carried quietly.
