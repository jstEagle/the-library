# Archive and Machine

Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Freya Winterbourne is a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years relaying messages between ships that would never meet, an experience she calls 'the loneliest kind of translation.' She writes from a converted lighthouse on the Norwegian coast, where she keeps an archive of extinct languages transcribed by hand.
