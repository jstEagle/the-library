# Astrid and the Echo

**by Tamsin Delacroix**

> Lior Thornbury, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, has spent ten years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep, and time is already running out.

## About the author
