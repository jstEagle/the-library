# Astrid and the Static

**by Lena Joubert**

> Ada Eriksen, the last human technician on an automated relay station, has spent twenty years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Lena Joubert is a former radio engineer who spent six years maintaining communications arrays in the North Atlantic before turning to fiction. She writes about machines that remember too much and people who remember too little, and lives in Tromsø with an aging shortwave receiver she refuses to replace.
