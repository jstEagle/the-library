# August and the Echo

**by Elodie Whitlock**

> Freya Yarrow, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before Earth's last transmission loop ends.

## About the author

Elodie Whitlock is a former field linguist who spent a decade documenting endangered languages across three continents before turning to science fiction. She writes from a converted radio station on the northern coast, where she still keeps one antenna pointed at the sky.
