# August and the Orbit

**by Felix Hartwell**

> Sable Blackwood, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Felix Hartwell is a former hydrologist who spent six years on remote research stations before turning to fiction, and his work is known for its quiet, weather-worn prose and characters who love machines more than people. He writes from a coastal town whose location he declines to confirm, insisting only that the fog is load-bearing.
