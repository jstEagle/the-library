# August and the Signal

**by O. M. Thornbury**

> Rosalind Amberdale, a clone who inherited the original's murder case, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

O. M. Thornbury is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years relaying messages between ships that had lost diplomatic contact. They write about distance, inheritance, and the people who keep signals alive when institutions would rather let them die.
