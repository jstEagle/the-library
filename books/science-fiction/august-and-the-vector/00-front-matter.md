# August and the Vector

**by Juniper Okonkwo**

> Lena Nystrom, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart before Earth's last transmission loop ends.
