# Beatrix and the Gravity

**by Mara Sandoval**

> Xanthe Pemberton, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, has spent seven years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Mara Sandoval is a former maritime radio operator who turned to science fiction after a decade of listening to voices cross empty distances. She writes about people who carry things — cargo, secrets, each other — across hostile space.
