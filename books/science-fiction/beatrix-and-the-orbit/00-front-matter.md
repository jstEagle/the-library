## About the author

Paloma Winterbourne is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent six years maintaining lighthouses off the Hebridean coast before turning to science fiction. Her work explores inherited guilt and the machinery of memory, and she writes all first drafts by hand in tide tables' margins.
