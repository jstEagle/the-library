# Beatrix and the Signal

**by C. M. Joubert**

## About the author

C. M. Joubert is the pen name of a former hydrologist who spent six years working remote environmental monitoring stations before turning to fiction. They write quiet, plant-forward science fiction about people who maintain things no one remembers to thank them for.
