# Bram and the Colony

**by Ines Thornbury**

> Freya Brightwater, the last human technician on an automated relay station, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Ines Thornbury is the pen name of a former radio engineer who spent a decade maintaining remote communications arrays in the subarctic, where silence taught her more about storytelling than any workshop. She writes quiet science fiction about people who keep the lights on at the edge of things.
