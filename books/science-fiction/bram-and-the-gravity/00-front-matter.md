# Bram and the Gravity

**by August Eriksen**

> Mara Amberdale, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
