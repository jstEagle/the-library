# Bram and the Orbit

**by Yara Underhill**

> Noor Reyes, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Yara Underhill is the pen name of a former hydrologist who spent six years on remote research stations in the Arctic and Andes, writing stories about people who keep things alive where nothing should grow. They now live in a coastal town with too many houseplants and one very opinionated cat.
