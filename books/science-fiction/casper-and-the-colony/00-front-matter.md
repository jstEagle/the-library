# Casper and the Colony

**by Coral Reyes**

> Wendell Pemberton, the last human technician on an automated relay station, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Coral Reyes is a former radio operator who spent six years maintaining remote communications arrays in the Andes before turning to fiction. Her work explores isolation, memory, and the ethics of systems that outlive the people they were built to serve.
