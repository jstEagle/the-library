# Casper and the Echo

**by J. M. Thornbury**

> Juniper Halloran, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, maintains the beacon nobody remembers commissioning. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

J. M. Thornbury is the pen name of a former orbital agriculture engineer who spent six years on remote habitat stations before turning to fiction. Their work explores isolation, institutional memory, and the small acts of maintenance that hold civilizations together.
