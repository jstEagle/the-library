# Casper and the Gravity

**by Emrys Eastwick**

> Ines Dunmore, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, has spent three years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Emrys Eastwick is a former shipping-logistics analyst who spent six years writing route reports for orbital freight companies before turning those ledgers into fiction. They write quiet, bureaucratic science fiction about people whose lives are measured in manifests, margins, and things left unsaid.
