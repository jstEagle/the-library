# Casper and the Horizon

**by Lior Hartwell**

> Ulyana Galloway, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Lior Hartwell is a former maritime cartographer who turned to science fiction after a decade charting coastlines that keep disappearing from the maps. They write about people who carry places inside them, and live between two port cities that no longer share a language.
