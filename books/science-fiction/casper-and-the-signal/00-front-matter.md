# Casper and the Signal

**by Wendell Pemberton**

> Sable Eastwick, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Wendell Pemberton is the pen name of a former shipping-route radio operator who spent eleven years listening to languages he never learned to speak. He writes from a decommissioned relay buoy off the coast of Maine, where he claims the static still talks back.
