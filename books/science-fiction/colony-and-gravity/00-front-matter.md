# Colony and Gravity

**by Edmund Zhou**

> Hazel Ravensworth, the last human technician on an automated relay station, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Edmund Zhou is a former orbital systems engineer who spent six years writing maintenance logs aboard a decommissioned communications array before turning to fiction. He writes quiet, claustrophobic science fiction about people who fix things no one notices breaking.
