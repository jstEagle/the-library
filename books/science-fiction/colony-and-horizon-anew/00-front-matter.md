## About the author

Rosalind Ishikawa is a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade on remote stations before turning to fiction, and her work is known for its quiet, salt-worn prose and its fascination with signals sent into the dark. She writes from a coastal town whose location she declines to specify, claiming the ambiguity keeps her honest.
