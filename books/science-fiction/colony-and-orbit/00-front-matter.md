# Colony and Orbit

**by Beatrix Almeida**

> Dominic Thackeray, the last human technician on an automated relay station, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. Then an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it.
