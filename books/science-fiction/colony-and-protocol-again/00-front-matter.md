# Colony and Protocol Again

**by Freya Zhou**

> Vesper Zhou, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. Then a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Freya Zhou is a former botanist who spent three years cataloguing seed banks in decommissioned research stations before turning to fiction. She writes slow, luminous science fiction about people who keep tending things no one asked them to save.
