# Colony and Signal

**by Hazel Reyes**

> Zephyrine Whitlock, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, has spent three years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Hazel Reyes is a former radio operator who spent a decade listening to static before turning to fiction. She writes about the spaces between languages, the families we build in transit, and the signals we can't help answering.
