# Colony and Vector

**by Cyrus Vane**

> Jude Whitlock, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it before Earth's last transmission loop ends.

## About the author

Cyrus Vane is the pen name of a former agricultural engineer who spent six years designing closed-loop habitats in the Atacama Desert before turning to fiction. They write slow-burn science fiction about people who maintain things — gardens, machines, lies — long after anyone remembers why.
