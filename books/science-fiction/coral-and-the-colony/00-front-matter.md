# Coral and the Colony

**by Ada Dravenmoor**

> Felix Winterbourne, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, has spent twelve years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Ada Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former orbital logistics analyst who spent six years writing technical manuals for cryogenic storage systems before turning to fiction. She writes near-future science fiction about people who trade in intangibles — memories, loyalties, time.
