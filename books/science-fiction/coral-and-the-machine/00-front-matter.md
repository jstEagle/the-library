**by Juniper Voss**

> Yara Montrose, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. ## About the author

Juniper Voss is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade transcribing dying oral histories along the Pacific coast. Coral and the Machine is their second novel, following the acclaimed The Salt Archive.
