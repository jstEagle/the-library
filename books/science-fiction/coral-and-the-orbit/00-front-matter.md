# Coral and the Orbit

**by Bram Okafor**

> Yara Nystrom, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Bram Okafor is a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade aboard container ships before turning to fiction. His work blends the loneliness of long-haul routes with an abiding fascination for what people choose to remember.
