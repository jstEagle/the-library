# Cyrus and the Colony

**by Beatrix Brightwater**

> Vesper Lockridge, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the jump window closes.

## About the author

Beatrix Brightwater is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing shipwreck testimony before turning to fiction. She writes science fiction about people history tried to lose, and lives near a lighthouse she refuses to identify.
