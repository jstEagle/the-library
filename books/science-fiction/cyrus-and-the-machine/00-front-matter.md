## About the author

Marguerite Brightwater is the pen name of a former hydrologist who spent a decade designing irrigation systems for marginal farmland before turning to fiction. She writes science fiction about people who maintain things — gardens, machines, fragile peace — and lives in a house full of seed catalogs.
