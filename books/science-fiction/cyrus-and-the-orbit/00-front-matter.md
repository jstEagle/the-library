# Cyrus and the Orbit

**by Silas Reyes**

> Lena Nystrom, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, has spent twenty years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Silas Reyes is a former maritime logistics analyst who spent a decade writing about supply chains before turning to fiction about the people those chains forget. He lives near a decommissioned launch site and writes at night, when the satellites are easiest to see.
