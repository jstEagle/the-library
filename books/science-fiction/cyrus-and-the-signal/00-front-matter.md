# Cyrus and the Signal

**by N. M. Galloway**

## About the author

N. M. Galloway is the pen name of a former hydrologist who spent six years working remote environmental monitoring stations in the Arctic before turning to fiction. Their work explores what survives when institutions fail: soil, memory, and the people who refuse to stop keeping records.
