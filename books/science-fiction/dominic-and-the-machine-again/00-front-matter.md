## About the author

Cyrus Joubert is the pen name of a former shipping-logistics analyst who spent a decade routing cargo between island territories and now writes science fiction about distance, duty, and the people who carry things across gaps. They live near a decommissioned relay station and insist the hum at night is just the wind.
