# Dominic and the Terminal

**by Casper Kavanagh**

> Kestrel Eriksen, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Casper Kavanagh is a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade aboard cargo vessels before turning to fiction, and his work is known for its salt-worn pragmatism and tenderness toward people who live between jurisdictions. He writes from a converted lightship off the coast of Maine.
