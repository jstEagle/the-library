# Echo and Machine Anew

**by Ivo Ishikawa**

> Ulyana Marchetti, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Ivo Ishikawa is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator turned novelist who writes about distance, signal, and the people we invent to survive long hauls. 'Echo and Machine Anew' is their third novel and first science fiction epic.
