# Edmund and the Archive

**by Zephyrine Thornbury**

> Mara Vane, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, maintains the beacon nobody remembers commissioning. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Zephyrine Thornbury is the pen name of a former radio telescope operator who spent six years listening to static before deciding to write what might be saying hello. Edmund and the Archive is their second novel, following the acclaimed The Quiet Frequencies.
