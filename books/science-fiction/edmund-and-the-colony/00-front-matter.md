# Edmund and the Colony

**by Marguerite Whitlock**

> Lior Joubert, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, has spent twelve years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option.
