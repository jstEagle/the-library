## About the author

Beatrix Pemberton is a former maritime insurance adjuster who spent a decade reconstructing disasters from paper trails and wreckage before turning to fiction. She writes from a converted ferry cabin in the North Atlantic, where she remains convinced that most catastrophes are filed as accidents on purpose.
