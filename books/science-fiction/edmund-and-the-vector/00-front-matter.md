# Edmund and the Vector

**by B. M. Coldharbour**

> Theo Delacroix, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. ## About the author

B. M. Coldharbour is the pen name of a former merchant-marine radio operator who spent eleven years listening to conversations between ships that officially never spoke. They write in a converted lighthouse on a coast they refuse to name.
