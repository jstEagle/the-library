# Elodie and the Colony

**by Emrys Sandoval**

> Lena Winterbourne, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Emrys Sandoval is the pen name of a former deep-sea acoustics researcher who spent five years listening to hydrophone recordings no one else wanted to hear. They write quiet science fiction about people who talk to things that cannot answer back.
