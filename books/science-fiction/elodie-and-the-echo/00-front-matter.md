## About the author

Juniper Vane is the pen name of a former hydroponics technician who spent six years aboard orbital research stations before turning to fiction. Their work explores the quiet heroism of maintenance workers, and 'Elodie and the Echo' was drafted during long night shifts watching things grow where nothing should.
