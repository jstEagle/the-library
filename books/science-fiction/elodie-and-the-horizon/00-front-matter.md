## About the author

Ivo Almeida is a former orbital logistics dispatcher who turned to writing science fiction after twenty years of night shifts listening to station chatter. Their work explores the bureaucratic ghosts of humanity's expansion, and 'Elodie and the Horizon' is their third novel.
