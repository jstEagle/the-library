# Emrys and the Gravity

**by Wren Okafor**

> Orsen Ishikawa, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, has spent three years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Wren Okafor is a former maritime radio operator who spent six years aboard supply vessels in the Gulf of Guinea before turning to fiction. Their stories about labor, machines, and the lies we tell to survive have appeared in several genre anthologies.
