# Freya and the Machine

**by O. M. Amberdale**

> Petra Crane, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, maintains the beacon nobody remembers commissioning. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before Earth's last transmission loop ends.

## About the author

O. M. Amberdale is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade alone on remote stations before turning to fiction. They write about isolation, unreliable companions, and the strange tenderness of machines.
