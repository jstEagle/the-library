# Freya and the Protocol

**by Theo Eriksen**

> Paloma Vane, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Theo Eriksen is a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade relaying messages between ships that were legally forbidden to speak to one another. He writes near-future fiction about the people who move information across borders that governments pretend don't exist.
