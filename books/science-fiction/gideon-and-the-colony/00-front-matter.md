# Gideon and the Colony

**by Astrid Nystrom**

> Gideon Oyelaran, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Astrid Nystrom is a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade listening to voices at the edge of transmission range before turning to fiction. She writes from a coastal town in northern Norway, where the long polar night gives her ample time to imagine what gets lost between languages.
