# Gideon and the Orbit

**by Mirabel Dunmore**

> Ada Iversen, the last human technician on an automated relay station, has spent three years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Mirabel Dunmore is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade relaying messages between ships that never saw each other. She writes about lonely people holding fragile lines of connection across vast distances.
