# Gideon and the Terminal

**by Lena Blackwood**

> Marguerite Thackeray, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, has spent seven years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart, and time is already running out.
