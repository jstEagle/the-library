# Gravity and Colony Anew

**by Mirabel Iversen**

> Casper Thackeray, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, has spent three years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it.
