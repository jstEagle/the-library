## About the author

Paloma Winterbourne is the pen name of a former agricultural engineer who spent six years designing closed-loop habitats before turning to fiction full-time. She writes from a converted greenhouse in the Pacific Northwest, where her novels are famously drafted among plants that refuse to die.
