**by Beatrix Dunmore**

Then the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Beatrix Dunmore is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade listening to ships talk to empty water before turning to fiction. She writes science fiction about people who carry other people's secrets, and lives within sight of a harbor she no longer trusts.
