# Gravity and Protocol

**by Ansel Blackwood**

> Lior Pemberton, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it, and time is already running out.
