# Halcyon Drift Rising Anew

**by Ines Eriksen**

> Cyrus Sandoval, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Ines Eriksen is a former maritime radio operator who spent nine years on remote stations where silence was part of the job description. She writes science fiction about people who keep records no one asked them to keep.
