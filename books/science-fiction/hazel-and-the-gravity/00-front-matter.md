## About the author

W. M. Voss is the pen name of a former maritime insurance adjuster who spent eleven years investigating 'impossible' losses at sea before turning to fiction. They write from a converted lightship off the Atlantic coast, where they maintain a small automated beacon as a hobby and, they insist, not an obsession.
