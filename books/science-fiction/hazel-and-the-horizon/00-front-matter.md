# Hazel and the Horizon

**by Marguerite Crane**

> Lior Halloran, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, has spent twenty years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Marguerite Crane is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who now writes science fiction from a coastal town that floods every spring. Their work explores how people build justice when the institutions around them have already been sold.
