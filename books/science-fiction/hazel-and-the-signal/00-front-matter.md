# Hazel and the Signal

**by Hazel Ravensworth**

> Beatrix Montrose, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it, and time is already running out.
