# Hazel and the Terminal

**by Paloma Underhill**

> August Winterbourne, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, has spent ten years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal, and time is already running out.
