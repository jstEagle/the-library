# Hazel and the Vector

**by Orsen Delacroix**

> Beatrix Winterbourne, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before the reactor finishes its countdown.
