## About the author

Juniper Lockridge is the pen name of a former radio technician who spent a decade maintaining long-range relay stations in remote northern latitudes, where silence was a daily companion. Their fiction explores the ethics of machines that love us imperfectly, and has appeared in several small-press anthologies.
