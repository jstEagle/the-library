# Horizon and Machine

**by C. M. Galloway**

> Hazel Montrose, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

C. M. Galloway is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years relaying messages between ships that rarely answered. They write about people who keep languages alive the way sailors keep lights burning.
