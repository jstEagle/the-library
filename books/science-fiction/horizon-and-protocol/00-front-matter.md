## About the author

Sable Halloran is the pen name of a former maritime claims adjuster who spent eleven years investigating losses at sea before turning to fiction. Writing from a coastal town that appears on no tourist maps, Halloran specializes in stories about paperwork, survival, and the lies communities tell to stay alive.
