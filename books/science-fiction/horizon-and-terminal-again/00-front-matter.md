# Horizon and Terminal Again

**by Freya Vane**

> Jude Reyes, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. ## About the author

Freya Vane is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent six years aboard supply vessels before turning to fiction. She writes near-future science fiction about people who keep systems running long after anyone remembers why.
