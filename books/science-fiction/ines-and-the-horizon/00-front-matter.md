# Ines and the Horizon

**by Rafael Quintana**

> Kestrel Ravensworth, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, has spent fifteen years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before the jump window closes.
