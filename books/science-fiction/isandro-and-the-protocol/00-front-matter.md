## About the author

Orsen Whitlock is the pen name of a former maritime claims adjuster who spent eleven years adjudicating losses at sea before turning to fiction. Writing from a houseboat off the Azores, Whitlock is known for procedurally precise science fiction about ordinary people caught in extraordinary ledgers.
