# Ivo and the Machine

**by Tamsin Yarrow**

> Marguerite Thornbury, a clone who inherited the original's murder case, has spent fifteen years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart before the reactor finishes its countdown.
