# Ivo and the Signal

**by Wren Vasquez**

> Jonah Blackwood, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, has spent ten years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before the reactor finishes its countdown.
