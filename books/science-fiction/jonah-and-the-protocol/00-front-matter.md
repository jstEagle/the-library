## About the author

Gideon Nystrom is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years maintaining automated lighthouse beacons along a shrinking coastline. Their fiction explores what machines remember after people forget, and has appeared in several small-press anthologies of quiet science fiction.
