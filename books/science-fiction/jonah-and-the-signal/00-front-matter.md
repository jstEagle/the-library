# Jonah and the Signal

**by Mara Delacroix**

> Jonah Eastwick, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before Earth's last transmission loop ends.

## About the author

Mara Delacroix is the pen name of a former radio astronomer who spent six years at a remote listening station in the Atacama before turning to fiction. Her work explores what gets transmitted, what gets lost, and who decides which voices survive the static.
