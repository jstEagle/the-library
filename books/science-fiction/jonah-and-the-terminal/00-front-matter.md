# Jonah and the Terminal

**by Zephyrine Lockridge**

> Rosalind Iversen, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
