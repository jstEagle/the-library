# Jude and the Archive

**by F. M. Dunmore**

> August Quintana, a decommissioned war android paying off its debts, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

F. M. Dunmore is a former maritime radio operator turned science fiction writer who lives aboard a converted trawler off the coast of Maine. Their work explores machines, memory, and the families people build when institutions fail them.
