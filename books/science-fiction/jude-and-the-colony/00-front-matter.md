## About the author

Freya Voss is the pen name of a former radio astronomer who spent six years at a remote listening post in the Atacama before turning to fiction. Her novels explore isolation, memory, and the machinery of forgetting, and she writes her first drafts by hand in notebooks she refuses to digitize.
