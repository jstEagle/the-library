# Jude and the Orbit

**by Ada Joubert**

> Ines Eastwick, the last human technician on an automated relay station, has spent fifteen years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before the jump window closes.
