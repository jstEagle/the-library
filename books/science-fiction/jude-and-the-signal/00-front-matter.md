# Jude and the Signal

**by Tamsin Reyes**

> Isandro Eastwick, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, has spent twenty years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author
