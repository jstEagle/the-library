# Juniper and the Gravity

**by Astrid Fairweather**

> Elodie Reyes, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Astrid Fairweather is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the personal effects of people who died intestate. She writes science fiction about paper trails, inheritance, and the bureaucratic violence of forgetting.
