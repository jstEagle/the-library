# Juniper and the Machine

**by Mara Yarrow**

> Ines Vasquez, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. Then gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before Earth's last transmission loop ends.
