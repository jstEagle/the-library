## About the author

Beatrix Vasquez is a former radio engineer who spent six years maintaining remote communications infrastructure in the Atacama Desert before turning to fiction full-time. Her work explores loneliness, machine intimacy, and the stubborn persistence of truth, and 'Juniper and the Signal' is her third novel.
