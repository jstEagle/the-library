# Kepler Reach Rising

**by Sable Kavanagh**

> Yara Winterbourne, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. ## About the author

Sable Kavanagh is a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the records of abandoned settlements before turning to fiction. They write about memory, erasure, and the people history prefers to forget, from a coastal town that has changed its name three times.
