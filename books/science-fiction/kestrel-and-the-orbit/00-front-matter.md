# Kestrel and the Orbit

**by Elodie Oyelaran**

> Marguerite Hartwell, a clone who inherited the original's murder case, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author
