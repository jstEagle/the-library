# Lena and the Colony

**by Gideon Vasquez**

> August Amberdale, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. ## About the author

Gideon Vasquez is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years relaying messages between ships that never met. He writes about distance, duty, and the voices we trust across the dark.
