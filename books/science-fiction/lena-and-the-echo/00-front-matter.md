# Lena and the Echo

**by Ada Sandoval**

> Emrys Coldharbour, a decommissioned war android paying off its debts, has spent ten years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before Earth's last transmission loop ends.
