# Lena and the Signal

**by L. M. Joubert**

> Lior Reyes, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart, and time is already running out.

## About the author

L. M. Joubert is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years relaying messages between ships that rarely answered. They write about language, distance, and the families people build in transit.
