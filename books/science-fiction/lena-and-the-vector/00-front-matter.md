# Lena and the Vector

**by Sable Amberdale**

> Coral Thornbury, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, has spent seven years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Sable Amberdale is the pen name of a former orbital logistics analyst who spent a decade writing technical manuals for life-support systems before turning to fiction. They live in a coastal town where they keep an improbable greenhouse and insist that every good story is, at heart, about who gets to wake up.
