# Lior and the Colony

**by Isandro Nystrom**

> Lena Ravensworth, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Isandro Nystrom is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years relaying messages between ships that rarely answered. They write about distance, translation, and the people who keep talking into silence.
