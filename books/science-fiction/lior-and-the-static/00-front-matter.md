# Lior and the Static

**by Gideon Ashgrove**

> Ines Hartwell, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep, and time is already running out.
