# Machine and Archive

**by Dominic Whitlock**

> Orsen Underhill, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, has spent ten years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Dominic Whitlock is the pen name of a former merchant marine radio officer who spent eleven years on deep-space supply runs before turning to fiction. He writes about people who keep systems running in places no one wants to look.
