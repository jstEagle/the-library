## About the author

Cyrus Eastwick is the pen name of a former merchant-marine radio officer who spent eleven years on deep-space relay contracts before turning to fiction. Their work explores debt, labor, and belonging at the edges of inhabited space, and they write from a decommissioned tugboat moored off the Outer Hebrides.
