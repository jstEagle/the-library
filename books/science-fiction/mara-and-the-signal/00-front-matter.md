# Mara and the Signal

**by Isandro Iversen**

> Odile Underhill, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, has spent twelve years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Isandro Iversen is a former maritime radio operator who now writes science fiction from a converted lighthouse on the Jutland coast. Their work explores how machines and misfits negotiate belonging in systems built to discard them.
