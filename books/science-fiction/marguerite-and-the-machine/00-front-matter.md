# Marguerite and the Machine

**by R. M. Yarrow**

> Wendell Winterbourne, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before the jump window closes.

## About the author

R. M. Yarrow is the pen name of a former maritime cartographer who turned to science fiction after a decade mapping coastlines that kept disappearing. Their work explores archives, grief, and what survives when institutions decide nothing should.
