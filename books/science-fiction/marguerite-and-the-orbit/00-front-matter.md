## About the author

Silas Winterbourne is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years relaying messages between ships that never met, and who now writes about people separated by distances only language can cross. He lives on a cold coast, keeps a shortwave rig tuned to dead frequencies, and claims his best ideas arrive as dreams he can no longer translate.
