# Mirabel and the Horizon

**by Oscar Okafor**

> Beatrix Fairweather, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Oscar Okafor is a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years on cargo routes between West African ports before turning to fiction. He writes about distance, translation, and the people who keep lonely signals alive.
