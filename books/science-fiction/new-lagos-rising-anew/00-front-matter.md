# New Lagos Rising Anew

**by Casper Marchetti**

> Emrys Galloway, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Casper Marchetti is the pen name of a former shipping-route dispatcher who spent eleven years writing letters for crews too superstitious to sign their own names. New Lagos Rising Anew is their second novel, written aboard cargo vessels between the Canary Islands and Recife.
