## About the author

Xanthe Dunmore is the pen name of a former marine systems engineer who spent six years writing technical manuals for orbital freight companies before turning to fiction. They live between Lisbon and a houseboat in Lagos, where they collect defunct satellite parts and stories about machines that outlive their makers.
