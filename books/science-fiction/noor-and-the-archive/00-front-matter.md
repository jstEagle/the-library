# Noor and the Archive

**by Noor Okafor**

> Xanthe Ravensworth, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option.
