## About the author

Coral Lockridge is a former maritime radio operator who spent six years aboard container vessels before turning to fiction, and she credits long night watches for her obsession with isolated crews and unreliable machines. She writes science fiction about ordinary workers caught in extraordinary systems, and lives quietly somewhere along the Atlantic coast.
