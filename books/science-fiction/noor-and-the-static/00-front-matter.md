# Noor and the Static

**by Ivo Montrose**

> Emrys Coldharbour, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Ivo Montrose is the pen name of a former orbital-mechanics engineer who left the industry after a contract dispute and now writes from a converted lighthouse on the Atlantic coast. Their fiction blends hard-science rigor with a stubborn belief that mercy is a form of engineering.
