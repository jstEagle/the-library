# Noor and the Terminal

**by Wren Brightwater**

> Zephyrine Thackeray, the last human technician on an automated relay station, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Wren Brightwater is a former radio engineer who spent six years maintaining remote Arctic listening posts before turning to fiction. Their work explores loneliness at the edge of infrastructure and the strange tenderness between people and the machines they keep alive.
