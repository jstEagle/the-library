# Odile and the Gravity

**by Mara Eriksen**

> Kestrel Thackeray, a clone who inherited the original's murder case, has spent twelve years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the fleet's truce expires.
