## About the author

Ada Oyelaran is a former orbital logistics analyst who turned to science fiction after a decade spent watching cargo manifests tell truer stories than the news did. She writes from a converted shipping container in Lagos, where she insists the best android characters are the ones who refuse to be metaphors.
