# Orbit and Signal Again

**by Ines Underhill**

> Marguerite Hartwell, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, has spent twelve years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Ines Underhill is a former maritime radio operator who spent six years on North Atlantic freight runs before turning to fiction. She writes about people who talk to machines for a living, and lives in a coastal town where the fog comes in on schedule.
