# Orbit and Signal

**by Elodie Nystrom**

> Cyrus Whitlock, the last human technician on an automated relay station, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Elodie Nystrom is a former radio engineer who spent a decade calibrating deep-space communication arrays before turning to fiction. She writes about lonely signals, stubborn machines, and the people who refuse to stop listening.
