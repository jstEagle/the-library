# Orsen and the Echo

**by Cyrus Ravensworth**

> Tamsin Ishikawa, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Cyrus Ravensworth is the pen name of a former shipping-route radio operator who spent eleven years listening to languages he couldn't understand cross the dark between stations. He writes about people who translate for a living and pay for it in sleep.
