# Orsen and the Machine

**by Ines Nakamura**

> Wren Brightwater, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ines Nakamura is a former orbital logistics analyst who wrote her first novel during a three-year posting on a decommissioned relay station. She lives between cities and writes about people who live between worlds.
