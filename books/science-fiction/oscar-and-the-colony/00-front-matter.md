# Oscar and the Colony

**by Mara Winterbourne**

> Lena Fairweather, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, has spent fifteen years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Mara Winterbourne is a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade listening to voices crossing empty oceans before turning to fiction about signals crossing emptier ones. She writes from a coastal town where the fog rolls in on schedule, like clockwork nobody winds.
