# Paloma and the Horizon

**by Jude Ravensworth**

> Casper Blackwood, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Jude Ravensworth is the pen name of a former radio astronomer who left observatory life to write about the people machines talk to. They live near a decommissioned tracking station and insist the static there still says things.
