# Paloma and the Orbit Again

**by Ansel Winterbourne**

> Marguerite Winterbourne, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, has spent twelve years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ansel Winterbourne is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade relaying messages between ships that distrusted each other. They write about language, distance, and the lies that keep people alive.
