# Paloma and the Orbit

**by Juniper Voss**

> Paloma Delacroix, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, has spent twelve years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Juniper Voss is the pen name of a former orbital logistics analyst who spent six years writing shipping manifests for stations she refuses to name. She writes about borders, cargo, and the people history loses between the lines.
