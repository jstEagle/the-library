# Paloma and the Static

**by Emrys Fairweather**

> Silas Iversen, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, maintains the beacon nobody remembers commissioning. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Emrys Fairweather is the pen name of a former radio engineer who spent a decade maintaining remote transmission stations across the northern archipelagos. Their fiction explores what machines remember that we choose to forget, and has appeared in several small-press anthologies of quiet science fiction.
