## About the author

Tamsin Sandoval is a former maritime insurance adjuster who spent a decade investigating losses at sea before turning to fiction about records that lie. She writes from a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage on the northern coast, where she maintains, on principle, a light nobody asked her to keep.
