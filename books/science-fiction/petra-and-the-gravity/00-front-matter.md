## About the author

Ivo Ishikawa is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent six years maintaining automated navigation beacons in the North Pacific before turning to fiction. Their work explores loneliness as a form of competence, and they write from a converted signal station on Hokkaido's coast.
