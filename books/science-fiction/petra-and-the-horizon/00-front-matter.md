# Petra and the Horizon

**by Odile Joubert**

> Bram Marchetti, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Odile Joubert is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent nine years relaying messages between ships that never met. She writes science fiction about people who keep records no one asked for.
