## About the author

Casper Montrose is the pen name of a former satellite ground-station technician who spent six years maintaining automated relays across the South Pacific before turning to fiction. Their work explores loneliness at machine scale, and they write from a converted shipping container office overlooking a decommissioned antenna array.
