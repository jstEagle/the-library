# Protocol and Terminal

**by Ivo Nakamura**

> Xanthe Winterbourne, a clone who inherited the original's murder case, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Ivo Nakamura is a former systems auditor who spent six years documenting abandoned industrial sites across three continents before turning to fiction. Protocol and Terminal is their fourth novel and the first written after a residency aboard a decommissioned orbital research station.
