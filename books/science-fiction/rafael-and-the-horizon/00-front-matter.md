# Rafael and the Horizon

**by Zephyrine Nystrom**

> Yara Amberdale, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. Then the colony's children start sharing the same dream — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Zephyrine Nystrom is the pen name of a former field linguist who spent six years documenting endangered languages across three continents before turning to fiction. They write about memory, translation, and the stories institutions would prefer forgotten.
