# Rosalind and the Gravity

**by Xanthe Okonkwo**

> Odile Kavanagh, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, maintains the beacon nobody remembers commissioning. ## About the author

Xanthe Okonkwo is the pen name of a former radio astronomer who left academia after a decade of listening to signals nobody claimed to have sent. Their fiction has appeared in three languages and one very confused anthology of maritime poetry.
