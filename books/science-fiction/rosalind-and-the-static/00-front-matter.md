# Rosalind and the Static

**by Lior Vane**

> Elodie Okonkwo, a decommissioned war android paying off its debts, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
