# Sable and the Protocol

**by Beatrix Oyelaran**

> Casper Villalobos, an insurance investigator of impossible accidents, has spent seven years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. Now they must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Beatrix Oyelaran is a former maritime insurance adjuster turned science fiction writer, drawing on years of auditing disasters that paperwork insisted were impossible. She writes from a houseboat in Lagos, where she collects dead languages and unreliable narrators.
