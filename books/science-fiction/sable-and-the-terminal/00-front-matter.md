# Sable and the Terminal

**by Rosalind Villalobos**

> Rafael Dunmore, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it, and time is already running out.
