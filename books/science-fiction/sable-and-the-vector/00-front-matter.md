# Sable and the Vector

**by Vesper Okafor**

> Silas Lockridge, a decommissioned war android paying off its debts, has spent seven years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Vesper Okafor is a former orbital logistics analyst who spent six years writing maintenance reports for machines she swears were listening. Sable and the Vector is her third novel and the first written entirely aboard a decommissioned freighter.
