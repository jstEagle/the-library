## About the author

Ivo Nystrom is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing obsolete agricultural data in Nordic seed banks before turning to fiction. Writing from a converted granary on the Baltic coast, Nystrom specializes in quiet, claustrophobic science fiction about people left behind by grand projects.
