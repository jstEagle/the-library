# Signal and Echo

**by Dominic Ashgrove**

> Rafael Halloran, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Dominic Ashgrove is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade relaying messages between ships that rarely answered. Signal and Echo is their second novel, written in harbor towns across three continents.
