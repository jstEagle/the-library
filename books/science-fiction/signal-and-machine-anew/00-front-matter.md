# Signal and Machine Anew

**by Ada Hartwell**

> Paloma Brightwater, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, has spent fifteen years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ada Hartwell is the pen name of a former radio engineer who spent a decade maintaining deep-space relay arrays before turning to fiction. Her work explores what survives when memory, identity, and distance are stretched to breaking.
