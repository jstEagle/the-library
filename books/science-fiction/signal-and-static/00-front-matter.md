# Signal and Static

**by Hazel Eastwick**

> Sebastian Ravensworth, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal, and time is already running out.
