## About the author

Paloma Lockridge is a former radio archivist who spent a decade restoring dead broadcasts from the early spaceflight era, an obsession that bleeds into every story she tells. She writes from a converted lighthouse on the northern coast, where she claims the fog helps her hear what her characters are trying not to say.
