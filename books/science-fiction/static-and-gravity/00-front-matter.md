# Static and Gravity

**by Marguerite Vasquez**

> Hazel Nystrom, the caretaker of Earth's abandoned seed vault, has spent seven years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Marguerite Vasquez is a former radio engineer from the Chilean coast who writes science fiction about people who keep machines alive long after institutions abandon them. Her work has appeared in several small-press anthologies exploring labor, memory, and justice at the edges of empire.
