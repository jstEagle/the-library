# Static and Signal

**by Isandro Reyes**

> Casper Pemberton, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Isandro Reyes is a former radio technician who spent six years maintaining communications towers in remote mountain ranges before turning to fiction. He writes about isolation, inherited obligations, and the strange intimacy of voices carried across great distances.
