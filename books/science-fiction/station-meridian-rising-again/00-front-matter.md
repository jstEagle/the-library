## About the author

Marguerite Oyelaran spent a decade as a maritime radio operator before turning to fiction, and her prose carries the salt-static of voices half-lost to interference. She writes from a coastal town whose name she declines to confirm, on the theory that every good story needs one unverified coordinate.
