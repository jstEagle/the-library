# Station Meridian Rising Anew

**by Astrid Hartwell**

> Bram Galloway, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. ## About the author

Astrid Hartwell is the pen name of a former radio archivist who spent a decade cataloguing dying languages for a public broadcaster before turning to fiction. She writes from a coastal town where she keeps a collection of obsolete recording equipment, all of it still functional.
