## About the author

Ada Winterbourne is the pen name of a former orbital logistics analyst who spent a decade writing technical manuals before turning to fiction. She lives in a coastal town where she keeps an ambitious, mostly failing greenhouse and writes about people who fix things that were designed to be unfixable.
