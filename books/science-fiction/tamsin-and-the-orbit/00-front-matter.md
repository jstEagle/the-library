# Tamsin and the Orbit

**by Marguerite Eastwick**

> Silas Whitlock, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, has spent fifteen years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it.
