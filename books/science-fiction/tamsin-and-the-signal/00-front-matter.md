# Tamsin and the Signal

**by A. M. Blackwood**

> Paloma Vasquez, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it.
