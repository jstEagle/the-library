# Tamsin and the Static

**by Felix Dunmore**

> Juniper Iversen, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before Earth's last transmission loop ends.

## About the author

Felix Dunmore is the pen name of a former radio engineer who spent a decade maintaining transmission relays in remote northern stations before turning to fiction. Their work explores what machines remember that we choose to forget.
