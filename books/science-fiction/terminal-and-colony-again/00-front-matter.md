# Terminal and Colony Again

**by Coral Dunmore**

Coral Dunmore is a former maritime radio operator who turned to science fiction after a decade of listening to voices crossing oceans that didn't want to hear each other. She writes from a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage, where she claims the fog does half the editing.
