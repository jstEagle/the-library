## About the author

Isandro Marchetti is the pen name of a former maritime insurance adjuster who spent eleven years investigating losses at sea before turning to fiction. Writing from a coastal town in southern Italy, Marchetti builds stories where paperwork hides ghosts and the truth is always a matter of who kept the records.
