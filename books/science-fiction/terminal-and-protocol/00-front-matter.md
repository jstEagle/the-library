# Terminal and Protocol

**by Xanthe Thackeray**

> Beatrix Dunmore, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, has spent seven years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before the reactor finishes its countdown.
