**by Gideon Underhill**

> Jude Sandoval, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option.
