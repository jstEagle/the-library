# Terminal and Vector

**by Jonah Sandoval**

> Paloma Blackwood, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, has spent seven years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before the jump window closes.

## About the author

Jonah Sandoval is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade listening to voices cross empty water before turning to fiction about voices crossing emptier space. They write from a coastal town where the fog comes in on schedule, like a jump window.
