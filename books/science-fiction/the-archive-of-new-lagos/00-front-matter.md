## About the author

Vesper Blackwood is the pen name of a former marine insurance adjuster who spent seven years documenting failed settlements along the West African orbital corridor before turning to fiction. They write slow-burn science fiction about bureaucracy, memory, and the paperwork of apocalypse from a houseboat in Lagos Lagoon.
