## About the author

Juniper Dravenmoor is the pen name of a former hydrologist who spent a decade on remote research stations before turning to fiction, and whose work is known for quiet characters caught inside vast, indifferent systems. She writes at high altitude, keeps a failing garden as a discipline, and claims her plots are planned the way water is routed: patiently, and always toward the lowest ground.
