# The Archive of the Kuiper Line Anew

## About the author

H. M. Yarrow is the pen name of a former orbital-mechanics instructor who writes near-future fiction from a converted lighthouse on the Atlantic coast. Their work obsesses over what survives us when we are copied, and whether survival is the same thing as living.
