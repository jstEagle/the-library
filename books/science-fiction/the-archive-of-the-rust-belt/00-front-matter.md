## About the author

Petra Lockridge is the pen name of a former industrial archivist from Youngstown, Ohio, whose family worked three generations in steel before the mills closed. She writes science fiction about places the future abandoned, drawing on rust-belt oral histories collected from her grandmother's union hall.
