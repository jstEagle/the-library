# The Bright Gravity

**by Rosalind Almeida**

> Kestrel Okonkwo, the caretaker of Earth's abandoned seed vault, has spent twelve years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Rosalind Almeida is a former botanist who spent six years cataloguing heirloom seeds in West Africa before turning to fiction. She writes science fiction about inheritance, both biological and moral, from a small farm she shares with her sister's family.
