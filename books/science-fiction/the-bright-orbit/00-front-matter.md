# The Bright Orbit

**by Xanthe Ravensworth**

> Freya Thackeray, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, has spent fifteen years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option.
