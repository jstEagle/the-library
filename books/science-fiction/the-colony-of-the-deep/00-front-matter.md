## About the author

Lena Montrose is the pen name of a former marine acoustics researcher who spent three years stationed in the Antarctic before turning to fiction. Her novels explore what survives of identity when memory can be copied, and she writes from a coastal cabin where the fog does most of her worldbuilding.
