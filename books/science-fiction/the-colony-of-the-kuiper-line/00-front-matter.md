## About the author

Oscar Montrose spent nine years as a marine salvage clerk in the North Sea before turning to fiction, a career he describes as 'reading other people's disasters for a living.' He writes from a converted lighthouse on the Scottish coast, where he insists the isolation improves both his prose and his paranoia.
