## About the author

A. M. Okafor is the pen name of a former hydrologist who spent six years working on closed-loop agricultural systems in extreme environments before turning to fiction. Their writing explores how ordinary people bear extraordinary moral weight, drawing on their fascination with ecosystems that persist where nothing should survive.
