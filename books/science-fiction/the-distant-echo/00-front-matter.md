# The Distant Echo

**by Isandro Joubert**

> Vesper Okafor, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, has spent seven years hauling cargo through contested orbits. ## About the author

Isandro Joubert is the pen name of a former maritime archivist who spent a decade cataloguing disputed colonial records before turning to fiction. Writing from a coastal town that has changed names three times, Joubert writes stories about who gets to keep the past.
