# The Distant Orbit

**by Vesper Coldharbour**

> Mirabel Reyes, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, has spent seven years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Vesper Coldharbour is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who now writes science fiction from a converted lighthouse on the Atlantic coast. Their work explores grief, machine memory, and the quiet heroism of people who were never supposed to matter.
