# The Distant Signal Anew

**by B. M. Joubert**

> Zephyrine Almeida, the last human technician on an automated relay station, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise, and time is already running out.

## About the author

B. M. Joubert is the pen name of a former radio engineer who spent a decade maintaining remote transmission arrays across three continents before turning to fiction. They write quiet, melancholy science fiction about people who keep the lights on at the edge of things.
