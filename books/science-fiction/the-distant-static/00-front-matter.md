# The Distant Static

**by Coral Montrose**

> Marguerite Blackwood, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, has spent seven years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before the jump window closes.

## About the author

Coral Montrose is a former maritime radio operator who turned to science fiction after a decade of listening to voices cross empty water. She writes about machines that remember too much and people who remember too little, from a coastal town where the fog never quite lifts.
