## About the author

Petra Dunmore is a former maritime radio operator who spent nine years relaying signals between ships that never saw each other, an experience that shaped her obsession with voices across distance. She writes from a converted lighthouse on the Norwegian coast, where she insists the fog is 'good for plot.'
