## About the author

Juniper Coldharbour is the pen name of a former hydrologist who spent three years working on closed-loop life-support experiments in the Atacama Desert before turning to fiction. Their work explores loneliness, ecological grief, and the quiet heroism of maintenance workers in futures that forgot them.
