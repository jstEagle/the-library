## About the author

Dominic Eriksen is the pen name of a former logistics analyst who spent a decade writing technical manuals for orbital freight contracts before turning to fiction. Drawing on that world of debt-bonded labor and contested shipping lanes, Eriksen writes science fiction about machines and people trying to buy their way free.
