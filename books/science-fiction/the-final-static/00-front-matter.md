## About the author

Cyrus Amberdale is the pen name of a former radio astronomer turned novelist who writes about the loneliness of long-distance signals and the people who listen to them. Their work has appeared in several genre anthologies, and they live off-grid with a shortwave receiver and too many unfinished translations.
