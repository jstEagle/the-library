# The Hollow Static

**by Yara Joubert**

> Freya Thornbury, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. Then gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. ## About the author

Yara Joubert is the pen name of a former radio archivist who spent a decade cataloguing oral histories from displaced communities before turning to fiction. They write science fiction about what survives when institutions decide what deserves to be remembered.
