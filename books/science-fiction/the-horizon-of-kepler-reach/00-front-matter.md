## About the author

Ulyana Underhill is a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade relaying messages between ships that never saw each other, an experience that haunts her fiction of lonely signals and borrowed voices. She writes from a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage, where she claims the fog does half her editing.
