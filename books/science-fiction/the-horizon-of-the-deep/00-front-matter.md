# The Horizon of the Deep

**by Sable Crane**

> August Crane, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before Earth's last transmission loop ends.

## About the author

Sable Crane is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade listening to voices fade across open water before turning to fiction. They write about distance, machines that love imperfectly, and the people who refuse to let the past be edited.
