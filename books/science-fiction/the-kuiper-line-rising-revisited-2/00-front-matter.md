## About the author

Mirabel Thornbury is the pen name of a former logistics analyst who spent six years scheduling cargo runs across remote northern routes, an experience that shapes her fiction's obsession with distances, timetables, and the people who live between destinations. She writes science fiction about quiet workers caught in loud histories, and lives somewhere with unreliable internet on purpose.
