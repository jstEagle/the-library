# The Last Colony Anew

**by Wren Joubert**

> Ulyana Blackwood, a decommissioned war android paying off its debts, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. ## About the author

Wren Joubert is a former maritime radio operator who turned to science fiction after a decade spent transcribing ships' logs that no one else would read. They live off-grid on a decommissioned ferry and write about machines, memory, and the people systems forget.
