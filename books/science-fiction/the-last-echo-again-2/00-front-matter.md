## About the author

Rosalind Hartwell is a former maritime insurance adjuster from Bristol who turned to science fiction after a career spent investigating losses no one wanted to explain. Her novels explore the paperwork of catastrophe — the forms, beacons, and omissions that outlive the people who filed them.
