# The Last Echo Again

**by Felix Delacroix**

> Xanthe Voss, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Felix Delacroix is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years relaying traffic between isolated stations before turning to fiction. They write about distance, translation, and the people who keep talking when no one answers.
