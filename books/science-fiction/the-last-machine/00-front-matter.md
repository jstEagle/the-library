# The Last Machine

**by Mara Ravensworth**

> Xanthe Brightwater, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Mara Ravensworth is the pen name of a former agricultural engineer who spent six years designing closed-loop habitats before turning to fiction. She writes science fiction about people who maintain things—gardens, machines, silences—and what it costs when those things finally break.
