# The Last Protocol Anew

**by Mirabel Marchetti**

> Casper Iversen, a clone who inherited the original's murder case, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Mirabel Marchetti is a former maritime radio operator who turned to science fiction after a decade of logging transmissions no one answered. She writes about people who carry messages across distances that were never meant to be crossed.
