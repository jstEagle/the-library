# The Last Signal Again

**by Mirabel Nystrom**

> Xanthe Ravensworth, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Mirabel Nystrom is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years relaying messages between ships that rarely answered. She writes about distance, translation, and the people who keep talking into the dark.
