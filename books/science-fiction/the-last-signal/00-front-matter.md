# The Last Signal

**by W. M. Lockridge**

> Coral Thackeray, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. Then an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before the jump window closes.

## About the author

W. M. Lockridge is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator turned novelist who writes about isolation, transmission, and the people history forgets. They live off-grid on a coastal island, where they claim the best stories arrive as static.
