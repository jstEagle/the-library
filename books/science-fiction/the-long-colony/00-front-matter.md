# The Long Colony

**by I. M. Oyelaran**

> Ada Almeida, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart before the jump window closes.
