# The Long Horizon

**by P. M. Zhou**

> Vesper Kavanagh, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. Then gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart before the reactor finishes its countdown.
