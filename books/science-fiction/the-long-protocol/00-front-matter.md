# The Long Protocol

**by Astrid Delacroix**

> Zephyrine Coldharbour, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, has spent ten years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before the jump window closes.

## About the author

Astrid Delacroix is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade listening to frequencies no one answered, and now writes about the people who do. They live in a coastal town where the fog comes in like weather from another story.
