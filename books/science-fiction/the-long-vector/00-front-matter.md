# The Long Vector

**by Isandro Whitlock**

> Mirabel Nystrom, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Isandro Whitlock is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade relaying messages between ships that had lost diplomatic contact. They write about people who carry other people's truths across distances no one else will cross.
