## About the author

Petra Sterling is the pen name of a former orbital logistics analyst who spent six years writing technical manuals for deep-space cargo routes before turning to fiction. She writes near-future science fiction about people the galactic economy forgets, and lives with two cats and a telescope she mostly uses to watch shipping lanes.
