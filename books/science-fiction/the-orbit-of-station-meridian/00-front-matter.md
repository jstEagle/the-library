**by Lena Coldharbour**

## About the author

Lena Coldharbour is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade aboard supply vessels before turning to fiction. She writes about people who live in the gaps between jurisdictions, where law thins out and conscience has to do the work.
