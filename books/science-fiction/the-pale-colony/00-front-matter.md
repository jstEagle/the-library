**by Quentin Thornbury**

## About the author

Quentin Thornbury is the pen name of a former maritime claims adjuster who spent eleven years investigating losses at sea before turning to fiction. He writes slow-burning science fiction about paperwork, memory, and the violence hidden inside bureaucratic language.
