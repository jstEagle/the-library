# The Pale Gravity

**by Orsen Amberdale**

> Jude Okonkwo, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Orsen Amberdale is the pen name of a former deep-sea acoustics technician who spent six years recording whale song off the Hebrides before turning to fiction. They write slow-burning science fiction about people who listen when everyone else has stopped.
