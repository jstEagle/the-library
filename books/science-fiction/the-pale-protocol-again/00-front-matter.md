# The Pale Protocol Again

**by Elodie Marchetti**

> Yara Pemberton, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, has spent seven years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the jump window closes.

## About the author

Elodie Marchetti is a former radio astronomer turned novelist who writes about language, memory, and machines that love imperfectly. She lives aboard a decommissioned research vessel in the Baltic, where she teaches her cat extinct words.
