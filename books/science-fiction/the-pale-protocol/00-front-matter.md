**by Gideon Galloway**

## About the author

Gideon Galloway is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent six years stationed on remote North Atlantic platforms before turning to fiction. Their work explores isolation, machinery, and the strange tenderness that grows between people and the systems that keep them alive.
