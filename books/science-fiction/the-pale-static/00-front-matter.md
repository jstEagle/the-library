# The Pale Static

**by R. M. Sterling**

> Sable Galloway, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, maintains the beacon nobody remembers commissioning. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

R. M. Sterling is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade listening to frequencies no one else claimed before turning to fiction. They write about people who keep machines running for ghosts, and live off-grid with a collection of decommissioned lighthouse lamps.
