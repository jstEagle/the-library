# The Pale Terminal

**by Gideon Blackwood**

> Ansel Galloway, a decommissioned war android paying off its debts, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it before Earth's last transmission loop ends.

## About the author

Gideon Blackwood is the pen name of a former orbital logistics analyst who spent six years writing technical documentation for decommissioned deep-space relay stations before turning to fiction. Their work explores the dignity of obsolete machines and the people who refuse to abandon them.
