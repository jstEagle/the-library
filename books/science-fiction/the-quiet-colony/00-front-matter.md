# The Quiet Colony

**by Odile Ravensworth**

> Lena Dunmore, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, has spent twenty years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep, and time is already running out.
