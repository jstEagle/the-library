# The Quiet Gravity Again

**by Wendell Zhou**

> Beatrix Crane, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Wendell Zhou is a former radio astronomer turned novelist who writes about people living in the margins of vast systems. He lives aboard a decommissioned ferry off the coast of British Columbia with his partner and an unreasonable number of tide charts.
