# The Quiet Machine

**by Sebastian Iversen**

But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Sebastian Iversen is a former maritime radio operator from Tromsø who spent a decade aboard Arctic supply vessels before turning to fiction. His work explores how people carry their pasts like cargo, and whether any of us can truly go off the record.
