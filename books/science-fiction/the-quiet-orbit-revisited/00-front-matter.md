## About the author

Dominic Okafor is a former maritime logistics analyst from Lagos who turned to science fiction after a decade spent tracking cargo through real-world contested waters. His novels are known for their blue-collar futurism, morally tangled protagonists, and quiet devotion to the families people build when institutions fail them.
