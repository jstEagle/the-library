# The Quiet Signal

**by Cyrus Pemberton**

> Hazel Sterling, a decommissioned war android paying off its debts, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Cyrus Pemberton is the pen name of a former orbital logistics analyst who spent a decade writing technical histories of failed colonial ventures before turning to fiction. He lives in a coastal town where he restores antique radio equipment and insists every story is, at bottom, about who gets remembered.
