# The Signal of the Kuiper Line

**by Wendell Lockridge**

> Bram Thackeray, a decommissioned war android paying off its debts, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart before Earth's last transmission loop ends.

## About the author

Wendell Lockridge is a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years relaying messages between isolated Arctic stations before turning to fiction. He writes about distance, duty, and the voices we keep alive across impossible gaps.
