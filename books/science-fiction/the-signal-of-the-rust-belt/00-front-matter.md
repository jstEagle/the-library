# The Signal of the Rust Belt

**by Freya Okafor**

> Hazel Crane, the last human technician on an automated relay station, has spent three years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before Earth's last transmission loop ends.
