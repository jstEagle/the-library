# The Slow Archive

**by Beatrix Marchetti**

> Sable Yarrow, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, has spent three years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before Earth's last transmission loop ends.

## About the author

Beatrix Marchetti is a former botanical archivist who spent a decade cataloguing heirloom seeds before turning to fiction. She writes slow-burning science fiction about people who tend things — gardens, records, grudges — longer than anyone expects to matter.
