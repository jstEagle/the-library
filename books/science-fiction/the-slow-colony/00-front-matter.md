# The Slow Colony

**by Freya Halloran**

> Jude Ishikawa, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. ## About the author

Freya Halloran is a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade listening to voices crossing empty water before turning to fiction about voices crossing empty space. She writes in a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage on the Atlantic coast.
