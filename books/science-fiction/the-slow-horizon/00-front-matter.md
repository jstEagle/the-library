# The Slow Horizon

**by O. M. Lockridge**

> Emrys Pemberton, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, has spent fifteen years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise, and time is already running out.

## About the author

O. M. Lockridge is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent nine years relaying traffic between ships that officially never spoke. They write near-future fiction about people whose jobs depend on records being wrong.
