# The Slow Static

**by U. M. Thornbury**

> Ivo Iversen, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, has spent ten years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

U. M. Thornbury is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent six years aboard container ships before turning to fiction. They write about people who live in the gaps between systems, and their short work has appeared in several small-press anthologies.
