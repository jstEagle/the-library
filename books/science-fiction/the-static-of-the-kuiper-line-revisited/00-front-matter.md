## About the author

Wendell Oyelaran is a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade relaying messages between ships that would never meet, a career he describes as 'narrating other people's arrivals.' He writes near-future fiction about people who survive by becoming someone else, and lives off-grid with a shortwave rig he refuses to explain.
