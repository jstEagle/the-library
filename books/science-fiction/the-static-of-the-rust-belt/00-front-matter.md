**by Dominic Nakamura**

But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Dominic Nakamura is a former Great Lakes shipping logistics analyst who turned to science fiction after a decade of tracking cargo through systems that never quite worked as advertised. He writes from a converted grain elevator in Toledo, where he insists the rust is load-bearing.
