## About the author

Elodie Whitlock is a former hydroponics technician who traded greenhouse shifts for writing science fiction about the people who maintain the future. She lives on a coastal island where she grows heirloom tomatoes and insists the sea sounds different at 3 a.m., which is when most of her books get written.
