# The Terminal of the Rust Belt

**by Ansel Brightwater**

> Edmund Dravenmoor, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, maintains the beacon nobody remembers commissioning. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Ansel Brightwater is the pen name of a former shipping clerk who spent eleven years in Great Lakes freight yards before turning to science fiction. Their work dwells on rust, radio, and the people who maintain machines everyone else has forgotten.
