## About the author

Rafael Delacroix is a former radio astronomer who spent six years at a remote listening post in the Atacama before turning to fiction, and his work is known for quiet, technical melancholy and characters who talk to machines. He lives in Lisbon, where he repairs antique clocks and insists he is not writing about them.
