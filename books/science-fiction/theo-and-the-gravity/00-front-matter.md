# Theo and the Gravity

**by Hazel Underhill**

> Quentin Sterling, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Hazel Underhill is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade listening to voices across impossible distances before turning to fiction. She writes quiet, character-driven science fiction about people who translate between worlds that would rather not meet.
