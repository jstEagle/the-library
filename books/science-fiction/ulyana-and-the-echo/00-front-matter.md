## About the author

Emrys Villalobos is a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years relaying messages between ships that had lost diplomatic contact, an experience that shapes every silence in their fiction. They write from a houseboat on the Tagus, where they collect broken clocks that still keep perfect time.
