# Ulyana and the Machine

**by Cyrus Marchetti**

> Vesper Thornbury, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Cyrus Marchetti is the pen name of a former shipping-route logistics analyst who spent eleven years writing dispatches from orbital freight terminals. Their fiction explores the quiet mathematics of people who belong to schedules more than places.
