# Ulyana and the Terminal

**by Ulyana Coldharbour**

> Ulyana Nystrom, a decommissioned war android paying off its debts, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. ## About the author

Ulyana Coldharbour is the pen name of a former orbital logistics dispatcher who spent eleven years routing cargo between colonies that had stopped answering each other's calls. They write about machines that outlive their wars and the quiet stubbornness of things left running.
