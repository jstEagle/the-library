# Vector and Archive Anew

**by Astrid Thackeray**

> Casper Voss, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, has spent ten years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Astrid Thackeray is the pen name of a former maritime logistics analyst who traded shipping manifests for starship ones in her fiction. She writes about people caught between the records kept about them and the lives they actually live.
