## About the author

Jonah Thackeray is a former merchant marine radio officer who spent a decade on deep-space supply runs before turning to fiction, and his work draws on the strange loneliness of long-haul crews and the paperwork of distant bureaucracies. He lives near a decommissioned tracking station and writes at night, when the satellites are loudest.
