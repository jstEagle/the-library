# Wendell and the Terminal

**by Elodie Eriksen**

> Lior Voss, the last human technician on an automated relay station, has spent three years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. Now he must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Elodie Eriksen is a former maritime radio operator from the Faroe Islands who writes science fiction about people who keep distant machines alive. Her work has appeared in several small-press anthologies exploring isolation, labor, and the ethics of inherited systems.
