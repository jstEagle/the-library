# When the Archive Falls Again

**by Rafael Halloran**

> Petra Joubert, the last human technician on an automated relay station, has spent five years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds — and she is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, she must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before Earth's last transmission loop ends.

## About the author

Rafael Halloran is the pen name of a former orbital logistics analyst who spent six years writing technical manuals before turning to fiction. Halloran writes near-future science fiction about people who keep machines running and machines that keep people running, and lives somewhere with poor internet on purpose.
