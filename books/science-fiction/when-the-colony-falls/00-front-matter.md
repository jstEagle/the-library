# When the Colony Falls

**by Tamsin Winterbourne**

> Yara Nystrom, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, has spent twenty years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then the colony's children start sharing the same dream — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Tamsin Winterbourne is a former hydrologist who spent six years on remote research stations before turning to fiction, and her work is known for treating ecology as memory made visible. She writes from a coastal town where she keeps a greenhouse of plants grown from seeds salvaged from decommissioned space-program archives.
