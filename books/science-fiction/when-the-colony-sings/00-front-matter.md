# When the Colony Sings

**by S. M. Vane**

> Rafael Brightwater, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before Earth's last transmission loop ends.
