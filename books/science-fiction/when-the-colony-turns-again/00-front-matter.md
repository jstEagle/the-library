# When the Colony Turns Again

**by Lior Eriksen**

> Ansel Kavanagh, a decommissioned war android paying off its debts, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when the colony's children start sharing the same dream, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal before Earth's last transmission loop ends.
