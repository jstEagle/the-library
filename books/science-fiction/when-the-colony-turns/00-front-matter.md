## About the author

Noor Villalobos is a former maritime radio operator who spent nine years relaying messages between ships that rarely saw each other, an experience that shapes her fiction's obsession with distance and duty. She writes from a converted lighthouse keeper's cottage, where she claims the fog does half her plotting.
