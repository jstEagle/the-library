## About the author

Mirabel Villalobos is a former maritime insurance adjuster who spent eleven years adjudicating claims for vessels lost in contested waters before turning to fiction. She writes from a houseboat off the coast of Veracruz, where she keeps a logbook of every ship that never came home.
