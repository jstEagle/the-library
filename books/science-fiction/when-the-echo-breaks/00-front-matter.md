# When the Echo Breaks

**by Mara Crane**

> Kestrel Nystrom, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, has spent twenty years hauling cargo through contested orbits. ## About the author

Mara Crane is the pen name of a former orbital logistics analyst who spent six years writing technical documentation for satellite relay systems before turning to fiction. She writes science fiction about people who fix things, and lives in a coastal town where the fog rolls in on a schedule as reliable as any satellite.
