# When the Echo Turns

**by Lior Yarrow**

> Dominic Marchetti, the last human technician on an automated relay station, has spent five years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before Earth's last transmission loop ends.

## About the author

Lior Yarrow is the pen name of a former radio engineer who spent six years maintaining remote communications arrays in the South Pacific before turning to fiction. Their work explores inherited silence—what families refuse to say and what machines remember anyway.
