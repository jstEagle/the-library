# When the Echo Wakes Again

**by Ivo Reyes**

> Hazel Galloway, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must deliver a message that will end the war — or start it, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Ivo Reyes is the pen name of a former field linguist who spent a decade documenting dying languages before turning to science fiction. Their work explores how memory survives in speech, and what it costs to be the last one listening.
