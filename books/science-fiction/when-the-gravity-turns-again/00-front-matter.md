# When the Gravity Turns Again

**by Petra Vasquez**

## About the author

Petra Vasquez is a former agricultural archivist who spent six years cataloguing heirloom seeds before turning to fiction full-time. She writes from a converted greenhouse in coastal Oregon, where she grows the same crops her characters preserve.
