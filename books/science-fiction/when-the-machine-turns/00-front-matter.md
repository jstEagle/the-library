## About the author

Isandro Amberdale is the pen name of a former orbital systems engineer who spent six years writing technical documentation for life-support arrays before turning to fiction. They write slow-burning science fiction about people who maintain things — beacons, gardens, lies — long after everyone else has stopped caring whether those things are true.
