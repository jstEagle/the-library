# When the Orbit Breaks Again

**by M. M. Delacroix**

> Lior Sandoval, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, has spent fifteen years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before Earth's last transmission loop ends.
