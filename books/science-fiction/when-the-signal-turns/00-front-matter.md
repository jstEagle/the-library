**by Lena Vane**

## About the author

Lena Vane is a former radio engineer who spent six years maintaining deep-space relay arrays before turning to fiction full-time. She writes science fiction about machines, memory, and the quiet violence of institutions, and lives somewhere with very good coffee and very bad weather.
