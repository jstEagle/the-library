# When the Static Falls

**by Freya Winterbourne**

> Petra Underhill, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. Then gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Freya Winterbourne is the pen name of a former radio archivist who spent a decade cataloguing the last broadcasts of abandoned research stations before turning to fiction. She writes science fiction about people history tried to lose, from a coastal town where the fog never quite lifts.
