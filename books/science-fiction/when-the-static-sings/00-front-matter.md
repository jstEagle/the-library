# When the Static Sings

**by Tamsin Okonkwo**

> Rosalind Thackeray, the caretaker of Earth's abandoned seed vault, has spent five years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds — and he is pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, he must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the fleet's truce expires.

## About the author

Tamsin Okonkwo is a former radio astronomer from Lagos who left academia after a decade of listening to signals no one else believed were real. She writes slow-burning science fiction about people who keep things running while history happens elsewhere.
