# When the Terminal Turns Again

## About the author

Emrys Yarrow is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent eleven years maintaining lighthouses off the northern coast before turning to fiction. Their work explores machines that outlive their masters and the quiet ethics of people nobody is watching.
