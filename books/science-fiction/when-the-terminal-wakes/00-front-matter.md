# When the Terminal Wakes

**by Orsen Zhou**

> Orsen Brightwater, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, has spent fifteen years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the jump window closes.

## About the author

Orsen Zhou is a former maritime radio operator turned science fiction writer who lives aboard a converted tugboat in Puget Sound with two cats and an unreliable autopilot he insists is plotting against him. His fiction explores machines that love imperfectly and people who survive anyway.
