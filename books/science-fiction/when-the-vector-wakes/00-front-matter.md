# When the Vector Wakes

**by Coral Hartwell**

> Noor Voss, a xenolinguist who can no longer dream in human languages, has spent ten years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. Now they must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Coral Hartwell is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who spent a decade listening to voices at the edge of signal range before turning to fiction. They write about translation, grief, and the families people build in the gaps between languages, and live somewhere with unreliable internet by design.
