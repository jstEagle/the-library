# Wren and the Colony

**by Freya Brightwater**

> Gideon Vasquez, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, maintains the beacon nobody remembers commissioning. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Freya Brightwater is a former botanist who spent three years cataloguing seed banks in remote research stations before turning to fiction full-time. She writes science fiction about solitude, stewardship, and the quiet heroism of people systems forget.
