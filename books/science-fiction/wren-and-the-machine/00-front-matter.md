# Wren and the Machine

**by Mara Iversen**

> Vesper Halloran, a memory-smuggler running contraband past orbital customs, has spent twelve years hauling cargo through contested orbits. Then a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward — and they are pulled straight into the middle of it. To get through this, they must keep a lie alive that holds two fleets apart before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Mara Iversen is a former maritime radio operator who spent six years on North Sea freighters before turning to fiction. She writes science fiction about people who carry things — cargo, secrets, other people's pasts — across dangerous distances.
