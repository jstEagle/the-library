**by Astrid Vasquez**

> Lena Whitlock, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when the station AI begins confessing to crimes nobody reported, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Astrid Vasquez is a former flight dispatcher turned novelist who grew up listening to air-traffic chatter through her bedroom wall and never stopped wondering what the machines were saying to each other. She writes from a converted hangar in the high desert, where she keeps a telescope and an unreasonable number of flight manuals.
