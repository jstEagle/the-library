# Wren and the Signal

**by Beatrix Joubert**

> Lena Joubert, a starship navigator haunted by the backups sleeping in the hold, has spent twenty years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when an unfamiliar name shows up on the manifest of a ship that never flew, they discover that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, they must prove a massacre happened when every record says otherwise before Earth's last transmission loop ends.

## About the author

Beatrix Joubert is the pen name of a former maritime radio operator who now writes science fiction from a converted lighthouse on the Atlantic coast. Their work explores how ordinary people carry extraordinary histories in silence.
