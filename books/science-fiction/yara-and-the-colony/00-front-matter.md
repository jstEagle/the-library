# Yara and the Colony

**by Mirabel Sterling**

> Freya Dunmore, the caretaker of Earth's abandoned seed vault, records the slow death of a colony everyone else forgot. But when a ship returns from deep space with everyone aboard aged backward, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must decide which half of humanity wakes from cryosleep before the reactor finishes its countdown.

## About the author

Mirabel Sterling is a former glaciologist who spent three winters at remote Arctic research stations before turning to fiction. She writes quiet, character-driven science fiction about people who keep the lights on at the edge of the world.
