# Yara and the Echo

**by Edmund Galloway**

> Hazel Eastwick, a pilot bonded to an AI that lies, has spent ten years hauling cargo through contested orbits. But when gravity flickers across the entire system for exactly nine seconds, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Edmund Galloway is the pen name of a former orbital-mechanics tutor who spent six years writing technical manuals for satellite firms before turning to fiction. He lives near a decommissioned tracking station and writes about people who talk to machines and regret it.
