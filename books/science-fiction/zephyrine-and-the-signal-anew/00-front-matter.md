# Zephyrine and the Signal Anew

**by Yara Lockridge**

> Mirabel Okafor, a terraformer tending a garden meant for people not yet born, runs the long route between worlds that no longer speak. But when a distress signal arrives in a language extinct for two centuries, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now she must reach the relay at the edge of dead space, racing the signal, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Yara Lockridge is a former radio astronomer turned novelist who writes about the quiet people who keep the far edges of civilization running. She lives in a converted signal station on the coast, where she still logs transmissions from ships that may or may not be real.
