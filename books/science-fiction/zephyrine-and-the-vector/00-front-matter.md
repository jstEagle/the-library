**by Ivo Thackeray**

## About the author

Ivo Thackeray is the pen name of a former merchant-marine radio officer who spent six years on deep-sea freighters before turning to fiction. He writes science fiction about people who keep secrets for a living, from a harbor town whose fog he insists is load-bearing.
