# Border and Protocol

**by Mara Amberdale**

> Lena Marchetti, an interpreter attached to a peace delegation destined to fail, has spent fifteen years following rules that keep getting rewritten. But when a burner phone that shouldn't exist starts ringing with a familiar voice on the line, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author

Mara Amberdale is the pen name of a former NGO field coordinator who spent a decade working along contested borders in Eastern Europe. She writes thrillers about the quiet people who translate between worlds that would rather destroy each other.
