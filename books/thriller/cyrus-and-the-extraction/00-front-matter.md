## About the author

Vesper Winterbourne is the pen name of a former transport-industry consultant who spent a decade inside the systems she now writes about, publishing under a name she invented so her family could read her work safely. She lives between Lisbon and Reykjavik, writing thrillers about ordinary people trapped inside extraordinary machinery of state.
