## About the author

Tamsin Yarrow spent a decade auditing shipping manifests in port cities before turning to fiction, and her thrillers are known for procedural precision wrapped around fiercely human hearts. She writes from a rented flat above a defunct travel agency, where she keeps a wall of unsolved flight numbers.
