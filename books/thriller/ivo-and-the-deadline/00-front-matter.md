## About the author

Rosalind Yarrow spent a decade in diplomatic support roles across three continents before turning to fiction, an experience that taught her how much truth hides inside polite translation. She writes from a coastal town she refuses to name, where the fog rolls in on schedule and the phones, thankfully, stay quiet.
