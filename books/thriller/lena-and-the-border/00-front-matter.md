## About the author

Rosalind Okonkwo is a former logistics coordinator for humanitarian missions who spent a decade crossing checkpoints in West Africa and Eastern Europe before turning to fiction. She writes thrillers about people whose loyalties are tested by geography, drawing on the border towns she knows too well.
