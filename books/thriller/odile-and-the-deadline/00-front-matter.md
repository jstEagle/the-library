## About the author

Ansel Oyelaran is a former compliance auditor who left the banking world after a case he still won't name, and now writes thrillers from a coastal town he moves between seasonally. His work is known for forensic detail about money, moral ambiguity, and protagonists who are never quite who they claim to be.
