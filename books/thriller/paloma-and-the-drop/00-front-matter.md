## About the author

Bram Kavanagh spent eleven years as a contract translator for diplomatic missions across Eastern Europe before turning to fiction, a career he describes as 'learning what people say when they mean something else.' He writes from a coastal town in Portugal and refuses interviews conducted in any language he speaks fluently.
