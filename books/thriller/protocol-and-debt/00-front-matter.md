# Protocol and Debt

**by M. M. Eriksen**

> August Nystrom, an interpreter attached to a peace delegation destined to fail, has spent twelve years following rules that keep getting rewritten. ## About the author

M. M. Eriksen is the pen name of a former conference interpreter who spent a decade working behind glass booths in Geneva, Brussels, and The Hague before turning to fiction. They write thrillers about the people history forgets—the translators, clerks, and couriers who hold secrets no one asked them to carry.
