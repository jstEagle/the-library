## About the author

Mara Halloran spent a decade working behind glass in conference booths across four continents before turning to fiction, and her thrillers are prized for their insider texture of diplomacy's back rooms. She writes under a pen name because, as she puts it, some stories can only be told by someone with nothing left to lose.
