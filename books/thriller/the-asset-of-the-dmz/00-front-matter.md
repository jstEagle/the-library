## About the author

Isandro Okonkwo is a former logistics coordinator for humanitarian convoys who spent a decade along contested borders before turning to fiction. He writes thrillers about people the system discards, drawing on the border towns where he learned that loyalty is a currency and everyone's exchange rate is different.
