## About the author

Coral Reyes spent a decade working port-side jobs along the Mediterranean before turning to fiction, drawing thrillers from the half-heard conversations of harbors at night. She lives between Marseille and Lisbon and writes under a pen name because, as she says, some stories are safer told by no one.
