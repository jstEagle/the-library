## About the author

Rosalind Okonkwo is a former conference interpreter who spent fifteen years working behind glass booths in Geneva, Vienna, and Central Asia before turning to fiction. She writes thrillers about the people history forgets — the translators, fixers, and witnesses standing just off-camera when power decides whose truth gets spoken.
