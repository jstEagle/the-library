## About the author

Gideon Nakamura is a former crisis-line counselor who spent six years listening to strangers talk themselves back from ledges before turning those voices into fiction. He writes thrillers about people whose jobs require them to be calm while everything burns, and he lives quietly somewhere with unreliable weather.
