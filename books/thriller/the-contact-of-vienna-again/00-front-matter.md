## About the author

Hazel Voss is the pen name of a former archivist who spent a decade cataloguing sealed institutional files before walking away with nothing but her notes. She writes thrillers about people who keep records the powerful would rather burn, and she lives, deliberately unfindably, somewhere along the Adriatic coast.
