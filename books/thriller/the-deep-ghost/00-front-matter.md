## About the author

Dominic Eriksen is the pen name of a former logistics contractor who spent a decade shuttling between diplomatic annexes in Central Europe before turning to fiction. He writes thrillers about people whose paperwork matters more than their names, from a farmhouse outside Ljubljana.
