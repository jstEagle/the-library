## About the author

Beatrix Almeida is a former crisis-line counselor who spent six years listening to people talk themselves back from the edge before turning those voices into fiction. She writes thrillers about the machinery of institutions and the people who break inside them, and lives in Lisbon with an unreasonable number of clocks.
