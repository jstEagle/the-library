## About the author

Elodie Hartwell is a former maritime insurance investigator who spent a decade documenting accidents in ports from Rotterdam to Singapore before turning to fiction. She writes thrillers about people who guard things worth stealing, drawing on her obsession with the gray economies of free ports.
