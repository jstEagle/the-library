## About the author

Oscar Nystrom is a former logistics consultant who spent a decade moving between border towns and writing in transit, a habit that shaped his obsession with checkpoints, identities, and the paperwork of disappearing. He lives under a name that may or may not be his own and publishes thrillers about people whose training outlives their employers.
