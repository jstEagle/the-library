## About the author

Juniper Yarrow is the pen name of a former logistics analyst who once audited shipping manifests for a defense contractor and now writes thrillers from a coastal town she refuses to name. Her novels are known for procedural precision, morally compromised protagonists, and endings that cost their heroes something real.
