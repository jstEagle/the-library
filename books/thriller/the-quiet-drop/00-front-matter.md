## About the author

F. M. Ishikawa is the pen name of a former risk consultant who spent a decade advising firms on information security before turning to fiction full-time. Writing from a coastal town they refuse to name, Ishikawa specializes in paranoid thrillers about ordinary people trapped inside systems that know too much.
