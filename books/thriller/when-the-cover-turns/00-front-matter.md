**by Freya Eastwick**

## About the author

Freya Eastwick is a former airline operations analyst who spent a decade inside airport control rooms before turning to fiction full-time. She writes thrillers from a farmhouse in Cornwall, where she interviews pilots, investigators, and air traffic controllers for her research.
