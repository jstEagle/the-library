## About the author

Paloma Sandoval is a former crisis-line counselor who spent six years listening to strangers talk themselves back from ledges before turning to fiction. She writes thrillers about people who keep other people alive for a living, and lives in Lisbon with two rescue dogs who have never once followed her instructions.
