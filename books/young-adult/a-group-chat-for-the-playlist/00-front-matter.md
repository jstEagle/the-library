## About the author

Hazel Ravensworth grew up in a small river town where every summer ended with a community concert on the courthouse steps, an experience that shaped her love of stories about place and inheritance. She writes young adult fiction from a cluttered desk overlooking a train line, where she collects old recipe boxes and the secrets tucked inside them.
