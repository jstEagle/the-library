## About the author

Edmund Ishikawa is a former pastry chef who spent a decade working in his family's bakery in a small Pacific Northwest town before turning to writing full-time. His stories blend food, memory, and quiet acts of rebellion, and he still judges a town by the quality of its morning bread.
