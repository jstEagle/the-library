## About the author

Gideon Hartwell is the pen name of a former boarding school archivist who spent three years cataloguing decades of confiscated notes, unsent letters, and yearbooks with faces cut out. They write young adult fiction about the students history tries to forget, and they still answer their group chat within four minutes.
