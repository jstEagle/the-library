## About the author

Casper Eriksen is the pen name of a writer who grew up in a coastal town where the high school parking lot covered the foundations of a cannery, and who has spent years collecting the recipes and rumors locals refused to write down. This is their second novel for young adults, following a debut praised for its salt-stung atmosphere and stubborn, tender protagonists.
