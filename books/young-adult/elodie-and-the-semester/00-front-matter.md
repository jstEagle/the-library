# Elodie and the Semester

**by Rosalind Hartwell**

> Mirabel Zhou, a baker's kid who bakes feelings into bread, stays under the radar: head down, grades up, locker shut. But when the school's legendary ghost starts leaving notes addressed by name — in impossible ink, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. ## About the author
