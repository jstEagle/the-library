## About the author

August Delacroix grew up in a small farming town where everyone knew everyone's business except their own, an experience that fuels his stories about teenagers outgrowing the silences their towns are built on. He writes from a converted grain barn, where he keeps his grandmother's county fair ribbons pinned above the desk.
