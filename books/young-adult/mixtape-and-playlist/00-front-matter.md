## About the author

Oscar Galloway is the pen name of a former small-town radio DJ who grew up in a community of fewer than a thousand people, where the annual talent show was the only thing that ever filled the gymnasium. They write stories about queer kids finding their voices in places that would rather they stayed quiet.
