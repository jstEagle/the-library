# Orsen and the Tryouts

**by Zephyrine Montrose**

> Zephyrine Vane, a coder whose anonymous account knows too much, counts days until graduation like a sentence being served. But when the college essay prompt asks one honest question nobody can stop answering, she discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, she must win the championship that doesn't matter — and the one that does before the final audition.

## About the author

Zephyrine Montrose is a former debate coach from coastal Maine who writes about teenagers forced to grow up in institutions that prefer them silent. Her short fiction has appeared in three small literary journals under other names, which she considers research.
