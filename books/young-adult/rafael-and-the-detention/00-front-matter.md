## About the author

Ada Delacroix is the pen name of a former high-school debate coach who spent six years watching teenagers argue beautifully about things that didn't matter and bravely about things that did. She writes from a small coastal town, where she keeps a drawer full of confiscated cheat sheets and unfinished speeches.
