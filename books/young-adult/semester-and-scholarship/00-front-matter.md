## About the author

Beatrix Winterbourne is the pen name of a former boarding school teacher who spent a decade watching brilliant students hide how much they were carrying. She writes young adult fiction about kids who learn that survival isn't the same as living. She lives near the coast with two rescue cats and an unreasonable number of annotated paperbacks.
