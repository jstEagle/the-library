## About the author

Ada Yarrow grew up in a small river town where the high school burned down twice and nobody ever said why, an experience that taught her how communities curate their own pasts. She writes young adult fiction about queer kids who refuse to be footnotes, and lives somewhere with better libraries than her hometown ever managed.
