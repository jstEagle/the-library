## About the author

Y. M. Dunmore grew up in a fading mill town where the neighborhood kids communicated almost entirely through one chaotic group chat, an experience that shapes every story they tell. They write young adult fiction about fierce, overlooked kids who discover that belonging is something you build, not something you're granted.
