## About the author

Juniper Marchetti is the pen name of a former line cook who grew up above her family's bakery in a rust-belt quarry town, where local legends were served alongside the morning rolls. She writes young adult stories about quiet kids who discover that small towns keep their secrets the way ovens keep heat.
