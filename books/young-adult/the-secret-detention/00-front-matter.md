## About the author

V. M. Eastwick is the pen name of a former night-shift nurse who wrote this debut on break-room napkins and lunch-hour drafts. Eastwick grew up in a rust-belt town where everyone knew what the school board preferred forgotten, and writes stories about kids who keep secrets they never agreed to carry.
