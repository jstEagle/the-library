## About the author

Sable Iversen grew up bouncing between public schools and a prestigious academy on financial aid, an experience that taught her how institutions protect themselves and how students survive them anyway. She writes young adult fiction about outsiders who refuse to stay quiet, from a small apartment full of plants and half-finished manuscripts.
