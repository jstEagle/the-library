# The Secret Semester

**by G. M. Vasquez**

> Gideon Ravensworth, a debate champion who can't win the argument at home, has spent seven years being so careful it hurts. But when the college essay prompt asks one honest question nobody can stop answering, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. To get through this, he must decide who to be when the labels come off before the last game of the season.

## About the author

G. M. Vasquez is a former high school debate coach who spent a decade watching teenagers argue beautifully about things they couldn't say at home. The Secret Semester is their second novel and was drafted entirely in the margins of tournament ballots.
