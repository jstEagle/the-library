## About the author

Ulyana Amberdale grew up above her grandmother's bakery in a rust-belt town where everyone knew everyone's business, which taught her early how secrets rise like dough. She writes young adult stories about quiet kids forced into loud choices, and still judges people by what they order at 6 a.m.
