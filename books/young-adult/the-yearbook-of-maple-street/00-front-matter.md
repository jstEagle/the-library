## About the author

Ines Blackwood grew up in a rust-belt town where the high school yearbooks were kept in a locked cabinet no one ever opened, a detail that haunted her into adulthood. She writes young adult fiction about kids who carry more than their share, and she still can't resist a story about old paper holding old secrets.
