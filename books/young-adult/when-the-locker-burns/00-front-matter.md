## About the author

Felix Pemberton is the pen name of a former high school debate coach who spent eleven years watching teenagers say true things only when no one was grading them. He writes from a small town in Vermont, where he still judges novice tournaments and keeps every unsent college essay students have slipped into his mailbox.
