## About the author

Lena Eastwick grew up in a shuttered mill town in the Pacific Northwest, where she spent more afternoons than she'll admit exploring condemned school buildings. She writes queer small-town gothics for teens who know that some ghosts are just truths nobody wanted buried with them.
