# When the Mixtape Wakes

**by Mirabel Brightwater**

> Odile Reyes, a debate champion who can't win the argument at home, stays under the radar: head down, grades up, locker shut. But when grandma's recipe box contains directions that aren't for food, he discovers that staying invisible was never really an option. Now he must win the championship that doesn't matter — and the one that does, and time is already running out.

## About the author

Mirabel Brightwater grew up in a border town where every kitchen smelled like someone else's grandmother's cooking, and she never stopped believing recipes could be spells. She writes young adult stories about kids who find their voices in the last place anyone thought to listen.
