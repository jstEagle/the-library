## About the author

Elodie Ravensworth grew up in a small river town where one annual festival decided everything, an experience that left her permanently suspicious of traditions nobody is allowed to question. She writes young adult stories about sharp-tongued girls who learn that winning an argument and changing a mind are two different victories.
