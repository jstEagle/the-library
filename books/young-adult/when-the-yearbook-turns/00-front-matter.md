## About the author

Edmund Kavanagh is the pen name of a former boarding-school teacher who spent a decade watching institutions protect themselves instead of their students. He writes young adult fiction about kids who refuse to be quiet, and lives near the coast with two rescue dogs and an unhealthy archive of old yearbooks.
