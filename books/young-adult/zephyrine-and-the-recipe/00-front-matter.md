## About the author

Quentin Amberdale is the pen name of a former small-town history teacher who spent a decade watching students discover that the past is never as tidy as the plaques claim. He writes YA contemporary fiction about outsiders who learn that truth-telling is a muscle, and lives in a restored farmhouse full of unreliable local archives.
